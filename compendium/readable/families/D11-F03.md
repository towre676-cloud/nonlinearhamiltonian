## Central question

What mathematical contract turns a fitted pattern into a justified inference about unseen outcomes?

## Synopsis

The inference strand develops from an identifiable logistic design and calibrated residual energy to finite strategy search, PAC–Bayes bounds, rank statistics and exact randomization. Its organizing distinction is between fitting a chosen sample, identifying a model parameter, calibrating a test under a null, and predicting a new draw. Each asks a different question. A low residual proves a relation within a specified sample geometry; it does not automatically validate a causal mechanism or a future performance claim. This family supplies positive mathematical tools for stronger questions: declare the sampling law, expose the selection step, reserve independent data, and report an uncertainty statement whose quantifiers survive that selection.

## Definitions

A parameter is identifiable if distinct admissible values produce distinct observation laws at the stated design.

For a fixed predictor f and bounded loss in [0,1], true risk R(f) is the expected loss under a declared sampling distribution, while empirical risk is the sample mean.

A valid p-value obeys P_0(p≤α)≤α for every α∈[0,1] under the stated null. Pairing alone does not supply the null.

AUROC is P(s_+>s_−)+(1/2)P(s_+=s_−) for independent uniform empirical positive and negative choices; its tie rule belongs to the definition.

## Theorem

For K fixed candidates evaluated on n iid validation observations with loss in [0,1], with probability at least 1−δ all risk errors are at most ε=√(log(2K/δ)/(2n)). The empirically best candidate then has true risk at most 2ε above the best candidate. A paired fair-sign randomization test has a finite superuniform p-value under its exchangeable sign null.

## Proof

Hoeffding's bound gives P(|Rhat_k−R_k|>ε)≤2e^{−2nε²} for one fixed candidate. A union bound over K choices yields δ at the stated ε. On the simultaneous event, R(fhat)≤Rhat(fhat)+ε≤Rhat(f*)+ε≤R(f*)+2ε. The candidates must be fixed independently of this validation sample, or their entire adaptive selection must be included in a stronger valid argument. For the randomization result, condition on the observed magnitudes and enumerate every equally likely sign assignment. Ranking the test statistic within that finite null population makes the upper-tail rank p superuniform; ties only make it conservative. This proof is finite and requires no normal approximation.

## Worked example: setup

Eight paired differences have illustrative magnitudes all one, and the observed signs are all positive. A separate two-class score example has positive scores (2,1) and negative scores (1,0).

## Worked example: calculation

There are 2^8=256 sign vectors. Only one gives signed sum eight, so the one-sided exact p-value is 1/256. The absolute statistic has two extremes, giving 1/128. For AUROC, the four positive-negative comparisons yield wins 1,1,0,1 with the tied comparison replaced by 1/2, so the total is 7/2 and AUROC=7/8. Every strictly increasing score transformation preserves these comparisons. Multiplying all paired magnitudes by a positive constant leaves randomization p unchanged, demonstrating that statistical evidence and practical effect size are different outputs.

## Worked example: meaning

The sources on regressions, periodic contrasts, spectral fits and normalized residuals become useful when attached to this contract. A period-matched difference can identify an exact trend in a declared model; a nested regression always improves training fit; neither alone supplies a future-risk bound. The best revised question names both the estimand and the independent evidence that could challenge it.

## Limits

No historical raw paired samples, strategy logs or model checkpoints were provided for replay. These are mathematical fixtures and guarantees under stated laws. Covariate transfer additionally needs support coverage and conditional invariance. A large correlation can arise from a common cause; an intervention requires causal assumptions beyond its joint table. Zero observed failures yields an upper confidence bound, never a deterministic zero-risk certificate.

## Open questions

For a specified adaptive search, construct a fresh holdout or a selection-aware guarantee and compare its sample cost.

Combine exact randomization inference with a prespecified practical-effect threshold and an independent correctness gate.
