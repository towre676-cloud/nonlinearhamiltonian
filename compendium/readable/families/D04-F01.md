## Central question

Which maps descend through a quotient, and which reversible constructions preserve the whole mathematical problem?

## Synopsis

The categorical thread becomes strongest when a named operation is replaced by a construction with a testable mapping property. A quotient first declares which distinctions are erased. A pushout identifies two copies of shared data. An adjunction converts one kind of mapping problem into another, while an equivalence supplies inverse functors and natural comparison maps. These statements organize mathematics across the archive because they separate an object from a chosen presentation and make compatibility part of the theorem. They also distinguish several levels of preservation: agreement of dimensions is weaker than an isomorphism, an isomorphism of individual objects is weaker than a natural equivalence, and an equivalence of categories must respect the specified morphisms.

The useful starting point is the category of linear dynamical systems. An object is a vector space with an endomorphism A; a morphism L satisfies LA=BL. This single equation guarantees L exp(tA)=exp(tB)L by the power series, so composition preserves dynamics rather than merely relabeling coordinates. The tensor–Hom correspondence then makes composition of structures explicit. Currying is available for arbitrary algebraic vector spaces, whereas a duality datum with coevaluation requires finite dimension. This distinction prevents an infinite formal basis sum from being treated as an algebraic tensor. Reconstruction has a similar demand: a finite group can be recovered from the natural automorphisms of the forgetful functor on all finite G-sets, using the regular G-set and its equivariant maps; one representation or one dimension table cannot carry the same information.

## Definitions

For linear maps i:A→B and j:A→C, their pushout is P=(B⊕C)/R with R={(i(a),−j(a)):a∈A}. The structure maps send b and c to their corresponding quotient classes.

A categorical equivalence consists of functors F and G with natural isomorphisms GF≅Id and FG≅Id. An adjunction instead supplies natural bijections of Hom sets, with unit and counit satisfying the triangle identities.

In algebraic vector spaces, a duality datum ev:W⊗V→k and coev:k→V⊗W must reconstruct every vector through both triangle identities. Every algebraic tensor is a finite sum.

## Theorem

The stated P is a pushout of i and j. Its dimension is dimB+dimC−rank(a↦(i(a),−j(a))). In particular the subtraction is dimA when at least one of i or j is injective. A vector space is dualizable under algebraic tensor product exactly when it is finite dimensional.

## Proof

Given compatible maps u:B→V and v:C→V with ui=vj, the map (b,c)↦u(b)+v(c) kills every (i(a),−j(a)). It therefore descends uniquely to P because the quotient map is surjective. Conversely every map from P gives such a compatible pair by composition. Rank-nullity gives the dimension formula, including the correction needed when both legs have a common kernel. For finite-dimensional V choose a basis e_r and its dual basis; coev(1)=Σe_r⊗e^r and evaluation give v=Σe^r(v)e_r. Conversely, if coev(1)=Σ_{r=1}^n v_r⊗w_r, one triangle identity writes every v as Σv_r ev(w_r⊗v). Thus finitely many v_r span V. This proves the finite-dimensional necessity directly, rather than inferring duality from an informal adjunction symbol.

## Worked example: setup

Take A=Q, B=C=Q², i(a)=(a,0), j(a)=(0,a). Let u(b₁,b₂)=b₁+2b₂ and v(c₁,c₂)=3c₁+c₂.

## Worked example: calculation

The relation is (a,0,0,−a), so P has dimension three. Coordinates (b₁+c₂,b₂,c₁) are invariant under this relation and identify P with Q³. The compatible functional descends to w(x,y,z)=x+2y+3z. For b=(2,1), c=(3,4), w(6,1,3)=17=u(b)+v(c). The equality is a certificate for this mapping problem, not just a matching dimension count.

## Worked example: meaning

Universal properties are practical interfaces: later calculations can use the descended map without choosing representatives. Quotienting is information loss with a declared kernel, while equivalence preserves the specified category. Neither operation automatically supplies a metric, topology, probability model or computationally efficient algorithm.

## Limits

The finite vector-space pushout is not a general homotopy pushout. A quotient metric may be only a pseudometric unless zero-distance classes are further identified. Higher adjunctions need their own coherence data; an equality of dimensions or a formal inverse symbol cannot replace units, counits and naturality.

## Open questions

For the actual proposed quotient, list all generating relations and determine whether their closure commutes with passage to each local carrier.

For a proposed equivalence, construct the two comparison natural transformations and verify their components and triangle identities on generators.
