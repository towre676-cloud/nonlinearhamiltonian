## Central question

How can an exact realization and an independent lower bound prove the smallest recurrence or register dimension?

## Synopsis

State reduction has two logically different tasks. A proposed smaller machine must reproduce every output, and every still smaller machine must be ruled out within a declared encoding class. For linear systems these tasks meet through intertwiners and Hankel rank. A matrix identity transports the entire dynamics, while a nonzero finite minor prevents a lower-dimensional realization. This is stronger than fitting a recurrence to a prefix and more informative than quoting the dimension of the original state. Reachable and observable modes must be considered jointly: the zero-initial input-output order lives on the reachable space modulo its invisible part. A scalar universal output recurrence differs from a recurrence for one initial state, and multiple-output recurrence degree need not equal state dimension. In the min–plus setting a two-by-two response table provides a separate additive-register obstruction; ordinary field rank must not be imported into that semiring without justification.

## Definitions

A linear sequence realization is y_n=cA^n b over Q. Its Hankel matrix has entries H_{ij}=y_{i+j}.

A reduction R with RA=GR, Rb=β and c=dR is an intertwining certificate for y_n=dG^nβ at every n.

The reachable space is span{A^k im B}; the invisible space is ⋂_{k≥0}ker(CA^k). The minimal zero-initial driven state is R/(R∩N).

A finite min–plus rank-one table has entries M_{ij}=u_i+v_j. Such a table has equal diagonal and off-diagonal sums on every two-by-two submatrix.

## Theorem

An r-state linear realization has Hankel rank at most r. An order-r forward recurrence has the same rank bound. Therefore an r-state realization together with a nonzero r-by-r Hankel minor proves exact minimal state dimension and recurrence order r. For scalar universal outputs on an observable quotient, the universal recurrence is the quotient operator’s minimal polynomial; its degree equals that observable quotient dimension.

## Proof

The intertwiner identity gives RA^n=G^nR by induction, and the input/output identities yield equality of every sequence term. Any finite Hankel block factors as rows cA^i times columns A^jb through the realization state space, so its rank cannot exceed the state dimension. A recurrence of order s expresses every later Hankel column as a fixed combination of the preceding s, giving the same lower bound. Conversely Cayley–Hamilton supplies a recurrence of order at most r for an r-state realization. For universal observation recurrences, N is A-invariant because CA^k(Av)=CA^{k+1}v. A polynomial p annihilates every future output exactly when p(A)V⊆N, or p(A_bar)=0. In the scalar observable case a lower-degree polynomial would make all observation rows dependent on fewer initial rows, contradicting observability.

## Worked example: setup

Take A=diag(1,2,3,4,5,6), b=(1,1,1,0,0,0)^T and c=b^T. Also test the stipulated min–plus table [[0,2],[2,0]].

## Worked example: calculation

Projection onto the first three coordinates gives y_n=1+2^n+3^n, beginning 3,6,14,36,98. The leading three-by-three Hankel determinant is 4, the squared Vandermonde determinant on 1,2,3. The recurrence y_{n+3}=6y_{n+2}−11y_{n+1}+6y_n is globally certified and has exact order three. For the min–plus table the diagonal sum is 0 and the other sum is 4, excluding a one-register additive factorization. A two-register factorization through the tropical identity gives a matching upper bound. This last obstruction concerns that specified linear min–plus encoding class, not arbitrary nonlinear memory.

## Worked example: meaning

A finite witness proves an infinite-time minimality claim only because a global realization identity supplies the model class and upper bound.

## Limits

Finite-prefix rank can underestimate later behavior without a proven finite-dimensional model. Approximate numerical rank does not establish exact recurrence order. Multiple-output universal polynomial degree may be smaller than observable dimension: A=C=I has degree one in any dimension. The historical determinant-four tropical matrix was not recovered; the two-by-two example is newly stipulated.

## Open questions
