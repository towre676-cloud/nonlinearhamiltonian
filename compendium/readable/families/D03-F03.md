## Central question

What does a specified symmetry force, and which numerical features remain free after imposing it?

## Synopsis

A symmetry label becomes mathematical information only after a group acts on a stated space. The collection repeatedly returns to Weyl groups, permutation characters, signed permutations, crystals and branching. These topics connect through invariant subspaces and orbit decompositions, not through shared large integers. The useful question is what the actual action fixes, which representations occur, and what independent parameters survive in the commuting algebra.

The elementary averaging projector is the organising construction. It turns a finite group action into an exact invariant subspace over a field where the group order is invertible. Character traces then measure multiplicities, and orbit splitting gives concrete restrictions and induced representations. Low-rank Weyl calculations provide a more structured version: a signed orbit sum becomes a polynomial identity after fixing the root coordinates and normalization. The A₂ denominator can be expanded completely; a nonconstant Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial requires its actual Bruhat interval and Hecke convention. Neither object is a universal score of computational complexity.

The signed-permutation examples are especially useful because they expose the limit of a symmetry inference. Over F₃, sign changes interchange the two nonzero entries, so the B₄ orbits are exactly the five Hamming-weight classes. Over the reals, invariance of a symmetric matrix under all independent sign changes kills off-diagonal entries, and permutation invariance makes its diagonal entries equal. This forces a scalar matrix on that irreducible coordinate representation, but two copies of a representation still admit independent scalar blocks and hence arbitrary positive frequency ratios. Symmetry narrows a model class; it need not pick a numerical constant, an empirical rank or a physical scale.

## Definitions

A representation is a homomorphism ρ:G→GL(V); its invariants are V^G={v:ρ(g)v=v for all g}.

The character χ_V(g)=trρ(g) records the trace of the actual action, not just dimV=χ_V(1).

The signed permutation group B_n=C₂^n⋊S_n changes coordinate signs and permutes coordinates.

A Weyl denominator is an alternating group-algebra sum; its signs and root lattice are part of the input.

## Theorem

If G is finite and |G| is invertible in k, R=|G|⁻¹Σ_gρ(g) is an idempotent with image V^G. For the B_n action on F₃^n, the orbits are indexed by Hamming weight j and have sizes 2^j binom(n,j).

## Proof

For h∈G, ρ(h)R=|G|⁻¹Σ_gρ(hg)=R because left multiplication permutes G. Hence every vector in imR is invariant. If v is invariant then Rv=v, so imR=V^G and R²=R. The division hypothesis is essential; in characteristic dividing |G| this formula is unavailable and invariant functors need not be exact. For the orbit claim, every group element preserves the number of nonzero coordinates. Conversely, two vectors of equal weight can first have their supports matched by a coordinate permutation, then have their entries matched by sign changes, since F₃\{0}={1,−1}. Thus weight is a complete orbit invariant. Choose the support in binom(n,j) ways and independently choose each nonzero sign in 2^j ways. This proves both completeness and the sizes without enumerating all group elements.

## Worked example: calculation

The orbit sizes are 1,8,24,32,16 and sum to 81. Their stabilizer sizes are 384,48,16,12,24, giving orbit×stabilizer=384 in each case. For a symmetric real κ invariant under every coordinate sign flip, choose the flip of coordinate i; its conjugation reverses every off-diagonal entry in row or column i, forcing those entries to zero. Conjugation by coordinate permutations then forces all diagonal entries equal. Thus κ=cI. On V⊕V, however, K=diag(a²I,b²I) commutes with ρ(g)⊕ρ(g) for every a,b>0. Its two oscillation frequencies have freely chosen ratio b/a, so no particular ratio follows from equivariance alone.

## Worked example: meaning

An orbit computation can be complete at a stated finite level while a proposed exceptional-group action remains unconstructed. Large familiar orders are consistency constraints, not replacements for matrices or relations.

## Limits

This family does not construct Monster moonshine, every KL polynomial, an E₈ crystal or a faithful W(E₈) action on F₃⁴. Equality of coefficients or dimensions requires a separately defined representation before it gains that meaning.

## Open questions
