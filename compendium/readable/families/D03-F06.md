## Central question

What do a faithful state and its dynamics determine exactly in a finite algebra?

## Synopsis

The operator-algebra discussions become most transparent when the algebra, state and representation are separated. In M_d(C), a faithful state is given by a positive density matrix ρ. It defines expectations on observables, an inner-product geometry on matrix space and a modular flow. A thermal Hamiltonian H defines another flow through conjugation. A Gibbs state links the two with a temperature-dependent rescaling and sign, but the state Hamiltonian −logρ is not the same object as the standard-form modular generator acting on matrices.

This distinction is one of the strongest connections across the later years. On the Hilbert space of Hilbert–Schmidt matrices, left multiplication represents the observable algebra and right multiplication represents its commutant. The modular operator is Δ=L_ρR_{ρ⁻¹}. Its eigenvalues on matrix units are ratios p_i/p_j, so its generator has spectral gaps rather than the original energy levels. A second faithful state produces a unitary relative cocycle with a twisted multiplication law. These are exact finite models of powerful general constructions, with all domains automatic and every formula inspectable.

Equilibrium also requires a declared dynamics convention. With α_t(A)=e^{itH}Ae^{−itH}, the Gibbs state satisfies φ(Aα_{iβ}(B))=φ(BA). The boundary identity follows from cyclicity of trace and extends to all matrices because finite-dimensional imaginary-time conjugation is entire. This offers a meaningful interactive calculation: change populations, inspect modular frequencies and test the KMS relation. It does not turn a fitted ratio q=e^{−βE} into a deformed quantum group, or establish infinite type-III modular inclusions, holographic identities or geometric field equations.

## Definitions

A faithful density matrix is ρ>0 with trρ=1, defining φ(A)=tr(ρA).

On Hilbert–Schmidt matrix space, L_A(X)=AX and R_A(X)=XA.

The finite modular operator is Δρ(X)=ρXρ⁻¹; J(X)=X* is antiunitary.

For α_t(A)=e^{itH}Ae^{−itH}, the KMS convention here is φ(Aα_{iβ}(B))=φ(BA).

## Theorem

For Ω=ρ^{1/2}, the Tomita map S(AΩ)=A*Ω satisfies S=JΔρ^{1/2}. If ρ=Z⁻¹e^{−βH}, φ is α-invariant and obeys the stated KMS identity. The modular generator −logΔρ has eigenvalues log(p_j/p_i) on E_ij.

## Proof

Every matrix X can be written Aρ^{1/2} because ρ is invertible. Therefore S(X)=(Xρ^{−1/2})*ρ^{1/2}=ρ^{−1/2}X*ρ^{1/2}. Meanwhile Δρ^{1/2}X=ρ^{1/2}Xρ^{−1/2}; applying J gives exactly the same expression. In a ρ-eigenbasis, ΔρE_ij=(p_i/p_j)E_ij, yielding the generator formula by finite functional calculus. For KMS, α_{iβ}(B)=e^{−βH}Be^{βH}. Multiply φ(Aα_{iβ}B) by Z and cyclically move the final e^{βH} to the front: tr(e^{−βH}Ae^{−βH}Be^{βH})=tr(Ae^{−βH}B)=tr(e^{−βH}BA). Division by Z proves the identity. Since ρ commutes with e^{itH}, cyclicity also proves φ(α_tA)=φ(A). Positivity and normalization follow directly from ρ≥0 and trρ=1.

## Worked example: setup

Take ρ=diag(3/4,1/4), with βE=log3 for H=diag(0,E), and use A=E₀₁,B=E₁₀.

## Worked example: calculation

The modular operator multiplies E₀₁ by three and E₁₀ by one third, fixing both diagonal matrix units. Its generator therefore has eigenvalues 0,0,−log3,+log3 on matrix space. The state Hamiltonian −logρ instead has just the two values log(4/3),log4 on the original two-dimensional space. At imaginary thermal time, α_{iβ}(B)=B/3. Hence φ(Aα_{iβ}B)=(1/3)(3/4)=1/4=φ(BA). Matrix-unit multiplication checks the same identity for a basis of every A,B. The zero modular frequencies form the diagonal commutant of ρ; degeneracy of populations enlarges that fixed algebra.

## Worked example: meaning

An operator state supplies a complete finite equilibrium model, including its faithful-state hypothesis and normalization. Reparametrizing a population ratio changes no multiplication law.

## Interactive contract: status

Worked two-level recipe; no general infinite operator-algebra or thermal simulator is implemented.

## Interactive contract: inputs

Positive definite density matrix, Hamiltonian, inverse temperature and observable matrices.

## Interactive contract: outputs

Modular ratios, fixed algebra, KMS boundary residual and faithful-state diagnostics.

## Interactive contract: validation

Check trace one, strict positivity and the chosen dynamics sign; verify basis identities exactly for rational population ratios.

## Limits

The standard form is finite and faithful. Singular states need support reduction; infinite von Neumann algebras can have no density matrix or trace. The finite derivation does not prove type-III, DHR, JLMS or entanglement-to-geometry claims.

## Open questions
