## Central question

Which uncertainty is reducible by precision, and which requires a different observation?

## Synopsis

The covariance strand connects finite quadratic forms, data fusion, singular maps and statistical observability. Its central revision is to separate an exact law from a linearized uncertainty approximation, and to separate noise in an observed coordinate from a target component that the observation never determines. Positive semidefiniteness is a structural constraint on covariances, not merely a numerical preference. The observation sigma-algebra determines what can be estimated; the loss determines what error means. Together these ideas give a precise route from a hidden coordinate to an irreducible risk, while preventing a zero Jacobian from being read as zero nonlinear uncertainty.

## Definitions

For X∈L², Σ=E[(X−EX)(X−EX)ᵀ] is positive semidefinite; singularity records an almost-sure linear relation.

The exact output law under f is the pushforward f_#μ. The delta approximation ε²JΣJᵀ applies to f(x_0+εZ) only as an asymptotic statement with appropriate moments and remainders.

For target T∈L² and observation Z, the squared-loss Bayes estimator is E[T|Z] and its risk is E Var(T|Z).

The Markov condition T→Z→Y means Y supplies no target information beyond Z; this includes adding independent measurement noise to Z.

## Theorem

Under T→Z→Y, E Var(T|Y)=E Var(T|Z)+E Var(E[T|Z]|Y). Thus the best noisy-observation risk is at least the noise-free hidden-target floor. Separately, for X∼N(0,σ²), Var(X²)=2σ⁴ even though the derivative of x² at zero vanishes.

## Proof

Write m=E[T|Z], e=T−m. Conditional independence gives E[e|Z,Y]=0, hence E[T|Y]=E[m|Y]. Expand T−E[T|Y]=e+m−E[m|Y]. Conditioning on Z,Y kills the cross term, so the mean squared error is E e²+E(m−E[m|Y])². Conditional-mean optimality itself follows by decomposing T−a(Y) into T−E[T|Y] and E[T|Y]−a(Y). The nonlinear example uses E X²=σ² and E X⁴=3σ⁴. Its full density follows by the two inverse branches ±√y and their Jacobians. The nonzero variance is of order σ⁴; the failed linear term would have been order σ², so the issue is an altered leading order rather than a contradiction.

## Worked example: setup

Take independent X_1 and Z_3∼N(0,σ²), target T=X_1+Z_3², and observe X_1 exactly. Compare independent repeated sensors with identical copies of one sensor.

## Worked example: calculation

The best estimate is X_1+σ² and risk is 2σ⁴. Repeating a noiseless observation of X_1 leaves the observation sigma-algebra unchanged. A sensor informative about Z_3 can improve the risk because it enlarges that sigma-algebra. For n independent observations of a scalar mean with noise variance s², averaging has variance s²/n. For n identical copies of one noise realization, Σ=s²11ᵀ and every unbiased weighted average has variance s². If local covariances C_j and means μ_j are mixed with p_j≥0, total covariance is Σp_jC_j+Σp_j(μ_j−μ)(μ_j−μ)ᵀ, preserving positivity.

## Worked example: meaning

This family connects experimental design directly to target information. More repetitions can remove measurement noise; a new kind of observation can remove hidden-target ambiguity. A coordinate covariance, a worst-case fibre width and a Bayes risk are distinct quantities with distinct units and quantifiers. The atlas makes those distinctions operational in its worked examples.

## Limits

Second moments and the Markov hypothesis are essential. An extra sensor correlated with the hidden residual is not a noisier version of the old observation. A signed correction can leave the PSD cone even if it improves one scalar statistic. The Gaussian example gives an exact model, not a calibration for unprovided observations. General nonlinear filtering and empirical conditional-variance estimation remain recipes.

## Open questions

For a specified polynomial target and Gaussian hidden variables, compute the first nonzero uncertainty order and bound its Taylor remainder.

Design the cheapest finite sensor panel whose observation fibres reduce a stated risk or decision margin below a specified threshold.
