## Central question

How far can exact local and group-law certificates take us toward rational rank, and where do descent and global conjectures enter?

## Synopsis

Elliptic arithmetic joins several structures whose numerical outputs must not be conflated. A point count over a finite field gives a Frobenius trace. A rational group-law calculation gives an exact point and its multiples. Good reduction can rule out rational torsion, producing a rank lower bound from one nonzero point. A height pairing then certifies independence and measures the covolume of a chosen subgroup, while a Selmer computation can give an upper bound with an additional Tate–Shafarevich contribution. Each is valuable on its own. The most coherent cross-year example is E:y²+y=x³−x, of discriminant 37: exact finite counts in 2024 recur as group and rank certificates in 2026. That continuity supports a reusable arithmetic laboratory recipe, while the stronger assertions of exact rank, BSD or complete integral points need separate proofs.

## Definitions

For a good prime p, a_p=p+1−#E(F_p) and the local polynomial is 1−a_p t+pt². Good reduction means the discriminant is nonzero modulo p.

For E:y²+y=x³−x, negation is (x,y)↦(x,−y−1). Rational points form an abelian group with identity O.

The canonical height pairing is positive definite on E(Q) modulo torsion. A Gram determinant for a subgroup of index I is I² times the full-lattice regulator.

## Theorem

For E:y²+y=x³−x, the rational torsion subgroup is trivial and P=(0,0) has infinite order, so rank E(Q)≥1. The exact point counts #E(F₅)=8 and #E(F₇)=9 suffice, provided the prime-to-p restriction on torsion reduction is respected.

## Proof

The discriminant is 37, so both primes are good. Suppose there is a rational torsion point of prime order ℓ. If ℓ≠5, injection of ℓ-torsion under reduction at 5 forces ℓ|8 and thus ℓ=2. Reduction at 7 then forces 2|9, impossible. If ℓ=5, reduction at 7 instead forces 5|9, also impossible. Every nontrivial finite abelian torsion group contains an element of prime order, so all rational torsion is excluded. The point (0,0) is nonzero and lies on E, hence has infinite order. This proves a rank lower bound without computing an L-function. For heights, bilinearity gives G′=MᵀGM under a generator-coordinate matrix M and therefore det G′=(det M)²det G. A positive Gram determinant proves independence of the displayed points but says nothing about saturation unless the index is certified. The exact Selmer sequence adds the separate quotient Sha[n] to E(Q)/nE(Q); a Selmer cardinality is therefore not automatically a count of visible rational generators.

## Worked example: calculation

Enumerating y²+y=x³−x over F₅ gives seven affine points plus O, while over F₇ it gives eight affine points plus O. At P=(0,0), the tangent slope is (3x²−1)/(2y+1)=−1, yielding 2P=(1,0). The line through P and 2P has slope zero, yielding 3P=(−1,−1). Exact addition gives 7P=(−5/9,8/27), where both sides of the equation are 280/729. Independently, for a short Weierstrass curve y²=x³+ax+b, clearing the denominator in the doubling formula gives a quartic condition on the x-coordinate of a half-point; every candidate must still satisfy the original curve and group equation.

## Worked example: meaning

The finite reductions establish the global non-torsion result through a stated theorem; the visible multiples verify the arithmetic implementation but are not themselves the proof of infinite order.

## Interactive contract: validation

## Limits

No general elliptic solver or certified height engine is implemented here. The rank-one lower bound is not an upper bound, and an analytic determinant analogy is not BSD. Halving quartics yield candidates requiring sign and exceptional-case checks. Genus-two counts require their own Frobenius degree and cannot use an elliptic quadratic factor.

## Open questions

Can a documented descent give an independent upper bound matching the exhibited non-torsion subgroup, with saturation certified?

For multiplication fibres, can the field of definition, rational torsion kernel and all exceptional doubling cases be incorporated into a complete exact candidate procedure?
