## Central question

What exact structure follows from conductor 1680, and which additional certificates are needed to connect it to another representation?

## Synopsis

The number 384 becomes mathematically productive when assigned a specific role: it is the degree of Q(ζ₁₆₈₀), not the order of a primitive root, the largest Galois-element order, a Lie algebra dimension or a category rank. Distinguishing those meanings allows a much stronger construction. The subgroup corresponding to Q(ζ₃₀) produces uniform fibres of size 48; trace transport is then a theorem of the field tower and can be checked through finite residue classes. This organizes a recurring numerical framework into an auditable arithmetic object. The same discipline applies to trace orthogonality: a coefficient p, rather than p+1, follows from which residue sums are actually being taken. Any proposed passage to an integral E₈ lattice must exhibit a basis matrix, its Gram form and an intertwining equation. Equal degree or a handful of coincident traces cannot supply those maps.

## Definitions

For N≥1, ζ_N=exp(2πi/N); Gal(Q(ζ_N)/Q) identifies with U_N=(Z/NZ)× by σ_a(ζ_N)=ζ_N^a.

The Ramanujan sum c_N(n)=Σ_(a∈U_N)ζ_N^(an) is an integer and equals Σ_(d|gcd(N,n))d μ(N/d).

For L⊆K and x∈L, Tr_(K/Q)(x)=[K:L]Tr_(L/Q)(x). Relative trace is a linear map, not a choice of an integral basis.

## Theorem

Let K=Q(ζ₁₆₈₀), L=Q(ζ₃₀), and η=ζ₁₆₈₀^56. Then [K:Q]=384, [L:Q]=8, [K:L]=48; reduction U₁₆₈₀→U₃₀ is onto with every fibre of size 48. Hence Tr_(K/Q)(η^n)=48c₃₀(n). The Galois group of K is C₄²×C₂³×C₃ and has exponent 12.

## Proof

Since 1680=16·3·5·7, the totient product is 8·2·4·6=384; φ(30)=1·2·4=8. The order of ζ₁₆₈₀^56 is 1680/gcd(1680,56)=30, giving L⊆K and the tower degree. A class coprime to 30 can be extended to a unit modulo 1680 by prescribing a compatible odd residue modulo 16 and a nonzero residue modulo 7; CRT proves surjectivity. A surjective finite-group homomorphism has fibres equal to kernel cosets, all of size 384/8. Summing η^(an) across these fibres proves the trace formula. For the group structure CRT gives U₁₆×U₃×U₅×U₇≅(C₄×C₂)×C₂×C₄×C₆, and C₆≅C₂×C₃. The least common multiple of these cyclic orders is 12, so the group cannot be cyclic of order 384. These conclusions use classical cyclotomic irreducibility as a structural input; the residue enumeration independently verifies the finite arithmetic.

## Worked example: calculation

Enumerating 0≤a<1680 with gcd(a,1680)=1 gives 384 residues. Reducing modulo 30 produces precisely 1,7,11,13,17,19,23,29, each 48 times. Inclusion–exclusion gives c₃₀(1)=−1, c₃₀(2)=1, c₃₀(15)=−8 and c₃₀(30)=8. The associated K-traces are −48,48,−384,384. Complex conjugation is the nontrivial involution a↦−a, so its fixed maximal real subfield has degree 192. This is distinct from the extension degree 48 and the number of target phases eight.
