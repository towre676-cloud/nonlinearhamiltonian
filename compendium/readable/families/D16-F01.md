## Central question

Which observations distinguish states, and which transformations preserve their physical probability structure?

## Synopsis

The quantum information strand is best organised around a state, an observation map and an allowed transformation. A density matrix carries both populations and coherences; a measurement sees only selected linear functionals of that matrix. A channel must preserve positivity even when the system is coupled to an arbitrary ancilla, which is stronger than preserving trace or ordinary positivity. An instrument additionally records the classical measurement branch. These distinctions connect the early coherence example to later dephasing, error correction, leakage and sequential postselection discussions.

The Bell state and its fully dephased mixture are an especially clear organising pair. Computational-basis probabilities, diagonal parity and both one-qubit marginals agree exactly, while an off-diagonal correlation separates them. This is a complete finite instance of observation-relative identifiability, linking quantum tomography to the atlas-wide question of what a target can be reconstructed from. It does not imply that all measurements are blind; it identifies one observation algebra and an explicit additional observable that resolves its collision.

The transformation side is equally constructive. Kraus operators certify complete positivity by turning the channel tensored with any ancilla into a sum of positive conjugations. Their completeness equation certifies total probability. Dephasing changes only selected coherences, amplitude damping transfers populations and depolarisation contracts every traceless component. Error correction requires an actual error family: the three-qubit repetition code corrects single bit flips but has a weight-one logical phase error, hence quantum distance one. Sequential instruments force the success probability to remain visible through every branch. Normalizing a successful state is legitimate conditional probability, but cannot convert a rare success into an unconditional implementation guarantee.

## Definitions

A density matrix ρ is positive semidefinite with trρ=1; an effect satisfies 0≤E≤I and has probability trρE.

A Kraus channel is E(ρ)=Σ_rK_rρK_r*, with Σ_rK_r*K_r=I.

An instrument has branches I_a(ρ)=Σ_rK_arρK_ar*; only their sum is required to preserve trace.

Knill–Laflamme correction for code projector P requires PE_a*E_bP=c_abP for the specified error family.

## Theorem

For Bell state ρ_B=|Φ⁺⟩⟨Φ⁺| and mixture ρ_M=(|00⟩⟨00|+|11⟩⟨11|)/2, all diagonal observations and both one-qubit marginals agree, but tr(ρ_B XX)=1 and tr(ρ_M XX)=0. Their trace distance is 1/2. Sequential complete instruments preserve total probability after summing all branches.

## Proof

The difference D=ρ_B−ρ_M=(|00⟩⟨11|+|11⟩⟨00|)/2 has zero diagonal, so tr(DE)=0 for every diagonal E. Partial trace removes the off-diagonal tensor terms, proving marginal equality. XX exchanges 00 and 11, so tr(DXX)=1. On their two-dimensional span D has eigenvalues ±1/2 and vanishes elsewhere; ||D||₁/2=1/2. For sequential instruments, let first-stage Kraus operators be K_ar and second-stage ones L_bs|a with Σ_arK_ar*K_ar=I and Σ_bsL_bs|a*L_bs|a=I for each a. The composed operators are L_bs|aK_ar. Summing their adjoint products yields Σ_arK_ar*(Σ_bsL_bs|a*L_bs|a)K_ar=I. The same Kraus form proves complete positivity. This algebra establishes the unconditional map; a selected branch has trace equal to its actual success probability.

## Worked example: setup

Apply dephasing to the first qubit of a Bell pair, with rational coherence parameter q=1/3.

## Worked example: calculation

The channel is E_q(ρ)=((1+q)/2)ρ+((1−q)/2)ZρZ. It produces Bell populations 1/2,1/2 and cross terms q/2. Hence the reduced states remain I/2, ZZ stays one and XX becomes 1/3. Its Kraus squared weights are 2/3 and 1/3, nonnegative and summing to one. The dephased state has eigenvalues (1±q)/2=2/3,1/3 on the Bell subspace, so it is positive. At q=0 it equals ρ_M; at q=1 it equals ρ_B. An independent repetition-code check gives PE_a*E_bP=δ_abP for {I,X₁,X₂,X₃}, while PZ₁P=diag(1,−1) on the code, demonstrating the exact boundary of the protection claim.

## Worked example: meaning

Coherence, entanglement, observation and protection each have explicit witnesses. Their connection is mathematical rather than an inference from a gate or code name.

## Limits

This is finite-dimensional mathematics, not a hardware threshold or a leakage-free anyon implementation. Negative dephasing time need not define a channel. Postselection costs and unobserved branches cannot be discarded.

## Open questions
