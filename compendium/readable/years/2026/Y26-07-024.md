## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does tensor–Hom adjunction prove for arbitrary vector spaces, and which extra finiteness hypothesis produces a dual?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The source’s tensor, internal-Hom and adjunction language has a concrete linear model. Currying works for arbitrary algebraic vector spaces. A coevaluation satisfying both triangle identities is a stronger requirement and forces finite dimension.

## Definitions

All tensor products are algebraic over a field k. Hom denotes all linear maps. A duality datum for V consists of a vector space W, evaluation ev:W⊗V→k and coevaluation coev:k→V⊗W satisfying the two triangle identities.

## Result name

Adjunction and the finite-dimensional duality criterion

## Result statement

Hom(V⊗W,U)≅Hom(V,Hom(W,U)) naturally, without a dimension restriction. A vector space V is dualizable in this tensor category if and only if V is finite-dimensional.

## Proof

Given F:V⊗W→U, send v to the map w↦F(v⊗w). Conversely a linear map v↦G_v gives the bilinear pairing(v,w)↦G_v(w), which factors uniquely through V⊗W. The constructions are inverse by evaluation on pure tensors; naturality follows by composition. For finite-dimensional V with basis e_i and dual basis e^i, take W=V*, ev(φ⊗v)=φ(v) and coev(1)=Σe_i⊗e^i. Either triangle identity reduces to v=Σe^i(v)e_i. Conversely every algebraic tensor coev(1) is a finite sum Σ_{i=1}^r v_i⊗w_i. The triangle identity then writes every v as Σv_i ev(w_i⊗v), so V is spanned by finitely many v_i. This proves necessity and explains why a formal infinite basis sum is not an algebraic tensor. Naturality can also be checked explicitly: replacingv byw(v) before currying is the same as precomposing the resulting mapV→Hom(W,U), and postcomposingF byU→U′ commutes with pointwise postcomposition. Thus the isomorphism respects the actual morphisms, which is the additional content of an adjunction beyond equality of two dimensions.

## Worked example: setup

Take V=Q², W=Q³, U=Q and F(v⊗w)=vᵀMw with M=[[1,2,3],[4,5,6]].

## Worked example: calculation

Currying sends v=(a,b) to the functional with coefficients(a+4b,2a+5b,3a+6b). For v=(2,−1), this is(−2,−1,0), giving F(v⊗(1,2,3))=−4 by either route. Coevaluation for Q² uses exactly e₁⊗e¹+e₂⊗e² and reconstructs(2,−1).

## Worked example: conclusion

The adjunction is an explicit universal-property statement; duality adds a finite-rank identity certificate.

## Limits

Topological tensor products and continuous duals belong to different categories and require separate hypotheses. This proof does not establish unspecified enriched or higher adjunctions.

## Extension

Specify the actual objects, morphisms and monoidal product before proposing higher duality, then test the triangle identities on a finite chain-complex model.
