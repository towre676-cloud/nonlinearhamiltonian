## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a tangent-kernel model actually guarantee, and which labels can it never learn?

## Definitions

For n training observations let f(θ)=Φθ∈R^n, with fixed matrix Φ, and use loss(1/2)||f−y||². Gradient flow is θ′=−Φᵀ(Φθ−y). Put K=ΦΦᵀ.

For a differentiable nonlinear model, K(θ)=J(θ)J(θ)ᵀ is its empirical tangent kernel. The fixed-matrix model is exact; replacing K(θ_t) by K(θ_0) in a nonlinear network requires an approximation theorem.

## Result name

A frozen tangent kernel gives an exact learning dynamics with a visible nullspace

## Result statement

The training residual r=f−y satisfies r′=−Kr and r(t)=e^(−tK)r(0). Its component in ker K remains constant. Its component in the positive spectral subspace decays at least as e^(−λ_min^+t), where λ_min^+ is the smallest positive eigenvalue.

## Proof

Differentiate f along gradient flow to get f′=−ΦΦᵀ(f−y). Diagonalize the symmetric positive-semidefinite K orthogonally. Each spectral coordinate solves r_j′=−λ_jr_j, hence r_j(t)=e^(−λ_jt)r_j(0). A zero eigenvalue preserves its coordinate, while positive coordinates decay. This proves the formula and rate, and also yields loss derivative −rᵀKr≤0. For a nonlinear model the chain rule still gives f′=−K(θ_t)(f−y), but the constant-coefficient exponential solution is unavailable unless the kernel is constant or an additional commutation/approximation argument is supplied. Small training error in either model also says nothing by itself about unseen inputs. That question needs a distribution, target class and a generalization theorem.

## Worked example: setup

Use Φ=(1,1)ᵀ, so K=[[1,1],[1,1]], and start θ=0.

## Worked example: calculation

For y=(1,1), predictions equal(1−e^(−2t))(1,1) and loss is e^(−4t). For y=(1,−1), the target lies in ker K, θ′=0 and loss stays1.

## Worked example: conclusion

The same nonnegative kernel perfectly fits one target direction and completely misses the other. Rank and spectrum determine this finite training conclusion.

## Limits

The model is an exact finite linear feature model. It does not prove a finite-width nonlinear network stays in its tangent regime or has improved out-of-sample performance.

## Extension

Assume a quantitative bound ||K(θ_t)−K_0||≤ε on a time interval and derive a variation-of-constants residual bound relative to the frozen model, with explicit dependence on the positive spectral gap.
