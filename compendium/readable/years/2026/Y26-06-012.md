## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: Why can a locally exact differential have a nonzero closed-loop period?

## Status

Classical theorem or newly stipulated exact model; speculative source assertions delimited

## Summary

The June source demotes a wavelet curl-flux heuristic and retains exact branch periods as a viable obstruction. The logarithm on the punctured plane is a constructive example: every local derivative is regular, but a loop around the missing point detects the obstruction to one global branch.

## Definitions

Let U=C\{0} and η=dz/z. A local branch of logz has differential η. A closed piecewise smooth loop γ in U has integer winding number wind(γ,0).

A globally exact one-form is dF for a single-valued differentiable function F. Such a form has zero integral around every closed loop.

## Result name

Branch periods detect monodromy that a local curl test misses

## Result statement

∮_γη=2πi wind(γ,0). In particular η is closed but not globally exact on U. Analytic continuation of a logarithm around γ changes its value by that period.

## Proof

For the unit circle γ(t)=e^{it},0≤t≤2π, the integrand is i dt and the period is2πi. More generally the map γ/|γ| to the circle has a continuous argument lift along the interval parameterizing the loop; its endpoint change is2πm for an integer m. Writing locally logγ=log|γ|+iargγ and integrating its derivative yields2πim, which is the winding number formula. Closedness follows because η is holomorphic on U, or directly by differentiating in real coordinates. If η=dF globally, the fundamental theorem along a closed loop would give zero, contradicting the unit-circle calculation. The failure is global topology, not a local nonzero curl. In a simply connected slit domain avoiding zero, a branch exists and every loop period vanishes, consistently with the theorem.

## Worked example: setup

Use a circle γ_R(t)=Re^{it} and a twice-wound version.

## Worked example: calculation

The first integral is2πi for everyR>0; the radius cancels. The twice-wound loop gives4πi. A small contractible loop centered away from zero gives zero. Therefore shrinking a loop without crossing the singularity does not remove its period, whereas a pointwise local curl statistic is zero everywhere on U.

## Worked example: conclusion

The source’s retained obstruction has a rigorous invariant and an explicit computation.

## Limits

A period of an actual multi-valued state-integral phase needs that phase’s differential and branch singularities. This example does not validate an unrecovered wavelet-derived obstruction or identify every possible monodromy.

## Extension

For a specified algebraic curve and logarithmic phase, choose a homology basis of cycles, compute all exact residue periods and track their integer monodromy under parameter continuation.
