## Summary

The retrieved static branch claimed an exact two-register lower bound. Such a claim needs an encoding model: arbitrary real encodings can hide many coordinates in one number. For min-plus linear state representations, a small tropical minor gives a clean exact obstruction.

## Definitions

A min-plus rank-one matrix has entries M_(i,j)=u_i+v_j for finite real row and column potentials. More generally a rank-r factorization is a min-plus product with r intermediate coordinates.

For a2×2 finite matrix, a tropical nonsingular minor has unequal diagonal and off-diagonal sums; one of its two tropical determinant terms is uniquely minimal.

## Result name

A two-by-two tropical minor can rule out a one-register linear factorization

## Result statement

The matrix M=[[0,2],[2,0]] cannot have min-plus rank one, but it has a min-plus rank-two factorization. The obstruction is the exact gap4 between its diagonal and off-diagonal sums.

## Proof

If M_(i,j)=u_i+v_j, then M₁₁+M₂₂=u₁+u₂+v₁+v₂=M₁₂+M₂₁. The displayed matrix instead has sums zero and four, contradicting rank one. Rank at most two follows by taking M itself and the2×2 tropical identity, whose diagonal entries are zero and off-diagonal entries infinity. Its product is M, so the lower and upper bounds meet exactly. This proof concerns linear factorization over the min-plus semiring; the gap is not an ordinary Euclidean determinant unless another representation is explicitly introduced.

## Worked example: setup

Interpret the rows as two initial conditions and the columns as two continuation probes in a new model.

## Worked example: calculation

A single additive register would write every response as row-potential plus probe-potential. Its predicted difference between the two probes would be independent of the row. In M that difference is two in the first row and minus two in the second, a contradiction. Retaining two registers reproduces all four responses exactly. This gives a finite observable witness of the necessary state dimension within the stipulated linear encoding class.

## Worked example: conclusion

A register lower bound has a precise model, a four-value obstruction and a matching realization.

## Limits

The historical determinant-four witness matrix was not recovered; this matrix is a newly declared example. The conclusion does not exclude nonlinear encodings, finite-control branching or a different algebra of registers.

## Extension

Extract a response submatrix from the actual continuation tensor, certify a tropical nonsingular minor and produce a matching factorization under exactly the same allowed register operations.
