## Revised question retained in the annual source

Do the beam directions and space-time sample points span all fifteen coefficient directions?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or explicitly defined worked model

## Summary

The local wind model has a definite linear inverse problem. Counting fifteen unknowns is only the beginning: many scalar observations can remain rank deficient if their beam directions or sampling geometry are repeated. A tensor-product design supplies an exact sufficient certificate.

## Definitions

Write u(x,t)=M z with z=(1,x₁,x₂,x₃,t)ᵀ and M a3×5 matrix. This contains three offsets, nine spatial derivatives and three time derivatives.

A scalar directional observation is y=nᵀMz. With column vectorization its design row is zᵀ⊗nᵀ.

## Result name

A local space-time affine velocity has fifteen coefficients but needs a full-rank observation design

## Result statement

Fifteen scalar observations identify M exactly if their15×15 design matrix is invertible. A concrete sufficient design uses five affinely independent space-time points and three independent beam directions at each. With only one fixed beam direction, the rank is at most five regardless of sample count.

## Proof

The scalar identity nᵀMz=(zᵀ⊗nᵀ)vecM follows by expanding coordinates. With the tensor-product design, the total matrix is a Kronecker product Z⊗N, where Z is the5×5 point matrix and N the3×3 beam matrix. Its rank is rankZ·rankN=15 when both are invertible. If every beam is the same n, observations depend only on the five coefficients in the row nᵀM, so the rank cannot exceed five. Two full velocity components can remain invisible even with arbitrarily many repeated samples.

## Worked example: setup

Choose z₀=(1,0,0,0,0) and z_i=z₀+e_(i+1) for i=1,…,4, with beams e₁,e₂,e₃.

## Worked example: calculation

Subtracting the first point row from the remaining four shows detZ=1, and N=I. The design is therefore exactly invertible. The first point gives u*, and the four differences give the three columns of the spatial gradient and the time derivative. These locations and beams define a new algebraic fixture; they are not claimed to be an actual radar scan.

## Worked example: conclusion

The affine model receives a complete identifiability theorem and a minimal full-rank construction.

## Limits

Real beam geometries may restrict available directions, and nonaffine remainder, noise and coordinate transforms require separate bounds. Fifteen samples are not sufficient merely because their count matches the number of coefficients.

## Extension

Compute the real correlated design Gramian, certify its smallest singular value and propagate a bound on the neglected second-order field remainder to each recovered derivative.
