## Revised question retained in the annual source

What exact memory does z_{t+1}=max(0,z_t−s_t) retain, and when does order matter?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

The reflected recursion records the largest negative suffix sum of its input. This exact queueing formula explains an endpoint difference between reordered feasible slacks, without calling a noninvertible scalar map a connection holonomy.

## Definitions

Let z₀=0 and z_{t+1}=[z_t−s_t]_+, where [x]_+=max(0,x) and s_t is a real slack.

For n≥1, a suffix sum is ∑_{j=k}^{n−1}s_j with 0≤k<n.

The update clips all negative states to zero and is therefore generally noninvertible.

## Result name

Closed form and a monotone stability comparison

## Result statement

z_n=max(0,max_{0≤k<n}[−∑_{j=k}^{n−1}s_j]). In particular equal total slack need not give equal endpoints. If every slack is at least δ>0, z_t reaches zero within ⌈z₀/δ⌉ steps and remains zero.

## Proof

For n=1 the formula is exactly the update. If it holds at n, subtract s_n from each candidate and take the maximum with zero. The old candidates become all suffixes ending at n, including the new one-term suffix from the old zero candidate; this proves the formula by induction. For positive lower-bounded slack, z_{t+1}≤max(0,z_t−δ), which iterates to the stated bound. More generally any cumulative deficit can build a backlog, while later surplus may erase it; clipping makes the order consequential even when the total input sum is fixed.

## Worked example: setup

Compare slack sequences (1,−1) and (−1,1).

## Worked example: calculation

Both sum to zero. The first gives states 0→0→1, while the second gives 0→1→0. Their suffix minima are −1 and zero respectively, reproducing the formula. For z₀=3 and every s_t=.4, zero is reached after eight updates. A claimed edge-rider effect can therefore be assessed by exact suffix deficits before introducing a geometric interpretation.

## Worked example: conclusion

The statistic measures a declared path-dependent accumulation and has an elementary closed-form certificate.

## Limits

Order dependence alone is not nonabelian holonomy, curvature or a security guarantee. A transport interpretation requires invertible maps or an explicitly defined categorical replacement, together with path composition and gauge rules.

## Extension

For bounded stochastic slacks derive stationary backlog conditions and tail bounds. For multiple constraints use a vector reflected process, specify its reflection directions and prove when a scalar summary retains enough information to distinguish admissible paths.
