## Revised question retained in the annual source

How does a local branch partition sum differ from pressure, and when is the pressure-root sensitivity formula valid?

## Status

## Summary

The source proposes a logarithm of a branch sum and a pressure-zero condition. True pressure records exponential growth under repeated composition. A finite positive matrix gives a completely explicit model with a rigorous parameter derivative.

## Definitions

For positive branch lengths φ_j>0 define L_s f=Σ_j e^(−sφ_j)f on a one-dimensional constant-function space, so λ(s)=Σ_j e^(−sφ_j) and P(s)=logλ(s).

For a positive finite matrix L_(s,t), pressure is log of its simple Perron eigenvalue; normalize left and right eigenvectors ℓᵀr=1.

## Result name

Root sensitivity for a declared potential perturbation

## Result statement

For branch potentials φ_j+tη_j, a simple root P(s(t),t)=0 satisfies s′(0)=−s(0) E_pη/E_pφ, where p_j=e^(−sφ_j)/Σe^(−sφ_j).

## Proof

Differentiate λ: ∂sP=−Σp_jφ_j and ∂tP=−sΣp_jη_j. The implicit-function equation gives s′=−P_t/P_s, yielding the result with its sign. The denominator is positive before the minus sign because every φ_j>0. For a general positive matrix, differentiate Lr=λr and multiply by ℓᵀ to get λ′=ℓᵀL′r; this supplies the corresponding equilibrium edge weights. A single log branch sum is pressure only in a structure such as this constant-branch model or after a proved eigenfunction reduction, not merely because a logarithm was taken.

## Worked example: setup

Use two branches with φ₁=φ₂=log3 and perturb both by η₁=η₂=1.

## Worked example: calculation

P(s,t)=log2−s(log3+t), so s(t)=log2/(log3+t). The unperturbed root is log2/log3≈0.63092975 and derivative −log2/(log3)²≈−0.5742970109. This matches the expectation formula exactly.

## Worked example: conclusion

Pressure roots and their sensitivities become reproducible after the operator is fixed.

## Limits

The source’s numerical root near 0.8802487 is not reproducible from a truncated potential and unspecified branch operator.

## Extension

Provide the complete branches and function space, certify a simple isolated leading eigenvalue and obtain rigorous truncation bounds for the claimed pressure root.
