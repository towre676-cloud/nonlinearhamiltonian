## Result name

Itô correction and a Feynman–Kac model with an exact solution

## Result statement

u(t,x)=E_x[exp(−∫_t^T V(X_s)ds)g(X_T)] whenever the stated martingale and classical-solution hypotheses hold. In particular, for dX=−κXdt+σdW, κ>0, V=0 and g(x)=x², u(t,x)=e^(−2κτ)x²+σ²(1−e^(−2κτ))/(2κ), τ=T−t.

## Proof

Itô expansion gives df(X_t)=f′(X_t)dX_t+f″(X_t)d[X]_t/2, with d[X]_t=σ(X_t)²dt. Apply the time-dependent formula and the product rule to M_s=exp(−∫_t^s V(X_r)dr)u(s,X_s). Its drift vanishes exactly when the displayed PDE holds. Localization and the stated uniform-integrability condition turn M into a true martingale; evaluating at t and T proves the representation. For the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck example, Itô gives d(X²)=(−2κX²+σ²)dt+2σX dW. Its expected value solves m′=−2κm+σ² with m(0)=x², producing the explicit formula. Direct differentiation of u verifies u_t−κxu_x+σ²u_xx/2=0 and the terminal condition, so probability and PDE agree without a numerical solver.
