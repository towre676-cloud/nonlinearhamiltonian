## Proof

The q-prefactors multiply to q^[(1+23)/24]=q. Translation multiplies the first eta by e^(πi/12) and the second by e^(23πi/12), whose product is one. For the Fricke substitution apply the inversion formula to each factor, obtaining√(−i23τ)√(−iτ)=−i√23τ. The branch equality holds on the positive imaginary axis and hence throughout the connected upper half-plane by analytic continuation. Below degree24 the23n product contributes only its constant term, so finite expansion of∏(1−q^n), or Euler’s pentagonal identity, gives the displayed coefficients. These calculations are exact, not recognized from a numerical plot. The initialq factor proves vanishing at the cusp∞ because the remaining product has constant term one and converges forImτ>0. It does not by itself check every cusp ofΓ₀(23), nor the full character law. Those require the eta-quotient transformation theorem or separate cusp calculations, rather than an inference from the two displayed transformations alone.
