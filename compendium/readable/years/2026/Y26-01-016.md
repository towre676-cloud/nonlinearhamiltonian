## Revised question retained in the annual source

What are the correct cyclotomic degree and normalized totient at n=840, and which performance claims do they leave open?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

Euler’s product gives exact arithmetic invariants and corrects a prior assistant’s mistaken correction. The same prime product counts admissible residue classes, but an architecture speedup does not follow from that density without an independently justified model.

## Definitions

Let ζ_m=e^{2πi/m}. The cyclotomic field Q(ζ_m) has degree φ(m), while its maximal real subfield has degree φ(m)/2 for m>2.

Define D(n)=φ(2n) and κ(n)=φ(2n)/(2n). Euler’s product is φ(m)=m∏_{p|m}(1−1/p).

Degree of a trigonometric value must be calculated from its actual field and symmetries, not identified automatically with the full cyclotomic degree.

## Result name

Exact values and an inclusion–exclusion interpretation

## Result statement

For n=840, 2n=1680=2⁴·3·5·7, so D=384 and κ=8/35. The maximal real subfield of Q(ζ₁₆₈₀) has degree 192. The historical claim that φ(1680)=256 is false.

## Proof

Among residues modulo m, a fraction 1/p are divisible by each prime p|m. Inclusion–exclusion, or the Chinese remainder theorem applied to the prime-power factors, gives the product formula. Substitution yields 1680·(1/2)(2/3)(4/5)(6/7)=384. Complex conjugation has order two and fixes the maximal real subfield; because ζ_m is nonreal for m>2, its extension over that fixed field has degree two. This proves the real-subfield degree without claiming that every particular sine generates it. For any set of distinct primes, ∏(1−1/p) is likewise the exact density of units modulo their product, so the same calculation applies to the January architecture passage.

## Worked example: calculation

Their product is 30030 and φ(30030)=5760, giving κ₆=192/1001≈.191808, not .154. Relative to κ=1/2, the power (κ₆/.5)^{2.3} is approximately .1104; its reciprocal is about 9.06. The direction of any claimed gain therefore depends on whether the proposed model uses the ratio or its inverse.

## Worked example: conclusion

The arithmetic is exact, while its operational interpretation remains a separate hypothesis.

## Limits

No categorical surgery, entropy-pinching inequality, throughput gain or universal time bound follows from φ alone. The source’s proposed T≤κ log D needs a defined time, units, dynamics and proof.

## Extension

For a specified resonance function, determine its exact stabilizer under Gal(Q(ζ_m)/Q), hence its degree. For a performance model, state a measurable dependence on κ and test both normalization and exponent on independent data.
