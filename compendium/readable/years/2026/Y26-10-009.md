## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which exact cubic identities can be checked directly, and what is still required to prove the claimed global uniqueness of a norm-equation solution?

## Status

## Summary

The retrieved monograph claims a complete solution of u³+4v³=107 and records two units. The available evidence does not include its slab bounds or unit-exponent exclusion certificate. We can nevertheless derive the exact norm form, replay the units and the displayed solution, and state the missing completeness obligation without substituting a finite search for it.

## Definitions

Let z³=−4 and use the integral basis 1,z,z² of the order Z[z]. Define N(a+bz+cz²) as the determinant of multiplication on this free Z-module.

An element with norm ±1 is a unit of this order because its integer multiplication matrix has an integer inverse.

## Result name

Exact cubic norm and unit identities

## Result statement

N(a+bz+cz²)=a³−4b³+16c³+12abc. The elements η=5−3z+2z² and ε=1−z−z² both have norm one. Also N(u−vz)=u³+4v³, and (u,v)=(−1,3) gives norm 107.

## Proof

Multiplication by a+bz+cz² in the indicated basis has matrix [[a,−4c,−4b],[b,a,−4c],[c,b,a]]. Expanding its determinant gives the stated cubic form, proving the identity without numeric algebraic approximations. For η, the four contributions are 125,108,128,−360, summing to one. For ε they are 1,4,−16,12, again summing to one. Determinants multiply, so multiplication by any ηʳεˢ preserves a norm equation. Setting (a,b,c)=(u,−v,0) gives u³+4v³; the displayed point gives −1+108=107. These calculations prove valid transport in a three-coordinate norm surface. They do not show that unit multiplication keeps c=0, nor that all norm-107 elements lie in an asserted seed orbit. A binary equation is an intersection of the norm surface with the plane c=0, so returning to that plane is an additional exact condition.

## Limits

The historical statement that (−1,3) is the only integer solution is not independently established here. A 3,631-candidate slab or modular period is not a global bound unless its reduction theorem and all excluded exponent classes are supplied and replayed.

## Extension

Produce a complete certificate consisting of norm-orbit representatives, a proved fundamental-domain reduction, an explicit bound on unit exponents satisfying c=0, and exact evaluation of every residual candidate; then the claimed uniqueness can be independently checked.
