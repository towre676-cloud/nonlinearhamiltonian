## Summary

## Definitions

Euler’s totientφ(n) counts units modulo n. For realσ>2 defineF(σ)=Σ_{n≥1}φ(n)n^{−σ}. Letμ be the Möbius function andid(n)=n.

The convolution identityφ=μ*id follows fromΣ_{d|n}φ(d)=n. Absolute convergence permits multiplication and rearrangement of the Dirichlet series.

## Result name

The totient Dirichlet series has the source zeta majorant on its correct half-plane

## Result statement

F(σ)=ζ(σ−1)/ζ(σ) forσ>2, soΣ_{n≥2}φ(n)n^{−σ}=ζ(σ−1)/ζ(σ)−1. The tail afterN is at mostN^{2−σ}/(σ−2).

## Proof

Count the integers1,…, n by theirgcdwithn: those withn/gcd equal tod are counted byφ(d), provingΣ_{d|n}φ(d)=n. Möbius inversion givesφ(n)=Σ_{d|n}μ(d)n/d. Sinceφ(n)≤n, its series converges absolutely forσ>2. Expanding the convolution and changingn=dm yieldsΣ_dμ(d)d^{−σ}Σ_mm^{1−σ}. The second factor isζ(σ−1). The first is1/ζ(σ) by the absolutely convergent Euler product forσ>1. This proves the identity. For the tail,φ(n)n^{−σ}≤n^{1−σ}, a decreasing positive function, soΣ_{n>N}n^{1−σ}≤∫_N∞x^{1−σ}dx=N^{2−σ}/(σ−2). No continuation beyond the absolute-convergence region is needed or used in this positive majorant.

## Worked example: calculation

The partial sum is rational and lies belowζ(2)/ζ(3). Its omitted tail is at most1/10. Excludingn=1subtracts exactlyone. A defect sequence bounded in absolute value byφ(n)n^{−3} therefore has an absolutely convergent total bounded byζ(2)/ζ(3)−1, provided the coefficientwise inequality is proved. The positivity of this majorant is important: an absolute-value bound can be summed without cancellation, whereas a conditionally convergent oscillatory series cannot be rearranged by the same argument. At σ=2 the comparison series is harmonic, and the stated tail estimate no longer gives a finite bound.
