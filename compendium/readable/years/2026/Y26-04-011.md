## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which part of the reported twelve-coordinate rank and gauge statement can be proved from a declared difference design?

## Status

## Summary

The source gives a twelve-pitch-class Jacobian with a uniform-shift gauge. Its complete numerical matrix is not recovered, so its determinant and singular values cannot be reconstructed. A natural cyclic difference model proves the structural rank statement exactly.

## Definitions

Index x∈R¹² modulo twelve. For offsets d∈{3,4,7}, let (J_dx)_i=x_(i+d)−x_i, and stack the three twelve-row matrices into J.

Uniform shifts lie in ker J. Fourier vectors have components exp(2πiki/12); the k-th Fourier eigenvalue of JᵀJ is λ_k=Σ_(d∈{3,4,7})|exp(2πikd/12)−1|².

## Result name

Exact rank of the declared model

## Result statement

ker J consists precisely of constant vectors; hence rank J=11. On the mean-zero subspace, JᵀJ is positive definite and the gauge-fixed least-squares minimizer is unique.

## Proof

If Jx=0, then x is invariant under shifts by three and four. Since 4−3=1, it is invariant under the shift by one and therefore constant. This establishes the kernel without any floating-point singular value threshold. The Fourier expression follows because each cyclic shift acts diagonally on Fourier vectors and JᵀJ is the sum of the squared difference operators. Its only zero eigenvalue is at k=0 by the kernel argument. Restricting to mean-zero vectors removes that mode; the remaining quadratic form ||Jx||² is strictly positive, so the normal equation has a unique solution on that subspace.

## Worked example: setup

Take the vector x_i=i for i=0,…,11 and compare it with x+5·1.

## Worked example: calculation

Every difference row gives exactly the same value on the two inputs, including wraparound rows. The stacked matrix has 36 rows and twelve columns, but its rank is eleven, not twelve. The zero-sum condition distinguishes a unique representative in every uniform-shift orbit.

## Worked example: conclusion

The gauge theorem is exact for a fully stated design.
