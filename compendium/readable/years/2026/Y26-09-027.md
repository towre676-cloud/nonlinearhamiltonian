## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which filtering or localization operation preserves the positive-semidefinite constraint?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or explicitly defined worked model

## Summary

The robust-scan source mentioned PSD stress cones, localization and Germano transfer. A covariance theorem gives their common exact algebraic core: normalized positive averages produce positive-semidefinite stresses, and nested averaging decomposes them into two PSD terms.

## Definitions

For vectors u_i∈Rᵈ and nonnegative weights w_i summing to one, define meanμ=Σw_iu_i and covariance C=Σw_i(u_i−μ)(u_i−μ)ᵀ.

If samples are grouped, let μ_j,C_j be each group’s mean and covariance and p_j its total weight.

## Result name

Positive averaging preserves stress positivity, while signed correction need not

## Result statement

C is positive semidefinite and satisfies the total-covariance identity C=Σp_jC_j+Σp_j(μ_j−μ)(μ_j−μ)ᵀ. Convex combinations preserve PSD, but arbitrary signed defect corrections can leave the cone.

## Proof

For any v, vᵀCv=Σw_i[v·(u_i−μ)]²≥0. For the grouped identity write u−μ=(u−μ_j)+(μ_j−μ), expand the outer product and average within each group. The cross terms vanish because the within-group mean deviation is zero. The two remaining terms are the stated conditional covariance and covariance of group means, both PSD by the first argument. This is the exact finite averaging mechanism behind a two-level stress transfer; identifying it with a particular filtering convention requires that convention’s weights.

## Worked example: setup

Take equal weights on(1,0),(−1,0),(0,2).

## Worked example: calculation

The mean is(0,2/3), and direct calculation gives C=diag(2/3,8/9), determinant16/27>0. As a contrasting correction, start at I₂ and subtract2e₁e₁ᵀ; the result diag(−1,1) is not PSD. A corrected matrix can therefore match a desired scalar defect while violating the covariance cone. Positivity must be imposed or proved, not inferred from the correction’s name.

## Worked example: conclusion

Stress positivity and transfer have a complete finite proof with exact rational data.

## Limits

General filters can have signed kernels, and localization operators can alter cross-covariances in ways not covered by simple convex averaging. No turbulence closure or physical stability theorem follows from PSD alone.

## Extension

Specify the actual localization map and prove that it is positive on covariance matrices; for a proposed defect correction, solve a constrained projection problem and certify its residual and PSD margin.
