## Revised question retained in the annual source

How can an internally abelian subgroup experience a nonabelian faithful action?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered W₉₆₀ example can be developed directly from its stated permutation module. It gives a genuine structural witness separating internal commutativity, centralizers and the action of the ambient group.

## Definitions

Let V={x∈F₂⁵:Σx_i=0}, dimension four, and let A₅ permute coordinates. Set W=V⋊A₅.

The embedded V is abelian. The commutator subgroup[V,A₅] is generated by differences v+gv; its action kernel is the permutations fixing every vector of V.

## Result name

An abelian kernel can have a perfect and centerless effective actor extension

## Result statement

|W|=16·60=960, [V,W]=V, C_W(V)=V and Z(W)=1. Using the classical perfectness of A₅, one also has W′=W.

## Proof

The even-sum condition is one independent linear equation, giving|V|=2⁴. A three-cycle sending e₂ to e₃ while fixing e₁ takes v=e₁+e₂ to e₁+e₃, so a commutator difference is e₂+e₃. Conjugating by A₅ produces every two-coordinate sum, which spans V. An element centralizing V must have a permutation fixing every pair vector e_i+e_j. Such a permutation fixes every two-element subset and hence every coordinate, so the action is faithful and the centralizer is V. A central element must then lie in V and be fixed by A₅. Transitivity makes its coordinates all equal; the all-ones vector has odd sum and is excluded, leaving zero. Finally W′ contains V from the commutators and surjects onto A₅′=A₅, proving perfectness.

## Worked example: calculation

V′ is trivial because V is additive. Nevertheless[V,W]=V is as large as possible and W/C_W(V)≅A₅ is nonabelian. The abelian action barrier is all of W because W is perfect, whereas an internally computed V′ would misleadingly suggest no obstruction. The verifier enumerates all sixty even permutations and all sixteen kernel vectors to check faithfulness, fixed vectors and the commutator span.

## Worked example: conclusion

This is a fully specified finite witness for the distinction the subgroup calculus was designed to capture.

## Limits

The proof uses the standard theorem that A₅ is perfect; the finite certificate checks the given action, not an unrelated Mathieu representation. No universal behavior-class count is inferred.

## Extension

Replace A₅ by another finite perfect group and determine exactly when a faithful module has zero invariants and full commutator span, yielding a family of centerless perfect extensions.
