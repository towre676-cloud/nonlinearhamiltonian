## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does disjoint block spectrum prove, and what estimate quantifies coupling between channels?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The source discusses tube transport through resolvent matrix elements and separated block spectra. Disjoint spectra do not mean their numerical resolvents are small at every frequency. A Neumann-series estimate gives the exact coupling condition.

## Definitions

Let H₀ be block diagonal and V a bounded coupling, with H=H₀+V. For z outside σ(H₀), write R₀(z)=(zI−H₀)⁻¹.

Assume ||R₀(z)V||<1. For self-adjoint finite matrices, ||R₀(z)||=1/dist(z,σ(H₀)).

## Result name

Resolvent perturbation certificate

## Result statement

R(z)=(I−R₀V)⁻¹R₀ and ||R−R₀||≤||R₀||²||V||/[1−||R₀||||V||] whenever the denominator is positive.

## Proof

Factor zI−H=(zI−H₀)(I−R₀V). The assumed norm bound makes the second factor invertible with inverse Σ_(n≥0)(R₀V)^n, giving the formula. Subtract the zeroth term and sum the remaining geometric norms to obtain the bound. A channel amplitude ⟨ψ_out,Rψ_in⟩ is at most ||ψ_out||||R||||ψ_in|| by Cauchy–Schwarz. For self-adjoint blocks, the distance formula follows by diagonalization; for nonnormal operators, spectral distance alone may underestimate the resolvent norm.

## Worked example: setup

Use H₀=diag(−1,1), V=[[0,ε],[ε,0]] and z=0.

## Worked example: calculation

||R₀||=1, so |ε|<1 certifies the expansion and the difference bound |ε|/(1−|ε|). Direct inversion gives R(0)=−H/(1+ε²), with eigenvalue distances √(1+ε²); this example is uniformly invertible even beyond the sufficient Neumann range.

## Worked example: conclusion

The bound distinguishes an exact sufficient condition from a necessary spectral statement.

## Limits

Proposed automorphic or Langlands interpretations of source “worm” sectors are not implied by this matrix calculation and require actual representations and local factors.

## Extension

Give the operator domains and coupling norm for the tube model, then certify a frequency window in which off-block transfer is uniformly small.
