## Summary

The recovered recursive field draft combines several mathematical motifs. The anti-linear reflection and the scalar recurrence are independently precise, but neither proves the combined construction’s convergence without a complete operator definition.

## Definitions

For real α define T(z)=e^(2πiα)conj z on C. This is real-linear and norm-preserving, though not complex-linear.

Separately consider c_(n+1,m)=q_m c_(n,m), q_m=λe^(−imα)+a, for a fixed Fourier mode m and complex constants λ,a.

## Result name

Exact involution and modal stability

## Result statement

T²=I and |Tz|=|z|. The scalar recurrence decays for every initial value if and only if |q_m|<1; a whole ℓ² field contracts uniformly only if sup_m|q_m|<1.

## Proof

Conjugate the phase as well as z: T(Tz)=e^(2πiα)e^(−2πiα)z=z. Taking absolute values proves isometry. For the recurrence, c_(n,m)=q_m^n c_(0,m); geometric convergence is equivalent to |q_m|<1. On ℓ², the diagonal multiplier norm is sup_m|q_m|, giving the uniform bound ||c_n||₂≤q^n||c₀||₂ when that supremum is q<1. Individual mode decay without a uniform gap need not give a common exponential rate. A condition for one frequency therefore cannot be promoted to a global field contraction.

## Worked example: setup

Take α=1/4 in T, so T(z)=i conj z; take a=0 and λ=1/2 in the recurrence.

## Worked example: calculation

T(1+2i)=2+i and applying T again returns 1+2i. Every Fourier multiplier has modulus 1/2, so ||c_n||₂≤2^(−n)||c₀||₂. With λ=1 and a=0, each modulus is one and no nonzero mode decays.

## Worked example: conclusion

The symmetry and contraction claims are exact and separately typed.
