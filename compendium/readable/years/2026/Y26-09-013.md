## Proof

Taylor expansion gives G(a+ph)≡G(a)+phG′(a) modp² because every higher term contains p². After division by p, the unit derivative gives one linear congruence and hence a unique h modulo p. Exact Horner evaluation gives G(2)=−33633, divisible by37; the correction is h=2 and2+37·2=76. At the second root, the correction is h=786, giving233+809·786=636107. Direct substitution modulo p² verifies both large-integer claims without rounding. The primality of37 and809 is checked by trial division up to their square roots.

## Worked example: calculation

At both primes the nonzero derivative makes the polynomial root simple and ensures a unique compatible branch to every higher p-power. A presentation matrix may still have several invariant factors or a different determinant valuation. Its local Smith exponent must be obtained from that actual matrix and minors, not from the polynomial root alone. Likewise two successful primes do not exclude additional primes elsewhere.
