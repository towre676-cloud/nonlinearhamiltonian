## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which decomposition is forced by commuting idempotents, and what cannot be concluded about a scalar defect cascade?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered finish-program contains commuting idempotents and triangular dependencies. A finite family of commuting projections has a complete exact algebraic decomposition. The theorem does not require interpreting the blocks as physical sectors or identifying every invariant with one scalar.

## Definitions

Let P₁,…,P_r be commuting idempotent linear maps on a finite-dimensional vector space. For a bit vector ε∈{0,1}^r define E_ε=∏P_i^(ε_i)(I−P_i)^(1−ε_i).

Orthogonal projections are a special case; arbitrary commuting idempotents yield a direct sum without necessarily giving orthogonal summands.

## Result name

Joint eigenspace decomposition

## Result statement

The E_ε are pairwise annihilating idempotents, their sum is I, and their images are the simultaneous eigenspaces on which P_i acts by ε_i. Hence V is the direct sum of these images.

## Proof

Commutativity allows factors to be regrouped. Each factor squares to itself, making E_ε idempotent. If ε and η differ at index j, their product contains P_j(I−P_j)=0. Expanding ∏(P_i+(I−P_i)) gives ΣE_ε=I. Therefore every vector is a sum of its E_ε components, and applying E_η to a relation among components proves uniqueness. The eigenvalue characterization follows by multiplying by P_i; conversely a simultaneous eigenvector survives exactly its matching E_ε. If the P_i are self-adjoint, each E_ε is also self-adjoint and distinct blocks are orthogonal.

## Worked example: setup

Use P=diag(1,1,0) and Q=diag(1,0,1) on R³.

## Worked example: calculation

The nonzero joint blocks are E_(1,1)=diag(1,0,0), E_(1,0)=diag(0,1,0) and E_(0,1)=diag(0,0,1); E_(0,0)=0. A vector (2,3,5) decomposes uniquely into (2,0,0)+(0,3,0)+(0,0,5).

## Worked example: conclusion

Commuting idempotents support an exact block theorem with fully declared hypotheses.

## Limits

A relation such as F=Q+ΣS_k needs the summands and convergence specified. Noncommuting idempotents do not admit this product construction.

## Extension

For an infinite family, specify a topology and prove convergence of the joint spectral resolution; for approximate commuting maps, quantify how block leakage affects the proposed defect hierarchy.
