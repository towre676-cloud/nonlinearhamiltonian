## Proof

D₈ roots choose two of eight positions and four signs, giving4C(8,2)=112. The half-spin weights are(±1/2,…,±1/2) with even minus parity, again128. Every displayed root has squared length two, and the count is112+128=240. Constructing e₈ additionally requires the so₁₆ action onS⁺ and a compatible equivariant bracket[S⁺,S⁺]→so₁₆ satisfying Jacobi; dimension arithmetic alone does not supply those maps. The weight count distinguishes a representation from a Lie-algebra construction. To recoverE₈ one must define anso₁₆-equivariant mapΛ²S⁺→so₁₆, choose its normalization and verify the Jacobi identity for triples of spinors. Dimension agreement and a plausible root count are strong consistency checks but do not replace that bracket calculation.
