## Proof

The step preserves m²−2n² by direct expansion. Conversely a positive solution has n≥2 and m>n. Multiplication by3−2√2 gives n′=3n−2m≥0 and n′<n: the first follows from m²=2n²+1≤(3n/2)² for n≥2, and the second from m>n. Also m′=3m−4n>0. Descent reaches(1,0), proving completeness; reversing gives the stated powers. Conjugation gives n_j=(E^j−E^(−j))/(2√2), so solving the exponential inequality proves the logarithmic count. An invertible map on a finite modular state set is a permutation, yielding periodicity and the accepted-index coefficient.

## Limits

A general Pell-type equation can have several inequivalent seeds and sign domains. A tested list or one orbit does not certify all of them. No unproved global A048624 shift uniqueness is inferred.
