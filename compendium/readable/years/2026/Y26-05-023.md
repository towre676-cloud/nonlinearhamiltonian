## Summary

The geological structured-object passage combines generators, admissible cones, coercivity and cohomological gluing. We preserve two mathematically precise components instead of asserting that an analogy supplies a universal geological spectral theory. Both models below are newly stipulated.

## Definitions

Let A act on a finite-dimensional Hilbert space with Re⟨x,Ax⟩≤−α₀||x||². Let perturbations E_i satisfy ||E_i||≤δ_i. Define α_eff=α₀−Σδ_i.

For local scalar data on a triangle, assign oriented edge differences a₁₂,a₂₃,a₃₁ with a_ji=−a_ij. A global potential means a_ij=u_j−u_i for some vertex values.

## Result name

A coercivity debit ledger and a finite gluing obstruction have independent exact meanings

## Result statement

The perturbed generator A+ΣE_i has coercivity margin at least α_eff, and a positive α_eff proves exponential norm decay. The triangle data glue to a potential exactly when a₁₂+a₂₃+a₃₁=0; this obstruction is unrelated to the sign of α_eff unless an additional model couples them.

## Proof

For the analytic statement, Re⟨x,E_ix⟩≤||E_i||||x||²≤δ_i||x||². Summing gives the new quadratic-form bound, and differentiating the squared norm along the flow proves decay as in the Lyapunov argument. The bound is sharp: scalar perturbations E_i=δ_iI applied to A=−α₀I attain equality. For gluing, summing potential differences around the cycle telescopes to zero, proving necessity. If the sum is zero, choose u₁=0,u₂=a₁₂,u₃=a₁₂+a₂₃; then the third difference is exactly a₃₁, proving sufficiency. Adding the same constant to all potentials is the only ambiguity. This constructive solution exhibits an actual cohomological compatibility condition in a finite setting. A small analytic perturbation norm neither forces that condition nor follows from it.

## Worked example: setup

Take α₀=1, δ₁=1/5 and δ₂=3/10, and edge data(2,3,−5).

## Worked example: calculation

The effective margin is1/2, so the norm decays at least as e^{−t/2}. The local edge data glue to potentials(0,2,5). Replacing the final edge by−4 gives obstruction one and makes gluing impossible, while leaving the analytic margin unchanged. Conversely increasing a debit past the baseline destroys the positive decay certificate even if the edge sum remains zero.
