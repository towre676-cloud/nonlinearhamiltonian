## Result statement

B has total Betti numbers(1,4,5,2), length eight and Hilbert function(1,3,3,1). Its socle is span{xz,y²z}, of dimension two, so this codimension-three algebra is not Gorenstein.

## Proof

The two resolutions are complexes of free modules over disjoint polynomial variables. Over a field their tensor total complex resolves the tensor quotient; exactness follows degreewise from the exact tensor functor or the elementary Künneth theorem. All matrix entries remain in(x,y,z), so the total resolution is minimal. Its total ranks are the coefficient convolution(1+3t+2t²)(1+t)=1+4t+5t²+2t³. The eight basis monomials are the four A monomials multiplied by1 or z. A socle element must be killed by z and hence have a z factor; its A coefficient must lie in socle(A), giving exactly the two stated classes. The symmetric Hilbert series(1+t)³ therefore again fails to imply type one. Tensoring the socles also gives a direct type certificate: the only elements killed byx,y,z are scalar combinations ofxz andy²z. Their independence follows from the eight standard monomials. Hence the last free-module rank two matches a genuinely two-dimensional socle, and the symmetric Hilbert function cannot rescue a proposed Gorenstein interpretation.
