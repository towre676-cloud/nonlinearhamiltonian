## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a quadratic energy over primes actually determine before asymptotic prime-density or zeta-zero corrections are invoked?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

A finite counting measure on primes gives an exact convex minimization problem. Its uniform minimizer and partition function depend only on the number of primes when every site has equal energy, so no fine prime-correlation information can appear in that model.

## Definitions

Let P_N={p prime:p≤N}, M=|P_N| and dπ_N=∑_{p∈P_N}δ_p. For real values a_p define F(a)=∑a_p².

Impose the affine constraint ∑a_p=A. Assume M≥1.

For constant site energy c², define Z_N(β)=∑_{p≤N}e^{−βc²}, with a finite fixed N.

## Result name

Uniform minimizer and degenerate partition statistics

## Result statement

F(a)≥A²/M, with equality exactly at a_p=A/M for every prime. For constant energy, Z_N=M e^{−βc²}; mean energy is c² and energy variance is zero at every β.

## Proof

Cauchy–Schwarz gives (∑a_p)²≤M∑a_p², and its equality condition forces all coordinates equal. This proves the unique constrained minimizer; a Lagrange multiplier gives the same result through 2a_p=λ. For the partition function every summand is identical, giving the closed form. Then −∂_βlog Z=c² and ∂²_βlog Z=0. Thus fine spacings or correlations between prime labels cannot influence these statistics, because replacing P_N by any M-element set leaves the model unchanged. Likewise (cp)/(p/c)=c² cancels p whenever c and p are nonzero; prime support remains in the index set rather than in that individual value.

## Worked example: setup

Take N=10, whose primes are 2,3,5,7, and A=8.

## Worked example: calculation

The minimizer is (2,2,2,2), with energy 16. The alternative (1,2,2,3) has the same sum but energy 18. For c=2, Z=4e^{−4β}; normalized probabilities are 1/4 each, independently of β, and the Shannon entropy is log4.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite measure and optimization are exact and show which information the model retains.

## Limits

Approximating dπ by dx/log x is an asymptotic statement requiring an error theorem; it is not an equality of measures. Zeta-zero corrections matter only for a quantity that actually depends on prime positions.

## Extension

Introduce a stated position-dependent energy E(p) or coupling between primes, derive its finite partition derivatives, and use partial summation to identify exactly where a prime-counting remainder enters an asymptotic estimate.
