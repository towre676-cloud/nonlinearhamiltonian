## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a computed n-Selmer group prove about rank, and which remaining obstruction prevents it from being an exact rank certificate?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

The Kummer sequence produces a precise short exact sequence connecting rational points, Selmer classes and Tate–Shafarevich torsion. A finite vector-space model explains why local solvability can give an upper bound while leaving an undetected global obstruction.

## Definitions

Let E be an elliptic curve over a number field K and n≥2. The n-Selmer group consists of classes in H¹(K,E[n]) whose local images satisfy the Kummer conditions.

Sha(E/K) is the kernel of H¹(K,E)→∏_vH¹(K_v,E). Its n-torsion records locally trivial torsors of order dividing n.

For n=2 write r=rank E(K), t=dim_F₂E(K)[2], and s=dim_F₂Sel₂(E/K).

## Result name

The exact rank relation with its obstruction term

## Result statement

There is a short exact sequence 0→E(K)/nE(K)→Sel_n(E/K)→Sha(E/K)[n]→0. In particular s=r+t+dim_F₂Sha[2], so r≤s−t, with equality only when Sha[2]=0.

## Proof

Apply Galois cohomology to 0→E[n]→E→[n]E→0 over an algebraic closure. Its connecting homomorphism injects E(K)/nE(K) into H¹(K,E[n]), with cokernel mapping into H¹(K,E)[n]. Impose the corresponding local Kummer conditions. A class maps to a torsor trivial at every completion exactly when its image lies in Sha[n], giving the displayed short exact sequence by the same local-global diagram. Mordell–Weil gives E(K)≅Z^r⊕T with finite T; reduction modulo two has dimension r+t, because the finite group’s quotient T/2T and its two-torsion have equal size. Dimensions in the short exact sequence prove the relation.

## Worked example: setup

Use the finite linear-algebra model s=3 and t=1.

## Worked example: calculation

If Sha[2] has dimension zero, the rank is two. If it has dimension one, the rank is one. The same Selmer dimension is therefore compatible with different ranks until additional global information is supplied. In the toy exact sequence F₂²↪F₂³↠F₂, the quotient has dimension one, directly checking the dimension accounting.

## Worked example: conclusion

A Selmer computation is a rigorous upper bound with an explicitly named missing obstruction.

## Limits

The source supplies no particular curve, certified Selmer computation or Sha calculation, so the numerical example is an abstract model. BSD remains a conjectural identification of algebraic rank with L-function vanishing order in general; it is not proved by local counts.

## Extension

For a supplied curve, give certified rational points for a lower bound and a documented descent for the upper bound, then eliminate the relevant Sha torsion or prove the bounds coincide. Keep analytic-rank evidence separate from unconditional algebraic certification.
