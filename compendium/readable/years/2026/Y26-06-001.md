## Summary

The source’s central rational/irrational phase distinction has a short exact proof. The finite construction below explains conductor dimension and the full matrix algebra generated by the clock and shift, without identifying a finite approximation with the irrational operator algebra.

## Definitions

Let q∈C× and U, V be invertible d-by-d matrices satisfying UV=qVU. If q has exact order L, use basis e_j, j modulo L, and define Ue_j=q^j e_j, Ve_j=e_{j+1}.

A representation is irreducible if it has no common nonzero proper invariant subspace. A commutant consists of matrices commuting with both generators.

## Result name

Finite Weyl pairs exist exactly at root-of-unity phases

## Result statement

Finite invertible solutions force q^d=1. For q of order L, the displayed L-dimensional unitary pair satisfies the relation and generates all L-by-L matrices; its commutant is the scalars and its representation is irreducible.

## Proof

Taking determinants gives det(U)det(V)=q^d det(V)det(U). Invertibility permits cancellation, proving the necessary root-of-unity condition and L|d. The displayed pair has UV e_j=q^{j+1}e_{j+1}=qVU e_j, including wraparound because q^L=1. The eigenvalues of U are distinct. Lagrange interpolation in U therefore produces each coordinate projection E_jj. Multiplying by powers of V produces every matrix unit E_ij, so the generated algebra is the full matrix algebra. Any invariant subspace is then invariant under every matrix unit and is either zero or the whole space. A matrix commuting with U is diagonal; commuting additionally with V forces all its diagonal entries equal. This proves all claims without invoking a classification of arbitrary representations. When q=e^{2πiθ} with irrational θ, q^d≠1 for every positive d, so no exact finite invertible pair exists.

## Worked example: setup

Take q=e^{2πi/3} and dimension three.

## Worked example: calculation

U=diag(1, q, q²), and V cyclically permutes the coordinates. The nine products U^aV^b, 0≤a, b<3, are Hilbert–Schmidt orthogonal because the trace of a nontrivial shift is zero and the trace of a nontrivial clock power is1+q+q²=0. Thus they form a basis of M₃(C).

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite conductor and the irrational obstruction are exact algebraic facts.

## Limits

Approximate commutation at a rational approximant is not an exact representation at irrational θ. Rational phase does not by itself identify a categorical realization, gate cost or an infinite Euler product.

## Extension

Given rational approximants to an irrational θ, derive a norm bound for commutation defects and specify which observables converge, keeping finite matrix approximation distinct from exact representation.
