## Revised question retained in the annual source

How does a retardance error translate into a worst-state fidelity bound, and what must a composite sequence prove?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The August17 source reports wide fidelity windows for composite modal retarders. Their actual sequence matrices were not recovered. A two-mode unitary calculation gives the exact scalar benchmark and a rigorous way to bound any later supplied composite product.

## Definitions

LetU(φ)=diag(e^{iφ/2},e^{−iφ/2}). The state fidelity between normalized pure statesψ,χ is|ψ*χ|². A phase errorε comparesU(φ)ψ andU(φ+ε)ψ; overall scalar phase is physically irrelevant in this mathematical comparison.

## Result name

Exact worst-state phase fidelity and product bound

## Result statement

For|ε|≤π, the minimum state fidelity iscos²(ε/2). Ifunitary productsU_m⋯U₁ andV_m⋯V₁ have per-factor norm errorsη_j=||U_j−V_j||₂, then their total norm difference is at mostΣη_j.

## Proof

Write|ψ₁|²=p and|ψ₂|²=1−p. The overlap ispe^{iε/2}+(1−p)e^{−iε/2}, whose squared magnitude is1−4p(1−p)sin²(ε/2). Sincep(1−p)≤1/4, its minimum iscos²(ε/2), attained by balanced states. For the product bound, insert and subtract mixed products one factor at a time. Each difference is a left unitary product times(U_j−V_j) times a right unitary product; unitary invariance of the norm and the triangle inequality give the sum. A composite sequence can cancel low-order phase derivatives and improve this loose bound, but that requires its actual axes, ordering and parameter dependence. Reported windows cannot be transferred between phase-error, fractional-frequency-error and amplitude-error conventions without a stated mapping.

## Worked example: calculation

The exact condition is|ε|≤2arcsin(1/√1000), approximately0.06326 radians. If the actual retardance is(1+δ)φ, thenε=δφ and the corresponding fractional-error bound scales inversely with|φ|. ## Worked example: conclusion

The fidelity metric and phase normalization are explicit, so numerical windows can be compared meaningfully once sequence matrices are supplied.

## Limits

No reported composite percentage or transport proxy is independently validated. The simple diagonal model omits coupling, loading and parameter-dependent axes.
