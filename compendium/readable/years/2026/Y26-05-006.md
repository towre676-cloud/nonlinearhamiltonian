## Summary

The May project connected Fibonacci slopes and Sturmian sequences to an energy statistic. The constructive result below concerns the words themselves: a telescoping formula yields uniform balance. A below-median energy claim remains a separate finite comparison with a specified null ensemble.

## Definitions

For irrational α∈(0,1) and real β define s_j=floor((j+1)α+β)−floor(jα+β), j≥0. Each s_j is zero or one.

A binary word is balanced if two contiguous blocks of the same length have numbers of ones differing by at most one. Here a block sum is a discrepancy from mα.

## Result name

Mechanical words give a constructive Sturmian balance certificate

## Result statement

For every j,m≥0, the length-m block has either floor(mα) or ceil(mα) ones. Therefore this mechanical word is balanced and its limiting frequency of ones is α. Irrationality makes the word aperiodic.

## Proof

Telescope the block: Σ_{r=j}^{j+m−1}s_r=floor((j+m)α+β)−floor(jα+β). Write jα+β=N+θ with 0≤θ<1. The sum is floor(mα+θ), hence one of the two stated adjacent integers. Any two blocks therefore differ by at most one. Starting at j=0 and dividing by m gives α plus an error with absolute value less than 1/m, proving the limiting frequency. If the infinite word had period d, its frequency would equal an integer number of ones per period divided by d, a rational number, contradicting irrational α. This also shows that the finite balance certificate is exact and does not require a visual pattern-recognition argument. The classical word-complexity characterization of Sturmian words is not needed for the proved discrepancy and aperiodicity statements.

## Worked example: setup

Take α=φ^{−2} and β=0, with φ=(1+√5)/2.

## Worked example: calculation

The first twelve symbols are 001001010010, containing four ones. The sum differs from 12α by about −0.5836, within the strict unit bound. For any length five, every block contains either one or two ones because 5α≈1.90983. Integer comparisons can be certified using rational lower and upper bounds for √5, avoiding ambiguous floor operations at floating endpoints.

## Worked example: conclusion

The sequence has a proved finite discrepancy, a limiting slope and aperiodicity.

## Limits

Balance alone does not imply minimization of a chosen Fourier energy, Markov spectrum functional or Hessian eigenvalue. Those objectives and their admissible competitors must be supplied.
