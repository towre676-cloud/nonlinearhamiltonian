## Summary

## Result statement

f is irreducible,Disc(f)=−23, and Q(t₀)=Q(ρ) with t₀=−1/ρ. The field has one real and one complex-conjugate pair of embeddings;ρ is a norm-one algebraic unit. The splitting-field Galois group is S₃.

## Proof

The only possible rational roots are±1, and neither vanishes. Substituting a=−1,b=0,c=1 gives4−27=−23. Its square-free discriminant implies the index of Z[t₀] in the full integer ring is one, since an index square must divide23. Negative discriminant gives signature(1,1). Substituting t=−1/ρ turns f(t) into(ρ³−ρ−1)/ρ³, proving the field relation. The monic polynomial forρ has constant−1, so its norm is one and it is a unit. An irreducible cubic has transitive subgroup A₃ or S₃; its nonsquare discriminant excludes A₃. Modulo two f is irreducible, while modulo five it factors(t−2)(t²+t+2) with irreducible quadratic, independently exhibiting3-cycle and transposition types. Square-freeness matters because the discriminant of an order equals the field discriminant times its index squared. Since23 has no square factor, the orderZ[t] cannot have index larger than one. This argument proves maximality of the order, but does not identify a fundamental unit: a visible unit might be a proper power of another unit.
