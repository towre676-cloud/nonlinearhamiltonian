## Result name

Tangram areas and chirality give exact invariants but do not classify all assemblies

## Result statement

The seven piece areas sum toone, so every lawful assembly has areaone. A labeled nonrectangular parallelogram and its mirror cannot be carried to each other by an orientation-preserving rigid map that respects the two labeled edge directions.

## Proof

Finite additivity of planar area on sets with disjoint interiors gives the first statement. The piece sum is 2(1/4)+1/8+2(1/16)+1/8+1/8=1. For the second, any plane orientation-preserving rigid map has linear part R with detR=1, so det(Ru, Rv)=det(u, v). A mirror has linear part J with detJ=−1 and reverses the sign. If the ordered labeled edge vectors have nonzero determinant, these signs cannot agree under a rotation. In an unlabeled polygon extra edge permutations can remove some apparent distinctions, so the exact chirality claim must retain its edge or face labeling. A physical flip permits a reflection and reverses that orientation. Coordinate calculations may be performed in Q(√2) because standard tangram cuts use diagonal directions and forty-five-degree rotations.
