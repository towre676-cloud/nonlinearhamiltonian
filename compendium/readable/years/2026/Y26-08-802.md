## Definitions

For column vectors q,k,v, an attention score without positional rotations is qᵀk; after RoPE it is qᵀR_δk with R_δ orthogonal. The value/output composition is Ov.

A SwiGLU block is D[SiLU(Gx)⊙Ux], where SiLU acts coordinatewise. A grouped-query pattern consists of g key/value groups, each shared by r query heads. All identities below require consistent changes of corresponding weights and biases.

## Result name

Exact module gauges and structural permutation group

## Result statement

For invertible A and B, q→Aq,k→A⁻ᵀk preserves unrotated scores, and v→Bv,O→OB⁻¹ preserves outputs. RoPE scores are preserved if Aᵀ commutes with every R_δ. SwiGLU allows a common hidden permutation and arbitrary nonzero diagonal rescaling of its linear U branch, compensated in D. Permutations preserving g equal sharing groups form S_r wr S_g=(S_r)^g⋊S_g.

## Proof

The score identity is qᵀAᵀA⁻ᵀk=qᵀk, while the rotated score becomes qᵀAᵀR_δA⁻ᵀk and therefore needs the commutation condition. In one genuine two-dimensional rotation plane with sinδ≠0, solve CR=RC entrywise: the commutant is C=aI+bJ, J=[[0,−1],[1,0]], invertible when a²+b²≠0. Degenerate or repeated frequencies enlarge the commutant and must be treated separately. For values, (OB⁻¹)(Bv)=Ov. A hidden permutation P commutes with coordinatewise SiLU and respects the product: (Pa)⊙(Pb)=P(a⊙b). Scaling only the linear U branch by diagonal Λ scales the hidden product by Λ, canceled by D→DΛ⁻¹; scaling the nonlinear gate arbitrarily is invalid because SiLU is not homogeneous. A sharing-preserving permutation first permutes the g groups, then independently permutes their r query labels, proving the wreath-product decomposition. Arbitrary independent QK gauges across heads sharing one K generally break the sharing constraint.
