## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: Which sign convention gives the Hamilton equations and which invariant identifies the pendulum separatrix?

## Status

## Summary

The May Invariant Action passage supplies phase space, symplectic form, flow composition and Poisson conservation. Fixing the contraction convention allows a direct theorem and a constructive nonlinear orbit, rather than treating all invariant language as an analogy.

## Definitions

On R² use ω=dq∧dp and define X_H by ι_{X_H}ω=dH. Then X_H=H_p∂_q−H_q∂_p. Define {f,H}=f_qH_p−f_pH_q.

For the pendulum take H(q,p)=p²/2+1−cosq. The unstable equilibria have q=π mod2π,p=0 and energy two.

## Result name

Hamiltonian conservation follows from the symplectic convention and has an exact pendulum separatrix

## Result statement

Along any Hamiltonian trajectory, df/dt={f,H}, so {f,H}=0 implies conservation. The pendulum has separatrix q(t)=4arctan(e^t)−π, p(t)=2sech t, which lies on H=2 and joins adjacent unstable lifts.

## Proof

The contraction of a vector a∂_q+b∂_p with dq∧dp is a dp−b dq. Equating with H_qdq+H_pdp gives a=H_p and b=−H_q. Applying the chain rule proves the Poisson derivative. Setting f=H gives zero because the two products cancel. For the explicit orbit, differentiating q gives 4e^t/(1+e^{2t})=2sech t=p. Standard trigonometric half-angle identities give sinq=2sech t tanh t, while p′=−2sech t tanh t, proving p′=−sinq. Also cosq=2sech²t−1, so p²/2+1−cosq=2sech²t+2−2sech²t=2. As t→±∞ the orbit approaches q=±π,p=0. Thus its energy and limiting saddle behavior are proved exactly, including the infinite time needed to reach either endpoint.

## Worked example: setup

At t=0 the orbit passes through q=0,p=2.

## Worked example: calculation

The energy is 2. The stable equilibrium q=p=0 instead has energy zero and quadratic Hessian diag(1,1); at q=π,p=0 the Hessian is diag(−1,1), giving a saddle. Energy levels below two oscillate around the stable point, whereas the displayed level marks the transition to rotational lifts.

## Worked example: conclusion

The invariant now distinguishes actual orbit topology and supplies an explicit solution.

## Limits

The chosen contraction convention fixes the Poisson sign. Quantization analogies do not establish a unitary model, and conservation alone does not imply numerical integrator accuracy.

## Extension

Construct a symplectic discretization and prove a step-size-dependent modified-energy bound away from the separatrix, then analyze why the long residence time near the saddle worsens uniform period estimates.
