## Result name

A certified arctangent acceleration follows from a geometric expansion

## Result statement

π/4=Σ_{k≥0}2^(k−1)(k!)²/(2k+1)!. The truncation after k=0,…,N−1 has positive error at most2^(−N). The terms satisfy t_{k+1}/t_k=(k+1)/(2k+3).

## Proof

Expand the denominator geometrically and integrate to get I=ΣJ_k/2^(k+1). To evaluate J_k, integrate the derivative of x(1−x²)^(k+1), whose boundary values vanish. This gives J_{k+1}=2(k+1)∫x²(1−x²)^kdx=2(k+1)(J_k−J_{k+1}), so J_{k+1}=2(k+1)J_k/(2k+3). Starting with J_0=1 yields J_k=2^(2k)(k!)²/(2k+1)!. Substitution gives the series and its term recurrence. The geometric remainder under the integral is [((1−x²)/2)^N]/(1+x²), which is between0 and2^(−N). Integrating over a unit interval proves the error bound. This supplies an identity, a recurrence certificate and a stopping criterion, rather than a floating agreement with π.
