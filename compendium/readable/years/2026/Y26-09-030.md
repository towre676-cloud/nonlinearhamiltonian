## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which integral lattice and leading-coefficient scaling make the norm equation equivalent to the binary cubic equation?

## Status

## Summary

The source gives enough coefficients to verify a genuine norm-form identity. It also records the unresolved global unit-exponent bound. The derivation below preserves both facts: finite unit classes modulo cubes are useful, but they do not by themselves complete a Thue solve.

## Definitions

Let H(u,v)=−3u³+9uv²−2v³ and let β satisfy β³−27β−18=0. Work in K=Q(β), with integral order Z[β].

For a binary cubic F(a,b)=c₀a³+c₁a²b+c₂ab²+c₃b³ and θ a root of F(t,1), put φ=c₀θ. The relevant elements are α=c₀a−bφ in a rank-two lattice Λ_F.

## Result name

The recovered Thue form has an exact norm identity, a rank-two lattice restriction and finite unit residue classes

## Result statement

N_K(−3u−βv)=9H(u,v). More generally N(c₀a−bφ)=c₀²F(a,b). The norm equation must retain α∈Λ_F. If a proven rank-r unit basis is available, units modulo cubes have3^r exponent classes after the roots-of-unity contribution is included.

## Proof

The cubic for β has no rational root and is irreducible by the rational-root test. Its conjugates have sum zero, pair sum−27 and product18. Thus N(a+bβ)=a³−27ab²+18b³. Substitute a=−3u,b=−v to obtain9H. In general F(a,b)=c₀∏(a−bθ_i); multiplying its argument by c₀ scales a cubic norm by c₀³, yielding c₀²F. The third power-basis coordinate of α is zero and its constant coefficient is divisible by c₀, so norm solutions outside this lattice need not represent binary-form solutions. For units ε₁,…,ε_r, divide each exponent as n_i=3q_i+r_i with0≤r_i<3. Cubes absorb the3q_i factors but leave the stated finite residues.

In basis1,β,β², multiplication by a+bβ has matrix[[a,0,18b],[b,a,27b],[0,b,a]], whose determinant is the displayed norm. Taking u=0,v=1 gives H=−2 and norm(−β)=−18, exactly9H. For this form c₀=−3 and φ=β, so Λ_H=−3Z+Zβ. A complete search over norm±9 elements must still impose that lattice membership.

## Limits

The exponent residues do not bound q_i, and no full unit basis or global bound was recovered. The historical completeness question remains open here. Order discriminants and field discriminants must also be distinguished.
