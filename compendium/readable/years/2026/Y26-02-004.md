## Revised question retained in the annual source

What explicit obstruction distinguishes local consistency from a globally compatible assignment?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

Differences prescribed on graph edges glue to vertex potentials exactly when their cycle sums vanish. This finite model gives a complete obstruction theory and clarifies when a rank-sized witness is merely linear redundancy rather than a theorem translating every mathematical branch.

## Definitions

Let B be the oriented incidence matrix of a connected finite graph. An edge datum a∈R^m is compatible with vertex potentials when a=Bᵀx for some x∈R^n.

A cycle vector c satisfies Bc=0. The obstruction functional is cᵀa.

For a finite collection of row vectors A_W, define r(W) as the least number of selected rows with the same row-space rank.

## Result name

Cycle obstruction criterion and minimal linear witness

## Result statement

The edge data glue if and only if cᵀa=0 for every cycle c. The solution is unique up to an additive constant. Moreover r(W)=rank(A_W), provided individual rows may be chosen freely.

## Proof

The orthogonal complement of im Bᵀ is ker B, since ⟨Bᵀx,c⟩=⟨x,Bc⟩. In finite-dimensional Euclidean space taking orthogonal complements again proves im Bᵀ=(ker B)⊥, giving necessity and sufficiency. If two potentials solve the equations their difference lies in ker Bᵀ, which consists of constants by connectivity. For r(W), any r selected rows have rank at most r, establishing the lower bound. A basis of the full row space can be selected from its spanning rows by Gaussian elimination, attaining the bound. This proves the source’s witness-size expression in its linear setting; nonlinear or constrained witnesses can behave differently.

## Worked example: setup

Orient a triangle as 1→2→3→1 and prescribe edge increments (2,3,−5).

## Worked example: calculation

The unique cycle sum is 2+3−5=0. Taking x₁=0 gives x₂=2 and x₃=5, and the final edge requires x₁−x₃=−5, which holds. Replacing the final datum by −4 gives cycle sum one and no global potential, although each individual edge equation has solutions. The three incidence rows have rank two, so two independent edge equations form a rank witness, but the third still supplies a consistency test on the right-hand side.

## Worked example: conclusion

Local solvability, coefficient rank and right-hand-side consistency are distinct and explicitly measurable.

## Limits

The result is a finite linear gluing theorem. It does not establish universal interoperability among proof systems or determine nonlinear obstruction classes without definitions and hypotheses.

## Extension

Replace scalar potentials by vectors or nonabelian group transports. Identify the appropriate cycle-holonomy obstruction, analyze gauge equivalence and determine which spanning-tree data plus residual loop checks form a complete certificate.
