## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which exact extension calculation distinguishes coherent sheaves on an elliptic curve from a semisimple model?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

A stable line bundle on a smooth projective elliptic curve has a one-dimensional self-extension space. Serre duality then explains why splitting claims and vanishing mixed extensions need actual category hypotheses rather than analogy with independent simples.

## Definitions

Let X be a smooth connected projective complex curve of genus one, and let L be a line bundle. Its canonical bundle is trivial.

For locally free L, Ext¹_X(L,L)=H¹(X,End L)=H¹(X,O_X). A line bundle is slope-stable because it has no proper positive-rank subsheaf of smaller rank.

A semisimple abelian category is one in which every short exact sequence splits, equivalently all its Ext¹ groups vanish.

## Result name

A one-dimensional family of nonsplit self-extensions

## Result statement

Hom(L,L)=C and Ext¹(L,L)=C. Every nonzero class gives a nonsplit exact sequence 0→L→E→L→0. More generally for slope-stable E on X, Ext¹(E,E)≅Hom(E,E)*≅C.

## Proof

Since End L=O_X and X is connected and projective, global regular functions are constant, giving Hom(L,L)=C. The genus definition gives dim H¹(O_X)=1. For general stable E, any endomorphism is scalar: subtract an eigenvalue and use stability to rule out a nonzero proper image and kernel of compatible slope. Serre duality gives Ext¹(E,E)≅Hom(E,E⊗ω_X)*, and ω_X≅O_X gives the result. A nonzero Ext class is nonsplit by the classification of extensions. Thus the coherent heart cannot be semisimple. This argument concerns an actual curve and actual coherent sheaves; it does not decide whether some separately defined exponential category is semisimple.

## Worked example: setup

Take L=O_X and choose a nonzero class in H¹(O_X).

## Worked example: calculation

For 0→O_X→E→O_X→0, the connecting map H⁰(O_X)→H¹(O_X) sends 1 to the chosen class and is therefore an isomorphism. Exactness gives h⁰(E)=1. The split bundle O_X⊕O_X instead has two independent global sections, so the extension’s nonsplitting is visible in a concrete dimension count.

## Worked example: conclusion

Extension data distinguish categories even when their simple objects or numerical ranks look similar.

## Limits

The recovered exponential-heart statement does not supply a complete category or vanishing theorem. No classification of all hearts follows from one Ext calculation, and slope-separated objects need not give a semiorthogonal decomposition on an elliptic curve.

## Extension

For a specified torsion pair in Coh(X), construct its tilted heart and compute extensions between the shifted and unshifted parts. Compare its central charge and Harder–Narasimhan property with a genuinely defined exponential model.
