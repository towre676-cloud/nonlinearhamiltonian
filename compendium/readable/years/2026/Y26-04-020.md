## Revised question retained in the annual source

How should the factor |cθ+d|⁻¹ be interpreted in a Morita-equivalent rotation algebra?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The source quotes a trace scaling for irrational rotations. The underlying additive-group calculation is exact and clarifies why the direction of the equivalence and the normalized unit must be specified.

## Definitions

Let θ be irrational and let a,b,c,d be integers with ad−bc=±1. Define θ′=(aθ+b)/(cθ+d), whose denominator is nonzero.

For an irrational rotation algebra, the trace range on K₀ is Γ_θ=Z+θZ. Morita equivalence preserves the ordered stabilized structure, but need not preserve the chosen normalized order unit.

## Result name

Exact trace-group scaling

## Result statement

Γ_(θ′)=(cθ+d)⁻¹Γ_θ as additive subgroups. Replacing the scalar by its absolute value gives the same subgroup and a positive normalization factor; the inverse-direction equivalence uses its reciprocal.

## Proof

Any m+nθ′ equals [(mc+na)θ+(md+nb)]/(cθ+d). The integer coefficient transformation has matrix [[d,b],[c,a]], with determinant ad−bc=±1, so it maps Z² bijectively to Z². Hence its numerators run through every element of Γ_θ, proving equality of groups. The positive factor |cθ+d|⁻¹ is compatible with choosing a positive trace, but the trace of the new unit must be normalized explicitly. This lattice identity underlies rather than replaces the deep theorem that the corresponding irrational rotation algebras are strongly Morita equivalent.

## Worked example: setup

Take θ=√2 and matrix [[0,1],[1,0]], so θ′=1/√2.

## Worked example: calculation

(1/√2)(Z+√2Z)=Z+(1/√2)Z, exactly the new group. A trace value √2 in the original stabilized group becomes one after scaling, demonstrating why the normalized units need not correspond unchanged.

## Worked example: conclusion

The scaling formula is proved with its orientation and order-unit caveat.

## Limits

The arithmetic calculation alone is not a construction of an imprimitivity bimodule. It also does not identify arbitrary spectra or every observable across Morita equivalence.

## Extension

Construct the specific equivalence module, compute the trace of its corner projection and verify the scaling on a chosen K₀ basis, including the positive cone.
