## Revised question retained in the annual source

What is a rigorous bounded-heart classification in a finite semisimple derived category, and which part fails for elliptic sheaves?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

The derived category of finitely many independent vector-space simples admits a transparent family of bounded hearts. The proof uses total vanishing of cross morphisms and higher extensions, making clear why it cannot be transferred to an unspecified geometric category.

## Definitions

Let A=Vec_C^r and D=Dᵇ(A), with simple objects S₁,…,S_r. Every object is a finite direct sum of shifts S_i[n].

A bounded t-structure supplies truncations and a heart; use the standard convention that objects of the heart shifted by [1] lie in D^{≤−1}.

Morphisms Hom_D(S_i[a],S_j[b]) vanish unless i=j and a=b, in which case they are C.

## Result name

Shifted semisimple hearts

## Result statement

Choosing integers n_i gives a bounded heart generated by S_i[n_i], equivalent to Vec_C^r. Every bounded t-structure on this D arises by choosing one cutoff independently on each simple shift-chain.

## Proof

Each component Dᵇ(Vec_C) is an orthogonal direct factor. In one component, closure of the aisle D^{≤0} under [1] means its set of allowed shifts is upward closed. Boundedness ensures that set is neither empty nor all integers, so it has a least element n. Orthogonality and truncation force the complementary coaisle cutoff, leaving S[n] as the unique simple in the heart. To see that the aisle is determined by its indecomposables, use the finite direct-sum splitting of every object and closure of a t-structure under direct summands. Apply the one-factor argument to each orthogonal component. Conversely the chosen cutoffs define truncations by simply retaining summands on the appropriate side, verifying the axioms directly.

## Worked example: setup

Let r=2 and choose n₁=0,n₂=2.

## Worked example: calculation

The heart contains S₁ and S₂[2], and every object is S₁^a⊕S₂[2]^b for nonnegative integers a,b. Cross Hom and every Ext¹ vanish, so exact sequences split. Shifting only S₂ again changes its embedding in D without producing new extension classes. In the elliptic-curve heart, Ext¹(O_X,O_X)=C, so the decomposition into independent orthogonal simple chains is unavailable.

## Worked example: conclusion

A complete classification is proved for a declared toy category, with the exact obstruction to transferring its proof identified.

## Limits

The result does not establish semisimplicity or heart classification for the historical exponential category. Noetherian and Artinian properties, mixed Ext vanishing and boundedness must be verified there separately.

## Extension

Given a nonsemisimple finite algebra, compute its Ext quiver and a torsion pair, perform one explicit tilt, and identify which new heart objects arise as genuine extensions rather than independent shifts.
