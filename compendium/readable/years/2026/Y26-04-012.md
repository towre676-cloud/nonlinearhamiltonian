## Revised question retained in the annual source

Under what exact symmetry does a family-only target produce the zero gauge-fixed solution?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The source isotropy claim has a simple proof when every cyclic difference family has uniform weight and target. Symmetry breaking changes the forcing term JᵀWb rather than somehow removing the need to solve the normal equations.

## Definitions

Use (J_dx)_i=x_(i+d)−x_i on a connected cyclic design. In each family d, take a constant target b_(d,i)=β_d and a constant positive weight w_d.

Minimize E(x)=1/2 Σ_(d,i)w_d((J_dx)_i−β_d)² over Σx_i=0.

## Result name

Exact symmetry cancellation

## Result statement

JᵀWb=0 and the unique mean-zero minimizer is x*=0. The residual energy is then 1/2 Σ_d n w_dβ_d², so zero displacement does not mean zero target mismatch.

## Proof

For every family, Σ_i(J_dx)_i=Σ_ix_(i+d)−Σ_ix_i=0 by permutation of indices. Consequently the cross term Σ_iw_dβ_d(J_dx)_i is zero for every x, equivalent to JᵀWb=0. Expanding E gives a constant plus 1/2||W^(1/2)Jx||². Connectedness makes the latter positive definite on the mean-zero subspace, so x=0 is uniquely minimizing. A nonconstant target or nonconstant row weights can destroy the cancellation; the new solution is then the gauge-fixed inverse of JᵀWJ applied to JᵀWb.

## Worked example: setup

Use a three-cycle with all offsets one and target β=1, weight one.

## Worked example: calculation

The objective is 1/2[(x₂−x₁−1)²+(x₃−x₂−1)²+(x₁−x₃−1)²]. The differences sum to zero, so E=3/2+1/2Σdifferences². Under x₁+x₂+x₃=0, its unique minimum is x=0 with E=3/2.

## Worked example: conclusion

The source’s qualitative isotropy mechanism is proved without claiming its unrecovered numerical residual.

## Limits

Family labels alone do not establish symmetry; actual row indexing and weight constancy are necessary. The original contextual target values are not reproduced.

## Extension

Decompose a target into its family-constant and symmetry-breaking parts and bound the response to the latter with the smallest positive weighted-Laplacian eigenvalue.
