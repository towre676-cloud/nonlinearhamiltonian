## Proof

Choosingx,y,d independently withthe stated nonzero constraint gives the count, and the tuple uniquely recovers its parameters. The image underΦ has base(Ax+b,Ay+c) and common nonzero incrementAd, so it is again a corner; invertibility supplies a permutation. With independentA,B the two arms have incrementsAd andBd. Requiring them to share the same increment for every nonzerod givesAd=Bd for all d and henceA=B. This proves a sufficient symmetry family and a necessity statement within that explicitly restricted affine form; it does not classify every possible unordered-corner automorphism. The distinction matters because reordering the three vertices or mixing coordinates may yield additional symmetries with different conventions.
