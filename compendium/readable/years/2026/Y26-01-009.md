## Revised question retained in the annual source

How can a single specified family connect critical values, a discriminant, a Gelfand–Leray form and coalescing stationary points?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

The cubic fold has two critical points that merge at one parameter value. Its caustic and its oscillatory scaling can be derived exactly, showing which singularity-theoretic constructions require regular fibers and why ordinary stationary phase ceases to be uniform at a collision.

## Definitions

Let f_s(x)=x³/3−sx, with real parameter s. A critical point satisfies ∂_x f_s=0; a critical value is its image under f_s.

For a one-dimensional regular fiber, the Gelfand–Leray equation df_s·ω_s=dx defines the scalar form ω_s=1/(x²−s). It is defined only where df_s≠0.

Interpret integrals over all of R with cubic phase as oscillatory integrals, or through a standard convergent regularization.

## Result name

Fold discriminant and uniform cubic rescaling

## Result statement

For s>0 the critical points are ±√s with values ∓2s^{3/2}/3. The critical-value locus in (s,u) is 9u²=4s³. Moreover ∫_R e^{iλf_s(x)}dx=2πλ^{−1/3}Ai(−λ^{2/3}s) for λ>0, in the oscillatory sense.

## Proof

Differentiation gives f_s′=x²−s and f_s″=2x. Substituting x=±√s yields the two values. Eliminating x gives the cusp equation. Equivalently the fiber polynomial x³−3sx−3u has discriminant 108s³−243u², vanishing precisely on that locus. Away from the critical points multiplication by 1/(x²−s) solves the Gelfand–Leray equation; its failure at the critical points is a geometric feature rather than a removable computational error. For the integral put x=λ^{−1/3}t. The phase becomes t³/3−λ^{2/3}st and the Jacobian is λ^{−1/3}. The defining Fourier representation of Ai gives the stated formula. For fixed positive s, two nondegenerate stationary points give separate λ^{−1/2} contributions, but their coefficients involve |2√s|^{−1/2} and diverge as s→0. The Airy expression stays uniform through that collision.

## Worked example: setup

Set s=1 and compare with s=0.

## Worked example: calculation

At s=1 the critical values are −2/3 and 2/3; 9u²=4 holds at both. At s=0 they merge at x=u=0. The exactly rescaled integral there is 2πAi(0)λ^{−1/3}, with Ai(0)=1/(3^{2/3}Γ(2/3)), rather than a λ^{−1/2} Gaussian law.

## Worked example: conclusion

Discriminant geometry predicts precisely where the asymptotic model must change.

## Limits

This is an A₂ fold model, not a classification of all ADE or Vassiliev discriminants. Monodromy requires a complex parameter loop and a specified homology local system; a real drawing alone does not define it.

## Extension

Complexify the family, choose vanishing cycles and calculate their Picard–Lefschetz transport. Then construct a uniform approximation for a compactly supported amplitude and prove an error bound across a stated neighborhood of the caustic.
