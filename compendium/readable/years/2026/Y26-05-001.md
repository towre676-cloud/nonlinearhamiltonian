## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What composition rule prevents a derived certificate from silently exceeding the authority of its premises?

## Status

Classical result or newly stipulated finite model; historical claims explicitly delimited

## Summary

The May artifact calculus proposed a six-coordinate authority profile. A precise finite poset model makes the intended monotonicity rule executable, while retaining the distinction between an order-theoretic accounting theorem and the truth of any mathematical assertion entered into the system.

## Definitions

Let A={0,1,2}⁶ with coordinatewise order and meet a∧b given by coordinatewise minimum. A dependency graph is a finite directed acyclic graph; every node has a local authority cap c(v).

For a source node set a(v)=c(v). At any later node define a(v)=c(v)∧∧_{u→v}a(u). The coordinates are abstract bookkeeping levels, not measured probabilities.

## Result name

Authority composition is a meet calculation with an explicit proof dependency graph

## Result statement

For every node v, a(v) is the meet of c(w) over all ancestors w of v, including v. It is the largest profile simultaneously bounded by all those caps. Adding an additional dependency can only decrease or preserve the profile.

## Proof

Order the graph topologically. The claim is immediate for a source. If it holds for the predecessors of v, substituting their ancestor meets into the defining equation gives exactly the union of their ancestor sets together with v; repeated factors do not matter because meet is idempotent. This proves the formula by induction. Any other profile bounded by every ancestor cap is bounded by their meet, so the formula is the greatest admissible profile. Enlarging the ancestor set introduces further meet factors and cannot raise any coordinate. None of these steps supplies a proof that an individual premise is true: that requires a separately checked derivation. A soundness statement is conditional: if the local inference rules preserve truth and the source propositions are true, induction through the same graph proves every output proposition true.

## Worked example: setup

Use two premises with profiles a=(2,2,1,2,1,2) and b=(2,1,2,2,2,1), and a local cap c=(2,2,2,1,2,2).

## Worked example: calculation

The output meet is (2,1,1,1,1,1). A later node whose only extra cap is (1,2,2,2,2,2) receives (1,1,1,1,1,1). Swapping premise order or duplicating a premise changes neither result. A forgotten dependency with first coordinate zero would reduce that coordinate to zero, exposing why the dependency set itself must be complete.

## Worked example: conclusion

The calculus now has a small genuine theorem: maximal admissible authority propagates by a meet, with a transparent dependency invariant.

## Limits

The numerical labels are newly stipulated ordinal levels. A profile is not a theorem prover, an uncertainty probability, or evidence that a reported software suite ran.

## Extension

Extend the rule to an arbitrary complete lattice and potentially infinite well-founded dependency graph; joins may model alternative derivations, but a derivation using a join must still identify which alternative actually supplies its premises.
