## Proof

The exclusion implication is direct set containment, but its force depends on whatS actually encloses. ReplacingS by a larger independent box remains safe and may become inconclusive; replacing it by an optimistic fitted body is unsafe. For uniqueness, a uniform matrix-norm bound||I−CJF(x)||≤q<1 implies Lipschitz contraction by integrating the derivative along line segments. Neither a finite grid nor a rounded total count supplies those uniform bounds.
