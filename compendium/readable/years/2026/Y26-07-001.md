## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which exact polynomial and boundary witnesses suffice to certify the seven-point spectrum?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The July1 source reports a rational spectral-rigidity theorem with support0,1,2,4,5,6,8. Its original matrix and boundary seeds are not fully retrieved. A complete general certificate states exactly what those missing data would need to establish, and gives an explicit rational model rather than adopting an unseen computation.

## Definitions

Let S={0,1,2,4,5,6,8} and p(t)=∏_{λ∈S}(t−λ). Let T be an endomorphism of a finite-dimensional rational vector space. Define e_λ(t)=∏_{ν∈S,ν≠λ}(t−ν)/(λ−ν).

## Result name

Lagrange spectral certificate

## Result statement

If p(T)=0, then E_λ=e_λ(T) are pairwise disjoint idempotents, sum to I and satisfy T=ΣλE_λ. The spectrum equals S exactly when every E_λ is nonzero; one vector with E_λv≠0 certifies each nonzero projector.

## Proof

On the seven roots, e_λ(ν)=δ_λν. Thus e_λ²−e_λ, e_λe_ν for λ≠ν, Σe_λ−1 and t−Σλe_λ all vanish on S and are divisible by the square-free polynomial p. Evaluating at T proves the identities. The image of E_λ consists of λ-eigenvectors because(t−λ)e_λ is divisible by p. Their direct sum is the entire space, so p(T)=0 also proves semisimplicity rather than merely listing possible eigenvalues. Conversely a nonzero E_λ supplies an eigenvector, making the support criterion exact. A spectral list alone would not exclude a Jordan block, for which p(T) could fail. The same projectors compute every polynomial observable: h(T)=Σh(λ)E_λ. For z outsideS they also give the exact resolvent(zI−T)^{-1}=ΣE_λ/(z−λ), verified by multiplication. Thus the certificate supplies inversion and spectral decomposition, not just an eigenvalue list. Its denominators are explicit rational differences between distinct roots.

## Worked example: setup

Take T=diag(0,1,2,4,5,6,8) and v=(1,1,1,1,1,1,1).

## Worked example: calculation

Each E_λv is the corresponding standard basis vector. All seven eigenvalues therefore occur. The minimal polynomial is p, and seven independent boundary projections certify the full support without numerical diagonalization.

## Worked example: conclusion

An exact rational certificate is a polynomial identity plus explicit support witnesses, with no floating eigenvalue tolerance.
