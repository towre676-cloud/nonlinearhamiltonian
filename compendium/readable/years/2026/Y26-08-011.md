## Result name

Path-basis flatness of the pencil

## Result statement

Every fibreA_[u:v] has dimension13. The family is locally free of rank13 overP¹ and hence flat. This statement concerns the underlying algebra family, not constancy of Hochschild cohomology.

## Proof

After imposingγ²=0 but before the final relation, a path basis consists of three vertex idempotents; four arrows; five length-two pathsγα,βγ,β′γ,βα,β′α; and two length-three pathsβγα,β′γα. There are no longer surviving paths because any repetition ofγ givesγ² and vertex2 is a sink. Thus this intermediate algebra has dimension14. The final relation is a nonzero vector inspan{βγα,βα} for every projective[u:v]. Both paths start at the source0 and end at the sink2, so multiplication by any nontrivial path on either side is impossible; their generated two-sided ideal is exactly the one-dimensional relation line. The quotient therefore has dimension13. Globally this is a quotient of the trivial rank14 path bundle by anO(−1) subbundle embedded as(u,−v) in a trivial rank-two summand. The map is nowhere zero, so its cokernel is locally free. The unaffected12-dimensional summand remains trivial and the rank-two quotient isO(1), yielding a vector-bundle descriptionO^{12}⊕O(1).
