## Proof

Count ordered symplectic bases(e₁, f₁, e₂, f₂). Choose e₁ nonzero in q⁴−1 ways. The equation⟨e₁, f₁⟩=1 is an affine hyperplane with q³ solutions. Their span is a nondegenerate plane; its orthogonal complement is a nondegenerate two-plane. Choose e₂ nonzero there in q²−1 ways, and f₂ with pairing one in q ways. The product is(q⁴−1)q³(q²−1)q=q⁴(q²−1)(q⁴−1). Every symplectic matrix is uniquely determined by the image ofa fixed symplectic basis, proving the order formula. For odd q the scalar symplectic matrices are±I and the standard center is exactly these; quotienting divides by two. At q=5 this gives625·24·624/2=4680000. A breadth-first closure under generators and their inverses terminates because the group or its projective action is finite, giving an exact orbit certificate when all operations are over the declared field.
