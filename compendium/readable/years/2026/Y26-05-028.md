## Definitions

Let H be the3-by7 binary matrix whose columns are all seven nonzero vectors of F₂³. Define C=kerH⊂F₂⁷. Hamming weight counts nonzero coordinates and distance is the weight of a difference.

A syndrome is Hv. Flipping coordinate j adds the j-th column of H to the syndrome.

## Result name

A binary Hamming code follows from a rank-three parity map

## Result statement

C has parameters [7,4,3]. Every received word at Hamming distance at most one from C has a unique corrected codeword, found by matching its nonzero syndrome to a column of H.

## Proof

The columns include the three standard basis vectors, so rankH=3 and rank-nullity gives dimension four. A weight-one codeword would correspond to a zero column, which is absent. A weight-two codeword would require two equal columns, also absent. Three columns x,y,x+y are distinct nonzero vectors and sum to zero, giving a weight-three codeword and proving minimum distance exactly three. If two codewords were both within distance one of a received word, their mutual distance would be at most two by the triangle inequality, contradicting the minimum distance. A one-bit error has syndrome equal to its column, so the proposed decoder recovers its location and removes it. The balls have size eight, and16 codewords times eight equals128, the total number of binary seven-vectors, so these disjoint radius-one balls cover the space.

## Worked example: setup

Order the columns as001,010,011,100,101,110,111.

## Worked example: calculation

The word1110000 is a codeword because001+010+011=000. If coordinate five is flipped, the received syndrome is101, identifying that coordinate uniquely. Enumerating the sixteen kernel words gives weight enumerator1+7z³+7z⁴+z⁷, whose coefficient sum is16 and whose smallest nonzero exponent is three.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite model has proved rank, distance, decoder and exact enumerator.

## Limits

This does not certify a historical BCH polynomial, a larger benchmark code or any noisy-channel performance estimate. The unique-correction guarantee is a worst-case one-error statement.

## Extension
