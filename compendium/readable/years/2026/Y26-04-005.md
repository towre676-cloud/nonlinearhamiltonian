## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does the proposed transport model prove about quotient fibers and noncommuting loops?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The source model U=e^(zI)e^(xE₁₂)e^(yE₂₁) separates a scalar direction from two shear directions. Its noncommutativity can be computed exactly. A quotient that forgets z has a vertical kernel, but it does not automatically create curvature for every chosen connection.

## Definitions

Let X=E₁₂ and Y=E₂₁. Since X²=Y²=0, e^(xX)=I+xX and e^(yY)=I+yY. Define π(x,y,z)=(x,y).

Then ker dπ is span{∂z}. The scalar observable O=z² varies along a fiber and therefore does not descend to the quotient.

## Result name

Exact shear commutator and descent test

## Result statement

The loop product (I+xX)(I+yY)(I−xX)(I−yY) equals [[1+xy+x²y²,−x²y],[xy²,1−xy]]. Its leading mixed term is xy[X,Y]=xy diag(1,−1).

## Proof

Multiply the four two-by-two matrices in order, using X²=Y²=0; the displayed polynomial matrix is exact and has determinant one. The commutator [X,Y]=XY−YX=diag(1,−1) identifies the first nontrivial infinitesimal loop term. The scalar factor e^(zI) commutes with every matrix and therefore cancels in such a loop. A function descends through π exactly when it is constant as z varies with x,y fixed; z² fails this criterion. The kernel statement is a derivative calculation, while curvature requires a specified connection form and its structure equation. One should not identify the vertical fiber with an observed matrix commutator without supplying that additional geometry.

## Worked example: setup

Use x=y=1.

## Worked example: calculation

The exact loop is [[3,−1],[1,0]], with determinant one and trace three, so it is not the identity. At (x,y,z)=(0,0,1) and (0,0,2), π gives the same point but O gives one and four.

## Worked example: conclusion

The model has demonstrable noncommutativity and a demonstrable quotient-observable obstruction.

## Limits

The calculation does not prove that a source transport atlas has nonzero curvature or that z² measures every obstruction.
