## Summary

The late-June source names closure, deficit, idempotence and order defects. A finite-set model supplies a genuine termination theorem, a telescoping deficit identity and an explicit failure of commuting intuition.

## Definitions

On the power set of a finite universeU, a closure operatorc is extensive, S⊆cS; monotone, S⊆T⇒cS⊆cT; andidempotent, c²=c.

For two closuresc, diterateS_{n+1}=c(d(S_n)). Define the counting deficit of a step as|S_{n+1}|−|S_n|.

## Result name

Finite closure iteration terminates by rank, but noncommuting closures can hide new elements

## Result statement

The iteration stabilizes after at most|U|−|S₀|strict increases. Its limit is the least set containingS₀fixed by both c and d. The sum of all step deficits is the final cardinality increase. A single passcdneed not itself be idempotent.

## Proof

Extensivity gives S_n⊆dS_n⊆c(dS_n)=S_{n+1}. Every strict increase adds an element of the finite universe, proving termination. At a fixed point S=c(dS), the inclusions S⊆dS⊆c(dS)=S force dS=S; then cS=S as well. If T contains S₀ and is fixed by both, monotonicity inductively gives S_n⊆T, proving leastness of the limit. The deficit sum telescopes because the successive intermediate cardinalities cancel. For nonidempotence of one pass, let U={a, b, c}, let c add b when a is present, and let d add c when b is present. Both are closure operators. Starting at {a}, cd gives {a, b}, while a second pass gives {a, b, c}. In the opposite order dc, the first pass already adds both. The ordered composition therefore records actual causal dependency between closure rules.

## Limits

A cardinality deficit is not automatically entropy, approximation error or computational cost. Infinite universes require an ordinal or convergence argument and may not stabilize in finitely many steps.
