## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which hypotheses make x^μ↦x^μf(x^γ)^{B(γ,μ)} a Poisson automorphism?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The August14 source lists generalized wall maps and a matrixB=[[0,1],[−2,1]]. That matrix is not skew in characteristic zero, so it cannot literally define an antisymmetric log-canonical Poisson bracket. A corrected theorem identifies the missing assumption and proves both inversion and Poisson preservation.

## Definitions

LetΛ be an integer lattice with skew integer pairingB. In a formal Laurent completion write{x^μ,x^ν}=B(μ,ν)x^{μ+ν}. Letf(z) be an invertible formal series withconstant term1, z=x^γ, anddefineθ_f(x^μ)=x^μf(z)^{B(γ,μ)}.

## Result name

Wall automorphism theorem

## Result statement

θ_f fixesz, has inverseθ_{f^{-1}}, satisfiesθ_fθ_g=θ_{fg} for the sameγ, and preserves the displayed Poisson bracket. These assertions needB(γ,γ)=0 and skew symmetry in the proof.

## Proof

Seta=B(γ,μ),b=B(γ,ν). Skew symmetry givesB(γ,γ)=0, soθ_f(z)=z and multiplicativity immediately supplies composition and inversion. For the bracket, logarithmic differentiation contributes to{ x^μf^a,x^νf^b } the two cross corrections−ab zf′/f and+ab zf′/f, which cancel. The remaining coefficient isB(μ,ν), proving Poisson preservation on monomials and hence on the completed algebra. If the exponent pairing is not skew, the bracket itself fails antisymmetry; ifB(γ,γ)≠0, the map can change its own argumentz and reciprocal substitution is generally not the inverse. These are algebraic issues, independent of an attractive wall-crossing analogy. A skew integer pairing need not be nondegenerate: a central monomialx^κ withB(κ,μ)=0 for allμ is fixed by the bracket and by every wall map of this form. The construction therefore works on a Poisson torus with Casimir directions as well as on a symplectic torus. Nondegeneracy is unnecessary for the stated algebraic preservation proof.
