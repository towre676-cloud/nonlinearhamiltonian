## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which trace groups can be realized constructively, and what additional theorem is needed for general Cantor systems?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The source invokes Herman–Putnam–Skau realization for finitely generated and countable ordered groups. Explicit elementary models clarify normalization and show that non-finitely generated trace ranges are possible without claiming a full dynamical realization proof from a summary.

## Definitions

For a unital C*-algebra A with tracial state τ, the induced map τ*:K₀(A)→R sends a projection class to its matrix-extended trace. Its range is an additive subgroup containing one.

For M_q(C), normalize τ=Tr/q. For the dyadic UHF algebra, take the inductive limit M₂→M₄→M₈→… under a↦diag(a,a).

## Result name

Exact cyclic and countable realizations

## Result statement

The trace range of M_q(C) is (1/q)Z. The dyadic inductive limit has trace range Z[1/2], a countable, dense, non-finitely generated subgroup of R containing one.

## Proof

In a matrix algebra, K₀ classes are determined by integer rank, and a rank-one projection has normalized trace 1/q; stabilized differences give every integer multiple. At the dyadic stage M_(2^n), the range is 2^(−n)Z. The diagonal embedding preserves normalized trace and doubles ordinary ranks, so the compatible union is exactly ∪_n2^(−n)Z. Density follows by rounding 2^nx to an integer with error at most 2^(−n). Non-finite generation follows because a finite set of dyadic rationals has a common denominator 2^N and cannot generate 2^(−N−1). These are constructive trace-group realizations. Realizing the same ordered group by a Cantor minimal system additionally uses a dimension-group realization theorem with its simplicity, interpolation and order-unit hypotheses.

## Worked example: setup

Take q=3 and the dyadic projection p of rank three in M₈.

## Worked example: calculation

The first algebra has trace range (1/3)Z and rank-one trace 1/3. The second has τ(p)=3/8. It also has projections of trace 1/16 at a later stage, unavailable inside the additive group generated by 1 and 3/8 alone.

## Worked example: conclusion

Normalization and non-finite generation are demonstrated exactly.

## Limits

The full general HPS realization theorem is referenced, not reproved here. Arbitrary countable groups do not qualify without an ordered dimension-group structure.

## Extension

State the precise ordered group, order unit and state, then construct its Bratteli diagram and verify minimality and unique-state hypotheses before passing to the crossed product.
