## Result name

Cascade laws and flux preservation

## Result statement

Power ratios multiply and their dB values add. A transfer matrixT preservesq exactly iffT*JT=J. Products of such matrices preserveq; their determinant magnitudes equal one. The determinant condition alone is insufficient.

## Proof

The logarithm identitylog(rs)=logr+logs proves the dB cascade law. The factor20 follows fromlog|a₂/a₁|²=2log|a₂/a₁|, and applies only when the power-to-amplitude normalization cancels; unequal impedances introduce another ratio. For flux, q(Ta)=a*T*JTa, so equality for everya is equivalent to the matrix identity by polarization. IfT₁ andT₂ satisfy it, (T₁T₂)*J(T₁T₂)=T₂*JT₂=J. Taking determinants gives|detT|²detJ=detJ and hence|detT|=1. The converse fails: determinant only constrains a product of singular values, not the whole indefinite form. Boundary matching and the physical normalization determine whichJ is appropriate; it cannot be copied between unrelated field variables.
