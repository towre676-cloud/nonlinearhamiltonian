## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can the proposed graph invariance and attraction estimates be proved in a fully specified nonlinear model?

## Status

## Summary

The source proposes a graph-transform theorem on a Hilbert space but leaves several hypotheses unresolved. An exact finite-dimensional example demonstrates what an invariant graph, tangency, nonresonance and normal attraction actually require.

## Definitions

Consider F(x,y)=(ax,by+cx²), where 0<b<a<1. Seek an analytic invariant graph y=h(x) with h(0)=h′(0)=0.

Graph invariance is h(ax)=bh(x)+cx². Nonresonance for an analytic scalar graph is a^k≠b for every integer k≥2.

## Result name

Exact invariant graph and transverse decay

## Result statement

If a^k≠b for all k≥2, the unique analytic graph tangent to the x-axis is h(x)=cx²/(a²−b). The vertical discrepancy η=y−h(x) obeys η_(n+1)=bη_n exactly.

## Proof

Expand h=Σ_(k≥2)h_kx^k and compare coefficients in the invariance equation. The k=2 coefficient is (a²−b)h₂=c; every higher coefficient satisfies (a^k−b)h_k=0 and therefore vanishes. The resulting polynomial is an actual analytic solution, so this calculation proves existence and uniqueness within analytic tangent graphs, not just a formal approximation. Subtract h(ax) from the second component: by+cx²−h(ax)=b[y−h(x)]. Iterating gives η_n=b^nη₀. On a bounded chart, distance to the graph is comparable to |η| by its bounded slope, yielding a geometric attraction estimate with explicit chart-dependent constants.

## Worked example: setup

Use a=1/2,b=1/10,c=1.

## Worked example: calculation

a²−b=3/20, so h(x)=20x²/3. No integer k satisfies 2^(−k)=1/10 because 2^k≠10. At x=3/10, h=3/5; an initial point with y=4/5 has η₀=1/5 and η_n=10^(−n)/5.

## Worked example: conclusion

The source theorem’s desired conclusions have an exact model with all hypotheses and constants visible.

## Limits

This proof does not establish a general Hilbert-space graph transform, compact NHIM persistence or a nested invariant manifold. Those require separately stated global or localized assumptions.

## Extension

For the actual operator split, certify a bounded local chart, a uniform inverse for each homological operator and a contraction estimate for the nonlinear graph transform.
