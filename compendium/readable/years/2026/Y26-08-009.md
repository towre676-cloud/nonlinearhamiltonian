## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which variance and pseudovariance pairs define a valid complex Gaussian likelihood?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The August17 source includes a complex likelihood withv=E|η|² andδ=Eη². These two moments must satisfy a positivity condition. Deriving the real covariance gives an exact normalization and exposes the degenerate boundary.

## Definitions

Letη=X+iY be centered jointly real Gaussian. Setv=E(X²+Y²) andδ=E(X²−Y²)+2iE(XY). The pseudovarianceδ vanishes for a proper centered complex Gaussian.

## Result name

Validity and normalized density

## Result statement

The real covariance isΣ=(1/2)[[v+Reδ,Imδ],[Imδ,v−Reδ]]. It is positive semidefinite iff v≥|δ|. Forv>|δ| its density atz∈C is exp{−[v|z|²−Re(δ̄z²)]/(v²−|δ|²)}/[π√(v²−|δ|²)].

## Proof

Solving the definitions forE X²,E Y² andE XY givesΣ. Its eigenvalues are(v±|δ|)/2, proving the validity condition includingv≥0. Its determinant is(v²−|δ|²)/4. Substitute the inverse of this2×2 matrix in the standard real Gaussian density(2π√detΣ)^{-1}exp(−rᵀΣ^{-1}r/2), and simplify usingr=(Re z,Im z) to obtain the displayed expression. Atv=|δ|>0 the distribution lies on a line and has no two-dimensional Lebesgue density; the apparent formula cannot be used by taking its denominator to zero. Under multiplicationη↦aη, the moments transform asv↦|a|²v andδ↦a²δ, making the phase dependence of pseudovariance explicit. The same covariance formula yields differential entropy log(πe√(v²−|δ|²)) when the covariance is nonsingular. For fixedv, this is largest atδ=0 and decreases to minus infinity at the degenerate boundary. This is a statement about a stipulated Gaussian family and a fixed two-dimensional reference measure, not a universal information claim for arbitrary non-Gaussian complex variables.

Takev=5,δ=3+0i.
