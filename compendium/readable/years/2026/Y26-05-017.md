## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What does a complete elementary momentum-level quotient look like, including its reduced Hamiltonian?

## Status

Classical result or newly stipulated finite model; historical claims explicitly delimited

## Summary

The source names canonical transformations, quotienting and Marsden–Weinstein reduction. A two-pair translation example provides a fully explicit model of the reduction mechanism, without assuming that an arbitrary quotient inherits a nondegenerate two-form.

## Definitions

On T*R² use coordinates (q₁,q₂,p₁,p₂) and ω=dq₁∧dp₁+dq₂∧dp₂. The additive group R translates q₂. Its momentum is J=p₂ under the convention ι_Xω=dJ.

Fix J=μ and quotient its level set by translations of q₂. The quotient is identified with (q₁,p₁). Let H=(p₁²+p₂²)/2+V(q₁), which is translation invariant.

## Result name

Symplectic reduction removes a cyclic coordinate only after fixing momentum

## Result statement

The level-set pullback of ω is dq₁∧dp₁; its null direction is precisely ∂_{q₂}. It descends to a nondegenerate reduced symplectic form, and the reduced Hamiltonian is H_μ=p₁²/2+V(q₁)+μ²/2. Projected full trajectories agree with its Hamiltonian flow.

## Proof

On the level p₂=μ, tangent vectors satisfy dp₂=0. Hence the pullback of the second wedge term vanishes. The remaining form pairs only q₁ and p₁, and its kernel within the three-dimensional level set is the one-dimensional translation direction. Because it is invariant and annihilates vertical vectors, it descends to the quotient; in the quotient coordinates its matrix is the standard invertible two-dimensional symplectic matrix. Full Hamilton equations are q₁′=p₁,p₁′=−V′(q₁), q₂′=p₂ and p₂′=0. Fixing p₂=μ and forgetting q₂ leaves exactly the equations of H_μ. The additive constant μ²/2 changes energy values but not the reduced vector field. This is the simplest complete reduction theorem, with regularity and freeness visible in coordinates.

## Worked example: setup

Let V(q₁)=q₁²/2 and μ=3.

## Worked example: calculation

The reduced flow is the unit harmonic oscillator. Starting at q₁=1,p₁=0 gives q₁=cost,p₁=−sint, with reduced energy five. The full trajectory also has q₂(t)=q₂(0)+3t and p₂=3. Quotienting removes this drift while preserving the physical equations in the remaining pair.

## Worked example: conclusion

The quotient has a proved descended form and an exact dynamical interpretation.

## Limits

If the group action is not free, the momentum value is singular or the Hamiltonian is not invariant, this coordinate proof does not apply. A generic structured-object quotient is not automatically a symplectic reduction.

## Extension

Analyze an S¹ action on C² at a nonzero regular momentum level, explicitly construct the projective quotient and its reduced area form, then identify what changes at the zero level.
