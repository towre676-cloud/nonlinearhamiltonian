## Revised question retained in the annual source

How does an ordered endpoint commitment differ from group-valued path transport, and why is neither automatically a complete history invariant?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

A nested hash update is order-sensitive rather than inherently abelian. Matrix transport gives a separate noncommutative loop invariant, and both constructions require declared equivalences and collision assumptions before they can certify histories.

## Definitions

A hash-chain update has h_{t+1}=H(h_t∥m_t), using unambiguous serialization. Its finite output is an endpoint summary of an ordered sequence.

For invertible edge transports U_e, path transport is their ordered product, and gauge changes conjugate loop holonomy at its basepoint.

The toy arithmetic hash H(h,m)=3h+m mod101 is only an exact counterexample to order-insensitivity, not a cryptographic primitive.

## Result name

Order sensitivity and explicit nontrivial loop transport

## Result statement

Starting at zero, the toy chains (1,2) and (2,1) end at 5 and 7. For U=[[1,1],[0,1]] and V=[[1,0],[1,1]], the commutator UVU⁻¹V⁻¹=[[3,−1],[1,0]] is nonidentity. If the recorded path language allows an edge followed by its reverse, holonomy identifies that two-step record with the empty path; the hash map also has collisions on unrestricted histories.

## Proof

The hash calculation gives H(H(0,1),2)=3·1+2=5 and H(H(0,2),1)=3·2+1=7. Thus nested endpoint accumulation is not an abelian sum. Conversely (0,3) and (1,0) both end at three for this toy hash, illustrating collisions. Any fixed b-bit hash has only 2^b endpoints, so arbitrarily many finite histories force collisions by pigeonhole; computational collision resistance is a different property. Direct multiplication of U,V and their inverses proves the commutator formula. When backtracking is allowed as recorded history, UU⁻¹=I gives an explicit endpoint collision with the empty word; no such claim is made for the restricted positive-word monoid. Under a basepoint change of gauge G, a closed path product becomes GHG⁻¹, so its conjugacy class, rather than its raw matrix, is the natural gauge invariant.

## Limits

The toy example does not implement TPM policy, cryptographic security or physical attestation. The historical assertion that PCR extension is abelian is mathematically inaccurate; irreversibility and commutativity are different properties.
