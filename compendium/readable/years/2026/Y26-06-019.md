## Result name

The proposed cubic-cancellation counterexample is actually an infinite exact Moyal family

## Result statement

For every r≥1, [qp³, q^{3r+2}p^r]_M={qp³, q^{3r+2}p^r}=−(8r+6)q^{3r+2}p^{r+2}. In particular S₅=0, contradicting the retrieved claimed nonzero fifth-order expression.

## Proof

The first input has degree four, so every k≥5 term is identically zero. Only the possible k=3 correction remains. For a=1, b=3, c=3r+2, d=r, the terms in S₃ with r-index zero or one vanish because(a)₃=(a)₂=0. The two remaining terms give S₃=3·(1)₁(3)₂(c)₂r−(3)₃(c)₃=18c(c−1)r−6c(c−1)(c−2)=6c(c−1)[3r−(c−2)]=0. Therefore every higher odd coefficient vanishes. The Poisson coefficient is ad−bc=r−3(3r+2)=−8r−6, and the resulting exponents are a+c−1=c and b+d−1=r+2, proving the formula. This argument is uniform in r, so a finite search is corroboration rather than its proof.

## Limits

Other monomial pairs of higher total degree can have S₃=0 but nonzero S₅. This result does not assert that cubic cancellation is always sufficient; it identifies why it is sufficient in this family.
