## Result name

CRT factorization and idempotent splitting give constructive finite closure

## Result statement

τ(161)=4 and τ(483)=8. CRT produces orthogonal idempotents summing to one for the coprime factors. Every finite-dimensional idempotent splits as V=imP⊕kerP, and the image has dimension trP.

## Proof

A divisor independently chooses an exponent between zero and a_i, proving the count. For coprime m, n choose inverses n^{−1} modm and m^{−1} modn. Then e_m=n(n^{−1} modm) is one modm and zero modn; e_n is the opposite. Their products and squares are checked componentwise by CRT, yielding e_m+e_n=1, e_me_n=0 and e_i²=e_i. For linear P, write v=Pv+(v−Pv); the second summand is killed by P, and a vector in both image and kernel is zero because P acts as identity on its image. In a basis adapted to this direct sum P is diag(I_r,0), giving trace r. Idempotent completion formally adjoins such image objects where an abstract category did not already contain them. It does not change the underlying polynomial identity P²=P into a physical measurement theorem.
