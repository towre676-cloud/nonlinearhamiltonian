## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which exact radical and extension data can an algebraic kernel extraction compute, and which are invariant under Morita equivalence?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

The dual-number algebra supplies a complete radical filtration and a nonsplit self-extension of its simple module. Its two-by-two matrix algebra has different dimension and element counts but the same module theory, showing why raw algebra size is not a Morita invariant.

## Definitions

Let k be a field and A=k[ε]/(ε²). Every element is uniquely a+bε. Its unique simple module is S=A/(ε).

The Jacobson radical J is the intersection of maximal left ideals; the Loewy length is the least positive r with J^r=0.

Two rings are Morita equivalent when their module categories are equivalent. For R=M₂(A), the matrix idempotent e=E₁₁ has eRe≅A.

## Result name

Explicit radical, self-extension and matrix equivalence

## Result statement

J(A)=kε, J²=0, and Ext¹_A(S,S)≅k. The exact sequence 0→S→A→S→0 is nonsplit. M₂(A) is Morita equivalent to A, with radical M₂(J), square-zero radical and the same Loewy length two.

## Proof

An element a+bε is a unit exactly when a≠0, with inverse a⁻¹−ba⁻²ε. Thus all nonunits form the ideal kε, whose quotient is the field k, proving it is the unique maximal ideal and the radical. Multiplication by ε on A has image and kernel both kε, giving an infinite projective resolution of S with every differential multiplication by ε. Applying Hom_A(−,S) makes every differential zero, so every positive Ext degree, in particular degree one, is k. If the displayed short exact sequence split, A would be S⊕S and ε would act as zero; it acts nontrivially on A because ε·1=ε. For matrix rings, the functors M↦eM and N↦A²⊗_A N are inverse through the matrix-unit identities, establishing Morita equivalence. The matrix radical is square-zero and its quotient M₂(k) is semisimple, giving the stated radical.
