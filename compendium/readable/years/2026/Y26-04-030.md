## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can the proposed D₄ root orbit be proved with explicit field automorphisms?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered example usefully distinguishes symmetry transport from motion. The four roots form a dihedral permutation representation once the splitting field and its degree are established. No force law or time parameter enters the statement.

## Definitions

Let a be the positive real fourth root of two. The roots of x⁴−2 are a,ia,−a,−ia, and the splitting field is K=Q(a,i).

Write D₄ for the symmetry group of a square of order eight. Define ρ(a)=ia,ρ(i)=i and let τ be complex conjugation.

## Result name

Explicit dihedral splitting group

## Result statement

[K:Q]=8 and Gal(K/Q)=⟨ρ,τ:ρ⁴=τ²=1,τρτ=ρ⁻¹⟩≅D₄.

## Proof

Eisenstein at two makes x⁴−2 irreducible over Q, so [Q(a):Q]=4. The field Q(a) is real and does not contain i; adjoining i doubles the degree, giving eight. Since [K:Q(i)]=4, the same polynomial is irreducible over Q(i), and replacing a by ia defines a Q(i)-automorphism ρ of order four. Conjugation τ has order two and sends ia to −ia; checking on the generators a and i gives τρτ=ρ⁻¹. The eight maps ρ^j and τρ^j are distinct: the first four fix i while the other four conjugate it, and their images of a distinguish j. A degree-eight splitting field has exactly these eight automorphisms.

## Worked example: setup

Order the roots as (a,ia,−a,−ia).

## Worked example: calculation

ρ acts by the four-cycle (1 2 3 4). τ fixes roots one and three and swaps two and four. Composing τρτ sends a to −ia, matching ρ⁻¹(a).

## Worked example: conclusion

The symmetry orbit is fully certified and its algebraic type is explicit.

## Limits

An automorphism permutes algebraic values while fixing Q; it is not a geometric transport law in time or evidence for a physical oscillator.

## Extension

Compare the root action with the square’s vertex representation, compute fixed subfields of its subgroups and identify which symmetry information survives each quotient.
