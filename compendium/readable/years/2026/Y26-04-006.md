## Definitions

The second iterate is R∘R. A bifurcation additionally needs a parameter family and a transversality condition; a fixed jet alone provides neither.

## Result name

## Result statement

R(R(s))=s−2(a²+b)s³+O(s⁴). Thus the quadratic term cancels, and a²+b is the leading cubic coefficient controlling the nondegenerate local flip normal form.

## Proof

Set u=−s+as²+bs³+O(s⁴). Then −u=s−as²−bs³+O(s⁴), u²=s²−2as³+O(s⁴), and u³=−s³+O(s⁴). Substitute these into −u+au²+bu³. The quadratic coefficients −a+a cancel, while the cubic terms sum to −b−2a²−b=−2(a²+b). For a family R_μ with multiplier passing transversely through −1, this coefficient contributes to the standard flip nondegeneracy check. Its sign alone is not a complete bifurcation theorem without that family and a consistent parameter orientation.
