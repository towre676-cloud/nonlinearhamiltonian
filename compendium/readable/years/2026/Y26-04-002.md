## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which quartic response can be proved in a fully declared independent-Gaussian model?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The source uses a quartic probe O_z=(z·ξ)^4/24 and cycle residues. Its original density and divergence operator are not fully specified. A transparent monomial model nevertheless explains why a quartic probe detects a four-edge structure and why parity matters.

## Definitions

Let ξ₁,…,ξ_m be independent centered Gaussians with variances A_e⁻¹, where A_e>0. For a four-element edge set C define P_C(ξ)=−s_C∏_(e∈C)ξ_e, with a fixed scalar s_C.

Define O_z=(Σz_eξ_e)^4/4! and R_C(z)=E[P_CO_z]. This is a newly specified model using the recovered probe, not a reconstruction of the unknown source density.

## Result name

Exact quartic residue

## Result statement

R_C(z)=−s_C∏_(e∈C)(z_e/A_e). For an odd-cardinality monomial P_C, the same quartic response vanishes by centered Gaussian parity.

## Proof

Expand the fourth power by the multinomial theorem. To have a nonzero expectation after multiplication by the four distinct factors in P_C, every one of those four indices must occur an odd number of times in the probe. Since the probe has total degree four, each must occur exactly once and no other index can occur. Its multinomial coefficient is 4!, canceled by the normalization. The remaining expectation is the product of four second moments, yielding ∏A_e⁻¹. For odd |C| the total degree |C|+4 is odd, and the joint Gaussian law is invariant under ξ↦−ξ, so expectation equals its negative. This proof applies whether or not z is a graph circulation; the incidence constraint is independent algebraic input.

## Worked example: setup

Take C={1,2,3,4}, all A_e=1, s_C=4, and z=(1,−1,1,−1).

## Worked example: calculation

The product of z entries is one, so R_C(z)=−4. Replacing the fourth entry by zero makes the response exactly zero. A cubic monomial paired with O_z gives zero regardless of z.

## Worked example: conclusion

The probe has a reproducible mechanism and an exact parity test.
