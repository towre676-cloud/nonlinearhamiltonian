## Result name

## Result statement

The solution is y(z)=(Li₂(z),−log(1−z),1). Its finite endpoint first coordinate is Li₂(1)=ζ(2)=π²/6, obtained from the ordered integral∫_0^1[∫_0^z dt/(1−t)]dz/z.

## Proof

The third coordinate is constant. The second therefore integrates to−log(1−z). Dividing it byz gives the derivative of the first coordinate, and termwise integration of the absolutely convergent logarithmic series produces Li₂(z). Monotone convergence atz=1 givesΣ1/n²; convergence also follows directly from the integral test. For completeness, the periodic Bernoulli polynomial B₂(x)=x²−x+1/6 has mean zero and, by two integrations by parts, nonzero Fourier coefficients1/(2π²n²). Their absolute convergence gives B₂(0)=Σ_{n≥1}1/(π²n²), provingζ(2)=π²/6. The ordered integral corresponds to first applying the B-channel and then the A-channel; their product AB=E_13 is nonzero while BA=0. Thus ordering has an exact algebraic effect. Higher iterated integrals can encode multiple-zeta values, but a general associator requires tangential-basepoint regularization and all of its compatibility identities, none of which is supplied by this one finite example.
