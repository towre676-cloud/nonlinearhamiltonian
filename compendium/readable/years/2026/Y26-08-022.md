## Proof

The first result follows by adding the edge inequalities: all internal coordinate values cancel. The credit version is the same telescoping identity with the inequality rearranged. This is a genuine global bound because every transition, including transient ones, is checked. A quotient of the state graph preserves it when all merged states have the same potential and matching resource-marked continuation behavior, or when an explicit simulation proves the required inequality. An unmarked quotient may merge states with different future resource availability and invalidate the argument. Nonnegative reduced edge costs are a dual certificate; a single allowed edge violating the condition does not refute every possible potential, but refutes that particular local proof.
