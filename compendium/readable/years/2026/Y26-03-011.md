Which jet quantities are determined by the recovered coefficients, and how do they transform under rescaling?

## Summary

The source supplies a six-term germ rather than its full defining analytic equation. Its normalized derivatives can nevertheless be computed exactly from the displayed decimal data, provided those decimals are treated as input approximations and the coordinate is declared.

## Definitions

Write F(h)=a₁h+a₂h²+…+a₆h⁶+O(h⁷), with a₁≠0. Define κ_n=F^(n)(0)/F′(0)=n!a_n/a₁.

For a rescaled coordinate h=cδ and normalized germ G(δ)=F(cδ)/(ca₁), one has G′(0)=1 and G^(n)(0)=c^(n−1)κ_n.

## Result name

Jet normalization and its limits

## Result statement

The ratios n!a_n/a₁ are invariant under multiplying F by a nonzero constant, but for n≥2 they are not invariant under a change in the independent coordinate. The logarithmic derivative I=F″/F′ satisfies I(0)=2a₂/a₁.

## Proof

Differentiate the power series term by term at zero: F^(n)(0)=n!a_n. A nonzero scalar multiple cancels in every ratio. Substituting h=cδ and dividing by ca₁ multiplies the nth coefficient by c^(n−1), proving the transformation rule. For I, divide the series 2a₂+6a₃h+… by a₁+2a₂h+…; the constant is 2a₂/a₁ and the linear term is 6a₃/a₁−4a₂²/a₁². Thus even the first few differential observables can be reproduced without knowing an ODE. Their transformation laws show precisely what must be fixed before comparing numerical curvature values from different charts.

## Worked example: setup

Use a₁=4.0539120913, a₂=−2.8914540799 and a₃=4.3080022014.

## Worked example: calculation

κ₂≈−1.42650063 and κ₃≈6.37606653. The slope I′(0)=κ₃−κ₂²≈4.34116248. Rescaling h=2δ doubles κ₂ and multiplies κ₃ by four; a claimed coordinate-invariant equality between raw values would therefore fail.

## Worked example: conclusion

The recovered local numbers have a clear reproducible meaning once normalization is fixed.

## Limits

Rounded coefficients do not certify the exact analytic germ or a global identity. The missing seventh coefficient cannot be deduced from normalization alone.
