## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which linear equations define the claimed transport space, and how can reduction modulo a prime certify that it is zero over Q?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or explicitly defined worked model

## Summary

The Wilson context passage supplied a transport equation and modular zero-Hom checks. Their mathematical mechanism is concrete: vectorize the unknown map, build a finite linear system and certify its column rank. One suitable prime can already prove a rational zero-kernel statement when all coefficients are integral.

## Definitions

For matrices A_i on Qᵐ and B_i on Qⁿ, an intertwiner T satisfies B_iT=TA_i for every i. Column vectorization gives equations(I_m⊗B_i−A_iᵀ⊗I_n)vec(T)=0.

Stack these equations into an integer matrix C after clearing denominators. A nonzero maximal minor modulo a prime proves that the same integer minor is nonzero.

## Result name

Intertwiner spaces are kernels of explicit integer matrices

## Result statement

If C has full column rank modulo p, its rational kernel is zero and hence the rational intertwiner space vanishes. Conversely a nonzero kernel modulo p can be an exceptional characteristic phenomenon and need not imply a rational intertwiner.

## Proof

Expand each entry of B_iT−TA_i linearly in the entries of T; this proves the vectorization formula. Full modular column rank supplies a square minor with determinant not divisible by p, so its integer determinant is nonzero and C has full column rank over Q. For the converse, a determinant may be a nonzero multiple of p and vanish only after reduction. An involution splitting source and target into plus/minus eigenspaces can reduce the unknowns further: its intertwiner equation forces the cross-parity blocks to zero.

## Worked example: setup

Use A=[[0,−1],[1,−1]], representing an order-three action, and ask for a row T=(x,y) mapping to the trivial action.

## Worked example: calculation

The equation TA=T becomes y=x and−x−y=y, so3x=0. Over Q only the zero map exists. Modulo three the rows(x,x) all solve it, producing an extra one-dimensional modular Hom space. Modulo five the determinant-three system is invertible and certifies zero rational Hom. This is an exact small analogue of the stated modular transport certificates.
