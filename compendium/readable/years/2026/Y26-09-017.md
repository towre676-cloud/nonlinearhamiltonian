## Revised question retained in the annual source

Does a relation absent from a small carrier become visible after several larger-carrier relations are combined?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or explicitly defined worked model

## Summary

The v49/v50 discussion identified a counterexample to P_N=Σ_(R_i≤N)R_i as a universal blindness rule. The construction does define natural quotient maps, but local generation can differ from the pullback of global relations. A two-dimensional example isolates the issue exactly.

## Definitions

Let A be an abelian group with distinguished subgroups R_i. For N≤A put P_N=Σ_(R_i≤N)R_i and Q_N=N/P_N.

Let P_A be the globally generated relation subgroup. Its restriction to N is N∩P_A, which always contains P_N but need not equal it.

## Result name

Local carrier quotients form a functor but can miss globally generated relations

## Result statement

Inclusions N≤M induce a covariant quotient functor Q_N→Q_M. However, Q_N need not equal N/(N∩P_A). For A=F₂² with R₁=span(e₂),R₂=span(e₁+e₂), and N=span(e₁), one has P_N=0 but N∩P_A=N.

## Proof

If a distinguished R_i lies in N it also lies in M, so P_N≤P_M and the inclusion descends to quotient groups; these induced maps compose by ordinary inclusion, proving functoriality. In the example neither R_i is contained in N, so the local sum is zero. But their sum is all of A because e₂+(e₁+e₂)=e₁. Thus the globally generated relation kills the entire small carrier. The natural map Q_N→A/P_A has nonzero kernel even though Q_N itself is nonzero.

## Worked example: calculation

The source Q_N is one-dimensional over F₂, while Q_A=0. Its only nonzero class dies on enlargement because relations become jointly available. As a separate character test, Hom(C₄²/⟨(2,0)⟩,C₂) still has dimension two over F₂: order-two characters already annihilate twice every vector. Thus mod-two characters can also miss a genuine integral relation.

## Worked example: conclusion

The carrier construction has valid natural maps, but its relation semantics and the separating power of its characters must be specified independently.

## Limits

The historical seven-node lattice and the claimed multiplier of L₃(4) were not reconstructed from a full presentation. The finite examples do not certify those group-specific data.
