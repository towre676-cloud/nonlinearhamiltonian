## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which word convention describes physical execution, and which Hankel factorization determines the minimal observed realization?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or explicitly defined worked model

## Summary

The Wilson work distinguished algebra-build cost from column-execution cost and later corrected reversed words. A small noncommutative example proves why both distinctions matter. The generated matrix algebra, reachable columns and observed word series need not have the same dimension.

## Definitions

For matrices A_a, define algebra words A_(a₁⋯a_l)=A_(a₁)⋯A_(a_l). Let f(w)=cᵀA_wb and H_(u,v)=f(uv).

Then H factors into rows cᵀA_u and columns A_vb. Applying instructions a₁ then a₂ to a column instead gives A_(a₂)A_(a₁)b, the reversed algebra word.

## Result name

Hankel rank measures reachable observable state, not the whole generated algebra

## Result statement

Every d-dimensional representation gives rankH≤d. If the reachable columns and observable rows both span dimension d, suitable finite submatrices have rank d and no smaller realization can produce the same series. The generated algebra can have dimension d².

## Proof

The factorization H_(u,v)=(cᵀA_u)(A_vb) gives the rank upper bound. If both spans are full, choose d independent rows and d independent columns; their square product is invertible, proving rank at least d. Any competing dimension-e realization has Hankel rank at most e, so e≥d. This proves minimality from observable behavior rather than from the number of algebra basis matrices. Word reversal follows directly from composing linear maps on columns in execution order.

## Worked example: setup

Take A=E₁₂,B=E₂₁ and b=c=e₁ in dimension two.

## Worked example: calculation

The unital algebra contains A,B,AB=E₁₁ and BA=E₂₂, spanning all four matrix units. Reachable columns b and Bb are e₁,e₂, and observable rows cᵀ and cᵀA are e₁ᵀ,e₂ᵀ. The corresponding2×2 Hankel submatrix using words empty,A on rows and empty,B on columns is identity, so the observed realization dimension is exactly two. Algebra word AB yields output one, while physically executing A then B annihilates b and yields zero.

## Worked example: conclusion

The dimension and ordering claims now have exact realizations and a minimality certificate.

## Limits

This does not reproduce the historical81/82 or92/114 cost gaps; their operation and support tables were not returned. Algebra rank alone cannot determine an execution-call budget.

## Extension

Build the actual finite Hankel submatrices under one declared word convention, certify reachable/observable bases and map every measured call to a column operation in that same convention.
