## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which counting and substitution statements really follow from the Fibonacci matrix?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

The same two-by-two matrix governs a substitution’s letter counts and a finite-state language’s path counts. Its eigenvalues give exact growth in both declared models, while a tight-binding transfer matrix is derived from a separate recurrence rather than identified with them by name.

## Definitions

Let M=[[1,1],[1,0]]. The substitution H↦HC,C↦H sends its letter-count vector to M times that vector.

For binary words forbidding consecutive C symbols, M is also the adjacency matrix on states H,C. A length-L word count is 1ᵀM^{L−1}1 for L≥1.

For a Jacobi recurrence t_{n−1}ψ_{n−1}+ε_nψ_n+t_nψ_{n+1}=Eψ_n with t_n≠0, transfer the pair (ψ_n,ψ_{n−1}).

## Result name

Exact Fibonacci counts and Jacobi transfer determinant

## Result statement

M²=M+I, its eigenvalues are φ=(1+√5)/2 and −φ⁻¹, and the forbidden-word count is F_{L+2}. The Jacobi transfer is T_n=[[(E−ε_n)/t_n,−t_{n−1}/t_n],[1,0]], with det T_n=t_{n−1}/t_n.

## Proof

Matrix multiplication proves M²=M+I, yielding its polynomial and roots. A legal word ending in H can follow either state, while one ending in C must follow H; this gives the M recursion. Counts at L=1,2 are two and three, fixing F_{L+2}. Binet’s formula or diagonalization then gives exponential growth rate logφ per symbol. For the Jacobi recurrence solve explicitly for ψ_{n+1}; the displayed transfer and determinant follow. A product over a periodic cell has determinant one when its endpoint hoppings agree, making a Floquet trace meaningful. These are separate concrete uses of transfer matrices, not a theorem that all their spectra coincide.

Take L=4 and four substitution steps from H.

## Worked example: calculation

The legal binary-word count is F₆=8; direct enumeration of the sixteen words rejects exactly those containing CC. Substitution counts are M⁴(1,0)=(5,3), with total length eight. Their equal totals at this step do not identify the word language with one deterministic substitution orbit. For uniform hopping one and ε_n=0, T=[[E,−1],[1,0]] has determinant one and trace E.

## Limits

A power-law Fourier exponent is not determined merely by this two-state growth calculation. The recovered sixth-order recurrence needs its initial data and parameter meanings before it specifies a unique sequence.

## Extension
