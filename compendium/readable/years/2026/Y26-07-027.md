## Result name

## Result statement

The base-twelve expansion of a/b terminates exactly when b divides12^N for some N, equivalently b has no prime factors except2 and3. Every rational has an eventually periodic expansion; after removing the2- and3-parts of the denominator, the period divides the multiplicative order of12 modulo its remaining part.

## Proof

AfterN steps the division identities telescope to a/b=Σ_{j=1}^N d_j12^{-j}+r_N/(b12^N). Termination is equivalent to r_N=0 and hence to b|12^N because gcd(a,b)=1. Conversely that divisibility forces zero remainder. Only finitely many remainders occur, so a nonzero remainder must eventually repeat and the deterministic update then repeats forever. When the denominator is coprime to12, the remainder is12^j a modb and the first return occurs at the multiplicative order of12. Powers of2 and3 contribute only a finite preperiod. An expansion ending in infinitely manyB digits is the familiar alternate representation of a terminating number; choosing the non-B-tail convention restores uniqueness. The same remainder identity certifies truncation error without a decimal approximation: afterN digits the omitted fraction is exactlyr_N/(b12^N), betweenzero and12^{-N}. For a repeating block oflengthh, multiplying by12^h and subtracting yields a rational denominator12^h−1. These two identities provide exact conversion in both directions.
