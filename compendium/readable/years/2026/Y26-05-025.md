## Status

## Summary

The OGL followup corrects the first Betti number and component-count formulas. Their common incidence-matrix proof is short, constructive and field-independent. It avoids interpreting a pictorial cluster or ring as a homology certificate without checking its edges.

## Definitions

For a finite graph with v vertices and e oriented edges, let B be its v-by-e incidence matrix, with one −1 and one +1 per nonloop column. Work over any field; over characteristic two the signs coincide.

With no filled faces, the chain complex is C₁→ᴮC₀. Let c be the number of connected components and β₁=dimkerB.

## Result name

Incidence rank computes cycle homology and remains separate from a drawing

## Result statement

rankB=v−c, dimH₀=c and β₁=e−v+c=e−rankB. Adding a bridge between distinct components decreases c by one and leaves β₁ unchanged; adding an edge inside a component increases β₁ by one.

## Proof

In each component every incidence column has coordinate sum zero, so the image lies in a subspace of dimension at most the component size minus one. Choose a spanning tree in each component. Its columns are independent: in any linear dependence, a leaf row contains only its incident tree coefficient, forcing that coefficient to vanish; remove the leaf and induct. These v−c independent columns attain the upper bound and prove the rank formula. Rank-nullity gives the kernel and cokernel dimensions. The two edge-update statements then follow immediately from the changes in e and c. Loops have zero boundary and contribute one independent cycle; parallel edges are allowed and the same proof applies. Filling a triangular face would introduce a C₂ boundary and could kill the corresponding H₁ class, so graph homology and a filled simplicial complex must remain distinct.

## Worked example: setup

Take two disjoint triangles and then join them with a single bridge.

## Worked example: calculation

The resulting graph has v=6,e=7,c=1, so rankB=5 and β₁=2. Before joining, v=6,e=6,c=2, again β₁=2. A single ring has β₁=1, while a connected tree has e=v−1 and β₁=0. Adding both triangular faces to the bridged graph makes the two face boundaries independent and reduces H₁ to zero.

## Limits

The incidence theorem does not prove that an inferred observation graph faithfully represents an underlying manifold or that a thresholded edge set is statistically stable.
