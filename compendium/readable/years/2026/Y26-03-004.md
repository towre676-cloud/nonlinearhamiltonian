## Revised question retained in the annual source

How do continuous-time energy and resolvent statements change after discretization?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The source correctly distinguishes AᵀP+PA from the discrete Stein equation. The distinction matters because a discretization may destroy stability even when its underlying continuous generator is stable. Exact scalar calculations show the admissible step range.

## Definitions

Let F be a real matrix with spectral radius less than one and Q>0. Define P=Σ_(k≥0)(Fᵀ)^kQF^k.

For x_(n+1)=Fx_n, the energy change is x_nᵀ(FᵀPF−P)x_n. Frequency response is evaluated at z=e^(iω) on the unit circle.

## Result name

Discrete Lyapunov identity

## Result statement

The convergent series is positive definite and satisfies FᵀPF−P=−Q. Conversely, a positive solution with Q>0 forces every eigenvalue of F to have modulus less than one.

## Proof

Shift the summation index in FᵀPF; all terms cancel except the k=0 term, yielding −Q. Positivity follows from the first summand. For an eigenvector Fv=λv, the equation gives (|λ|²−1)v*Pv=−v*Qv, hence |λ|<1. A forced harmonic trajectory x_n=z^n u with input z^n b satisfies (zI−F)u=b. Thus the harmonic gain is controlled by ||(zI−F)⁻¹||, rather than by the continuous resolvent with an unchanged frequency variable. The proof makes no claim that the numerical time step has a negligible effect on the spectrum.

## Worked example: setup

Apply explicit Euler with step h to x′=−2x, giving F=1−2h and Q=1.

## Worked example: calculation

Stability is |1−2h|<1, exactly 0<h<1. At h=1/4, F=1/2 and P=Σ4^(−k)=4/3, so F²P−P=−1. At h=3/2, F=−2 and trajectories diverge although the original ODE decays.

## Worked example: conclusion

The energy certificate must be recomputed or preserved by the chosen discretization.

## Limits

Finite-dimensional spectral radius suffices for the series, whereas an infinite-dimensional bounded operator can require stronger power bounds. Sampling aliasing also changes frequency interpretation.

## Extension

Compare implicit Euler, exact sampling and a structure-preserving scheme through a uniform discrete coercivity estimate on a declared step interval.
