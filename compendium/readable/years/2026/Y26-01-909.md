## Result statement

## Proof

The local part admits the geometric expansion with its integral remainder; the remaining contour has real part bounded away from zero and contributes an exponentially small term asz↓0. The formal ODE follows by differentiating the series: z²F′=F−z. For the Laplace integrals it follows by differentiation and integration by parts, with endpoints controlled by exponential decay and the matched contour bypass. Their difference is a closed clockwise contour around the pole. The residue of e^(−t/z)/(1−t) is−e^(−1/z), so clockwise residue evaluation gives2πie^(−1/z).
