## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which representation acts on states and targets, and what does its derivative prove at a symmetric state?

## Status

## Summary

The hidden-route program introduced equivariant derivative bundles and stabilizers. Their concrete local content follows by differentiating an equivariance equation. A symmetric observation can still lose both discrete labels and continuous directions, so equivariance is a structure condition rather than an observability certificate.

## Definitions

Let a finite group act linearly by P_g on Rⁿ and Q_g on Rᵐ. A differentiable map F is equivariant if F(P_gx)=Q_gF(x).

The stabilizer of x consists of g with P_gx=x. An invariant observation uses Q_g=I.

## Result name

An equivariant derivative intertwines stabilizers but does not remove hidden coordinates

## Result statement

DF(P_gx)P_g=Q_gDF(x). At a fixed state the derivative is an intertwiner for its stabilizer. For the S₃-invariant observation h(x)=(Σx_i,Σx_i²), the Jacobian has rank two at(1,2,3), with hidden direction(1,−2,1), and rank one when all coordinates are equal.

## Proof

Differentiate the equivariance identity in an arbitrary direction v and use the chain rule to obtain the matrix equation. If P_gx=x, both derivatives are evaluated at the same state, proving the stabilizer statement. For h, the rows of Dh are(1,1,1) and2(x₁,x₂,x₃). They are independent unless all coordinates coincide. At(1,2,3), both rows annihilate(1,−2,1), which spans the one-dimensional kernel. Permuting coordinates leaves both components of h unchanged, but can change a labeled target such as x₁. Therefore that target cannot be a globally defined function of h.

The derivative-bundle idea yields exact intertwining and rank statements while preserving the distinction between symmetry and sufficient observation.

## Limits

Finite-group orbits have no automatic nonzero tangent directions. Derivative blindness is a local statement and does not prove constancy of a nonlinear target on an entire finite-amplitude fiber.

## Extension

Stratify the state space by stabilizer type, compute the observation rank on each stratum and test whether the chosen target descends to its orbit and observation quotients.
