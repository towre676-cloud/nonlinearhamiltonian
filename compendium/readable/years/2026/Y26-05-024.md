## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: Which observer statistic is a mathematical identity, and why can a logarithm of mutual information fail metric axioms?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered OGL formulas include mutual and conditional mutual information, a modular Hamiltonian and a proposed logarithmic distance. We separate exact finite facts from geometric interpretation. In particular, two-site support cannot establish emergent locality in a system containing only two sites.

## Definitions

Use entropy in bits, S(ρ)=−trρlog₂ρ, and I(A:B)=S_A+S_B−S_AB. For a strictly positive two-qubit density matrix, K=−logρ has an expansion in the sixteen Pauli strings.

Every nonidentity Pauli string on two qubits has support one or two. Define OCI as the squared norm of those components divided by the squared norm of all nonidentity components, when that denominator is nonzero.

## Result name

Two-qubit Pauli locality is tautological, while mutual information need not define a metric

## Result statement

For every such nonmaximally mixed two-qubit state OCI=1, independently of entanglement or geometry. The proposed D(A,B)=−log[I(A:B)/(√(S_AS_B)+ε)] is not generally a metric: it can be negative and can be infinite for distinct independent systems.

## Proof

The Pauli expansion is an orthogonal basis decomposition for the Hilbert–Schmidt inner product. Since no string on two sites has support greater than two, the numerator and denominator select the same coefficients, proving the ratio. At the maximally mixed state K is scalar, so the ratio is undefined as 0/0 unless an additional convention is imposed. For a Bell state, reduced entropies are one and joint entropy zero, so I=2. With ε=0 the logarithmic formula gives −log2<0; any 0<ε<1 still gives a negative value. Conversely an independent nontrivial product state has I=0, giving an infinite logarithm. Strict positivity of ρ is also necessary for a bounded finite-dimensional modular Hamiltonian: rank-deficient Bell states have an infinite logarithm outside their support. The mutual-information counterexample remains valid because entropy itself extends continuously to zero eigenvalues.

## Worked example: setup

Use the strictly positive classical state ρ=diag(3/8,1/8,1/8,3/8).

## Worked example: calculation

Its marginals are uniform. It is (I+(1/2)Z⊗Z)/4, so its modular Hamiltonian has only identity and ZZ components, with ZZ coefficient −(log3)/2. Thus OCI=1 even though the state is a separable classical mixture. Its mutual information is 1−h₂(1/4)≈0.188722 bits.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite identities explain exactly what the observer statistic can and cannot certify.

## Limits

Nonnegativity and strong subadditivity are classical entropy theorems, but no general metric or geometry follows from them through this logarithmic formula. The quoted Haar floors remain empirical source claims.

## Extension

Design a distance on a specified family using a proven metric such as an appropriate state-space divergence, and require a reconstruction theorem before interpreting its fitted coordinates as physical geometry.
