## Summary

The source asserts dense SU(5) monodromy and supplies braid, central-phase and determinant relations. The absent residues and matrices prevent an independent closure proof. Nevertheless the determinant twist, triangle-group quotient and structure of the proposed full compact group are exact conditional consequences that can be proved completely.

## Definitions

Let ζ=exp(2πi/17). Assume unitary5×5 matrices R₁,R₂ satisfy the braid relation, det R_i=ζ⁹ and (R₁R₂)³=ζ⁴I.

Set T_i=ζ⁵R_i and G={g∈U(5):det(g)¹⁷=1}. The hypothesis that a generated subgroup is dense is separate from these finite relations.

## Result name

Exact twist and split determinant group

## Result statement

T_i∈SU(5), they satisfy the braid relation, and (T₁T₂)³=I. If additionally T_i³⁴=I, they define a quotient of Δ(2,3,34). Moreover G≅SU(5)×μ₁₇ as compact groups, via (S,ξ)↦ξS.

## Proof

Twisting a5×5 matrix by ζ⁵ multiplies its determinant by ζ²⁵; thus det T_i=ζ³⁴=1. Both sides of the braid relation gain the same scalar ζ¹⁵. The central relation gains ζ³⁰, cancelling ζ⁴ because ζ³⁴=1. Put a=T₁T₂T₁ and b=T₁T₂. The braid relation gives a²=b³=I, and b⁻¹a=T₁, so the extra relation T₁³⁴=I gives the triangle presentation with orders dividing2,3,34. For the group decomposition, ξ∈μ₁₇ has determinant ξ⁵. Since5 is invertible modulo17, this scalar determinant map is an automorphism; its inverse exponent is7. Given g∈G, set ξ=det(g)⁷ and S=ξ⁻¹g. Then det S=det(g)^(1−35)=1. This is a continuous inverse to (S,ξ)↦ξS, and the intersection SU(5)∩μ₁₇I is trivial, proving the product isomorphism.

## Limits

Finite order, irreducibility or a finite list of nonzero projector blocks does not alone prove density in SU(5). The actual matrices, unitarizing form and exclusion of finite and proper closed subgroups are not recovered; no claimed holonomy residual or Lean certificate is replayed.

## Extension
