## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which future functional is evaluated after the observation, and how does the dynamics transport posterior uncertainty to it?

## Status

## Summary

The reported direct-versus-driver variance reversal has a rigorous mechanism. A linear SDE has an exact finite-time transition, and a measurement should be assessed against the target at its declared horizon. A sensor that measures today’s target precisely can leave its future driver uncertain.

## Definitions

For dX=AXdt+LdW with constant matrices, define F=e^(AΔ) and Q=∫₀^Δe^(As)LLᵀe^(Aᵀs)ds.

A Gaussian prior covariance P updated by scalar y=hᵀX+noise of variance r>0 becomes P⁺=P−PhhᵀP/(hᵀPh+r). A future target gᵀX_Δ has variance gᵀ(FP⁺Fᵀ+Q)g.

## Result name

Exact SDE propagation can make a driver measurement more informative than a direct present-target measurement

## Result statement

The exact transition is X_Δ=FX₀+η, with centered independent Gaussian η of covariance Q. In the new constant-driver model A=[[0,1],[0,0]], P=I, L=0, Δ=10, and r=1/100, observing the present first coordinate leaves future first-coordinate variance100+1/101; observing the driver leaves only201/101.

## Proof

Variation of constants gives η=∫₀^Δe^(A(Δ−s))LdW_s. Itô isometry gives the stated covariance after changing variables. For the nilpotent A, F=[[1,Δ],[0,1]]. The two measurement updates are diag(1/101,1) and diag(1,1/101). The terminal first-coordinate row is(1,10), producing the displayed quadratic forms. Thus present precision and future-target precision are mathematically different optimization objectives.

## Worked example: calculation

With L=(0,1)ᵀ, the integrand is[[s²,s],[s,1]], so Q=[[Δ³/3,Δ²/2],[Δ²/2,Δ]]. At Δ=2 this is[[8/3,2],[2,2]], positive definite with determinant4/3. This independent process term must be added after the measurement update; it cannot be removed by a more accurate present sensor. Both sensor choices are compared at the same terminal horizon and against the same prior, which makes the contrast meaningful.
