## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does the depth law c_{d+1}=c_d² prove, and what extra hypothesis is needed to certify an execution history?

## Status

Developed mathematical treatment with explicit hypotheses, derivation and checked example

## Summary

The double-exponential recurrence is solved exactly and linearized by a double logarithm. An elementary bad-event union bound explains a conditional composition claim, while showing that public endpoint consistency alone does not certify how an endpoint was computed.

## Definitions

Fix c₀>1 and integer d≥0, with c_{d+1}=c_d². Let a claimed trajectory contain both its endpoints and a declared local policy.

A verifier is algebraically sound with error α if an accepted false algebraic statement occurs with probability at most α under its defined experiment.

A second verifier is sound with error β for its own execution statement; a composed verifier accepts only when both accept.

## Result name

Closed form and conditional composition bound

## Result statement

c_d=c₀^{2^d} and log log c_d=d log2+log log c₀. If every invalid composed claim implies failure of at least one of the two stated soundness properties, then its acceptance probability is at most α+β.

## Proof

Induction proves c_d=c₀^{2^d}; taking two logarithms gives the affine coordinate. For the probability statement let E₁,E₂ be the two bad-acceptance events. The hypothesis says invalid acceptance is contained in E₁∪E₂, so the union bound gives P≤P(E₁)+P(E₂)≤α+β, without assuming independence. The implication must actually be established for the composed statement; using a security parameter label does not establish it. The placeholder identity (c g)/(g/c)=c² is valid for nonzero g and c, but cancellation removes all dependence on g, including whether g is prime.

## Worked example: setup

Set c₀=313 and d=0,1,2.

## Worked example: calculation

The first values are 313,97969,9597924961. The double-log increments are exactly log2. A program can compute the endpoint by exponentiation without reproducing a prescribed intermediate history. Also pitch octave equivalence is multiplication f↦2f; squaring f↦f² is not that same transformation unless an additional coordinate convention is chosen.

## Worked example: conclusion

Endpoint arithmetic, depth coordinates, music analogies and execution claims are separately defined and checkable.

## Limits

Public deterministic SDS is not a secrecy or hardness assumption. A Lipschitz policy or total-variation rule must be verified on supplied intermediate states; endpoint mean consistency alone does not force a unique path.

## Extension

Define an explicit trajectory language, prove which local constraints are committed and checked, and reduce invalid accepted histories to a precisely stated proof-system soundness event. Keep sequentiality assumptions distinct from the elementary recurrence theorem.
