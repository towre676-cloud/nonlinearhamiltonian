## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which integral presentation defines the module, and can its arithmetic support be computed without relying on a few local samples?

## Status

## Summary

The requested next step was an integral module, a Fitting ideal and contraction to Z. A complete small order shows how these fit together. It also explains why local Smith data at selected primes do not by themselves certify global support.

## Definitions

Let R=Z[t]/(t²−2) and M=R/(t−2). Its one-generator presentation is R→^(t−2)R→M→0.

The zeroth Fitting ideal of a finitely presented module is generated by the maximal presentation minors; here it is(t−2). Its contraction consists of the integers lying in that ideal.

## Result name

A Fitting ideal and its contraction certify global prime support in a declared quotient

## Result statement

M≅Z/2 as an abelian group, Fitt₀(M)=(t−2) in R, and(t−2)∩Z=(2). Thus this particular module has exactly the prime support{2}.

## Proof

Quotienting R by t−2 substitutes t=2 into t²−2, leaving the integer relation two=0. Evaluation therefore induces an isomorphism R/(t−2)≅Z/2. Its restriction to Z has kernel(2), proving the contraction. The Fitting claim follows from the one-by-one presentation minor. Independently, multiplication by t−2 on the integral basis1,t has matrix[[-2,2],[1,−2]], determinant two. Its entry gcd is one, so its Smith form is diag(1,2), giving the same additive quotient. These independent presentations link the order calculation, Fitting ideal and integer support.

## Worked example: setup

Generalize the same principal quotient to a monic P(t)∈Z[t].

## Worked example: calculation

For R=Z[t]/P and M=R/(t−a), evaluation gives M≅Z/(P(a)); hence its contracted ideal is(P(a)). If P(a)≠0, its prime divisors are the complete support, not just sampled candidates. Monicity ensures R is finite free in the standard power basis, unlike an arbitrary nonmonic polynomial presentation.

## Worked example: conclusion

The intended global calculation becomes a concrete elimination theorem with a short exact certificate.

## Limits

This is a newly declared order, not the historical quintic module. Its actual presentation and integral basis were not returned, so no global{37,809} theorem follows from this example.

## Extension

Obtain the real integral order and presentation matrix, compute all relevant minors, and prove the contracted ideal by two containment certificates or an exact elimination basis.
