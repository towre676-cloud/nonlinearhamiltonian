## Definitions

For an integer matrixA, its relation lattice isL=ker_Z A. A sublatticeL′⊂L is saturated whenkv∈L′ withv∈L andk≠0 forcesv∈L′. A finite graph withV vertices,E edges andc connected components hascycle rankβ₁=E−V+c.

## Result statement

L=ker_Z A is saturated. A rational spanning sublattice need not equalL. ForL=Z(1,−1), the sublattice2L hasindex two and its binomialx²−y² defines an extra characteristic-zero component compared withx−y. For a graph,β₁ follows from the exact incidence rankV−c.

## Proof

Ifkv∈ker_Z A, thenkAv=0 in the torsion-free groupZ^m, henceAv=0; this proves saturation of the full kernel. But2L andL have the sameQ-span, so a rank computation misses their index. In characteristic zero,x²−y²=(x−y)(x+y), and the second factor gives the unwanted componentx=−y; Smith normal form detects the missing factor of two. For graphs, the oriented incidence matrix has one linear row relation per connected component and otherwise rankV−c, as proved by a spanning forest or propagation of constant vertex potentials. Since there are no two-cell boundaries, the edge-cycle dimension isE−(V−c). Adding two-cells would change first homology and requires their own boundary matrix.
