## Proof

Comparing coefficients gives (4−a²)S″−aS′=4, with S(0)=S′(0)=0: the constant term is8c₁=4, and all later coefficients cancel by the ratio. Set u=arcsin(a/2) and v=√(4−a²). For F=2u², u′=1/v gives F′=4u/v and F″=4/v²+4au/v³. Hence (4−a²)F″−aF′=4 with the same initial data. Uniqueness of the analytic power-series solution proves S=F. For the specified positive a, π/840 lies in the principal arcsine interval, so 2(π/840)² gives the asserted denominator.
