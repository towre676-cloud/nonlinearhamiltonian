## Definitions

Let A=Q^r have idempotent basis e_i, with e_ie_j=δ_ije_i and unit1=Σe_i. Choose nonzero rational weights w_i and counit ε(e_i)=w_i.

The pairing is⟨a,b⟩=ε(ab). Its matrix is diag(w_i), hence it is nondegenerate. Define Δ(e_i)=w_i^(−1)e_i⊗e_i and handle operator H=μΔ.

## Result name

Two-dimensional sewing has a concrete Frobenius-algebra model

## Result statement

These maps satisfy associativity, coassociativity, the counit laws and the Frobenius compatibility Δ(ab)=(a⊗1)Δ(b)=(1⊗b)Δ(a). The associated connected closed genus-g scalar is Z_g=ε(H^g1)=Σ_iw_i^(1−g).

## Proof

All identities can be checked on the idempotent basis. Multiplication is diagonal, and each coproduct has support only on e_i⊗e_i, so the two iterated coproducts both send e_i to w_i^(−2)e_i⊗e_i⊗e_i. Applying ε to either output factor of Δ returns e_i. The Frobenius identity vanishes for i≠j and equals w_i^(−1)e_i⊗e_i for i=j. Finally H(e_i)=w_i^(−1)e_i, so applying g handles to the unit and then ε gives the stated sum. These are the algebraic sewing identities behind this two-dimensional example. The general equivalence between oriented two-dimensional TQFTs and commutative Frobenius algebras is a separate structural theorem; the explicit calculations here instantiate its algebraic side rather than prove the classification.

## Limits

This is a two-dimensional commutative Frobenius model. It is not a Chern–Simons theory, a modular tensor category or a claim about quantum gravity.
