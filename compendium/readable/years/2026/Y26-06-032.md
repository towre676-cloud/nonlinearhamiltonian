## Result statement

## Proof

Modulo two, f becomes g=y⁵+y³+1. It has no root in F₂. The only irreducible quadratic is y²+y+1; reducing g gives y+1. The only irreducible cubics are y³+y+1 and y³+y²+1; their remainders are respectively y²+1 and y²+y+1. Thus g has no irreducible factor of degree one, two or three. Any proper factorization of a degree-five polynomial has a factor of degree at most two, so these checks already suffice; the cubic checks provide an independent surplus check. Therefore g is irreducible, and Gauss’s lemma gives irreducibility over Q. In K reduce α⁵=2α⁴−3α³−2α²+2α+1. Repeated multiplication produces the exact matrix of h. Rational elimination gives determinant 39681. Multiplyingh by  the stated inverse numerator and reducing by the same relation gives the constant 39681 with all four higher coefficients zero, independently certifying the inverse. Since h has nonzero representative of degree below five, it is nonzero in the field even before computing its norm.
