## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can the reported raw-state uniqueness be proved for every state with a small, independently checkable exact certificate?

## Summary

The explicit recurrence allows a global theorem stronger than a collection of decoding tests. Equality of output words becomes a short-vector problem in an integer lattice. Exact dual bounds exclude every nonzero collision at once. The supplied certificate covers all 126  choices of five positions within nine steps; no enumeration of2^107 states and no trust in an LLL implementation is required.

## Definitions

Let M=2^107−1, A=3^121 moduloM, and b=2^26. States are integers0≤x_i<M with x_{i+1}=Ax_i moduloM; the raw observation is y_i=x_i modulo b. Since M≡1 modulo3, A is invertible moduloM.

For positions0≤p_0<⋯<p_4≤8 put g_j=p_j−p_0 and Λ_p={d∈Z^5:d_j≡A^{g_j}d_0 moduloM forj=1,…,4}. Let D=⌊(M−1)/b⌋=2^81−1.

## Result name

Exact raw-state observability

## Result statement

For every five-position subset of{0,…,8}, the selected raw words uniquely determine the initial state. Four raw words cannot determine every state. Every supplied integer basis B_p has rows inΛ_p, |det B_p|=M^4, and DΣ_i|(B_p^(−1))_{ij}|<3/8 for each columnj.

## Proof

The homomorphism Z^5→(Z/MZ)^4 sending d to(d_j−A^{g_j}d_0)_{j=1}^4 is onto: vary the final four coordinates while keeping d_0=0. Its kernelΛ_p therefore has indexM^4. The checker verifies lattice membership of every basis row and determinant magnitudeM^4, proving that its row lattice equalsΛ_p rather than merely a sublattice. It computes the inverse over rational numbers, verifies B_pB_p^(−1)=I, and checks the strict column bounds for all 126 position subsets. These properties alone suffice; the LLL routine used to find the bases is not trusted.

Suppose two states have the same five observed words. At each selected step their state difference is b d_j for an integerd_j with |d_j|≤D. The recurrence implies d_j≡A^{g_j}d_0 moduloM because b is invertible moduloM. Hence d=cB_p for some integer rowc. Each coordinate satisfies |c_j|=|(dB_p^(−1))_j|≤DΣ_i|(B_p^(−1))_{ij}|<3/8<1. Thusc=0, all selected state differences vanish, and invertibility of A takes equality atp_0 back to equality of the initial states. Four words range over at mostb^4=2^104 possibilities, whereas even the nonzero state set has M−1>2^104 elements. Pigeonhole therefore proves four-word impossibility.

The same dual bounds yield a decoder. Write selected states as y_j+bz_j with0≤z_j≤D. Their congruences define an affine lattice v+Λ_p, using v_0=0 and v_j=b^(−1)(A^{g_j}y_0−y_j) moduloM. For z=v+cB_p, each integerc_j lies in the exact interval obtained by applying the corresponding inverse column to the box0≤z≤D. Its width is below3/8, so it contains at most one integer. Round the interval endpoints exactly, reconstruct z and check all state bounds and observations; then reverse A^{p_0}. This is an actual exact decoder, not only an existence result.

## Limits

Raw observations are consecutive generator steps at known positions, with the source recurrence and output convention stated above. Rejected/accepted-output sequences have hidden step counts and require a different theorem. This certificate makes no claim about their six-output threshold.
