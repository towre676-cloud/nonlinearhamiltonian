## Summary

## Result name

Exact coherence bound and degeneracy

## Result statement

0≤C≤1 byCauchy–Schwarz, with equality1 exactly when the weighted nonzero coefficient vectors are proportional. For a single coefficient the statistic is always1.

## Proof

ApplyCauchy–Schwarz to the vectors(√w_jx_j) and(√w_jy_j). Their squared inner-product magnitude is at most the product of their squared norms, proving the bound. Equality inCauchy–Schwarz requires linear dependence, giving the stated criterion after ignoring zero weights. With one nonzero weighted slot, the numerator isw²|x|²|y|² and the denominator is identical. Therefore the unsmoothed pointwise ratio cannot distinguish two nonzero signals. The meaningful information comes fromphase and amplitude consistency across the averaging neighborhood, not merely from writing a normalized product.
