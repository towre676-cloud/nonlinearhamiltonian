## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: Which degree does the Betti cycle map use, and what elementary motivic group already disproves the stated all-bidegree comparison?

## Status

Developed mathematical repair or classical result; speculative source claims delimited

## Summary

The source asserts H_mot^{p,q}(X)⊗C≅H_sing^{2q−p}(Xan,C) for every smooth proper complex X. This is false. The higher-Chow index2q−p is not the singular cohomological degree, and realization can discard nontrivial arithmetic or cycle data.

## Definitions

For smooth complex X, integral motivic cohomology has a Betti cycle map to H_sing^p(Xan,Z(q)); the target degree is p. The map is not generally an isomorphism.

Use the classical low-degree identity H^{1,1}(Spec k,Z)=k×. The complex point Spec C is smooth and proper.

## Result name

A counterexample at a point

## Result statement

At X=Spec C and(p,q)=(1,1), the source comparison has nonzero left side C×⊗_ZC but zero right side H_sing¹(point,C). Consequently it cannot be a universal isomorphism, even under its own smooth-proper hypothesis.

## Proof

A point is contractible, so its positive-degree singular cohomology vanishes. The motivic weight-one complex yields the stated units group. To see the tensor on the left is nonzero, log|·|:C×→R is a surjective homomorphism of abelian groups; tensoring gives a surjection onto R⊗_ZC, which is nonzero. For example, the multiplication map R⊗_ZC→C sends1⊗1 to1. Thus the source cannot repair its claim merely by tensoring with C. The valid higher-Chow formula H^{p,q}=CH^q(X,2q−p) uses2q−p as a chain index, which is a different role from Betti degree.

## Worked example: setup

Compare the weight-one group at a point with the Chow class on P¹.

## Worked example: calculation

H^{1,1}(point)=C× maps to zero singular degree one. In contrast H^{2,1}(P¹)=CH¹(P¹)=Z maps to the degree-two generator of H²(P¹(C),Z(1)). The source’s formula would send that class to degree zero instead, disguising the wrong grading because both target groups happen to have rank one in this particular example.

## Worked example: conclusion

Passing a small rank test on P¹ is therefore not sufficient to validate a universal comparison implementation.

## Limits

The low-degree motivic identities are classical inputs, not proved by a JavaScript test. This counterexample refutes the stated universal isomorphism; it does not refute valid finite-coefficient comparison theorems in their stable ranges.

## Extension

For each realization, encode its source category, coefficient change, grading shifts and theorem range as part of the type. Include tests with nontrivial units and Picard-zero classes, not only cellular ranks.
