## Revised question retained in the annual source

What link homology can be checked when a recovered Betti table omits torsion?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered Betti numbers resemble a rational homology sphere but do not determine integral homology. For the explicit linksΣ(2,a,b) witha odd,b even andgcd(a,b)=1, an exact determinant calculation detects nontrivial torsion. This is a useful refinement of the conditional Brieskorn interpretation.

## Definitions

LetΣ(2,a,b) be the link ofz²+x^a+y^b=0, oriented as a complex link. It is the double branched cover ofS³ over the(a,b) torus knot.

A classical branched-cover theorem states that the order ofH1 is|Δ_(a,b)(−1)| when nonzero, whereΔ is the knot Alexander polynomial normalized up to units.

## Result name

Exact torsion order for the recovered examples

## Result statement

For odda and evenb withgcd(a,b)=1, |H1(Σ(2,a,b);Z)|=a. ThusΣ(2,5,12) hasH1≅Z/5 andΣ(2,41,840) hasH1≅Z/41, while their first and second rational Betti numbers vanish.

## Proof

The torus-knot polynomial isΔ_(a,b)(t)=[(t^(ab)−1)(t−1)]/[(t^a−1)(t^b−1)]. At t=−1, both theab andb factors vanish, but their ratio tendsa(−1)^(ab−b)=a becauseb is even. The remaining ratio(t−1)/(t^a−1) tends1 becausea is odd. Hence the determinant is a, which is finite and nonzero. The branched-cover theorem gives a finite first homology of that order. In the two examples a is prime, so the only abelian group of that order isZ/a. RationalH1 therefore vanishes; Poincaré duality on the closed oriented link gives rationalH2=0 andb0=b3=1.

## Worked example: calculation

gcd(5,12)=1 andgcd(41,840)=1. The corresponding orders are5 and41, not1. Thus the recovered Betti pattern can hold rationally while the statement “integral homology sphere” would be false for these links.

## Worked example: conclusion

Torsion supplies mathematical information that a real-valued or rational Betti table discards.

## Limits

The result uses the stated link interpretation and classical branched-cover/Alexander theorems. It does not classify the full fundamental group or validate an undefinedM model.

## Extension

Construct an explicit Seifert presentation or surgery matrix and compute its Smith normal form as a second independent certificate of the integral group.
