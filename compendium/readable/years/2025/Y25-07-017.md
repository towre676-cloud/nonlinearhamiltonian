## Revised question retained in the annual source

What concrete identity illustrates period multiplication before invoking a motivic Galois symmetry?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The historical synthesis mentioned multiple zeta values and motivic symmetry. A convergent double-sum identity is a rigorous local piece of that programme. It demonstrates an algebraic relation while keeping the motivic lift and its symmetry group as additional constructions.

## Definitions

For a,b>1 define ζ(a)=Σ_{m≥1}m^(−a) and ζ(a,b)=Σ_{m>n≥1}m^(−a)n^(−b). Finite truncations use the same upper bound N on both indices. These positive series converge absolutely.

## Result name

A stuffle identity

## Result statement

ζ(2)ζ(3)=ζ(2,3)+ζ(3,2)+ζ(5). The identical decomposition holds for finite harmonic sums when all summation ranges are truncated consistently.

## Proof

Expand the product as a sum over all ordered pairs(m,n). Partition the index set into m>n,n>m and m=n. On the first part the summand is m^(−2)n^(−3), giving ζ(2,3). Interchanging names on the second yields ζ(3,2). The diagonal gives Σm^(−5). Absolute convergence justifies partitioning and reindexing; alternatively perform the identity for finite N and use monotone convergence. This proof is an exact relation among analytic numbers. Calling them motivic periods requires a specified category and comparison map; the summation identity by itself does not construct the motivic Galois group or prove algebraic independence.

## Worked example: setup

Truncate at N=2.

## Worked example: calculation

H₂^(2)=5/4 and H₂^(3)=9/8, whose product is45/32. The m>n contributions are1/4 and1/8, while H₂^(5)=33/32. Their sum is(8+4+33)/32=45/32 exactly.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example provides a replayable finite certificate and a convergent limiting proof, with no numerical-recognition step.

## Limits

One product identity does not classify all multiple-zeta relations, determine transcendence degrees or establish a geometric Langlands correspondence.

## Extension

Define the relevant iterated integrals, derive their shuffle relations as well, and compare the two algebra structures before proposing a motivic interpretation.
