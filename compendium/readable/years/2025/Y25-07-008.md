## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which merge law makes duplicate and reordered messages harmless, and what remains undecided for concurrent events?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The platform discussion named operation DAGs and vector clocks. Their mathematical core is a join semilattice. A finite model proves the merge law while showing why it neither orders all events nor establishes arbitrary payload consistency.

## Definitions

Order N^d componentwise and set(x∨y)_i=max(x_i,y_i). Under a vector-clock discipline, a replica merges observed clocks and increments its own coordinate for a new local event. Incomparable clocks denote concurrency in that declared model.

## Result name

Componentwise maximum is a least upper bound

## Result statement

The merge ∨ is associative, commutative and idempotent, and is the least upper bound in the componentwise order. Every finite multiset of received clocks therefore has an order-independent merged value, unchanged by repetition.

## Proof

Each identity reduces coordinatewise to the corresponding elementary maximum identity. If z bounds x and y, every z_i≥max(x_i,y_i), proving leastness. Repeated associativity and commutativity remove message ordering; idempotence removes duplicates. A total order selected later can linearize concurrent events, but adds a tie-breaking convention absent from the partial order. Further, these clock laws do not prove that every payload operation commutes: the payload must have a compatible monotone merge or separately justified operation semantics. A causal DAG and its topological order are consequently bookkeeping structures, not automatic certificates of an arithmetic computation.

Take a=(2,0,1),b=(1,3,1),c=(0,2,4).

## Worked example: calculation

a and b are incomparable because each exceeds the other in one coordinate. Both(a∨b)∨c and a∨(b∨c) equal(2,3,4); replaying b changes nothing. By contrast, writing a register to7 then9 can differ from reversing those writes unless a conflict rule is specified.

## Worked example: conclusion

The exact merge theorem identifies both the invariant state and the source of unresolved concurrency.

## Limits

Vector clocks alone do not establish consensus, serializability, security or availability. Clock discipline and payload semantics are essential assumptions.
