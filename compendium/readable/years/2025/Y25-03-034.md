## Result statement

HH0(A)=A has dimension2. EveryHH_n(A) forn≥1 has dimension1. After tensoring the resolution withA overA^e, its differentials alternate0 and multiplication by2ε.

## Proof

InA^e the productuv=x²−y² iszero. On the basis1,x,y,xy, multiplication byu has image span(u,xy) and kernel span(v,xy); multiplication byv has the reverse image and kernel. Both have rank2. The augmentation kernel isspan(u,xy), so the complex is exact before tensoring and is a free resolution. Tensoring setsx=y=ε; u becomes0 andv becomes2ε. HenceHH0=A. In odd positive degree the quotient isA/(2εA), one-dimensional. In even positive degree the kernel of2ε multiplication isspanε, again one-dimensional. The circle/factorization identification is a classical structural input; the algebraic chain computation itself is explicit.
