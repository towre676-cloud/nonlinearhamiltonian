## Result statement

## Proof

Necessity follows by telescoping potential differences around a cycle. For sufficiency choose a root potential zero and integrate labels along a spanning tree. Two paths give the same value precisely when their difference cycle has zero sum. Tree edges are independent and all remaining incidence rows lie in their span, proving the rank. In the source list every mod5 label is zero except c/s=17≡2. Yet c/τ=55,τ/μ=0 and s/μ=55 force x_c=x_τ=x_μ=x_s, contradicting x_c−x_s=2. If that one edge is removed, the zero potential satisfies all remaining ten equations. Thus the source’s claimed maximum-eight family cannot be reconstructed from edge phases alone; its original matrix contains missing structure or different semantics.
