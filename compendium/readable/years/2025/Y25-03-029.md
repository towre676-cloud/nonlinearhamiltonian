## Definitions

LetB_j be independent fair bits andX=Σ_(j≥1)2B_j/3^j. LetX_n be the firstn terms andμ_n its distribution.

## Result name

Quantitative weak convergence

## Result statement

For everyL-Lipschitz real functionf, |∫fdμ−∫fdμ_n|≤L3^(−n). More generally a continuousf on[0,1] has error at most its modulus of continuity at3^(−n).

## Proof

ConstructX andX_n on the same probability space. Their omitted tail is nonnegative and at mostΣ_(j>n)2/3^j=3^(−n). Therefore|f(X)−f(X_n)|≤L3^(−n), or≤ω_f(3^(−n)) for a continuousf. Taking expectations proves the estimates and weak convergence because the modulus tends0 by uniform continuity. The coupling also supplies aWasserstein-1 bound without requiring density functions; the limiting measure is supported on theCantor set. The theorem has actual sample variables, weights and a topology of convergence.
