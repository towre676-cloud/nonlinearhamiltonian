## Revised question retained in the annual source

What finite identity is tested by an associativity pentagon, and what can a tolerance of0.07 actually imply?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

For ordinary vector-space tensor products the five parenthesizations are canonically identified and the pentagon commutes exactly. Approximate unitary identifications accumulate error according to a stated norm, not an undefined coherence percentage.

## Definitions

The five parenthesizations of four ordered factors are(((AB)C)D),((A(BC))D),A((BC)D),A(B(CD)),(AB)(CD).

Each associator sends a pure tensor to the same ordered four-tuple of entries, changing only brackets.

For intended unitary mapsU_j and implemented unitaryV_j assume||V_j−U_j||≤ε_j in operator norm.

## Result name

Exact pentagon and quantitative perturbation

## Result statement

The two directed associator paths from(((AB)C)D) toA(B(CD)) are equal. If their edge counts are three and two, their approximate products differ by at most the sum of the five edge errors.

## Proof

Pure tensors span the tensor product. Along either path the elementary associators retain the same ordered entriesa,b,c,d and end at a⊗(b⊗(c⊗d)). The two linear maps are therefore equal. For approximate maps, compare each path product with its exact product using a telescoping sum. Unitary left and right factors have norm one, so one path error is at most the sum of its three edge errors and the other at most the sum of its two. Their exact middle products agree, and the triangle inequality gives the five-error bound. This proves a precise residual statement while avoiding any claim that arbitrary fusion-category matrices satisfy the pentagon.

## Limits

Nontrivial fusion categories require their actual F-symbols, admissible labels, basis conventions and pentagon equations. Checking only that one F-matrix is unitary or involutive is insufficient. No hardware success rate follows from these algebraic identities.

## Extension

Given exact fusion rules, enumerate every admissible labeled pentagon and certify the maximum residual with interval arithmetic. Separate truncation error, arithmetic error and experimental error before selecting a tolerance.
