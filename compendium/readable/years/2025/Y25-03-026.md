## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which elementary energy inequality must any claimed fixed-exhaust-speed scaling satisfy?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered source combines a fixed effective speed with power and force projections. Restricting the discussion to its mathematics, kinetic-energy accounting gives a simple necessary inequality. It can reject an inconsistent set of numbers without constructing an engineering model.

## Definitions

For a mass flowṁ≥0 leaving at speedv>0, momentum flux isF=ṁv and kinetic power isP_kin=(1/2)ṁv².

Assume the declared available powerP bounds that kinetic power, with no additional energy source hidden in the model.

## Result name

An exact necessary bound

## Result statement

F≤2P/v. At fixedv, a force scalingF=cP^(1+δ) withc>0 andδ>0 cannot satisfy this bound for arbitrarily largeP.

## Proof

Eliminateṁ from the two definitions to obtainP_kin=Fv/2. The assumptionP_kin≤P gives the inequality by division byv/2. For the power law, the ratioFv/(2P)=(cv/2)P^δ grows without bound ifδ>0, so some finiteP violates the energy condition. This is an asymptotic obstruction, not a universal ban on superlinear behavior over a bounded range or on changing exhaust speed. A quantitative proposal must declare howv and efficiency depend onP. The dimensions also agree: watts divided by meters per second are newtons.

## Worked example: setup

TakeP=100000 watts andv=65000 meters per second, as in the recovered numerical combination.

## Worked example: calculation

The bound is200000/65000=40/13≈3.076923 newtons. A force of2150newtons at that same speed would require at leastFv/2=69,875,000 watts of kinetic power, about698.75 times the declared100kW. At10kW, the upper bound is4/13newtons≈0.30769.

## Worked example: conclusion

The necessary inequality is exact and makes the numerical inconsistency reviewable.

## Limits

The result is conditional on the explicitly stated energy budget and momentum-flux model. It does not evaluate a device, give operational advice or prove a full fluid or electromagnetic model. Additional energy sources would change the hypothesis and must be quantified.

## Extension

Rewrite all claimed scaling laws with dimensionless efficiencyη=P_kin/P≤1 and a declaredv(P), then identify a parameter range where every necessary inequality is satisfied.
