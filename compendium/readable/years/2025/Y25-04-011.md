## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which quotient actually produces the finite Jacobian algebra, and why is the displayed extra denominator fatal?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered Brieskorn-module formula includes all exact top forms in the denominator. For polynomial forms overC, every top form is exact, so that quotient is zero. A nearby correct quotient recovers the Milnor algebra; a genuine Brieskorn module uses a different denominator.

## Definitions

LetΩ^j denote polynomial differentialj-forms in n variables overC.

The Jacobian top-form quotient isΩ^n/(df∧Ω^(n−1)). A standard Brieskorn module instead usesdf∧dΩ^(n−2), with indexing conventions stated.

## Result name

Exact vanishing and the repaired finite quotient

## Result statement

dΩ^(n−1)=Ω^n, soΩ^n/(df∧Ω^(n−1)+dΩ^(n−1))=0. MeanwhileΩ^n/(df∧Ω^(n−1))≅C[x1,…,xn]/(∂1f,…,∂nf) times the volume form.

## Proof

For a polynomialp, integrate it termwise inx1 to obtain a polynomialP with∂1P=p. Then d(Pdx2∧⋯∧dxn)=pdx1∧⋯∧dxn; terms differentiated in other variables wedge a repeated differential and vanish. Thus every top form is exact, proving the first assertion. For the second, wedge df with a general(n−1)-form. Its coefficient of the volume form is an arbitrary linear combination of the partial derivatives, so its image is precisely the Jacobian ideal times that form. Taking the quotient proves the identification. This does not identify that finite quotient with the full Gauss–Manin or Brieskorn module, which retains deformation-parameter information.

## Worked example: setup

Takef=x³+y² in two variables.

## Worked example: calculation

The Jacobian ideal is(x²,y), so the repaired top-form quotient has basisdx∧dy andx dx∧dy, dimension2. Both forms are nevertheless exact:dx∧dy=d(xdy) andx dx∧dy=d((x²/2)dy). Adding all exact forms kills the entire two-dimensional quotient.

## Worked example: conclusion

The difference between the denominators changes the object from a meaningful invariant to zero; it is not a notation choice.

## Limits

The proof is for polynomial or convergent local forms in characteristic0. A full Brieskorn-lattice theorem, freeness and period connection require further structure.
