## Definitions

Letm²>0,R_k≥0 andZ_k(J)=∫_Rexp[−(m²+R_k)x²/2+Jx]dx. PutW_k=logZ_k.

Defineφ=∂JW_k andΓ_k(φ)=sup_J[Jφ−W_k(J)]−R_kφ²/2.

## Result statement

Γ_k(φ)=m²φ²/2+(1/2)log(m²+R_k)−(1/2)log(2π), so∂kΓ_k=(1/2)(Γ_k″+R_k)⁻¹∂kR_k. Its unique minimizing field isφ=0 for allk.

## Proof

Complete the square in the integral:Z_k=√(2π/(m²+R_k))exp[J²/(2(m²+R_k))]. Thusφ=J/(m²+R_k) andJ=(m²+R_k)φ. Substitute into the Legendre transform and subtractR_kφ²/2 to obtain the formula. Twoφ derivatives giveΓ_k″=m²; differentiating its additive logarithmic term in k gives the right-hand side exactly. The curvature is positive, so the unique minimizer is0. The quadratic coupling does not run in this free model, yet no nonzero scale or mass ratio is selected from that fact.
