## Definitions

Let L_k={0,…, k}withi⊕j=min(i+j, k).

For a monoid Sdefine M=S×L_kwith(s, i)·(t, j)=(st, i⊕j), andtr(s, i)=i.

## Result name

Associative capped product and absorbing trace layer

## Result statement

M is a monoid with identity(1_S,0), andtr(xy)=min(trx+try, k). The top layer S×{k}is a two-sided ideal. If S is a group andk>0, the idempotents are exactly(1_S,0)and(1_S, k).

## Proof

Capped addition is associative because both(i⊕j)⊕ℓandi⊕(j⊕ℓ)equalmin(i+j+ℓ, k). Its identity is zero. Product associativity and identity follow coordinatewise from S. If either trace isk, the product trace isk, proving the ideal statement. In a group, s²=simpliess=1_S. The remaining equationmin(2i, k)=ihas onlyi=0ori=k:if0<i<k, either2i<kand2i≠i, or2i≥kandk≠i. This proves the idempotent characterization. More general Scontributes its own idempotents in those two layers.
