## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which additivity law underlies a motivic expression, and how does a concrete open/closed decomposition act on cohomology?

## Status

## Summary

The projective line minus two points gives a fully specified localization example. It has classL−1 and compactly supported cohomology in degrees one and two; no undefined infinite motivic series is needed.

## Definitions

In the Grothendieck ring of varieties,[X]=[Z]+[X∖Z] for a closed subvarietyZ. SetL=[A¹].

LetX=P¹_C,Z={0,∞},U=C*. The open/closed trianglej_!k_U→k_X→i_*k_Z produces a long exact compact-support cohomology sequence.

The existence of this sheaf-theoretic localization sequence is standard background; its maps and dimensions are computed below.

## Result name

Exact class and compact-support cohomology ofC*

## Result statement

[C*]=L−1. Over a fieldk,H_c¹(C*;k)=k,H_c²(C*;k)=k and the other compact-support groups vanish. Its compact-support Euler characteristic is zero.

## Proof

DecomposeP¹ intoA¹ and the point at infinity to obtain[P¹]=L+1. Removing two points givesL−1. In the long exact sequence, H⁰(P¹)=k maps toH⁰(Z)=k² by the diagonal mapc↦(c,c). Its kernel is zero and cokernel is one-dimensional, soH_c⁰(U)=0 andH_c¹(U)=k. SinceH¹(P¹)=0,H¹(Z)=0,H²(P¹)=k andH²(Z)=0, exactness givesH_c²(U)=k. Hence the Euler characteristic is−1+1=0. The cohomological subtraction includes connecting maps and degrees; it is not literal subtraction of ordinary vector spaces. Its numerical Euler realization agrees with evaluatingL at one in the classL−1.

## Worked example: calculation

The column(1,1)ᵀ has rank one. The quotientk²/diag(k) is detected by(a,b)↦b−a. Thus two removed points create one compact-support degree-one class, even thoughC* itself has no compact connected component.

## Worked example: conclusion

This is a constructive interpretation of additivity, six-operation notation and a motivic class in one exact example.

## Limits

A motivic integral over arcs additionally needs a measure, cylinder normalization and a completed coefficient ring. The recovered sumΣ[X_n]L^−n does not automatically converge or define that measure. No quantum volume is inferred.

## Extension

ForP¹ withr punctures, repeat the same diagonal-map calculation to obtainH_c¹ dimensionr−1 andH_c² dimension one, and verify the classL+1−r. Then state what additional completion would be needed for an actual arc-space sum. Over field coefficients, the compact-support Künneth theorem gives dimensions 1,2,1 in degrees two, three and four for (C*)². Its class is (L−1)² and its Euler characteristic is again zero. These matching additivity and product rules provide a second consistency check without identifying all motivic information with Euler characteristic.
