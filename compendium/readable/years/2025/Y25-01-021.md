## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which explicit constructions connect the classical oscillator to prequantization and a quantum ground state?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

On the symplectic plane one can write the prequantum connection and verify its curvature directly. The constant-Poisson Moyal product and a normalized Gaussian then give exact, separately checkable quantization statements.

## Definitions

Use coordinates(q,p),ω=dq∧dp,H=(q²+p²)/2 andħ>0. DefineX_H byι_Xω=dH, givingX_H=p∂q−q∂p.

On the trivial complex line bundle set∇=d−(i/ħ)q dp. The formal Moyal product isf⋆g=f exp[(iħ/2)(←∂q→∂p−←∂p→∂q)]g.

The Schrödinger operator isĤ=−(ħ²/2)d²/dq²+q²/2 on an appropriateL² domain.

## Result name

Exact curvature and quantization checks

## Result statement

The connection has curvature−iω/ħ. The star commutatorq⋆p−p⋆q=iħ, andH⋆H=H²−ħ²/4. The Gaussianψ₀=(πħ)^−1/4exp(−q²/(2ħ)) is normalized and has energyħ/2.

## Proof

Because the connection one-form is scalar, its wedge square vanishes and∇²=d[−iq dp/ħ]=−i dq∧dp/ħ. Forq,p the bidifferential expansion stops after first derivatives, yieldingqp±iħ/2. ForH⋆H the first Poisson term is zero; the second derivative combination isH_qqH_pp+H_ppH_qq=2, so its coefficient is−ħ²/8 times two. Higher derivatives vanish. Differentiating the Gaussian givesψ₀″=(q²/ħ²−1/ħ)ψ₀, so substitution cancelsq² and leavesħψ₀/2. Its squared norm is one by the elementary Gaussian integral. Formal associativity of the Moyal product follows because the constant-coefficient operators on three tensor factors commute, making both parenthesizations the same exponential of the sum of pair operators.

## Worked example: setup

Setħ=1 and compare the classical fixed point with the Gaussian.

## Worked example: calculation

The classical point(q,p)=(0,0) hasH=0. The normalized quantum state has energy1/2 and variances〈q²〉=〈p²〉=1/2, givingΔqΔp=1/2. The difference is a quantified statement about a specified representation, not a change in the classical curvature formula.

## Limits

Prequantization alone does not choose a polarization or prove the full oscillator spectrum. The formal star product is not automatically a convergent product on every function space.

## Extension

Construct the ladder operators, prove their commutator on Schwartz functions and derive all Gaussian–Hermite eigenstates. Then justify completeness and the self-adjoint operator domain rather than relying only on formal algebra. Since H is quadratic, every third derivative vanishes and its Moyal commutator with a polynomial equals iħ times the Poisson bracket exactly. In particular (q⋆H−H⋆q)/(iħ)=p and (p⋆H−H⋆p)/(iħ)=−q, reproducing the classical linear oscillator equations with no higher-order correction.
