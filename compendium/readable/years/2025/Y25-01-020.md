## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can a claimed nonzero flux Jacobiator be distinguished from the identically zero Jacobiator of associative operator commutators?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

Associative commutators satisfy Jacobi by cancellation. An explicit octonion triple has a nonzero Jacobiator, furnishing a concrete nonassociative example without identifying it with a physicalR-flux.

## Definitions

For a bilinear multiplication define[a,b]=ab−ba andJ(a,b,c)=[a,[b,c]]+[b,[c,a]]+[c,[a,b]].

Use octonion unitse₁,…,e₇ with squares−1 and oriented Fano triples(123),(145),(176),(246),(257),(347),(365); reversing an ordered pair changes sign.

The associator is(a,b,c)=(ab)c−a(bc). Parentheses cannot be omitted in a nonassociative product.

## Result name

An exact nonzero Jacobiator

## Result statement

For(a,b,c)=(e₁,e₂,e₄), the associator is2e₇ andJ=−12e₇. In every associative algebra the same commutator formula hasJ=0.

## Proof

In an associative algebra expand the three nested commutators. Each of the six ordered triple products occurs twice with opposite signs, giving zero. In the specified octonion table,e₂e₄=e₆,e₁e₆=−e₇, so[e₁,[e₂,e₄]]=−4e₇. Alsoe₄e₁=−e₅,e₂e₅=e₇, giving the second term−4e₇. Finallye₁e₂=e₃,e₄e₃=−e₇ gives the third−4e₇. Their sum is−12e₇. Separately(e₁e₂)e₄=e₃e₄=e₇, wherease₁(e₂e₄)=e₁e₆=−e₇, giving associator2e₇. This verifies the sign convention rather than appealing to a generic nonassociative slogan. Left multiplication still represents every octonion as a linear map, but it is not a product-preserving representation: L_{e₁}L_{e₂}e₄=−e₇ whereas L_{e₁e₂}e₄=e₇. Commutators of the linear maps satisfy Jacobi, while the octonion commutators need not. This prevents confusing an operator embedding with an associative algebra embedding.

## Worked example: setup

## Worked example: calculation

Within that four-dimensional subalgebra multiplication is associative and every commutator Jacobiator vanishes. The chosen triple involvinge₄ leaves that subalgebra and gives the nonzero value above. Thus a large algebra can contain associative sectors without being associative globally.

## Worked example: conclusion

A nonzero Jacobiator is now attached to a declared multiplication table and a checked triple.

## Limits

Ordinary bounded matrices cannot realize this multiplication associatively while preserving all products, since their commutators always satisfy Jacobi. A physicalR-flux interpretation needs a separately defined bracket, observables and consistency rules; it is not derived from the table.

## Extension

Test alternativity(a,a,b)=(b,a,a)=0 on the full basis and extend by polarization. Then compare the resulting commutator algebra with the Malcev identity, which replaces Lie Jacobi in this setting.
