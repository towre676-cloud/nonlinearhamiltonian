## Revised question retained in the annual source

What precisely follows from the recovered finite-difference disorder statistic?

## Summary

The recovered Navier–Stokes program contains a valid local geometric estimate. Its conversion to a weighted gradient lower bound requires an explicit intermittency factor; that factor is a hypothesis, not a consequence of unit-length directions.

## Definitions

Let τ:T³→S² be smooth, w∈L²(T³) nonnegative, and ρ_r∈L¹(R³) nonnegative with total mass one and support |h|≤2r. Evaluate torus functions periodically for h∈R³.

Set A(r)=∫∫w(x)w(x+h)[1−(τ(x)·τ(x+h))²]ρ_r(h)dhdx.

## Result name

Exact weighted FTC–Jensen bound

## Result statement

A(r)≤4r²∫||∇τ(y)||²J_r(y)dy, where J_r(y)=ess sup_(s∈[0,1])∫w(y−sh)w(y+(1−s)h)ρ_r(h)dh. If J_r≤C_int w² almost everywhere for a finite C_int>0, then ∫w²||∇τ||²≥A(r)/(4r²C_int).

## Proof

For unit vectorsu, v,1−(u·v)²=(1−u·v)(1+u·v)≤2(1−u·v)=||u−v||². Along the straight torus displacementh, the fundamental theorem gives τ(x+h)−τ(x)=∫₀¹∇τ(x+sh)h ds. Jensen bounds its squared norm by|h|²∫₀¹||∇τ(x+sh)||²ds. Insert this in A, use|h|²≤4r², and change variablesy=x+sh. Tonelli applies because all terms are nonnegative. The resultingh-integral at fixeds, yis bounded by J_r(y); integratingsgives the claimed inequality. The final lower bound follows only after inserting J_r≤C_int w². The alternative assumption J_r≤C_int w instead yields a lower bound for ∫w||∇τ||²; these are different hypotheses. If w vanishes where J_r is positive, no finite factor exists. Essential suprema avoid dependence on representatives in the interpolation parameter.

## Worked example: calculation

Then J_r=1and A(r)≤4r²∫||∇τ||². A constant τ has A=0andzero gradient. The inequality goes in the useful lower-bound direction only when a positive disorder Ais independently known; it cannot create a positive lower bound from the definitions alone.

## Limits

This is a direction-field estimate, not global Navier–Stokes regularity. It supplies neither the required disorder lower bound nor control of a nonlinear production term. Its assumptions may fail at vorticity zeros or under strong intermittency.

## Extension

Derive or falsify a uniform C_intfor the actual solution class, and state any nonvanishing or regularized direction convention before using the estimate in an enstrophy argument.
