## Result statement

At fixed P, multiplying area by1.7 divides temperature by1.7^(1/4). Multiplying both area by1.7 and temperature by1.7^(1/4) instead multiplies P by1.7²=2.89. The source’s150/555 and150/277.5 ratios are≈.27027 and.54054 before auxiliary power.

## Proof

For efficiency, conservation under a steady boundary requires all positive energy inputs in the denominator.
