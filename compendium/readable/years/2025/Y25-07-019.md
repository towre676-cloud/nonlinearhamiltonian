## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which combinatorial operators and weight multiplicities make a crystal claim precise?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The July17 synthesis named crystal bases and quantum groups. The rank-one crystal supplies an explicit, checkable combinatorial structure and character identity. It does not turn a crystal diagram into a physical lattice or an arbitrary categorical symmetry.

## Definitions

For m≥0, B(m)={0,…,m} has weight wt(k)=m−2k. Define lowering f(k)=k+1 when k<m and raising e(k)=k−1 when k>0, with an undefined symbol outside the chain. Its formal character is χ_m(z)=Σ_{k=0}^mz^(m−2k).

## Result name

A rank-one character decomposition

## Result statement

For m≥1, χ_m(z)(z+z⁻¹)=χ_{m+1}(z)+χ_{m−1}(z). Raising and lowering change weights by±2 and are inverse wherever both are defined.

## Proof

The operator assertions follow directly from the chain definitions. For the character identity multiply the finite geometric-weight sum by z and z⁻¹. The extreme weights m+1 and−m−1 occur once; every interior weight from m−1 to−m+1 occurs twice. χ_{m+1} supplies one copy of every weight in that range, and χ_{m−1} supplies the second interior copy. This proves the identity as a Laurent polynomial. It matches the classical sl₂ tensor decomposition V_m⊗V_1≅V_{m+1}⊕V_{m−1}, but constructing a full quantum-group crystal basis entails a module, integral form and limiting procedure beyond this combinatorial verification.

## Worked example: setup

Use m=2.

## Worked example: calculation

B(2) has weights2,0,−2. Multiplication byχ₁=z+z⁻¹ gives z³+2z+2z⁻¹+z⁻³, equal toχ₃+χ₁. Evaluating at z=1 gives3·2=4+2, the corresponding dimension check.

## Worked example: conclusion

The diagram and coefficients are now defined objects with exact identities, rather than symbols standing in for unconstructed symmetry.

## Limits

The rank-one example does not produce an E₈ crystal, a canonical basis for every representation or a link to the proposed ribbon operators.
