## Summary

The user source names quantum doubles. The finite abelian exampleG=Z2 is completely calculable and produces four labels rather than merely repeating the original two. It is a constructive model of the distinction between an algebra and its center.

## Definitions

For a finite abelianG, simple sectors of its untwisted double are pairs(g,χ), withg∈G andχ a character.

Fusion is(g,χ)(h,ψ)=(g+h,χψ), and the twist isθ_(g,χ)=χ(g).

## Result name

Exact Z2 double data

## Result statement

ForG=Z2 the labels are1,e,m,em, all dimension1, with twists1,1,1,−1. The double braiding ofe andm is−1, and itsS matrix is the four-label bicharacter matrix of the preceding model.

## Proof

Z2 has two group elements and two charactersχ0,χ1, withχ1(1)=−1. Pairing them gives four labels. Componentwise fusion makes every nonidentity label square to1. The twist formula gives−1 only when both flux and charge are nontrivial. The monodromy between(g,χ) and(h,ψ) isχ(h)ψ(g), so choosing a pure charge and a pure flux gives−1. Character orthogonality makes the normalizedS matrix invertible, as can also be checked by squaring its explicit integer-sign matrix. These calculations are the finite instance of the general theorem that the Drinfeld center of a suitable fusion category is nondegenerate.

## Limits

The untwisted abelian calculation does not cover nonabelian conjugacy-class centralizers or cocycle-twisted doubles. It also does not identify an unspecifiedSU4/Q8 framework with this category.
