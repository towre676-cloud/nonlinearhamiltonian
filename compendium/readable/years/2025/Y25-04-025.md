## Proof

In theZ eigenbasis, e^(itH/ℏ)=diag(e^(iEt/ℏ),e^(−iEt/ℏ)). ConjugatingX makes its upper off-diagonal entrye^(2iEt/ℏ) and its lower entry the conjugate. Writing these ascos±isin gives the displayedX,Y combination with the stated sign. The+X vector hasexpectations〈X〉=1,〈Y〉=0, so its evolved expectation is the cosine. More generally an off-diagonal matrix unit between energiesEa,Eb acquires phasee^(it(Ea−Eb)/ℏ). This is a direct spectral difference, not an automatic spatial motion law.
