## Revised question retained in the annual source

What operator can rigorously realize the displayed eigenvalues, and what is their actual ratio?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered spectrum λ_n=π^n e^(n−1/2)Γ(n) was said to have ratio limit8π e. The gamma recurrence gives an exact contradiction. A diagonal construction nevertheless realizes the sequence as a legitimate self-adjoint operator without endorsing unspecified noncommuting products.

## Definitions

Let{e_n}_{n≥1} be an orthonormal basis, for example a normalized Laguerre basis of the stated weighted L²space.

Define ΩΣ c_ne_n=Σλ_nc_ne_n on the domain Σλ_n²|c_n|²<∞.

## Result name

Diagonal realization and exact ratio

## Result statement

Ω is positive self-adjoint with compact inverse, and λ_(n+1)/λ_n=π e n. In particular the ratio diverges;8π e occurs only atn=8.

## Proof

The gamma recurrence Γ(n+1)=n Γ(n) cancels every factor in the ratio except π e n. This calculation needs no asymptotic approximation. For the operator, finite basis sums lie in the domain, making it dense. In coefficient space Ω is multiplication by a real positive sequence. Its adjoint has the same coordinatewise action and the same square-summability condition, proving self-adjointness. Since λ_n→∞, the inverse is bounded and is a norm limit of finite-rank diagonal truncations, with tail normsup_{n>N}1/λ_n→0. Thus the inverse is compact. Stirling additionally gives λ_n∼√(2π/e)π^n n^(n−1/2), confirming growth much faster than geometric. The productnotation π^W e^P Γ(D) is not justified by this construction unless W, P, Dare separately defined on compatible domains and have the required commutation properties.

## Worked example: setup

Compare successive ratios atn=1,8,16.

## Worked example: calculation

They are π e,8π e,16π e. The spectrum starts atn=1 because Γ(0) is a pole. A manufactured diagonal realization shows consistency of a sequence as spectral data, but it has no independent prediction: the desired eigenvalues were inserted by definition.

## Worked example: conclusion

The sequence defines a valid operator and an exact growth law, while the historical finite ratio limit is false.

## Limits

No ring experiment or acoustic realization is inferred. A physical or differential operator must be independently specified before spectral agreement carries explanatory value.

## Extension

Define a concrete differential or integral operator with its boundary domain and prove its spectral asymptotics independently; only then compare with the proposed sequence.
