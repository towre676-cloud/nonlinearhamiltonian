## Revised question retained in the annual source

What exact sequence relates rational points, the n-Selmer group and the Tate–Shafarevich group?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The historical skeleton used simplified cocycles rather than verified Euler-system arithmetic. The Kummer sequence states the genuine object a descent computation must certify and explains exactly why Selmer dimension is a rank bound rather than automatically the rank.

## Definitions

Let E/K be an elliptic curve over a number field and n≥2. Define Ш(E/K)=ker[H¹(K,E)→∏_vH¹(K_v,E)]. Sel_n consists of H¹(K,E[n]) classes whose image in every H¹(K_v,E) is zero.

## Result name

Kummer–Selmer exact sequence

## Result statement

There is an exact sequence0→E(K)/nE(K)→Sel_n(E/K)→Ш(E/K)[n]→0. For n=p prime, if the Selmer dimension is s and dim_Fp E(K)[p]=t, then rank E(K)≤s−t.

## Proof

Multiplication by n on E over an algebraic closure is surjective with kernel E[n]. Its cohomology sequence gives0→E(K)/nE(K)→H¹(K,E[n])→H¹(K,E)[n]→0, and the analogous local sequences hold. Taking preimages of locally trivial global classes proves the displayed sequence: every n-torsion class of Ш has a Kummer lift, and each local image of that lift is locally Kummer. Finite generation writes E(K)=Z^r⊕T, so E(K)/pE(K) has dimension r+t. Its injection into Sel_p yields the inequality. Equality requires ruling out the remaining Ш[p] contribution; a norm-square condition by itself need not enforce every local condition.

## Worked example: conclusion

An actual rank proof needs both a valid local-condition upper bound and a certified independence lower bound.

## Limits
