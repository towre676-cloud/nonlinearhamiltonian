## Summary

The July23 form has a real arithmetic obstruction. It can be proved by completing squares modulo seven and descending through powers of49, without any speculative tmf or Langlands construction. This is stronger than checking a few missing values numerically, but it remains a necessary condition rather than a complete representation classification.

## Definitions

Let F(x,y,z)=x²+2y²+3z²+xz+yz on Z³. For N≠0 write v₇(N) for its7-adic valuation. The nonzero quadratic residues modulo seven are1,2,4.

## Result name

A local nonrepresentation family

## Result statement

If N=7^(2k+1)u with7∤u and u a quadratic residue modulo seven, then F never represents N. In particular7,14,28,56,63,77 are excluded.

## Proof

Modulo seven, F=(x+4z)²+2(y+2z)². Because−2≡5 is not a square modulo seven, F≡0 forces x≡3z and y≡5z. Write x=3z+7a,y=5z+7b. Expansion gives F/7=10z²+7az+21bz+7a²+14b², hence F/7≡3z². If v₇(F)=1, its unit part must lie in{3,5,6}, the nonresidues, not{1,2,4}. If49 divides F, this congruence forces z≡0 and then x,y≡0; dividing all coordinates by seven replaces F by F/49. Iterating reduces any odd valuation to the first case, proving the theorem. Positivity and a finite search bound follow independently from8F=2(2x+z)²+(4y+z)²+21z².
