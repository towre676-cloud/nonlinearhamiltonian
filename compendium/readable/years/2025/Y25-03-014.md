## Revised question retained in the annual source

How can a modular cocycle be verified exactly before using it as a transport or geometric flow?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The retrieved passage requests a modular-cocycle explanation but truncates the actual formula. A faithful finite-state example gives a precise replacement, including the twisted law that distinguishes a cocycle from an ordinary one-parameter group.

## Definitions

For faithful density matricesρ,σ defineu_t=ρ^(it)σ^(−it). Letασ_t(A)=σ^(it)Aσ^(−it).

A cocycle forασ satisfiesu_(s+t)=u_s ασ_s(u_t).

## Result name

Finite cocycle and intertwining

## Result statement

The matricesu_t are unitary, satisfy the stated cocycle law, and obey αρ_t(A)=u_t ασ_t(A)u_t*.

## Proof

Positive functional calculus makesρ^(it) andσ^(it) unitary. Multiplyingu_sασ_s(u_t) givesρ^(is)σ^(−is)σ^(is)ρ^(it)σ^(−it)σ^(−is)=ρ^(i(s+t))σ^(−i(s+t)), exactlyu_(s+t). Notice that commutingρ withσ was never assumed; only powers of the same matrix were combined. For intertwining, the adjacentσ factors cancel in u_t σ^(it)Aσ^(−it)u_t*, leavingρ^(it)Aρ^(−it). Ifρ andσ fail to commute, u_su_t need not equalu_(s+t); replacing the twisted composition by an ordinary group law is therefore an additional unjustified step.

## Worked example: setup

For a transparent commuting example takeρ=diag(3/4,1/4),σ=diag(1/2,1/2).

## Worked example: calculation

u_t=diag((3/2)^(it),(1/2)^(it)). Hereασ is trivial, so the cocycle happens to be an ordinary unitary group. Replacingσ by a nontrivially rotated diagonal state retains the twisted identity but generally loses that simplification.

## Worked example: conclusion

The exact algebra supplies the transport law and identifies which simplification relies on commutativity.

## Limits

This is the finite faithful-state case of a more general operator-algebraic construction. The historical categorical state space, morphisms and infinite-dimensional domains were not recovered.

## Extension

Use two explicit noncommuting2×2 states, verify the cocycle numerically at several times with residual bounds, and calculate its derivative generator without assuming it is time independent.
