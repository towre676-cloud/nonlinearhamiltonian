## Revised question retained in the annual source

How does a good-prime point count determine Euler coefficients, and how do these differ from Frobenius power sums?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The July8 material connected modular forms, Galois representations and elliptic curves. The precise elementary bridge is a formal reciprocal quadratic. Once the local trace is declared, it gives an exact recurrence without proving the deeper modularity or representation-theoretic theorems.

## Definitions

At a good prime p put a_p=p+1−#E(F_p), and define A(T)=Σ_{r≥0}a_{p^r}T^r=(1−a_pT+pT²)⁻¹. Its constant coefficient is1. All coefficient calculations take place formally, so analytic convergence is irrelevant.

## Result name

Good-prime recurrence

## Result statement

For r≥2, a_{p^r}=a_pa_{p^{r−1}}−pa_{p^{r−2}}. If α,β solve X²−a_pX+p=0, then a_{p^r}=Σ_{j=0}^rα^jβ^{r−j}, whereas the Frobenius power sum is α^r+β^r.

## Proof

Multiplying A by its denominator and comparing coefficients gives the recurrence and uniquely determines its formal inverse. Alternatively expand(1−αT)⁻¹(1−βT)⁻¹ and collect degree r. In particular a_{p²}=a_p²−p, but α²+β²=a_p²−2p. The extension-field count uses the latter: #E(F_{p²})=p²+1−(α²+β²). This distinction prevents an Euler-series coefficient from being silently substituted for a point-count trace. For a weight-k eigenform with character χ the determinant term instead is χ(p)p^{k−1}.

## Worked example: setup

Take E:y²=x³−x at p=5.

## Worked example: calculation

For x=0,1,2,3,4 the y-counts are1,1,2,2,1. Infinity makes eight points, so a₅=−2. The local reciprocal has coefficients1,−2,−1,12,−19 through degree4. The extension-field formula gives #E(F₂₅)=26−(4−10)=32, while the Euler coefficient a₂₅ is−1.

## Worked example: conclusion

An exact short calculation now separates local traces, prime-power Euler coefficients and extension-field counts.

## Limits

Bad primes require different local factors. Matching a finite recurrence does not construct a Galois representation, verify an Euler system or prove BSD.

## Extension

Enumerate several finite extensions for a named good prime and verify the trace relation separately from the formal Euler recurrence.
