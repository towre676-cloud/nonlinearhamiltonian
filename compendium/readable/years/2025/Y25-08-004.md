## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which normalization turns an adjacency matrix into a stochastic transition matrix, and why does its entropy equal logλ?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The August9 PF/KMS discussion can be grounded in a finite primitive adjacency matrix. Its Perron eigenvectors define an exact stationary Markov chain. A relative-entropy calculation proves the relevant variational statement for this specified class of chains.

## Definitions

Let A be a primitive0–1 matrix with positive right/left PF vectors Ar=λr,ℓᵀA=λℓᵀ,ℓᵀr=1. Set P_ij=A_ij r_j/(λr_i) and π_i=ℓ_i r_i.

## Result name

Perron Markov chain and entropy maximization

## Result statement

P is stochastic,π is stationary, and its entropy rate is logλ. Every stationary Markov chain Q supported on the same allowed edges has entropy rate at most logλ.

## Proof

The PF equations giveΣ_jP_ij=1 andΣ_iπ_iP_ij=π_j. On allowed edges logP_ij=logr_j−logr_i−logλ. Averaging with stationary edge weightsπ_iP_ij cancels the r terms, giving−Σπ_iP_ijlogP_ij=logλ. For another stationary chain(Q,ν), nonnegativity of the rowwise KL divergencesΣ_iν_iΣ_jQ_ijlog(Q_ij/P_ij) yields h(Q)≤−Σν_iQ_ijlogP_ij=logλ, again by stationarity. This proves the finite Markov variational problem without silently extending it to every dynamical or C*-algebraic setting.

## Worked example: setup

Use A=[[1,1],[1,0]] and φ=(1+√5)/2.

## Worked example: calculation

Take r=(φ,1),ℓ=r/(φ²+1). Then P=[[1/φ,1/φ²],[1,0]], andπ=(φ²,1)/(φ²+1). The entropy rate is logφ. A KMS inverse-temperature interpretation requires a separately specified algebra and time evolution.

## Worked example: conclusion

The normalization, stationary law and pressure value are explicit rather than conflated.

## Limits

The full pressure variational principle and a particular KMS theorem need additional categories, measures and dynamics. A PF eigenvalue alone does not provide those identifications.

## Extension

Introduce a declared edge potential W_ij>0 and repeat the eigenvector construction and KL proof for weighted pressure.
