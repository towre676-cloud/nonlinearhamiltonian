## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What shifts appear in localization, and which exact calculation checks a torus or blow-up implementation?

## Status

Developed mathematical repair or classical result; speculative source claims delimited

## Summary

Mayer–Vietoris, Gysin and blow-up formulas are not interchangeable rank rules. Each has maps, shifts and geometric hypotheses. The one-dimensional torus supplies an exact low-degree test; a point blow-up of the plane supplies a second test for exceptional classes.

## Definitions

For a smooth X, smooth closed Z of codimension c and U=X∖Z, motivic purity/localization gives …→H^{p−2c,q−c}(Z)→H^{p,q}(X)→H^{p,q}(U)→H^{p−2c+1,q−c}(Z)→… . This is a stated classical theorem.

For a field k, G_m=Spec k[t,t^(−1)] has units c t^n, with c∈k× and n∈Z.

## Result name

The torus units and an exceptional-divisor rank check

## Result statement

H^{1,1}(G_m)=k×⊕Z, with the extra Z measured by the exponent of t. The codimension-one boundary map records that exponent. The classical blow-up formula adds CH^{r−1}(point) to CH^r(P²) when blowing up a point, so CH¹ gains one independent exceptional class.

## Proof

To prove the units assertion directly, write any nonzero Laurent polynomial with least and greatest exponents a≤b. Under multiplication over a field the extreme coefficients cannot cancel, so exponent width b−a adds. A product equal to one has width zero, forcing each unit to be one monomial c t^n. Multiplication adds exponents, giving k[t,t^(−1)]×≅k×⊕Z. The classical identity H^{1,1}(Y)=O(Y)× identifies this with the motivic group. For X=A¹,Z={0},c=1, localization therefore compares k× with k×⊕Z and its quotient Z, matching the shift to H^{0,0}(Z). For a smooth codimension-c blow-up, the classical Chow formula contributesΣ_{i=1}^{c−1}CH^{r−i}(Z); at a point of P² only one extra codimension-one generator appears. The theorem is an explicit input, while these indices and ranks are its checked consequences.

## Limits

Purity needs regular embeddings or the appropriate replacement theorem. Singular centers, arbitrary coefficients and nonflat products require additional hypotheses. No general computation of tori, quadrics or complete intersections is supplied by these two tests.

## Extension

Encode localization and blow-up as exact triangles with verified shifts and maps. Derive Mayer–Vietoris by an actual cover and test exactness rather than using an unproved universal rank-addition function.
