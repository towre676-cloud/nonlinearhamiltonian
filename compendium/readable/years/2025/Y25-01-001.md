## Revised question retained in the annual source

What algebraic data can actually be extracted from a specified unipotent monodromy before adding a quantum correction?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

A two-dimensional unipotent matrix supplies an exact logarithm, a centered weight filtration and a compatible sl₂ representation. This concrete calculation isolates the linear algebra used in degeneration theory from an unprovided quantum enrichment.

## Definitions

Let V=Qe₁⊕Qe₂ and M=[[1,1],[0,1]]. Its nilpotent logarithm is N=logM=M−I because(M−I)²=0.

A monodromy weight filtration centered at one is increasing, satisfiesN(W_k)⊂W_{k−2}, and makesNʲ:Gr^W_{1+j}→Gr^W_{1−j} an isomorphism for every j≥0.

## Result name

Explicit monodromy weight calculation

## Result statement

The filtration isW_k=0 fork<0,W₀=W₁=Qe₁,W_k=V fork≥2. The mapN:Gr²_W→Gr⁰_W is an isomorphism. The nontriviality of this filtration does not alone define a limiting mixed Hodge structure.

## Proof

SinceNe₂=e₁ andNe₁=0, image and kernel ofN are bothQe₁. The proposed filtration is stable underN with the required two-step decrease. Both nonzero graded pieces are one-dimensional and the inducedN maps[e₂] to[e₁], proving the required isomorphism. Higher powers are zero and relate zero graded pieces. For an sl₂ triple setE=N,F=[[0,0],[1,0]],H=diag(1,−1). Multiplication gives[H,E]=2E,[H,F]=−2F,[E,F]=H. Weight index1−H assigns index zero toe₁ and two toe₂, agreeing with the filtration. This is the exact finite representation-theoretic content; a Hodge filtration, polarization and geometric degeneration are additional data. Over the integer lattice, replacing M by M_n=I+nN changes the coinvariants to Z⊕Z/nZ for n>0: the image is nZe₁. Tensoring with Q removes this torsion and leaves the same rational rank. This gives an explicit arithmetic distinction invisible to the real matrix rank alone.

## Worked example: setup

ApplyM repeatedly and calculate the invariant subspace and coinvariants.

## Worked example: calculation

Mⁿ=I+nN=[[1,n],[0,1]] for every integern. Invariants areQe₁; coinvariantsV/NV are generated by[e₂]. They each have dimension one, yet one is a subspace and the other a quotient. Confusing them would erase the nonzero connecting mapN.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example supplies explicit degeneration linear algebra instead of an undefined list of corrected filtrations.

## Limits

The computation is not a proof of the geometric monodromy theorem or the Clemens–Schmid sequence. A genuine limiting mixed Hodge structure must satisfy its Hodge and polarization requirements; attaching the word quantum does not provide them.

## Extension

For a supplied nilpotent matrix, compute its Jordan strings. A lengthr string receives centered weights1−(r−1),1−(r−3),…,1+(r−1). Prove the gradedNʲ isomorphisms string by string and then test compatibility with an independently specified Hodge filtration.
