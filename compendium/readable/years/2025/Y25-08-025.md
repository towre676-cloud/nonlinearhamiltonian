## Result statement

r≡33a+16b mod48. Among sixteen ternary samples, entropy is maximized by counts(6,5,5) up to permutation, giving H/log3≈0.9965. This does not prove equidistribution or normality of x.

## Proof

The CRT coefficients satisfy33≡1 mod16,33≡0 mod3,16≡0 mod16 and16≡1 mod3, proving reconstruction and uniqueness. For entropy, the function−t logt is concave. If two integer counts differ by at least two, moving one from the larger to the smaller increases the sum. Repeating yields the balanced integer partition6,5,5. Thus the reported entropy scale can arise simply from the best-balanced count pattern at this sample size. Decimal normality requires limiting frequencies of all blocks; neither sixteen residues nor a cryptographic manifest proves it. Binary floating multiplication by10^n can also lose fractional information and must not be treated as exact real arithmetic.
