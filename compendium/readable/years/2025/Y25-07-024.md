## Result statement

## Proof

Eisenstein at two proves x⁴−2 irreducible. Q(α) is real and therefore omits the root iα, so it is not a splitting field. Adjoining i doubles the degree. Automorphisms r:α↦iα,i↦i and s:α↦α,i↦−i obey r⁴=s²=1 and srs=r⁻¹, giving the eight-element dihedral group. For the ray group, Q has trivial ordinary class group, and positive principal generators leave the full residue-unit group modulo136=8·17. CRT gives orderφ(8)φ(17)=4·16=64.
