## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which explicit group action defines a hyperbolic quotient, and why do finite volume and compactness remain different properties?

## Summary

The translation quotient of the upper half-plane gives a concrete cusp geometry with an exact area and shrinking circular sections. This supplies real quotient mathematics without deriving an automorphic economic model from notation.

## Result name

Exact cusp area and noncompactness

## Result statement
