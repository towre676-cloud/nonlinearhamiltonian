## Revised question retained in the annual source

What exactly are Δ and K in a finite faithful-state model?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered modular expressions are classical, but several operators share the name modular Hamiltonian. A faithful matrix-state model separates the density-matrix logarithm from the standard-form generator on Hilbert–Schmidt operators.

## Definitions

Letρ be a positive definite density matrix. OnHilbert–Schmidt matricesX, defineΔρ(X)=ρXρ⁻¹ andJ(X)=X*.

The density Hamiltonian ishρ=−logρ, whereas the standard-form generator isKρ=−logΔρ.

## Result name

Exact finite-dimensional modular structure

## Result statement

For matrix unitsEij in aρ-eigenbasis with eigenvaluespi, ΔρEij=(pi/pj)Eij andKρEij=log(pj/pi)Eij. Modular flow on left observables isσ_tρ(A)=ρ^(it)Aρ^(−it).

## Proof

Left multiplication byρ and right multiplication byρ⁻¹ commute and are positive self-adjoint on Hilbert–Schmidt space. Their action onEij gives the eigenvaluepi/pj. Functional calculus yields the logarithm and imaginary powers, proving every formula. WithΩ=√ρ, the Tomita map initially sendsAΩ toA*Ω. WritingY=A√ρ givesS(Y)=ρ^(−1/2)Y*ρ^(1/2), which isJΔρ^(1/2)Y. AlsoΔρΩ=Ω, soKρΩ=0. By contrastTr(ρhρ) is the generally nonzero von Neumann entropy. Conflating these two expectations would invalidate a first-law calculation.

## Worked example: setup

Takeρ=diag(3/4,1/4).

## Worked example: calculation

Δρ has eigenvalues1,3,1/3,1 onE11,E12,E21,E22. Thusσ_tρ(E12)=3^(it)E12 andσ_tρ(E21)=3^(−it)E21. The density Hamiltonian has eigenvalueslog(4/3),log4, whileKρ has0,−log3,+log3,0.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite model realizes Tomita–Takesaki notation exactly and exposes its two distinct logarithms.

## Limits

Infinite von Neumann algebras may have no density matrix or trace. Their modular theory is deeper; this finite derivation does not establish a categorical version or a geometric backreaction equation.

## Extension

For a proposed categorical analogue, identify its algebra, faithful state, Hilbert-space representation and an actual modular operator before asserting covariance or a first law.
