## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which covariance condition turns cylinder data into an actual Hilbert-valued random variable?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The August9 synthesis invoked Sazonov and Minlos. A diagonal Hilbert-space construction proves a concrete sufficient condition and exhibits the failure of identity covariance. This keeps a genuine measure theorem separate from formal consistency or category names.

## Definitions

Let H=ℓ² with basis e_n and independent standard normals Z_n. For q_n≥0 consider X=Σ√q_nZ_ne_n. The covariance has diagonal entries q_n.

## Result name

Trace-summable construction and identity-covariance failure

## Result statement

IfΣq_n<∞, X converges almost surely in H and in mean square, with characteristic function exp(−Σq_nh_n²/2). For q_n=1 the coordinate law givesℓ² probability zero.

## Proof

The nonnegative random sum S=Σq_nZ_n² has expectationΣq_n by monotone convergence. Finite expectation implies S<∞ almost surely, which is exactly square summability of the coordinates. The tail mean-square error equalsΣ_{n>N}q_n, proving L² convergence. Finite Gaussian characteristic functions multiply, and dominated convergence yields the displayed limit. The resulting Borel probability on the Polish spaceℓ² is Radon. By contrast the strong law gives N⁻¹Σ_{n≤N}Z_n²→1 almost surely when q_n=1, forcing divergence of the squared norm. Consistency of all finite marginals remains true, but their product measure is supported outsideℓ².

## Worked example: setup

Choose q_n=2^(−n),n≥1.

## Worked example: calculation

The covariance trace and E||X||² are one. Truncation after N has squared mean error2^(−N). Identity-covariance truncations have expected squared norm N and their probability of lying in any fixed-radius ball tends to zero.

## Worked example: conclusion

Support and tightness, not just finite consistency, are the decisive missing hypotheses.

## Limits

This proves the diagonal case, not the general Sazonov or nuclear-space Minlos theorem. Cameron–Martin vectors and typical samples also have different meanings.

## Extension

Specify the actual topological vector space and covariance, then prove the continuity/tightness assumptions of the chosen extension theorem.
