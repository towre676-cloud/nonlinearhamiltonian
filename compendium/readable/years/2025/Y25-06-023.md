## Summary

The program description names B₄,E₆,E₇,E₈ and coordinate reflections. Signed coordinate permutations give a genuine B₄ action with an exact finite classification. ## Definitions

Let B₄ be the group of signed4×4 permutation matrices over F₃. Each sign is±1, distinct in this field. It acts on the81 vectors in F₃⁴.

Hamming weight w(x) is the number of nonzero coordinates. The reduced quadratic sumΣx_i² equals w(x) modulo three, a coarser invariant.

## Result name

Order, orbits and a faithful-representation obstruction

## Result statement

|B₄|=2⁴·4!=384. Its orbits are exactly the five Hamming-weight classes, of sizes1,8,24,32,16. A faithful linear action of W(E₈) on F₃⁴ is impossible because|W(E₈)|=696729600 exceeds|GL₄(F₃)|=24261120.

## Proof

Choose a permutation of the four coordinates and an independent sign for each output coordinate; the resulting384 matrices are distinct. They preserve the number of nonzero coordinates. Conversely any vector of weight r can be moved to(1,…,1,0,…,0) by a permutation and sign flips, proving one orbit for each r. Choose its support in binomial(4,r) ways and its nonzero signs in2^r ways, giving the sizes. Their sum is81. For the obstruction, a faithful linear action embeds its group into GL₄(F₃). Counting ordered independent columns gives(81−1)(81−3)(81−9)(81−27)=24261120, too small for W(E₈). The quoted E₈ order is a classical root-system input; the cardinality contradiction is elementary.
