## Revised question retained in the annual source

How should an effective operator be defined when a tensor-network coarse map changes dimension?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered MERA expression uses an inverse that may not exist for a dimension-reducing map. An isometric embedding gives a precise compression. It preserves Hermiticity but generally loses an invariant subspace; the loss can be measured by an explicit residual.

## Definitions

LetZ:C^r→C^d satisfyZ*Z=I_r and letH=H* onC^d. DefineH_eff=Z*HZ andP=ZZ*.

The discarded coupling isR=(I−P)HZ; it vanishes exactly when the embedded subspace isH-invariant.

## Result name

Compression and a residual identity

## Result statement

H_eff is Hermitian and HZ−ZH_eff=R. IfH_effv=λv with||v||=1, then||(H−λ)Zv||=||Rv||, so a self-adjoint residual bound certifies spectral proximity by||Rv||.

## Proof

Taking adjoints gives(Z*HZ)*=Z*HZ. NextZH_eff=ZZ*HZ=PHZ, and subtracting yieldsR. SinceZ preserves norms, Zv is a unit vector. The eigenvalue equation then gives(H−λ)Zv=Rv. For a HermitianH, diagonalize it and expandZv in its orthonormal eigenbasis: the residual norm squared isΣ|c_j|²(E_j−λ)², at leastdist(λ,σ(H))². Thusdist≤||Rv||. No inverse ofZ is used. WhenR=0 the restriction is genuinely invariant and the compressed eigenvalues are exact eigenvalues; otherwise compression is a variational approximation.

## Worked example: setup

TakeH=diag(0,2) andZ=(1,1)ᵀ/√2, a2×1 isometry.

## Worked example: calculation

H_eff=1, whileHZ−ZH_eff=(−1,1)ᵀ/√2 has norm1. The candidate1 is exactly distance1 from the true spectrum{0,2}. Thus a valid Hermitian compression can produce an intermediate value rather than an exact level.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example supplies both the correct effective operator and a quantitative certificate of what it loses.

## Limits

An arbitrary tensor-network map need not be isometric, and repeated compressions accumulate errors. No MERA efficiency, emergent metric or arithmetic correspondence follows from this finite example.

## Extension

For a sequence of blocking maps, track each discarded coupling and prove a cumulative bound on the target spectral cluster.
