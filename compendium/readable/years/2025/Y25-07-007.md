## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which witness lets a small checker verify an optimization or rank answer returned by external code?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The historical pipeline mixed Lean, opaque foreign pointers and arithmetic engines. A binding alone is not a proof. Exact linear certificates illustrate how complex untrusted computation can propose data whose decisive properties are checked with elementary mathematics.

## Definitions

For min cᵀx subject to Ax≥b,x≥0, the dual is max bᵀy subject to Aᵀy≤c,y≥0. A rank-r witness can pair a nonzero r×r minor with an exact factorization A=UV through an r-dimensional space.

## Result name

Matching primal and dual values certify optimality

## Result statement

Feasible x,y with cᵀx=bᵀy are optimal. A matrix with a nonzero r-minor and a factorization through dimension r has exact rank r.

## Proof

For any feasible pair, bᵀy≤(Ax)ᵀy=xᵀAᵀy≤cᵀx by nonnegativity. Equality at one pair excludes a smaller primal value and a larger dual value. For rank, a nonsingular r-minor gives r independent columns, while A=UV bounds the image dimension by r. These proofs use only exact equalities, inequalities and dimensions. The search procedure may be complicated, but its correctness is unnecessary once the complete witness is independently checked. Conversely a solver’s status string, foreign pointer or unproved theorem declaration is not such a witness.

## Worked example: setup

Minimize x+2y with x+y≥3 and x,y≥0.

## Worked example: calculation

The primal point(3,0) has value3. The dual scalar λ=1 satisfies λ≤1,λ≤2 and has the same value3. Therefore the optimum is exactly3. A floating answer2.999999 needs a feasibility/error certificate rather than being silently rounded into a theorem.

## Worked example: conclusion

A meaningful mathematical API returns the witness and every hypothesis used by its checker.

## Limits

A bounded model check proves only its finite domain. This linear certificate does not validate a nonlinear model or an elliptic-rank answer lacking genuine local and height hypotheses.

## Extension

Specify exact rational reconstruction and certificate dimensions; measure checker soundness separately from search success.
