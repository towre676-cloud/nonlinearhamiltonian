## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which part of the3-SAT-to-CLIQUE construction is proved, and what extra condition would make its homology map injective?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The July2 response conjectured homology injectivity from a standard decision reduction. Correctness of a reduction concerns the existence of solutions, whereas injectivity concerns every cycle and boundary. These are different statements, and a small simplicial map shows the gap.

## Definitions

For a k-clause formula, create one graph vertex per literal occurrence. Join vertices from different clauses when their literals are not contradictory. A simplicial map sends vertices to vertices and every face to a face; repeated images may lower dimension.

## Result name

The standard clique equivalence and the missing topological theorem

## Result statement

The formula is satisfiable exactly when the occurrence graph has a k-clique. A simplicial map in general need not induce an injective homology map, even when it preserves an existence predicate.

## Proof

A satisfying assignment chooses a true literal in each clause. The chosen occurrences are pairwise noncontradictory and lie in different clauses, hence form a k-clique. Conversely a k-clique contains one occurrence from each clause, since no same-clause vertices are adjacent. Its literals are consistent and extend to a full satisfying assignment. Nothing in this argument constructs a chain-level retraction or proves a nonzero cycle survives. For a counterexample to automatic injectivity, map every vertex of a simplicial circle to one vertex. This is a valid simplicial map and induces the zero map H₁(S¹)→H₁(point)=0. An injective vertex map also would not suffice if the target fills a cycle with new two-simplices.

## Worked example: setup

Map the boundary of a triangle into the filled triangle by inclusion.

## Worked example: calculation

The source has H₁≅F and the target H₁=0, so even an inclusion of simplicial complexes can kill homology. A genuine homology embedding needs a separate argument such as a chain retraction or a suitable relative-homology criterion.

## Worked example: conclusion

The decision reduction remains valid; the claimed obstruction hierarchy requires substantially more than that equivalence.

## Limits

NoP-versus-NP separation or no-reduction theorem follows. Historical PHP Betti numbers are not adopted without their actual complex and boundary matrices.

## Extension

Build the proposed chain maps explicitly and either construct a left inverse up to homotopy or exhibit a cycle that is killed.
