## Revised question retained in the annual source

What is a correctly typed small spectral-flow calculation, and which form-degree mismatch must be repaired?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered formula integrates a conventional even Chern-character component over an odd-dimensional sphere. Such an expression needs a specified odd character or transgression. A finite path and a unitary loop give exact examples of the two different constructions.

## Definitions

For a Hermitian path with invertible endpoints and transversal zero crossings, spectral flow counts negative-to-positive crossings minus positive-to-negative crossings.

The conventional curvature characterch_n(F) is a2n-form. For a unitary loopU:S¹→U1, the odd degree-one form is(2πi)⁻¹U⁻¹dU.

## Result name

Two exact integer calculations

## Result statement

ForD(t)=diag(t−1/4,t−3/4),0≤t≤1, spectral flow is2. ForU(t)=e^(2πit), the odd-character integral is1.

## Proof

The first diagonal eigenvalue crosses zero att=1/4 and the second att=3/4. Each derivative is+1, so both crossings are positive and the total is2. Endpoints have no zero eigenvalue. For the loop,dU=2πiUdt, hence(2πi)⁻¹U⁻¹dU=dt and its integral over one parameter period is1. This integral is well-typed because its degree equals the dimension. A2n-form cannot be integrated as a top-degree form overS^(2n+1) to yield the claimed invariant without additional data. In infinite-dimensional Fredholm families, spectral-flow/index and odd-Chern formulas are genuine theorems with hypotheses, not consequences of matching notation.
