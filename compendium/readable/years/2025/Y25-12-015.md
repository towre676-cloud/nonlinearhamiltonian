## Revised question retained in the annual source

What exact optimization problem underlies a tube-aligned proximal step?

## Status

Classical or newly chosen explicit model developed with proof; historical conjectures delimited

## Summary

The recovered optimizer narrative combined a Mahalanobis geometry, spectral tubes, a hostileℓ¹term and delayed commitment. A finite convex update is well-defined and solvable; a low-dimensional projection can also erase the entire useful gradient. Both facts can be proved without claiming an optimizer improvement.

## Definitions

Let M be symmetric positive definite. Considerargmin_d{g^Td+(1/(2η))d^TMd+λ||d||₁}.

Let P be an orthogonal projector onto a proposed retained subspace.

## Result name

Exact diagonal proximal solution and projection obstruction

## Result statement

If M=diag(m_i), the minimizer isd_i=−(η/m_i)sign(g_i)max(|g_i|−λ,0). If a gradient lies inker P, a projected update Pg is zero even when the objective is not stationary.

## Proof

The diagonal objective separates by coordinate. On a positive or negative half-line, differentiation givesg_i+(m_i/η)d_i+λ sign(d_i)=0; checking the subgradient interval atzero yields the soft-threshold formula. Strict convexity from M>0makes the minimizer unique. For projection, if Pg=0then the projected iteration takes no descent direction althoughgmay be nonzero. For example the quadratic objectivef(x, y)=(x−1)²/2+(y−1)²/2at(1,0)has gradient(0,−1). Projecting onto thex-axis kills it, so the constrained point is stationary only within the chosen tube, not in the ambient problem.

## Worked example: setup

Take M=diag(1,4), η=1, g=(3,1), λ=2.

## Worked example: calculation

The update isd=(−1,0). The second coordinate is suppressed by thresholding, not by a proof that it is irrelevant. In the projection example a spectral gap or low turnover of the retained subspace cannot detect the losty-direction:the projector may remain perfectly stable while optimization stalls.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite update has an exact solution, and premature tube commitment has a concrete mathematical failure mode.

## Limits

No theorem says that observed gap, spike, turnover or effective rank predicts a useful invariant optimization subspace. The reported negative-control failure is historical experimental evidence, not a universal result.

## Extension

Prove a bound on the discarded gradient||(I−P)∇f|| and include it in the descent estimate before replacing an ambient optimizer by its projected version.
