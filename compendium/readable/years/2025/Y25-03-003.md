## Summary

## Result statement

OverQ, degree-one homology of this filtration is zero before1, one-dimensional for1≤t<2, and zero from2 onward. Its single interval is[1,2).

## Proof

The vertex–edge boundary matrix has columns(−1,1,0),(0,−1,1),(1,0,−1). Its rank is2 and its kernel is spanned byc=(1,1,1). The face boundary is exactlyc, so∂1∂2=0. Before edges appear,C1=0. With all edges and no face,H1=ker∂1 is one-dimensional. When the face appears, its boundary fills that kernel, makingH1=0. Each inclusion sends the cycle to the same chain until it becomes a boundary, proving the stated persistence rather than merely counting dimensions. Cohomology gives the dual vector-space result overQ, but uses transposed coboundaries and reversed arrows.

## Limits

A smooth surrogate for a rank or argmax need not preserve this barcode. Changing adjacency or thresholding can alter the complex and requires its own comparison theorem.

## Extension

Publish the actual filtration rule and certify cell-time errors. Then prove an interleaving before claiming that a compressed or learned approximation preserves homology.
