## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a modular symbol encode, and which relations can be proved before invoking a modular-form computation?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The recovered request sought direct mathematics on modular symbols. The degree-zero cusp-divisor presentation supplies an exact starting point. It explains the triangle relation and the role of a base cusp, while keeping numerical integration and congruence-group equivariance as further requirements.

## Definitions

For S=P¹(Q), let Δ₀=ker[Z[S]→Z], where the map sums coefficients. Let M be generated by {a,b}, with {a,a}=0 and {a,b}+{b,c}={a,c}. A weight-k modular symbol additionally uses a declared group action and coefficient module V_{k−2}.

## Result name

Triangle presentation of cusp divisors

## Result statement

The map {a,b}↦[b]−[a] identifies M with Δ₀. In particular {a,b}=−{b,a}, and every symbol is a difference of symbols based at one cusp.

## Proof

The relations map to zero by telescoping. Fix o∈S and send a degree-zero divisor Σn_a[a] to Σn_a{o,a}. The triangle relation gives {a,b}={o,b}−{o,a}, so this is inverse on each generator. Conversely Σn_a([a]−[o])=Σn_a[a] because Σn_a=0. Thus both injectivity and surjectivity are proved. Equivariance can subsequently be imposed relative to a specified congruence subgroup; it does not follow from arbitrary cusp labels or a randomly populated matrix. Integration of an actual differential must likewise respect paths and convergence at cusps.

## Worked example: setup

Use o=∞ and cusps0,1,∞.

## Worked example: calculation

{∞,0}+{0,1}+{1,∞}=0 maps to([0]−[∞])+([1]−[0])+([∞]−[1])=0. Therefore {0,1}={∞,1}−{∞,0}. This exact relation is checkable without numerical periods.

## Worked example: conclusion

The algebra identifies a genuine presentation rather than a theorem-shaped software record. A valid implementation must state which further quotient, coefficient representation and Hecke operators it computes.

## Limits

This does not compute a cusp-form space or period lattice. A formal triangle relation alone does not certify the mocked modular-symbol routines mentioned in the historical code.
