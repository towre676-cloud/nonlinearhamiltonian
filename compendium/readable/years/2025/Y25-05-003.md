## Result statement

## Proof

The fourth-power equality and gcd are finite integer certificates, checked without rounding by the verifier. For the parity statement, every fourth power is zero or one modulo sixteen. Thus the number of odd summands on the left, an integer from zero through three, must equal zero or one modulo sixteen according to the parity of d. It is therefore zero or one exactly. If all three left entries were even, d would also be even, violating primitivity. Exactly one must be odd and d must be odd. For the totient, factor 1680=2⁴·3·5·7 and multiply 1680(1/2)(2/3)(4/5)(6/7)=384. The angle π/840 is indeed 2π/1680, compatible with a primitive 1680th root. These correct cyclotomic facts do not create a logical implication about the quartic equation; an additional theorem would have to state and prove that bridge, and the explicit counterexample prevents the proposed exclusion.
