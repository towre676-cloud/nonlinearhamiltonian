## Revised question retained in the annual source

When does the recovered weighted-translation matrix have a basis-invariant rank, and what capacity bound follows?

## Status

Classical or newly chosen explicit model developed with proof; historical conjectures delimited

## Summary

The recovered RG framework usedt_i=μ_i⊙ΔΦ_iandrank Tas an intrinsic memory count. Ordinary rank is invariant under invertible matrix changes, but a componentwise product is coordinate-dependent unless its factors have a compatible transformation law. Sparsity identifiability requires still more information.

## Definitions

Let Tbe a matrix whose columns aret_i. Rank is the dimension of their linear span.

A linear observation map J=BT factors through that span and therefore hasrank J≤rank T.

## Result name

Exact rank invariance and a Hadamard counterexample

## Result statement

rank(LTR)=rank Tfor invertible L, R. But transforming both μ and ΔΦ by the same invertible Ldoes not generally transform μ⊙ΔΦ by L, and can change its rank.

## Proof

Invertible multiplication preserves the dimensions of the image and kernel, provingrankinvariance for LTR. Image(BT)⊆B(image T)gives the rank bound. For the counterexample, take one column with μ=(1,0), ΔΦ=(0,1), so the Hadamard product is zeroandrank T=0. Use L=[[1,1],[1,−1]], which is invertible. The transformed factors are(1,1)and(1,−1), whose Hadamard product is(1,−1), so the newrankis one. Thus a claimed scheme-invariant UV count needs a tensor or coordinate rule beyond applying the same matrix to both factors. Also rankralone does not ensure unique recovery ofr-sparse coefficients:the1×2matrix[1,1]hasrank onebut sends both distinctone-sparse vectorse₁, e₂to the same datum.

## Worked example: calculation

T=diag(1,10^(−13))has exactrank two, but a relative threshold10^(−12)reportsone. Under a perturbation of norm ε, the singular values can shift by at most ε; stable numericalrankrequires an explicit gap around the threshold, not merely a chosen tolerance.
