## Revised question retained in the annual source

When can repeated refinement be replaced by a single orthogonal spectral projection?

## Status

Classical or newly chosen explicit model developed with proof; historical conjectures delimited

## Summary

The recovered IMP narrative identified a slow singular subspace with a subdivision limit. A symmetric contraction has exactly such a limit, but general singular vectors do not determine eigenvalue dynamics. A rotation and a nonnormal stochastic matrix show why the assumptions matter.

## Definitions

Let Mbe a real symmetric matrix with all eigenvalues in[0,1]. Let Pproject onto its1-eigenspace.

A singular-value decomposition describes one-step norm amplification; it is not generally an eigendecomposition.

## Result name

Exact contraction limit and a counterexample to an unrestricted SVD rule

## Result statement

If every other eigenvalue is at most1−γ with γ>0, then||M^n−P||≤(1−γ)^n. For a general contraction, theunit-singular subspace can contain rotating directions and need not be the fixed space.

## Proof

The spectral theorem writes M=Σλ_j P_jwith orthogonal projections. Powers give M^n−P=Σ_(λ_j<1)λ_j^n P_j, whose operator norm is the largest surviving|λ_j|^n, proving the estimate. For a planar rotation R_θ with θ not a multiple of2π, both singular values areone and the retained unit-singular subspace is the whole plane, yet R_θ^ngenerally fails to converge and its fixed subspace may bezero. Nonnormal convergence can also yield an oblique projector:the row-stochastic matrix T=[[1,0],[a,1−a]],0<a<1, has T^n=[[1,0],[1−(1−a)^n,(1−a)^n]], so its limit is[[1,0],[1,0]], which is idempotent but not symmetric.

## Worked example: setup

Take M=diag(1,1/2,1/4)and compare Tata=1/2.

## Worked example: calculation

M^nconverges todiag(1,0,0)with exact error2^(−n). T^nconverges to an oblique projection, whose action sends(x, y)to(x, x). Neither the singular vectors nor an orthogonal projector chosen from a one-step tolerance alone identifies that latter limit.

## Worked example: conclusion

A one-step replacement can be rigorously justified in the stated symmetric model, and its approximation error is explicit.

## Limits

This proves no universal equality between an IMP/SVD construction and a subdivision limit surface, transformer, quantum channel or diffusion sampler. Each operator requires its own invariant subspaces and peripheral-spectrum analysis.

## Extension

For a specific refinement matrix, prove eigenvalueoneis semisimple and all other eigenvalues lie strictly inside the unit disk, then compute its possibly oblique spectral projector exactly.
