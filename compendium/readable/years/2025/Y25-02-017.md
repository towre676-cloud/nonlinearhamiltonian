## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a heat trace measure in a finite model, and which analytic conditions are needed before a proposed operator is a spectral triple?

## Status

## Summary

A four-cycle gives an exact scale-dependent heat dimension. A separate diagonal model shows that compact resolvent and finite heat trace do not imply finite-power zeta summability.

## Definitions

For the four-cycle graph Laplacian eigenvalues are0,2,2,4. SetP(t)=Tr(e^{−tL})/4 andd_s(t)=−2dlogP/dlogt.

For a diagonal positive operatorD with eigenvaluen of multiplicityd_n=ceil(e^{√n}), each eigenspace is finite dimensional.

A spectral-triple claim needs a represented algebra, a self-adjointD, compact resolvent and bounded commutators; its summability properties require additional estimates.

## Result name

Exact heat dimension and a summability distinction

## Result statement

For the cycle,P(t)=(1+e^{−2t})²/4 andd_s(t)=8t/(e^{2t}+1), tending to zero at both ends. The diagonalD has compact resolvent andTr(e^{−tD})<∞ for everyt>0, butTr(D^{−p})=∞ for every finitep>0.

## Proof

Sum the four heat eigenvalues and differentiate the logarithm to obtain the first formula. At smallt the numerator tends to zero; at larget exponential growth in the denominator dominates. ForD, eigenvalues tend to infinity with finite multiplicity, so the resolvent eigenvalues tend to zero and finite-rank truncations converge in norm. For fixedt>0,√n≤tn/2 for all sufficiently largen, givingd_ne^{−tn}≤2e^{−tn/2}, a convergent tail. Butd_n n^{−p} does not even tend to zero, so the power trace diverges. This example distinguishesθ-summability from finitep-summability without requiring a conjectural Monster spectrum.

## Worked example: calculation

It equals4/(e+1), approximately1.07577, while its large-time limit is zero because the constant zero mode dominates. A single reported dimension value therefore needs its scale, trace normalization and zero-mode convention.

## Worked example: conclusion

The result provides analytic tests for a proposed spectral construction instead of treating a named algebra and a formalD as sufficient.

## Limits

The model multiplicities are newly chosen and are not claimed to be the exact gradedMonster dimensions. A termlogL₀ is undefined on a zero eigenspace unless its domain or regularization is specified.

## Extension

For a supplied graded multiplicity sequence, prove asymptotic bounds strong enough to determine heat and zeta convergence. Then check commutator domains and the actual spectral-action expansion before assigning physical constants. For the finite graph, removing the constant mode changes the large-time behavior completely: the remaining normalized heat trace decays as e^{−2t}, so its logarithmic derivative grows linearly in t rather than tending to zero. Zero-mode subtraction is therefore a definition-changing choice, not a harmless numerical cleanup.
