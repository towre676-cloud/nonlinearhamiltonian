## Summary

The recovered nesting places a holonomy inside another exponential without stating a new connection or its gauge transformation. A graph holonomy is an exact model: edge elements multiply along a loop, and the trace of that word is gauge invariant.

## Result name

Gauge covariance and an explicit noncommutative loop

## Result statement

A based loop product transforms by conjugation at its basepoint, so its trace is invariant. ForU=iσx,V=iσz, the commutator is−I with trace−2, whereas commutingU,V giveI with trace2.

## Proof

Multiply the transformed edge elements in loop order: every adjacentgj⁻¹gj cancels and the endpoints leaveg_base Wg_base⁻¹. Cyclicity of trace proves invariance. In the Pauli exampleσxσz=−σzσx and bothU,V square to−I; thusUV=−VU. Multiplying this relation byU⁻¹V⁻¹ givesUVU⁻¹V⁻¹=−I. This is a genuine nested loop word: the topology specifies concatenation and reversal, and the group law specifies multiplication. It does not require treating a group-valued holonomy as a Lie-algebra-valued1-form under an undefined outer integral.
