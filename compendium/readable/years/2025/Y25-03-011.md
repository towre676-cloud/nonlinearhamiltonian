## Result statement

The operationsm_n are symmetric and compatible with substitutions. In the filtered complex the associated graded differential is zero, but the next filtration-dropping differential takesv tow and kills both classes; the total complex is acyclic.

## Proof

For the operations, any iterated product obtained by substitutingm_r into one slot ofm_n has exactly the same ordered factors as the single productm_(n+r−1); associativity proves equality, and commutativity removes order dependence. A strict commutative algebra can be viewed through a chosen E∞-operad augmentation to the commutative operad, but a genuinely homotopy-coherent construction must state that operad and its maps. For the complex,d²=0 because there is no degree2 space. On each fixed filtration grade,d has zero same-grade component, so the first graded page has the two classes. The actual differential lowers filtration by1 and induces the next arrow v↦w, an isomorphism. Directly,ker(d:C⁰→C¹)=0 andcokerd=0, confirming acyclicity.

## Worked example: conclusion

Operations, coherence maps and differentials are decisive data. Naming a spectral-sequence target does not supply them.

## Limits

The example does not prove the recovered motivic-to-K-theory spectral sequence for an unspecified derived object. Its existence and indexing require hypotheses that were not recovered.

## Extension
