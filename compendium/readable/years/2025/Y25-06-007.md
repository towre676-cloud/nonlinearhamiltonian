## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What exact finite theorem models Grothendieck Galois reconstruction, and which naturality data replace a bare claim of modal verification?

## Status

Developed mathematical repair or classical result; speculative source claims delimited

## Summary

Grothendieck’s Galois theory is a substantial theorem about categories and fibre functors. A finite group version admits a short complete proof and identifies the essential naturality requirement. It does not assert that the historical modal implementation exists.

## Definitions

Let G be a finite group, FinGSet its category of finite left G-sets and equivariant maps, and F its forgetful functor to finite sets.

A natural automorphismη of F is a family of bijectionsη_X:F(X)→F(X) commuting with every equivariant map. Its components are not required to be equivariant themselves.

## Result name

Aut(F) is canonically G

## Result statement

The map a∈G↦[η_X(x)=ax] is a group isomorphism G→Aut(F). Natural automorphisms are determined by their action on the identity element of the regular G-set.

## Proof

For any a, equivariance of a map f gives f(ax)=af(x), so the family is natural; the inverse family uses a^(−1). Conversely letη be natural and let a=η_G(1), where G carries its left regular action. For any G-set X and x∈X, the map f_x:G→X, g↦gx, is equivariant. Naturality givesη_X(x)=η_X(f_x(1))=f_x(η_G(1))=ax. Thus every natural family has the displayed form and its a is unique. Composition of families corresponds to multiplication ab, proving the group law. The proof uses all these maps f_x; isolated orbit sizes or a single matrix do not supply the reconstruction.

## Worked example: setup

Take G=C₃ acting regularly on{0,1,2} by cyclic addition.

## Worked example: calculation

The three possible natural families restrict to the three cyclic shifts on the regular set. A transposition fails to commute with its equivariant right translations and therefore cannot be a natural automorphism. The verifier enumerates all six permutations and retains exactly three.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite model shows what is reconstructed and why categorical compatibility is mathematical data rather than a label.

## Limits

The scheme-theoretic theorem needs a Galois category, a geometric fibre functor and a profinite group with continuity. No universal reconstruction of arbitrary schemes from an unspecified modality is claimed.

## Extension

For a finite field, identify finite étale covers with finite sets carrying Frobenius permutation. Recover orbit lengths and extension degrees, then pass to the profinite limit only after verifying the relevant categorical hypotheses.
