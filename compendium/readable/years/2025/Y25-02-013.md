## Revised question retained in the annual source

What finite example demonstrates the actual data required by a categorical equivalence and induction compatibility?

## Status

## Summary

Matrix Morita equivalence and a finite Frobenius-reciprocity calculation supply complete constructive models. They illustrate categorical language without asserting the recovered broad FLE formula under missing level and support hypotheses.

## Definitions

LetA=M₂(k),e=E₁₁. For a leftA-moduleM defineF(M)=eM; for a vector spaceV defineG(V)=k²⊗V withA acting onk².

For finite groupsH⊂G, induction isk[G]⊗_{k[H]}V and restriction forgets part of the group action.

A claimed equivalence needs functors and natural isomorphismsFG≅Id,GF≅Id, not only a pair of category names.

## Result name

Explicit Morita inverse and induction adjunction

## Result statement

F,G are inverse equivalences. AlsoHom_G(Ind_H^G V,W)≅Hom_H(V,Res W), naturally. ForG=S₃,H=A₃,V=1, the induced representation is1⊕sgn.

## Proof

The mapk²⊗eM→M sendsbasis vector e_i⊗m toE_{i1}m. Its inverse sendsm toΣ_i e_i⊗E_{1i}m. The matrix-unit identities show both composites are identities and that the maps respectA actions. On the other side,e(k²⊗V) identifies directly withV. For Frobenius reciprocity, send aG-linear mapT to theH-linear mapv↦T(1⊗v). Conversely anH-linear mapf definesT(g⊗v)=gf(v); the tensor relation makes this well defined. For the exampleG/H has two cosets, and its permutation representation splits into the constant and alternating lines, giving1⊕sgn.

## Worked example: calculation

Their images arek andk², and the inverse tensor construction restores them. The functor preserves multiplicity but changes the presentation of the algebra. The two-coset induced example verifies dimensions and restriction compatibility exactly.

## Worked example: conclusion

The mathematical content is now a pair of explicit equivalence/adjunction proofs rather than an unrestricted categorical slogan.

## Limits

This does not prove a critical-level Kac–Moody/oper equivalence. Fundamental local equivalences have precise groups, levels and categories; the recovered formula suppresses data essential in primary statements.

## Extension

For a proposed geometric correspondence, write its actual adjoint functors, units and counits and specify compactness/support conditions. Test their compositions on stated generators before claiming an equivalence of whole categories. The explicit inverse maps also preserve all morphisms, not only dimensions. A linear map eM→eN induces the A-linear map reconstructed by the matrix units, and restricting that reconstruction returns the original map. This full-faithfulness check is essential: two categories can have matching object counts while having different morphism spaces.
