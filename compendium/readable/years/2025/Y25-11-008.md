## Summary

The recovered Lamé equation has a precise classical meaning independent of the speculative RH visualization. Takingn=1 yields three exact elliptic eigenfunctions and a factorization that proves a ground-state bound on a stated periodic domain.

## Definitions

Let0<k<1, m=k², and K=K(k). Define H=−d²/dx²+2m sn²(x, k) on4K-periodic H²functions.

Jacobi identities aresn′=cn dn, cn′=−sn dn, dn′=−m sn cn, cn²+sn²=1, dn²+m sn²=1.

## Result name

Three exact eigenfunctions and positivity

## Result statement

Hdn=m dn, Hcn=cn, Hsn=(1+m)sn. Since dn is positive on the real axis, H−m=A*A≥0 with A=d/dx−dn′/dn, so the lowest periodic eigenvalue ism.

## Proof

Differentiate the Jacobi derivative relations. They givesn″=−(1+m)sn+2m sn³, cn″=(2m sn²−1)cn, anddn″=(2m sn²−m)dn. Substitution into Hproves the three identities. Letw=dn′/dn, a smooth real periodic function because dn≥√(1−m)>0. The adjoint of A=∂−w on the periodic domain is−∂−w, and A*A=−∂²+w′+w²=−∂²+dn″/dn=H−m. Hence⟨f,(H−m)f⟩=||Af||²≥0. Since Adn=0, the lower bound is attained. The functionsdn, cn, sn are all4K-periodic; dn has the shorter2Kperiod whilecn andsn change sign after2K. This fixes an otherwise consequential periodic-versus-antiperiodic convention.

## Worked example: calculation

The energies are1/2,1,3/2. The factorization excludes any lower4K-periodic eigenvalue. In the limitm→0, the potential tends tozero, sn→sin, cn→cos, dn→1, and the two excited levels merge at1, matching the free periodic operator after K→π/2.

## Limits

The three eigenfunctions alone are not a proof of every finite-gap theorem. No Lamé-to-zeta-zero map or counterfactual “off-critical catastrophe” establishes RH.
