## Summary

The August12–13 assistant asserted literal equivalence across four domains. A finite cochain complex gives a valid obstruction test, while an explicit counterexample shows why arbitrary adapters do not induce isomorphisms. The historical random surrogate matrices are not adopted as geometric data.

## Definitions

A cochain complex has C⁰→δ⁰ C¹→δ¹ C² with δ¹δ⁰=0. Its H¹ is kerδ¹/imδ⁰. A cochain map F satisfies Fδ=δF. A cochain homotopy equivalence has an inverse up to maps of the form δh+hδ.

## Result name

Homotopy equivalence preserves obstruction classes

## Result statement

A cochain homotopy equivalence induces an isomorphism on H¹. An arbitrary cochain map, even between three-patch constructions, need not be injective or surjective.

## Proof

A cochain map sends cocycles to cocycles and coboundaries to coboundaries, hence induces cohomology maps. If GF−I=δh+hδ, then on a cocycle z the difference GFz−z=δhz is a coboundary. Thus the induced composite is the identity; the other homotopy proves the inverse composite. Without these hypotheses the zero map between identical nontrivial complexes is already a counterexample to injectivity. Cover preservation and finite-limit preservation by themselves are not a substitute for the specific descent and comparison hypotheses of a sheaf-cohomology theorem.
