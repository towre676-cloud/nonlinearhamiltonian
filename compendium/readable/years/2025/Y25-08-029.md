## Summary

## Definitions

For n≥2 let1∈R^n be the all-ones vector and S(t)=I+t11ᵀ. Put P=11ᵀ/n, the orthogonal projection onto the constant line. Then S=(I−P)+(1+nt)P.

## Result name

Spectrum, inverse and positivity

## Result statement

S has eigenvalue1+nt on the constant line and1 on its orthogonal complement. detS=1+nt; for t≠−1/n, S⁻¹=I−[t/(1+nt)]11ᵀ. It is positive definite exactly when t>−1/n.

## Proof

Since1ᵀ1=n,P²=P and P*=P. The direct-sum expression gives the eigenvalues and determinant immediately. Invert separately on the two orthogonal subspaces to obtain(I−P)+(1+nt)⁻¹P; expanding P gives the displayed rank-one inverse. Positive definiteness is equivalent to positivity of both scalar eigenvalues. The spectral norm is max(1,|1+nt|), and the condition number follows from the ratio of largest to smallest singular values. No sheaf or cohomology assumption is used anywhere. The source decimals α=0.0333996828 and β=0.6732050807568877 are rational numbers when taken literally as finite strings.
