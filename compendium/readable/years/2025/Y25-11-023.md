## Revised question retained in the annual source

How can a decaying curvature block be destabilized by harmonic forcing, and what does a Schur complement actually certify?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered program described unstable transport feeding a contracting curvature sector and proposed flattening the off-diagonal blocks. Both mechanisms have exact elementary models. Their consequences depend on the actual blocks, not on labeling them cohomological.

## Definitions

Consider κ_(n+1)=a κ_n+c α_n and α_(n+1)=d α_n.

For a real symmetric block matrix S=[[A, B],[B^T, D]], assume Dpositive definite.

## Result name

Exact forcing law and positive Schur criterion

## Result statement

Ifa≠d, κ_n=a^n κ₀+c(d^n−a^n)α₀/(d−a). Ifa=d, the forcing term iscn a^(n−1)α₀. Also S≥0if and only if A−BD^(−1)B^T≥0.

## Proof

Iterating the scalar recurrence gives κ_n=a^n κ₀+c Σ_{j=0}^{n−1}a^(n−1−j)d^j α₀. The finite geometric sum is(d^n−a^n)/(d−a); when the two values coincide allnterms equal a^(n−1). Thus|a|<1alone does not guarantee curvature decay if|d|>1andc α₀≠0. For the Schur statement, complete the quadratic form:x^TAx+2x^TBy+y^TDy=x^T(A−BD^(−1)B^T)x+(y+D^(−1)B^Tx)^TD(y+D^(−1)B^Tx). Positivity of the two right terms proves sufficiency. Choosingy=−D^(−1)B^Txproves necessity. This is an energy statement for a symmetric matrix, distinct from the evolution eigenvalues of an arbitrary block update.

## Worked example: setup

Takea=1/2, d=2, c=1, κ₀=0, α₀=1. Separately take A=2, B=1, D=1.

## Worked example: calculation

κ_n=(2^n−2^(−n))/(3/2), which grows despite the contractinga. Settingc=0removes forcing and restores κ_n=(1/2)^n κ₀. The symmetric matrix[[2,1],[1,1]]has Schur complement1and is positive definite; increasing the coupling to B=2makes the Schur complement−2and destroys positivity.

## Worked example: conclusion

The recovered stability mechanism has an exact solution and a sharp matrix criterion that can guide a real block analysis.

## Limits

Flattening a block can change the intended geometry or dynamics; no universal optimizer speedup follows. The historical curvature eigenvalues and claimed ML gains were not independently supplied by stencils or experiments.

## Extension
