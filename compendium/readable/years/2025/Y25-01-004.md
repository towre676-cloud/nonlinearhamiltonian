## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does returning to the starting point establish, and when can a smooth closed path be replaced by a polygon without changing its winding?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

A loop is a continuous map with equal endpoints; it need not be polygonal or noncontractible. A uniformly close polygonal approximation preserves winding whenever the straight-line interpolation avoids the reference point.

## Definitions

Letγ:[0,1]→C∖{0} be a closed piecewise smooth curve andd=min_t|γ(t)|>0.

Letp be a closed polygonal path withsup_t|p(t)−γ(t)|<d. Winding about zero is the integer degree ofγ/|γ|, or its logarithmic-derivative integral when smooth enough.

A polygon additionally consists of finitely many straight segments. Closure alone supplies neither this regularity nor a nonzero degree.

## Result name

A quantitative polygonal replacement theorem

## Result statement

The interpolationH(s,t)=(1−s)γ(t)+sp(t) is a homotopy of closed curves avoiding zero, soγ andp have equal winding. Every continuous nonvanishing loop admits such a polygonal approximation.

## Proof

The reverse triangle inequality gives|H(s,t)|≥|γ(t)|−s|p(t)−γ(t)|>d−d=0. Endpoints agree for every intermediate curve becauseγ andp are both closed. Homotopy invariance of degree gives equal winding. For existence, uniform continuity provides a partition fine enough thatγ changes by less thanε<d/2 on each subinterval. Linear interpolation between successive sample values then stays within2ε ofγ there; choosing a smallerε gives the required strict bound. This proof explains why polygonal approximation preserves a topological invariant only with a separation margin from the excluded point.

## Worked example: setup

Compare the unit circle, the constant loopγ≡1 and the triangle joining the cube roots of unity.

## Worked example: calculation

The circle and triangle each wind once around zero; the constant loop has winding zero although it returns to its start. Refining equal-angle inscribed polygons of the circle makes the uniform interpolation error tend to zero. The strict distance condition prevents an approximating edge from cutting through zero.

## Worked example: conclusion

Strings, loops and polygons are now distinguished by precise properties rather than by an assumed progression from closure to a particular shape.

## Limits

Winding is relative to a chosen excluded point. Self-intersections are permitted, and a loop of winding zero need not have a simple image. A path through zero is outside this invariant’s domain.

## Extension

For a sampled curve with a known Lipschitz constantL and meshh, bound interpolation error byLh and compare it with a certified distance lower boundd. This produces a finite sampling condition for preserving winding under approximation.
