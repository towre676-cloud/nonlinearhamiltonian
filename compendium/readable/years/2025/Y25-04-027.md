## Revised question retained in the annual source

What fully solved oscillator model can demonstrate growth from periodic parameter variation?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered resonance condition is not a proof of an instability band. A piecewise-constant oscillator has an exact monodromy matrix, letting symplecticity and exponential growth be checked without an uncontrolled averaging approximation.

## Definitions

Forq″+ω²q=0, the state(q,p) withp=q′ evolves byS_ω(t)=[[cosωt,sinωt/ω],[−ωsinωt,cosωt]].

Alternate frequenciesω1,ω2>0 for quarter periodsT1=π/(2ω1),T2=π/(2ω2), repeating the cycle.

## Result name

Exact Floquet multipliers

## Result statement

The one-cycle matrixS_(ω2)(T2)S_(ω1)(T1)=diag(−ω1/ω2,−ω2/ω1). Its determinant is1 and one multiplier has modulus>1 wheneverω1≠ω2.

## Proof

Solve the constant-frequency equation with its two initial conditions to obtainS_ω. At a quarter period, it becomes[[0,1/ω],[−ω,0]]. Multiply the two matrices in chronological order to get the diagonal expression. Their reciprocal multipliers prove symplectic area preservation and, if the frequencies differ, exponential growth along one state axis under repeated cycles. The other axis contracts. This supplies an exact instability example without claiming that all periodically driven oscillators or all frequencies near a symbolic2ω condition behave identically.

## Worked example: setup

Takeω1=1,ω2=2.

## Worked example: calculation

The cycle duration is3π/4 and the monodromy isdiag(−1/2,−2). Starting at(q,p)=(0,1), aftern cycles the state is(0,(−2)^n). The exponential growth rate per unit time islog2/(3π/4)=4log2/(3π).

## Worked example: conclusion

A defined periodic system has a proven Floquet instability and an exact rate.

## Limits

The coefficient jumps require an external parameter change; this is not a closed-system energy-conservation claim. It does not prove a quantum particle-production rate or validate a recoveredDCE apparatus.

## Extension

Replace the jumps with a specified smoothperiodic modulation and enclose the monodromy trace to certify stable and unstable parameter bands.
