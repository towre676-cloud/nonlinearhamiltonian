## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does the literal avoidance complex encode, and is the claimed universal2-SAT topology correct?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The early July project proposed a homological complexity hierarchy. Its simplicial construction can be made coherent, but its asserted topological classification needs independent proof. Even the empty2-CNF formula provides a decisive counterexample to universal contractibility.

## Definitions

For n Boolean variables use vertices x_i,¬x_i. A face contains no opposite pair and no set consisting of all negated literals of a clause. A full face chooses exactly one literal per variable; it represents the corresponding truth assignment. Reduced homology uses an explicitly chosen coefficient field.

## Result name

Satisfying facets and a counterexample

## Result statement

Full(n−1)-dimensional faces correspond exactly to satisfying assignments. With no clauses, the complex is the join of n two-point sets, the boundary of the n-dimensional crosspolytope, homeomorphic to S^(n−1). It is not contractible for n≥1.

## Proof

A full face satisfies a clause precisely when it does not choose all the clause’s negations. Applying that test to every clause proves the assignment correspondence. With no clauses, only opposite-pair exclusions remain. Each variable contributes a choice of either endpoint of a0-sphere, and joining these choices gives the crosspolytope boundary. Its reduced homology in degree n−1 is one-dimensional. The formula with no clauses is a valid2-CNF formula and is trivially satisfiable, yet has nontrivial topology. Therefore neither satisfiability nor ease of decision implies contractibility for this construction. An entropy or Betti profile may still be studied as an observable, but cannot inherit a complexity theorem from its name.

Use two variables and no clauses.

## Worked example: calculation

The vertices x,¬x,y,¬y form a four-cycle with edges joining choices from different variables. It has four vertices,four edges and no triangles, so β₁=1. Adding suitable clauses can delete faces and change the topology without establishing a monotone decision-complexity scale.

## Worked example: conclusion

The construction survives, while the universal2-SAT/HCH classification fails for an explicit simplest input.

## Limits

This does not settle any relation between topology and worst-case complexity. A filtration and an allowed reduction class must be defined before persistence or hierarchy claims are meaningful.

## Extension

Fix a functorial construction and test which reductions actually preserve or reflect the chosen homology; state resulting implications as theorems or explicit conjectures.
