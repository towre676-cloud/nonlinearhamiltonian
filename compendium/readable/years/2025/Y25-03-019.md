## Result statement

For a lifted inner action, F(g,q)=(g u_q,q) is a group isomorphism SU4⋊_αQ8→SU4×Q8. Another valid action uses a homomorphismQ8→C2 followed by complex conjugation onSU4.

## Proof

For the first claim,F((g,q)(h,r))=(g u_q h u_q⁻¹u_(qr),qr)=(g u_q h u_r,qr), usingu_(qr)=u_qu_r. This equalsF(g,q)F(h,r) in the direct product. The inverse is(a,q)↦(a u_q⁻¹,q). To obtain a concreteu, use the standard faithful2-dimensional quaternion matrices inSU2 and embed them asdiag(u_q,I2) inSU4. For the second action, any nontrivial sign character ofQ8 factors through its abelianizationC2×C2. Complex conjugation has order2 and preservesSU4, so composing gives a homomorphism into automorphisms. It acts on the central elementiI4 by inversion, whereas every inner action fixes that center.
