## Revised question retained in the annual source

What information is contained in a graded character, and how can character arithmetic be verified before attaching a physical interpretation?

## Status

## Summary

A graded trace separates dimension from the action of a symmetry. A smallS₃ representation gives a complete exact decomposition, while the familiar moonshine dimensions are retained as representation-theoretic data.

## Definitions

For a graded representationV=⊕V_n of a finite group, its character series isTr_V(gq^{L₀})=ΣTr(g|V_n)qⁿ, formally when every graded piece is finite.

Atg=1 the coefficients are dimensions. Away from the identity they are traces, which can differ even for representations of equal dimension.

TheS₃ classes are identity, transpositions and three-cycles, with sizes1,3,2. Its standard character is(2,0,−1).

## Proof

The permutation representation on three coordinates splits into the constant line and the plane with coordinate sum zero. Counting fixed coordinates gives its trace(3,1,0), so subtracting the constant line givesW character(2,0,−1). Tensor traces multiply, makingW⊗W character(4,0,1). Summing the characters of1,sgn andW gives(1,1,1)+(1,−1,1)+(2,0,−1)=(4,0,1). Finite-group character orthogonality, proved by averaging matrix coefficients, makes equality of characters equivalent to isomorphism overC. This is a genuine representation calculation; the moonshine dimension sums alone do not prove the existence of a vertex operator algebra or identify physical particle states.

## Worked example: calculation

The tensor traces are zero and one, despite the representation having dimension four. The decomposition reproduces both:1−1+0=0 and1+1−1=1. A count of conjugacy classes describes how many irreducible characters exist, not a count of energy levels, measured transitions or spatial directions.

## Worked example: conclusion

Graded characters provide exact constraints, with their mathematical and observational meanings kept separate.

## Limits

The Monster moonshine theorem and construction ofV^natural are deep established results, not proved by two integer equalities. The old links to biology, gravity and character-ratio constants are not inherited.

## Extension

Given a finite graded trace table, compute each irreducible multiplicity by the class-weighted character inner product. Nonintegral or negative multiplicities reject a proposed representation immediately; passing these tests still does not supply its multiplication or VOA axioms.
