## Summary

The user explicitly restricted this proposal to grounded hybrid control and optimization. For a prescribed integer mode, the action weight is a positive arithmetic scalar. It cancels from the interior Euler–Lagrange equation when constant, but it matters at a switch and in the globally optimal allocation of velocity.

## Definitions

Let n(t) be a prescribed piecewise constant positive integer with finitely many fixed switch times. Put w(t)=φ(n(t))/σ(n(t))>0.

For a continuous piecewise C² path q, use S[q]=∫w(t)L(q,qdot,t)dt with fixed endpoints. Variations are continuous at switches; no jump penalty is included.

## Result name

Interior equation and weighted momentum continuity

## Result statement

On each constant-mode interval, d/dt(L_v)−L_q=0. At a fixed switch t₀, a stationary path must satisfy w_-L_v(q,qdot_-,t₀)=w_+L_v(q,qdot_+,t₀). Differentiating φ at an integer is neither needed nor defined by this model.

## Proof

Take the first variation ∫w(L_qδq+L_vδqdot)dt separately on every interval. Integration by parts gives the interior factor w[L_q−d(L_v)/dt], which vanishes because w is constant and positive. Boundary terms at a shared fixed switch combine as (w_-L_v^-−w_+L_v^+)δq(t₀). Arbitrary continuous variation there gives the momentum condition. For L=v²/2, the entire optimization also follows from Cauchy–Schwarz: (q(T)−q(0))²=(∫sqrt(w)v·w^(−1/2)dt)²≤(∫wv²dt)(∫w^(−1)dt). Equality requires wv constant, so S_min=(Δq)²/[2∫w^(−1)dt]. This is a global certificate for the stated convex problem.
