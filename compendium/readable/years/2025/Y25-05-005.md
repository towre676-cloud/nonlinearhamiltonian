## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: Which transfer-map and Krylov hypotheses make a coarse correction an energy projection rather than a claimed automatic convergence accelerator?

## Status

Developed mathematical repair or classical result; speculative source claims delimited

## Summary

A named interpolation or Krylov space does not by itself give a convergence theorem. For a positive definite operator the Galerkin construction does give a precise best-approximation property. This is the finite mathematical core shared by coarse-grid correction and projection onto a Krylov subspace.

## Definitions

Let A∈R^{n×n} be symmetric positive definite and P∈R^{n×r} have full column rank. Define A_c=PᵀAP and Π=P A_c^(−1)PᵀA.

Use the energy inner product ⟨x,y⟩_A=xᵀAy. A Krylov space K_m(A,v)=span(v,Av,…,A^(m−1)v) is a particular candidate subspace; choose independent columns before forming P.

## Result name

An exact energy projection and its limitation

## Result statement

A_c is positive definite, Π²=Π and Π is A-self-adjoint. The coarse correction e↦(I−Π)e satisfies ||(I−Π)e||_A=min_z||e−Pz||_A≤||e||_A. This alone need not contract errors in the A-orthogonal complement of range(P).

## Proof

For z≠0, zᵀA_cz=(Pz)ᵀA(Pz)>0 because P is injective. Multiplying the formula for Π by itself cancels PᵀAP=A_c and proves idempotence. Also AΠ=AP A_c^(−1)PᵀA is symmetric, which is precisely energy self-adjointness. The normal equation PᵀA(e−Pz)=0 has solution z=A_c^(−1)PᵀAe. Splitting e−Pz into its projected and orthogonal pieces proves the Pythagorean identity and the minimization claim. If PᵀAe=0 then Πe=0, so the same error is unchanged. A multigrid rate therefore requires an additional smoothing/approximation argument; transfer dimensions and a subspace name cannot supply it.

## Worked example: setup

Choose A=diag(1,4), P=(1,1)ᵀ and e=(1,0)ᵀ.

## Worked example: calculation

Then A_c=5, Π=[[1/5,4/5],[1/5,4/5]], and (I−Π)e=(4/5,−1/5). Its squared energy is 16/25+4/25=4/5, less than the original one. The vector (−4,1) is A-orthogonal to P and remains unchanged. The verifier checks idempotence, A-self-adjointness and both examples exactly with fractions.

## Worked example: conclusion

The useful guarantee is an energy best approximation with a clearly identified untouched complement.

## Limits

A periodic Laplacian has a constant kernel, so apply this positive-definite argument on its mean-zero subspace or fix a gauge first. Arbitrary transfer maps, singular coarse matrices or nonsymmetric operators require modified hypotheses.

## Extension

Combine this projection with a specified smoother S and prove ||(I−Π)S||_A≤q<1. A bound uniform over grid size would then be a genuine multilevel convergence theorem, stronger than a finite residual decrease.
