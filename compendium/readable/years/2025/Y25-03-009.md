## Revised question retained in the annual source

What finite algebraic model provides an actual gluing rule before higher-cobordism analogies are used?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered cobordism and fair-division notation does not specify objects, morphisms or a functor. A two-dimensional oriented surface theory has a classical algebraic model. Its elementary calculations expose the multiplication, pairing and sewing identities that a gluing claim needs.

## Definitions

LetA=Q[ε]/(ε²), withunit1 and linear traceτ(1)=0,τ(ε)=1.

The Frobenius pairing is〈a,b〉=τ(ab). Its nondegeneracy permits a coproduct adjoint to multiplication.

## Result name

A complete small Frobenius calculation

## Result statement

The pairing matrix is[[0,1],[1,0]]. The coproduct isΔ(1)=1⊗ε+ε⊗1,Δ(ε)=ε⊗ε; it is coassociative and satisfies the Frobenius compatibility.

## Proof

The displayed pairing has determinant−1, so it is nondegenerate. The basis dual to(1,ε) is(ε,1), givingΔ(1)=1⊗ε+ε⊗1. Multiplying either tensor factor byε givesΔ(ε)=ε⊗ε. Checking the two basis vectors, both(Δ⊗id)Δ and(id⊗Δ)Δ map1 to1⊗ε⊗ε+ε⊗1⊗ε+ε⊗ε⊗1 andε toε⊗ε⊗ε. The identityΔ(ab)=(a⊗1)Δ(b)=Δ(a)(1⊗b) follows by the same basis check. These are explicit sewing constraints. The theorem identifying commutative Frobenius algebras with oriented2D TQFTs is a cited structural result, not a consequence of assigningBord to an undefined model.
