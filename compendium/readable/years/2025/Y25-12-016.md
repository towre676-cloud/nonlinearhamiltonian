## Definitions

For a nonzero scale s, uniform nearest quantization is Q_s(x)=s round(x/s).

Layer normalization on R^m is LN_ε(x)=(x−mean(x)1)/√(m^(−1)||x−mean(x)1||²+ε), with ε>0.

## Result statement

Coordinatewise|Q_s(x_i)−x_i|≤s/2 without clipping. The Jacobian norm of LN_ε is at most1/√ε, so||LN_ε(x)−LN_ε(y)||≤||x−y||/√ε.

## Proof

Nearest-integer rounding changes a scalar by at mostone half, proving the first bound after multiplication bys. For LN, let P=I−11^T/m, z=Px, andv=||z||²/m. Its derivative is(1/√(v+ε))P−zz^T/[m(v+ε)^(3/2)]. On the centered subspace, directions perpendicular tozhave eigenvalue1/√(v+ε); thezdirection has eigenvalue ε/(v+ε)^(3/2). The constant direction has eigenvaluezero. All are nonnegative and at most1/√ε. Integrating the derivative along the segment betweenxandygives the Lipschitz bound. If ε=0, normalization is undefined for constant inputs and no global bound exists.
