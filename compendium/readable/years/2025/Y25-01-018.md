## Revised question retained in the annual source

What is the difference between a filtration, its associated graded object and a chosen direct-sum splitting?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

A two-step filtered vector space admits many splittings, none invariant under all filtration-preserving automorphisms. This elementary example identifies the extra choice suppressed by writing a weight filtration as a direct sum.

## Definitions

LetV=ke₁⊕ke₂,W₀=ke₁,W₁=W₀,W₂=V, with the evident zero and full extensions outside this range.

The associated graded isGr⁰_W=ke₁ andGr²_W=V/ke₁. A splitting selects a lift of each quotient vector and identifiesV with their direct sum.

A canonical splitting would have to be preserved by every automorphism of the given filtered object.

## Result name

No functorial splitting for this filtered object

## Result statement

Every complement ofW₀ has the formk(e₂+ce₁). The filtered automorphismsT_a(e₁)=e₁,T_a(e₂)=e₂+ae₁ act transitively on these complements. No complement is fixed by allT_a.

## Proof

A line complementary toke₁ has a vector with nonzeroe₂ coordinate; rescaling it gives e₂+ce₁, proving the classification. ApplyingT_a sends this line to the line with parameterc+a. Choosing any nonzeroa changes it, so no complement can be invariant under the whole automorphism group. EachT_a induces the identity on both graded quotients, showing that the associated graded forgets genuine extension automorphisms. The equality of graded dimensions withdimV therefore does not provide a distinguished decomposition. In mixed Hodge theory additional structures constrain the extensions, and certain splittings may exist after changing scalars or adding hypotheses; those theorems must be stated rather than replacingW_k by an unexplained Hodge-index sum.

## Worked example: setup

Compare the complementske₂ andk(e₂+3e₁).

## Worked example: calculation

Both project isomorphically toGr²_W and yield identical graded dimensions(1,1). T₃ exchanges the first with the second while acting trivially onGr_W. A numerical routine that returns one complement has selected coordinates, not found an invariant hidden direct-sum decomposition.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite calculation explains exactly why filtrations and decompositions encode different information.

## Limits

This is a filtered-vector-space theorem, not a classification of mixed Hodge extensions. A pure Hodge decomposition is a different structure and can be canonical under its own hypotheses. The historical formulaW_k=⊕_{p+q=k}H_M^{p,q} conflates indices unless its objects are redefined.

## Extension

Add a specified nondegenerate inner product and take the orthogonal complement. Then identify the smaller symmetry group preserving that metric and show the splitting is natural only for that smaller category. This exposes the cost of the additional choice.
