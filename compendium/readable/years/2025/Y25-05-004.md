## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What are the sign, kernel and spectral gap of the stated periodic p³-grid operator, and which asymptotics genuinely follow from its formula?

## Status

## Summary

The exact periodic operator is one of the grounded parts of the May14 framework. Its sign must be fixed before writing a heat semigroup or a spectral gap. Primality of p is unnecessary for the diagonalization: the same calculation works for every integer p≥3.

## Definitions

On G=(Z/pZ)³ let (Lu)(x)=Σ_{j=1}³[u(x+e_j)+u(x−e_j)−2u(x)]. Define the positive operator A=−L.

For k∈G use normalized Fourier characters χ_k(x)=p^(−3/2)exp(2πik·x/p). Orthogonality is with the finite counting inner product.

## Result name

Complete eigenvalues and the gap asymptotic

## Result statement

Lχ_k=−4Σ_j sin²(πk_j/p)χ_k, so A is positive semidefinite with a one-dimensional constant kernel. Its smallest positive eigenvalue is γ_p=4sin²(π/p)∼4π²/p². The contractive heat evolution is e^(−tA)=e^(tL), not e^(−tL).

## Proof

A shift by ±e_j multiplies χ_k by e^(±2πik_j/p). Adding the two shifts and subtracting two gives 2cos(2πk_j/p)−2=−4sin²(πk_j/p). Summing proves the eigenvalue formula. The character inner products are products of geometric sums, zero unless the indices agree, so these p³ characters form a full orthonormal basis. Every eigenvalue of A is nonnegative, and it vanishes only when all k_j=0 modulo p. The least positive sine square occurs at k_j=±1 in one coordinate with the other coordinates zero. The limit sin z/z→1 proves the asymptotic. In this eigenbasis the norm of e^(−tA) is one, while its restriction to mean-zero functions has norm e^(−tγ_p). The opposite sign e^(−tL) amplifies nonconstant modes and is not a dissipative heat operator.

## Worked example: setup

Take p=4 as a transparent exact check, even though this grid size is not prime.

## Worked example: calculation

Each one-coordinate contribution to A is 0,2,4,2. Thus the three-dimensional eigenvalues are sums of three such entries, the kernel is constants and the gap is two. The eigenvalue at k=(1,1,1) is six. The verifier constructs the 64-site matrix and Fourier-mode residuals independently, and checks the exact row sum and quadratic-form identity.

## Worked example: conclusion

The actual operator yields a p^(−2) gap. A different logarithmic gap law needs a different, fully specified graph and cannot be inferred from arithmetic terminology.
