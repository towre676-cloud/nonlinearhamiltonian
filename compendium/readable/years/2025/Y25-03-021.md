## Revised question retained in the annual source

When does a coarse-graining map really preserve cycle information?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered blocking proposal asserts cohomology preservation. That property can be proved by exhibiting chain maps and a chain homotopy. A subdivided circle gives a complete integer certificate, which also illustrates exactly what arbitrary edge deletion could lose.

## Definitions

LetC have verticesv0,v1 and oriented edgese0:v0→v1,e1:v1→v0. LetD have one vertexv and one loopa.

Define r(v0)=r(v1)=v,r(e0)=0,r(e1)=a; define i(v)=v0,i(a)=e0+e1.

## Result name

An exact chain-homotopy equivalence

## Result statement

The mapsr,i commute with boundaries, ri=id_D, andir−id_C=∂h+h∂ for h(v0)=0,h(v1)=−e0,h(C1)=0. Therefore they induce inverse maps on integer homology.

## Proof

The boundary ofe0 isv1−v0 and ofe1 isv0−v1; bothr-boundaries vanish, so r is a chain map. The chaini(a)=e0+e1 has zero boundary, proving the same fori. The identityri is immediate. Onv1, ir−id givesv0−v1=∂(−e0), and onv0 it is zero. One0,e1 the difference is respectively−e0,+e0, equal toh∂ of those edges. This checks the homotopy formula on an integer basis. Ifc is a cycle, (ir−id)c=∂hc is a boundary, so ir acts as identity on homology; together withri=id this proves the claim. Dualizing gives the corresponding cohomology equivalence.

## Worked example: calculation

## Worked example: conclusion

A short exact certificate establishes preservation in a declared model, rather than inferring it from a category label.

## Limits

The maps do not preserve every metric, local observable or dynamical energy. Homology equivalence is a specific algebraic guarantee, not universal information preservation.

## Extension

For the actual tensor blocking, give integer incidence matrices and chain maps, then solve the homotopy equation. If no such maps exist, quantify the cycles lost instead of asserting preservation.
