## Summary

The recovered phase-diagram script drew ridges from a small second derivative. Such a set can be a useful visualization, but it need not be singular, critical in the optimization sense, or thermodynamic. A pair of elementary models separates these possibilities.

## Definitions

For a smooth surface E(S, T), an inflection locus may be defined by E_SS=0.

A nonanalytic minimized free energy can arise after optimizing over an order parameterm.

## Result name

Smooth ridge versus a true variational singularity

## Result statement

For E(S, T)=S³+TS², the zero-second-derivative locus is the smooth line T=−3S even though E is polynomial everywhere. For F(m, a)=m⁴/4−am²/2, min_m F is0 fora≤0 and−a²/4 fora≥0, which is C¹ but not C² ata=0.

## Proof

Differentiate the polynomial E twice in S to get6S+2T; its zero set is a line with nonvanishing gradient(6,2). The implicit-function theorem confirms it is a regular smooth submanifold, not a singularity of E. Replacing equality by an ε-threshold merely thickens the line. For F, stationarity givesm(m²−a)=0. Ifa≤0, the sum of nonnegative quartic and quadratic terms is minimized atzero. Ifa>0, the critical points±√a give−a²/4, below the valuezero at m=0. The resulting minimized function has first derivativezero on the left and−a/2 on the right, continuous ata=0; second derivatives0 and−1/2 disagree. The singularity arises from minimization and a change of minimizer, not from an arbitrary curvature threshold.

## Worked example: calculation

At(S, T)=(0,0), E_SS=0 but E and every derivative are finite. At a=0, the minimizer of F changes from one point to two symmetric nonzero points as a becomespositive, producing the proved nonanalyticity in the minimized objective.

## Worked example: conclusion

The visualization can honestly represent an inflection band, while a phase-transition claim needs an actual order parameter and limiting or minimization argument.

## Limits

No historical E, Gor Ricci–Perelman model was specified sufficiently to derive its phase boundary. A memory-efficient animation or a fitted ridge is not a theorem about its dynamics.

## Extension

Define the governing evolution or free energy, state the control parameters, and prove which observable or derivative becomes singular before labeling a critical manifold.
