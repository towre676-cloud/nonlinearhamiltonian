## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which normalization and cancellation make a weighted atomic pairing provably bounded?

## Status

## Summary

## Limits

It does not prove full weightedH¹–BMO duality on hyperbolic space. RelatingBMO₂ toBMO₁ requires geometric/weight hypotheses such as an appropriate doubling and John–Nirenberg theorem.

## Extension

State an admissible ball family and weight class, prove its measure estimates and the required oscillation comparison, then establish both directions of the completed duality. Treat that as a theorem to prove, not a Monte Carlo conclusion. In the weighted two-point example, the normalized atom is proportional to b−b_B with the opposite sign, which is precisely the equality condition in Cauchy–Schwarz. The sharp constant therefore has an explicit optimizer. If cancellation were imposed with unweighted masses instead, a constant b could already produce a nonzero weighted pairing.
