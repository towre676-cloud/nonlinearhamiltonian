## Result statement

The24 D₄vectors each have one preimage. Each of the24 shortF₄vectors has eight preimages. Zero has24 preimages. Thus24+24·8+24=240, and squared projected lengths are2,1 and0.

## Proof

Integral roots supported among the first four coordinates give6·4=24 D₄vectors. Those supported among the last four give24 zero images. Mixed support selects one of four visible and one of four hidden coordinates and two signs, giving64 roots: for each visible±e_i there are four hidden-coordinate choices and two hidden signs, hence eight preimages. For a fixed visible half-sign pattern, the last four signs must have a prescribed parity. Exactly eight of their sixteen patterns satisfy it, producing128 half-root images with multiplicity eight. This exhausts both original root types. The length assertions follow by summing coordinate squares and explicitly show why this projection is not an isometry or a bijection of root systems.
