## Revised question retained in the annual source

What exact statistical quantity can be calculated for the recovered g² topic?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered user narrative namesg^(2) without a probability model. The zero-delay count version is a normalized factorial moment. Poisson, fixed-count and geometric distributions yield three exact values, so a measured value must be interpreted through its declared sampling model.

## Definitions

For a nonnegative integer random variableN with0<EN<∞ and finite second moment, defineg2=E[N(N−1)]/(EN)².

SinceE[N(N−1)]=VarN+(EN)²−EN, g2=1+(VarN−EN)/(EN)².

## Result name

Exact model values

## Result statement

A Poisson count hasg2=1; a deterministic countN=n≥1 hasg2=1−1/n; a geometric countP(N=k)=(1−q)q^k,0<q<1, hasg2=2.

## Proof

ForPoissonλ, differentiate its generating functionG(z)=exp[λ(z−1)] twice:EN=G′(1)=λ andE[N(N−1)]=G″(1)=λ². A fixed count has numeratorn(n−1) immediately. For the geometric lawG(z)=(1−q)/(1−qz), givingG′(1)=q/(1−q) andG″(1)=2q²/(1−q)². The ratio is2. These calculations also show why ordinary second moments would give different normalizations: factorial moments remove the self-count contributionEN. Independent binomial thinning with efficiencyη scales first and second factorial moments byη andη², leavingg2 unchanged when the assumptions hold.

## Worked example: calculation

All three haveEN=2, but their factorial second moments are4,2,8 respectively, so theirg2 values are1,1/2,2. Equal mean flux therefore cannot identify the correlation model.

## Worked example: conclusion

The statistic distinguishes explicit count laws while remaining independent of an unsupported resonance story.

## Limits

Time-dependentg2(τ), detector dead time, backgrounds and non-independent thinning require their own model. The formulas do not validate a hardware architecture or a nonclassical interpretation from a single number alone.
