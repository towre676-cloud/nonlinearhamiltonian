## Revised question retained in the annual source

What exact spectrum can be extracted from a weighted bipartite incidence matrix?

## Status

Classical or newly chosen explicit model developed with proof; historical conjectures delimited

## Summary

The recovered incidence construction uses a genuine block operator. Its spectrum is determined by singular values of the incidence block; diagonal conjugation preserves it, but arbitrary reweighting does not. This gives a useful finite theorem without inventing arithmetic period data.

## Definitions

Let Bbe anm×ncomplex matrix and A=[[0, B*],[B,0]]on C^n⊕C^m.

A similarity A↦LAL^(−1)requires invertible L; a congruence LAL*is a different operation.

## Result name

Bipartite singular-value spectrum

## Result statement

For every positive singular value σ of B, Ahas eigenvalues±σ with the same multiplicity; its remaining eigenvalues arezero. In particular Tr A=0and A²=diag(B*B, BB*).

## Proof

Block multiplication gives the square formula and Hermiticity. If Bv=σ uand B*u=σ vwith unit singular vectorsu, v, then A(v, u)=σ(v, u)and A(v,−u)=−σ(v,−u). Null vectors of Bin the first block and B*in the second give the remaining kernel. The singular-value decomposition supplies a complete orthogonal decomposition, proving the full spectrum. Similarity preserves the characteristic polynomial bydet(t I−LAL^(−1))=det(L(t I−A)L^(−1)), whereas congruence need not; even A=Iand L=2Ichanges Iinto4Iunder congruence.

## Worked example: setup

Take B=[[1,1],[0,1]].

## Worked example: calculation

B*B=[[1,1],[1,2]]has characteristic polynomialt²−3t+1and eigenvalues(3±√5)/2. Its singular values are φ and1/φ, so Ahas eigenvalues±φ,±1/φ and characteristic polynomialt⁴−3t²+1. This algebraic appearance of φ comes from the chosen finite matrix and is fully explained by its polynomial.

## Worked example: conclusion

The incidence spectrum can be computed exactly and used in a real refinement or graph analysis.

## Limits

An algebraic eigenvalue does not by itself define a Frobenius action, motive, regulator or L-function. The recovered characteristic recurrences and evaluator need their actual matrices before further claims can be checked.

## Extension
