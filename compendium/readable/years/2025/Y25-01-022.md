## Revised question retained in the annual source

What isH*_{S¹}(R²) for the oscillator rotation action, and why does contractibility not make it ordinary point cohomology?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

The plane contracts to its origin equivariantly, so its equivariant cohomology isH*(BS¹)=k[u]. Ordinary plane and free-loop-space cohomology are instead justk. These are different constructions with different retained symmetry data.

## Definitions

LetS¹ rotateC≅R². The Borel construction isES¹×_{S¹}C, and equivariant cohomology is its ordinary cohomology.

Use characteristic-zero coefficients andu∈H²(BS¹;k), withBS¹≃CP∞. Its cohomology ring isk[u].

The ordinary free-loop spaceLC consists of continuous mapsS¹→C; it does not include the Borel quotient unless explicitly added.

## Result name

Complete equivariant calculation for the plane

## Result statement

H*_{S¹}(C;k)≅k[u], with one generator in every nonnegative even degree and none in odd degree. H*(C;k)=H*(LC;k)=k in degree zero only.

## Proof

The homotopyr_s(z)=(1−s)z commutes with rotations and ends at the fixed origin. It descends to a deformation retraction ofES¹×_{S¹}C ontoES¹×_{S¹}{0}=BS¹. The standard cell decomposition ofCP∞ has one cell in every even dimension and no odd cells; its ring generatoru has nonzero powers, yieldingk[u]. For the ordinary loop space, contract every loop pointwise byr_s. This is a continuous contraction to the zero loop, giving the ordinary cohomology claim. Thus an exterior factor from the two-dimensional tangent space does not belong automatically to Borel cohomology; such a factor would require a separately specified complex or theory.

## Limits

A claimed spectral-sequence degeneration needs its actual fibration or filtered complex and differential bidegrees. No such general degeneration follows merely from the oscillator notation.

## Extension

Replace the weight-one rotation byz↦g^m z and compute the equivariant Euler classmu of the normal complex line. Check fixed-point localization only after specifying the coefficient localization and an equivariant integration map. Remove the origin and the rotation action becomes free. The Borel construction of C* is homotopy equivalent to its radial quotient (0,∞), so its equivariant cohomology is k. The restriction map from k[u] therefore kills u. This example shows exactly where a fixed stabilizer contributes additional classes.
