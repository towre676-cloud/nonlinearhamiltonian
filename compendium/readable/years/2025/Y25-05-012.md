## Definitions

For a stipulated system boundary let E_out≥0 be useful returned energy and E_in>0 invested energy in the same units and time horizon. Define R=E_out/E_in.

For a rated power P_max>0 over duration T, define capacity factor CF=E_out/(P_maxT), assuming output never exceeds that rating. A duty fraction measures time on, which is a different quantity.
