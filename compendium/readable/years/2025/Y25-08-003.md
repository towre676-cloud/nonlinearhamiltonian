## Revised question retained in the annual source

Do the finite TL relations and a declared trace actually define a positive inner product?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The August9 synthesis used Temperley–Lieb, Markov traces and thermodynamics together. A two-strand calculation isolates a necessary positivity condition. It does not claim that a condition sufficient for this small algebra is sufficient for every level of a TL tower.

## Definitions

Let TL₂(δ) have basis1,e with e*=e and e²=δe,δ real and nonzero. Declare tr(1)=1,tr(e)=1/δ. The trace inner product is⟨x,y⟩=tr(x*y).

## Result name

Two-strand positivity criterion

## Result statement

For x=a+be, tr(x*x)=|a|²+2Re(overline a b)/δ+|b|². It is positive semidefinite exactly when|δ|≥1, and positive definite when|δ|>1.

## Proof

Expand x*x using e²=δe and apply the declared trace. In the basis1,e the Gram matrix is[[1,1/δ],[1/δ,1]], with eigenvalues1±1/δ. Both are nonnegative exactly when|δ|≥1. Ifδ=±1 there is a nonzero null vector and a quotient is needed for a Hilbert-space realization. At higher TL levels additional positivity restrictions arise; passing one finite Gram test cannot justify a full C*-tower or KMS identification. The algebraic relation and a trace functional are distinct data, and positivity must be proved after choosing them.

## Worked example: setup

Compare δ=2 and δ=1/2.

## Worked example: calculation

Atδ=2 the Gram eigenvalues are3/2 and1/2. Atδ=1/2, x=1−e has tr(x*x)=2−2/δ=−2, so this cannot be a squared Hilbert norm.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example converts a named trace into a verifiable mathematical condition and identifies a concrete failure.

## Limits

The full Markov-trace tower, Jones parameter classification and graph-C*-algebra dynamics are not constructed by this two-dimensional computation.

## Extension

Compute Gram matrices at successive diagram levels and identify the null ideal or parameter restrictions before asserting a positive tower representation.
