## Revised question retained in the annual source

What is the correct commutator of time differentiation and the scalar Laplace–Beltrami operator under Ricci flow?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered manuscript included a gradient term in the Ricci-flow Laplacian commutator without the companion term that cancels it. The general metric-variation formula and contracted Bianchi identity produce a clean corrected statement.

## Definitions

Use Δ f=g^{ij}∇_i∇_j f and Ricci flow∂_tg_ij=−2Ric_ij.

For a metric variationh=∂_tg, let(divh)_j=∇^ih_ij andtrh=g^{ij}h_ij.

## Result name

Correct scalar commutator

## Result statement

For a smooth scalarf,[∂_t, Δ_g]f=2Ric^{ij}∇_i∇_jf. More generally δΔ f=−h^{ij}∇_i∇_jf−(divh−(1/2)d trh)·∇f.

## Proof

Differentiateg^{ij}g_jk=δ^i_k to obtain δ g^{ij}=−h^{ij}. In local coordinates, differentiate Δ f=g^{ij}(∂_i∂_jf−Γ^k_ij∂_kf). The connection variation is δΓ^k_ij=(1/2)g^{kℓ}(∇_ih_jℓ+∇_jh_iℓ−∇_ℓh_ij). Contracting i, j gives(divh)^k−(1/2)∇^ktrh, proving the general formula. Substitutingh=−2Ric gives2Ric:Hessf+(2div Ric−d R)·∇f. Contracted Bianchi saysdiv Ric=(1/2)d R, so the entire gradient coefficient cancels. If f depends on time, the Δ∂_tf term cancels in the commutator definition; the same formula remains.

Its connection is unchanged by the spatially constant scale and Δ_t=(1−2t)^(−1)Δ₀. Hence∂_t Δ_t=2(1−2t)^(−2)Δ₀. Raising the indices of Ric=g₀ withg(t) yields Ric^{ij}=(1−2t)^(−2)g₀^{ij}, giving exactly the same right-hand side.
