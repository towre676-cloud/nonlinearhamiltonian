## Revised question retained in the annual source

What finite gluing diagram and algebraic quotient illustrate the limit and colimit constructions being invoked?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

Functions on a finite cover satisfy an explicit equalizer formula. A circle’s algebraic factorization example leads toHH₀(A)=A/[A,A], which is calculable and different from merely naming a colimit.

## Definitions

For a finite setU, letF(U)=k^U with restriction maps. A coverU=U₁∪U₂ produces the equalizer ofF(U₁)⊕F(U₂)⇉F(U₁∩U₂).

For an associative algebraA, the first Hochschild boundary isb(a⊗b)=ab−ba, soHH₀(A)=A/span{ab−ba}.

The general identification of factorization homology of a circle with Hochschild homology is background; the finite quotient below is proved directly.

## Result name

Explicit gluing and trace quotient

## Result statement

Compatible functions onU₁,U₂ glue uniquely. ForA=M₂(k) in characteristic zero,HH₀(A) is one-dimensional and is detected by trace.

## Proof

Define the glued function atx by either local value. Compatibility on the overlap makes it well defined, and every point lies in one cover member, proving uniqueness. Thus the equalizer is genuinely isomorphic tok^U. For matrices, every commutator has trace zero by cyclicity. Conversely the traceless basisE₁₂,E₂₁,E₁₁−E₂₂ consists of commutators[E₁₁,E₁₂],[E₂₂,E₂₁],[E₁₂,E₂₁]. Hence the commutator span is exactly the three-dimensional trace-zero subspace, and the quotient has dimension one. These are complete finite computations of two categorical constructions with all maps supplied.

## Worked example: setup

UseU={1,2,3},U₁={1,2},U₂={2,3}.

## Worked example: calculation

Local pairs(a,b) and(c,d) glue precisely whenb=c, giving the global triple(a,b,d). For matricesdiag(2,−2) vanishes inHH₀, whileI represents a nonzero class because its trace is two.

## Worked example: conclusion

A limit or colimit notation becomes mathematical content only after its indexing category and maps are fixed.

## Limits

IncludingU itself as a terminal test object can make an unrestricted formulaF(U)=lim_{V→U}F(V) tautological rather than a descent theorem. The higher factorization-homology construction needs its algebraic and geometric hypotheses.

## Extension

Specify a cover or disk-indexing diagram, construct its maps and compare the resulting derived limit/colimit with the intended object. Compute higher homology rather than identifying a single vector space with an entire persistence module. For a commutative algebra, every commutator is zero and HH₀(A)=A itself; for k×k it has dimension two. Thus replacing M₂(k) by an algebra with the same number of displayed matrix entries or a different decomposition can change the quotient. Algebra multiplication, not vector-space size alone, determines this invariant.
