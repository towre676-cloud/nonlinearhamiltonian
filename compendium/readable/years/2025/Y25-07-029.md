## Revised question retained in the annual source

What Galois groups are even possible for the two recovered quartic polynomials?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The July28 framework associated quartics with M₁₂ and M₂₄. A degree bound already rules out a literal identification of their splitting-field Galois groups. The quartics themselves are legitimate algebraic objects whose irreducibility can be checked exactly.

## Definitions

Let f=x⁴−4x³+1 and h=x⁴−6x³+4x²+2x+1. A characteristic-zero splitting-field group acts faithfully by permutations of the four roots, hence embeds in S₄ and has order at most24.

## Result name

Irreducible quartics with small possible Galois groups

## Result statement

Both f and h are irreducible over Q, but neither can have splitting-field group M₁₂ or M₂₄. A separate construction would be needed to relate their roots to a Mathieu representation.

## Proof

A rational root of either monic polynomial with constant1 must be±1, and direct substitution excludes both. A remaining factorization would be two monic integral quadratics by Gauss’s lemma. Their constant terms must both be e=1 or both e=−1. Write(x²+ax+e)(x²+bx+e). Its cubic coefficient is a+b and linear coefficient e(a+b). For f these would have to be−4 and0, impossible for e=±1. For h they would have to be−6 and2, likewise impossible. Hence both are irreducible. Independently, any automorphism fixing all four roots fixes the splitting field they generate, proving the faithful S₄ embedding. The Mathieu groups have orders greater than24, excluding them without numerical root fitting.

Check the constant/cubic/linear factor constraints.

## Worked example: calculation

For f, e(a+b)=±4 can never be0. For h it is±6 and never2. This proof does not determine whether the actual transitive subgroup is S₄,A₄,D₄,C₄ or V₄; discriminant and resolvent tests are still needed.

## Worked example: conclusion

A degree-four formula may participate in a larger representation, but its literal root-permutation group obeys an unavoidable degree bound.

## Limits

No Mathieu action or field discriminant was recovered. Polynomial discriminants, splitting fields and unrelated Pell fields must remain distinct.

## Extension

Compute exact discriminants and cubic resolvents, then identify each actual transitive subgroup and state any additional representation map explicitly.
