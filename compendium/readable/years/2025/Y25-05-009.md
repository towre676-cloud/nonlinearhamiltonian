## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What can the tropicalization of x+y+1=0 prove, and what information is lost before any proposed enriched decoder is added?

## Status

## Summary

A valuation map has a precise and useful loss of information. Its tropical balancing condition follows from the nonarchimedean triangle inequality, but it does not determine the original coordinates or a reconstruction advantage. The source’s additional filtered object needs an independently defined filtration.

## Definitions

Let K be a nonarchimedean valued field with additive valuation v, so v(xy)=v(x)+v(y) and v(x+y)≥min(v(x),v(y)). Set v(0)=∞.

For nonzero x,y on X={x+y+1=0}, define trop(x,y)=(u,w)=(v(x),v(y)). Use the minimum convention.

## Result name

A tropical line and a strict information-loss example

## Result statement

Every such point satisfies that min(u,w,0) is attained at least twice. Its possible finite valuation pattern lies on the three rays (u,0) with u≥0, (0,w) with w≥0, or (u,u) with u≤0. Valuations alone do not test an individual proposed lift.

## Proof

If one of three nonzero summands has strictly smaller valuation than the other two, the sum of the other two has larger valuation and cannot cancel it. Therefore x+y+1=0 forces a repeated minimum. Enumerating which two entries attain it gives the three rays. Conversely, interpreting the rays as the complete tropical variety requires a lifting theorem over a suitable valued algebraic closure; only the elementary necessary condition is used here. The loss of unit data is concrete: over Q₃, (x,y)=(1,−2) lies on X and has valuations (0,0), while (1,1) has the same valuations but gives x+y+1=3≠0. Thus one valuation pair supports both an actual solution and a nonsolution depending on the leading units.

## Worked example: setup

Compare Q₃ points (1,−2), (3,−4) and the proposed pair (1,1).

## Worked example: calculation

The first has valuation (0,0), the second (1,0); each has a repeated minimum and exactly satisfies the equation. The third has the same tropical coordinates as the first but fails the original equation. Multiplication also illustrates retained structure: v₃(3·(−4))=1+0=1. These exact integer valuations are checked without a p-adic numerical library.

## Worked example: conclusion

Tropicalization gives an exact coarse constraint, and the counterexample identifies the additional residue information that a reconstruction method would need.
