## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a rank-k reduction preserve, and which error can be certified before assigning speed claims?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; recovered claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered tensor-processing proposal names SVD and rank reduction but gives no measured complexity certificate. The matrix problem has a sharp mathematical answer: singular values determine the best Frobenius error. This supplies a reproducible approximation statement independently of any hardware throughput.

## Definitions

Let A be an m×n real matrix with singular values s1≥s2≥…≥0. The Frobenius norm squares and sums all entries.

A rank-k approximation is a matrix B with rank at mostk; arithmetic cost and approximation error are different quantities.

## Result name

Truncated SVD optimality

## Result statement

The minimum of ||A−B||_F² over rank(B)≤k isΣ_(j>k)s_j², attained by retaining the firstk singular terms.

## Proof

LetP be the orthogonal projection onto the column space ofB, with rank at mostk. Decompose A−B=(I−P)A+(PA−B); the two summands have orthogonal column spaces, so its squared norm is at least||(I−P)A||_F²=Tr(AAᵀ)−Tr(PAAᵀ). In an eigenbasis ofAAᵀ the latter trace isΣs_j²p_jj, where0≤p_jj≤1 andΣp_jj≤k. Its maximum is the sum of the k largest squared singular values. This proves the lower bound. TakingB=Σ_(j≤k)s_ju_jv_jᵀ attains it, completing the proof including ties.

## Worked example: setup

Use A=diag(5,3,1).

## Worked example: calculation

Its total squared norm is35. The optimal rank-one matrixdiag(5,0,0) has error9+1=10, and rank two has error1. A relative squared error below1/20 requires rank two, since10/35>1/20 but1/35<1/20.

## Worked example: conclusion

The retained rank can now be chosen from a declared error target, rather than from a headline reduction factor.

## Limits

The theorem concerns a matrix and the Frobenius norm. A tensor’s chosen unfolding can change the ranks, and a matrix error bound does not directly certify a downstream control or topology decision.

## Extension

For the actual contraction, state dimensions and an operation-count model, bound the propagated contraction error with operator norms, and measure runtime separately.
