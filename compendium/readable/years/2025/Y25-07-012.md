## Revised question retained in the annual source

Under which separate fixed-metric assumptions does the proposed diffusion equation actually dissipate its quadratic energy?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

A fixed-domain heat equation gives a rigorous comparison for the historical entropy proposal. It separates a solvable linear evolution from a coupled geometric-flow claim. Both the zeroth-order and gradient energies can be differentiated exactly.

## Definitions

On a closed fixed Riemannian manifold use Δ=div grad, so ∫vΔv=−∫|∇v|². Let v_t=Δv−μv with μ≥0 and smooth real v. For λ≥0 define Θ=∫(v²+λ|∇v|²)dA.

## Result name

Dissipation of a Sobolev quadratic energy

## Result statement

Θ′=−2∫|∇v|²−2λ∫(Δv)²−2μΘ≤0. A Laplacian eigenmode with−Δφ=κφ decays as e^(−(κ+μ)t).

## Proof

Differentiate∫v² to obtain2∫v(Δv−μv), then integrate by parts. Differentiate the gradient term to get2λ∫∇v·∇(Δv−μv). A second integration by parts gives−2λ∫(Δv)²−2λμ∫|∇v|². Adding the two identities proves the formula. Boundary terms vanish because the manifold is closed; periodic boundaries provide the same cancellation. Substituting v=a(t)φ into the PDE gives a′=−(κ+μ)a. If μ=0, the constant mode is conserved, showing why nonincreasing energy does not imply convergence to zero without a mean-zero condition or a spectral gap.

## Worked example: setup

Use a2π-periodic circle, v(x,0)=cosx, μ=1 and λ=2.

## Worked example: calculation

Here κ=1 and v=e^(−2t)cosx. Both∫cos²x and∫sin²x equalπ, so Θ=3πe^(−4t) and Θ′=−12πe^(−4t), exactly matching the three terms in the energy identity.

## Worked example: conclusion

A claimed entropy now has a declared norm, an explicit PDE and an equality proving its monotonicity.

## Limits

The metric and measure are fixed here. Applying this proof to an evolving curvature smoothing would omit terms and is not justified by the historical formal analogy.

## Extension

For a time-dependent metric, retain every volume and gradient variation and determine what extra conditions recover a dissipative functional.
