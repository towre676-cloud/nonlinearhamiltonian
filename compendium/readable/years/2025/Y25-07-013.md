## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can log(1+dim Ext¹) detect whether a particular exact sequence splits?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The July12 toy asserted entropy zero exactly when a triangle splits. That statement is generally false if entropy uses the dimension of the whole Ext group rather than the particular extension class. An elementary dual-number module gives both a split and a nonsplit sequence with identical endpoint Ext dimension.

## Definitions

Let R=k[ε]/(ε²) and let k=R/(ε). Equivalence classes of short exact sequences0→k→M→k→0 form Ext¹_R(k,k), whose zero class is the split extension. Group dimension counts all possible classes, not the chosen one.

## Result name

An exact counterexample to a dimension-based splitting test

## Result statement

Ext¹_R(k,k)≅k. Both the split extension with M=k⊕k and the nonsplit extension with M=R have the same endpoints. Thus log(1+dim_k Ext¹)=log2 for both, although only the first splits.

## Proof

A free resolution of k has every positive differential multiplication by ε: …→R→R→R→k→0. Its exactness follows because ker(ε)=εR=im(ε). Applying Hom_R(−,k) makes every differential zero, since ε acts trivially on k. Therefore Ext¹≅k. The sequence0→k→R→k→0, with injection1↦ε and quotient reduction, cannot have an R-linear section: the image v of1 would satisfy εv=0, forcing v∈εR, but then its quotient is zero. The direct-sum sequence splits by its coordinate inclusion. Their Ext dimension is identical because endpoints are identical.

## Worked example: setup

Compare the two middle modules R and k⊕k.

## Worked example: calculation

In R the nilpotent ε acts nontrivially, linking the two factors; on k⊕k it acts as zero. Both have vector-space dimension two, so dimensions alone cannot distinguish the extension. The actual obstruction is the zero or nonzero class in Ext¹.

## Worked example: conclusion

A useful obstruction functional must depend on the class and a justified zero test, not just the size of its ambient group.

## Limits

Over ordinary vector spaces all short exact sequences split, so that category supplies an even weaker toy. This correction does not construct a derived-motivic or BSD entropy.

## Extension

Represent the actual extension cocycle, compute whether it is a coboundary, and only then design a class-sensitive invariant with a proved splitting criterion.
