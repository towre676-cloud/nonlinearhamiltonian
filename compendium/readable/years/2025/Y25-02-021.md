## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a specified completely positive update actually preserve, and which coherence decays under repetition?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

The qubit depolarizing channel has an exact Kraus certificate and contraction factor. Trace preservation does not mean preservation of an input’s off-diagonal information or a claimed operational-coherence percentage.

## Definitions

For0≤p≤1 defineE_p(ρ)=(1−p)ρ+pI/2 on trace-one qubit matrices.

A Kraus representationE(ρ)=ΣK_jρK_j† is completely positive, andΣK_j†K_j=I gives trace preservation.

Trace distance isD(ρ,σ)=||ρ−σ||₁/2. Writeρ=(I+r·σ)/2 in Bloch coordinates.

## Result name

Exact depolarizing contraction

## Result statement

Kraus operators are√(1−3p/4)I and√(p/4)X,Y,Z. The channel sendsr to(1−p)r and satisfiesD(E_p^nρ,E_p^nσ)=(1−p)^nD(ρ,σ).

## Proof

The identityXρX+YρY+ZρZ=2Tr(ρ)I−ρ follows by expandingρ in the Pauli basis. Substituting the four Kraus terms gives(1−p)ρ+pI/2, and their squared coefficients sum to one, proving the channel properties. On a difference of two states the identity term cancels, soE_p(ρ−σ)=(1−p)(ρ−σ). Homogeneity of the trace norm proves the exact distance formula, with iteration giving its power. This is stronger and more specific than saying the update is coherent: it quantifies the information that is intentionally lost.

## Worked example: setup

Takep=1/5,ρ=|0⟩⟨0|,σ=|1⟩⟨1| andn=5.

## Worked example: calculation

Their initial trace distance is one; after five updates it is(4/5)^5=1024/3125≈0.32768. Each state approachesI/2. Off-diagonal input entries shrink by the same factor, so no nontrivial qubit coherence is exactly protected by this channel.

## Worked example: conclusion

The update’s positivity, normalization and observability behavior have independent exact certificates.

## Limits

A genericCPTP map need not have this contraction rate or a unique stationary state. The historical95% phrase has no mathematical meaning until its observable, norm, sample and confidence rule are defined.

## Extension

For a specified multi-qubit channel, determine its fixed-point algebra and peripheral spectrum. State which information is recoverable by an encoding and recovery map rather than equating complete positivity with error correction. At p=0 every state is fixed and there is no mixing. At p=1 every input becomes I/2 in one step. For 0<p≤1 the fixed state is unique because a fixed Bloch vector must satisfy r=(1−p)r. These endpoint and uniqueness cases are part of the exact channel theorem.
