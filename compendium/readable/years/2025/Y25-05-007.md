## Summary

The source’s GHZ endpoint is consistent, but a slightly negative tripartite-information caveat for a pure W endpoint is not. For a pure state of exactly three parties, the quantity vanishes identically. This is a structural obstruction to using it as the proposed transition marker, independent of numerical precision.

## Definitions

For a density matrix ρ define S(ρ)=−tr(ρlog₂ρ), with 0log0=0. For a pure state on A⊗B⊗C define I₃=S_A+S_B+S_C−S_AB−S_AC−S_BC+S_ABC.

GHZ=(|000⟩+|111⟩)/√2 and W=(|001⟩+|010⟩+|100⟩)/√3. The retrieved parameterized state ψ(g) was not supplied.

## Result name

Pure-state cancellation and unequal entanglement spectra

## Result statement

Every finite-dimensional pure three-party state has I₃=0. GHZ has all nontrivial one-party and two-party entropies equal to one; W has them equal to h₂(1/3)≈0.9182958341. The two states therefore share I₃ despite different reduced spectra.

## Proof

The Schmidt decomposition across A versus BC gives identical nonzero eigenvalues for ρ_A and ρ_BC, so S_A=S_BC. The other two bipartitions give S_B=S_AC and S_C=S_AB. Purity gives S_ABC=0. Substituting cancels every term. For GHZ, tracing two qubits leaves diag(1/2,1/2). For W it leaves diag(2/3,1/3); permutation symmetry makes this true for each party. The two-party entropies follow from the same Schmidt argument, avoiding a larger eigenproblem. This proves exact zero rather than an approximately small or slightly negative value.

## Worked example: calculation

Every one-party entropy is h₂(sin²θ), every two-party entropy is the same, and I₃=0 throughout. At θ=0 it is a product; at θ=π/4 it is GHZ. The entropy changes continuously, while I₃ contains no information about this change. The verifier checks the reduced diagonal eigenvalues for GHZ/W and the exact entropy cancellations numerically.

## Limits

Mixed three-party states can have nonzero I₃, and a subsystem of a larger pure system is generally mixed. The theorem is for a globally pure state of exactly A,B,C. No historical ψ(g) is reconstructed from endpoint labels.
