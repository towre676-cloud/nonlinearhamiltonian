## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: Where does a damped oscillator maximize displacement, and when is subtracting regenerated energy from electrical input a valid efficiency denominator?

## Status

Developed mathematical repair or classical result; speculative source claims delimited

## Summary

A24Hz target, a lock interval and a power-reduction criterion are experimental specifications, not a resonance theorem. A linear oscillator supplies a precise frequency response. Energy accounting independently determines whether a regeneration term has already been subtracted by the meter.

## Definitions

Let mx″+cx′+kx=F cosωt with m,k,c>0. Its steady displacement amplitude is A(ω)=F/√[(k−mω²)²+c²ω²].

Define gross electrical draw E_g, separately returned energy E_r and useful work W. Net input is E_g−E_r only when E_g is a gross measure; if the input is already net, subtracting E_r again double counts.

## Result name

A resonance location and a net-efficiency identity

## Result statement

If c²<2km, the positive displacement peak occurs atω_r²=k/m−c²/(2m²). Otherwise the maximal amplitude is at zero frequency. The accounting efficiency is W/(E_g−E_r) under a positive, complete net denominator, not a theorem that energy has been created.

## Proof

Minimize the squared amplitude denominator D(ω)=(k−mω²)²+c²ω². Its derivative is2ω[2m²ω²+c²−2km]. Thus an interior positive minimum exists precisely under the stated inequality, and its location is the displayedω_r. At high frequency D grows without bound, confirming the maximum. For accounting, write the control-volume balance E_g−E_r=W+E_loss+ΔE_store. Under steady storage and nonnegative losses this gives W≤E_g−E_r. The inequality depends on including every source and return in the same boundary and horizon; an incomplete denominator can manufacture an apparent efficiency.

## Limits

The linear oscillator omits nonlinear aerodynamic loading and controller dynamics. A frequency lock or40% reduction target requires measurements and a baseline definition. Positive regeneration numbers alone do not guarantee overall efficiency gain.

## Extension

Identify the frequency response with uncertainty intervals, distinguish displacement and power peaks, and prove stability of the actual feedback loop. Report gross, returned and net energies separately so any claimed reduction is reproducible.
