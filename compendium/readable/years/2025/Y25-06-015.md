## Summary

## Definitions

Let q,p∈R^d, M,K positive definite symmetric matrices and target q*. Define H=pᵀM^(−1)p/2+(q−q*)ᵀK(q−q*)/2.

Use qdot=M^(−1)p and pdot=−K(q−q*)−D M^(−1)p, with D symmetric positive semidefinite. The variables may be declared logarithmic tuning coordinates in a new model.

## Result name

Conservation without damping and dissipation with it

## Result statement

Hdot=−vᵀDv≤0 with v=M^(−1)p. If D=0, H is conserved and nontrivial trajectories need not converge. In the scalar model M=1,K=4,D=2, all solutions converge to the target with characteristic exponents−1±i√3.

## Proof

Differentiate H along the trajectory. The kinetic derivative is vᵀpdot, and the potential derivative is(q−q*)ᵀKqdot. Their K terms cancel, leaving−vᵀDv. In the undamped scalar case the positive energy level is an ellipse and motion remains on it, so the target cannot be reached asymptotically unless the initial energy is zero. With the specified damping, write x=q−q* to obtain x″+2x′+4x=0. Its characteristic polynomial(s+1)²+3 has the stated roots; every real solution is e^(−t)(Acos√3t+Bsin√3t), proving convergence directly rather than invoking an unspecified optimizer.
