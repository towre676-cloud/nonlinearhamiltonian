## Summary

The retrieved equations omit data that distinguish complex Betti comparison from crystalline-to-de-Rham comparison and from p-adic étale comparison. These are separate classical theorems, not interchangeable modal identities. A projective-line calculation provides a transparent consistency check.

## Definitions

For finite-type X over C, the classical comparison identifies étale Q_l cohomology with singular Q_l cohomology of X(C). For a smaller characteristic-zero field one must choose an embedding into C and base change.

For smooth proper X₀ over a perfect field k of characteristic p, put K₀=Frac W(k). A smooth proper lift𝔛/W(k) gives a crystalline–de-Rham comparison after tensoring with K₀. Relating p-adic étale cohomology generally uses a period ring such as B_cris and additional good-reduction hypotheses.

## Result name

Typed comparison statements and a rank/Frobenius test

## Result statement

Under the lift hypotheses, H_crys^i(X₀/W(k))⊗K₀≅H_dR^i(𝔛_K₀/K₀). For P¹, both sides have rank one in degrees zero and two and vanish in degree one. Over F_p the crystalline Frobenius multiplies the hyperplane class by p.

## Proof

The two comparison theorems are cited classical inputs rather than re-proved here. Their fields and spaces are part of the statement: a positive-characteristic X has no X(C) without a different characteristic-zero object, and X_K is undefined until a lift or model is given. For P¹ in characteristic zero, the Čech de-Rham calculation on its two affine charts leaves the constant in degree zero and the class dx/x in total degree two; positive and negative Laurent powers other than x^(−1) are removed by the chart subcomplexes. Equivalently its hyperplane cycle gives the unique degree-two class. Pullback under the p-power map takes O(1) to O(p), so the first Chern class is multiplied by p. This independently fixes the expected Frobenius factor for the cellular test object.
