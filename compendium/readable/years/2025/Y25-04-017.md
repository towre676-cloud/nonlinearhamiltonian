## Revised question retained in the annual source

What finite operator actually corresponds to a rational flux parameter?

## Status

## Summary

The recoveredΩ framework supplies rationalα and references Hofstadter gaps. A rational flux determines a finite projective translation relation after boundary phases are chosen. This gives a precise matrix model without identifying its size with a Milnor number.

## Definitions

Forcoprime p,q, letUe_j=e_(j−1) cyclically andVe_j=e^(2πipj/q)e_j, withj modq. ThenUV=e^(2πip/q)VU.

Define the Hermitian clock–shift matrixH=U+U*+V+V*. Other boundary phases give a family of Bloch matrices.

## Result name

An exact half-flux spectrum

## Result statement

Atp=1,q=2,U=X,V=Z andH=2X+2Z, with eigenvalues±2√2. In general the declared model isq×q, soα=41/840 yields840 dimensions.

## Proof

The commutation relation follows by applyinge_j to both products and comparing the phase atj versusj−1. The adjoint pairs makeH Hermitian. Forq=2, X²=Z²=I andXZ=−ZX, soH²=4(X+Z)²=8I. SinceTrH=0, its two eigenvalues are opposite and equal in magnitude to√8. This computes a genuine finite spectrum. The matrix dimension comes from the denominator of the rational projective phase and not fromφ(q),φ(2q) or(a−1)(b−1)(c−1) of a different polynomial.

## Limits
