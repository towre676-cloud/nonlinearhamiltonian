## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which topology remains unchanged between a sphere and a convex polyhedral boundary, and which symmetry data can distinguish the Platonic cases?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

Radial projection identifies every convex polyhedral boundary surrounding the origin withS². Its homology is therefore the same across the Platonic solids, while face counts and rotational group orders distinguish their geometry.

## Definitions

LetP⊂R³ be a convex polytope containing zero in its interior. Every ray meets∂P in exactly one point.

Use homology over a field. The boundary is a two-dimensional cell complex; including the filled interior changes the space to a contractible ball.

For a regular solid withF regularp-gon faces, rotations act transitively on faces and a face stabilizer has orderp.

## Result name

Topological invariance and finite symmetry counts

## Result statement

∂P is homeomorphic toS² and has Betti numbers(1,0,1). The tetrahedron,cube,octahedron,dodecahedron,icosahedron have(V,E,F) respectively(4,6,4),(8,12,6),(6,12,8),(20,30,12),(12,30,20), with rotational group orders12,24,24,60,60.

## Proof

Map a boundary pointx tox/||x||. Convexity and the interior-origin condition give a unique positive intersection on every ray, so this map is bijective. It is continuous from a compact space to the Hausdorff sphere, hence a homeomorphism. The classical homology ofS² gives the Betti numbers. For the listed regular solids, each face hasp edges and every edge bounds two faces, soFp=2E. Their Euler countsV−E+F=2 agree with the topological result. Orbit–stabilizer gives rotation orderFp: a face can be sent to any ofF faces, withp rotations preserving its center and cyclically permuting its vertices. In the tetrahedral case, the induced even vertex permutations realizeA₄; cube/octahedron rotations realizeS₄, and the remaining pair realizesA₅.

## Worked example: calculation

The boundary has chain dimensions4,6,4 and boundary ranks3,3, giving Betti numbers1,0,1. Adding the single3-cell has nonzero boundary equal to the oriented sum of the four faces, killingH₂ and leaving onlyH₀. This is an exact certificate of the difference between boundary and filling.

## Worked example: conclusion

Homology alone cannot distinguish the five regular boundaries, but the underlying cell and group structures can.
