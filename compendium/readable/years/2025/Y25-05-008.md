## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What finite Hodge-Laplacian certificate distinguishes a weighted perturbation from a genuine filtration event?

## Status

## Summary

The historical state, weights and filtration were not recovered, so its claimed transition parameters cannot be recomputed. A completely declared triangle filtration nevertheless demonstrates the exact distinction needed: positive weights change eigenvalues, whereas adding a filling simplex can change homology. This is a new mathematical example, not an inferred ψ(g).

## Definitions

Orient triangle edges 01,12,20 and use boundary B₁=[[-1,0,1],[1,−1,0],[0,1,−1]]. Without a face, B₂ is absent; with a face its boundary column is (1,1,1)ᵀ.

Use the unweighted edge Hodge Laplacian Δ₁=B₁ᵀB₁+B₂B₂ᵀ. A filtration is an increasing sequence of simplicial complexes, including all faces of every entered simplex.

## Result name

The harmonic edge disappears exactly when the face is added

## Result statement

For the unfilled triangle, Δ₁ has eigenvalues 0,3,3 with harmonic vector (1,1,1). After adding the face, Δ₁=3I and its kernel is zero. This matches β₁=1 and β₁=0 respectively.

## Proof

The boundary sum B₁(1,1,1)ᵀ is zero, so B₁B₂=0 and the complex is valid. Direct multiplication gives B₁ᵀB₁=3I−J, where J has every entry one. J has eigenvalue three on constants and zero on their orthogonal complement, producing the unfilled spectrum. Adding B₂B₂ᵀ=J yields 3I. More generally xᵀΔ₁x=||B₁x||²+||B₂ᵀx||²; therefore its kernel consists of cycles orthogonal to boundaries and represents homology in this finite inner-product model. Replacing the inner products by positive definite weights preserves the dimension of that harmonic space; positive weights do not by themselves create a new simplex or a new homology class.

## Worked example: setup

Let all edges enter at threshold zero and the face enter at threshold two.

## Worked example: calculation

The degree-one persistence interval begins at zero and ends at two. Before two there is one independent edge cycle; afterwards it is the boundary of the new face. A weighted eigenvalue crossing zero caused only by a weight becoming singular would instead leave the positive-definite setting and requires a different explanation. The verifier checks both spectra through exact matrix identities and ranks.

## Worked example: conclusion

The certificate separates a genuine combinatorial event from an unproved wall-crossing interpretation of an unspecified entropy curve.

## Limits

A weighted hypergraph is not automatically a simplicial complex. Its boundary maps, orientations, filtration and inner products must be supplied before this Hodge argument applies. No historical transition value is validated.
