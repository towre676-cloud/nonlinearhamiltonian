## Revised question retained in the annual source

How do parameter uncertainty and simulation error differ in a finite reliability model?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The proposal named Bayesian risk and Monte Carlo disruption models without a recovered dataset. A fully stipulated Bernoulli model yields exact posterior predictions and reveals dependence caused by a shared unknown parameter.

## Definitions

Let p have Beta(1,1) prior and let observations be independent Bernoulli(p) conditional on p. After s successes and f failures the posterior is Beta(s+1,f+1). No actual reliability measurements are inferred here.

## Result name

Posterior moments govern joint predictions

## Result statement

After three successes and one failure, E[p]=2/3 and E[p²]=10/21. Two future successes therefore have probability10/21, exceeding the plug-in value4/9 by2/63.

## Proof

The likelihood multiplies the uniform prior by p³(1−p), giving Beta(4,2). Beta-integral ratios give E[p]=4/6 and E[p²]=4·5/(6·7). Conditional independence yields P(X₁=X₂=1|p)=p²; averaging uses E[p²], not(E[p])². The difference is Var(p)=2/63 and represents parameter uncertainty. Separately, for N independent Bernoulli simulations at a fixed stipulated probability, Hoeffding’s inequality gives P(|p̂−p|≥ε)≤2exp(−2Nε²). That bound controls simulation sampling error under the model, not the model’s adequacy or posterior uncertainty. It cannot justify independence of actual failures.

## Worked example: setup

Compare exact prediction with a separate fixed-probability simulation target ε=0.05,δ=0.05.

## Worked example: calculation

The posterior two-success probability is0.476190…, while plug-in independence gives0.444444…. The Hoeffding sample bound is N≥ceil(log40/(2·0.05²))=738 for the separate simulation problem.

## Worked example: conclusion

A simulation may be accurate about an assumed model while uncertainty in its parameter remains substantial.

## Limits

No supply-chain or financial probability is estimated. The prior, likelihood and conditional independence are hypotheses; correlated observations require another model.

## Extension

Compare a shared-parameter model with a specified dependence model and report posterior predictive and Monte Carlo uncertainty separately.
