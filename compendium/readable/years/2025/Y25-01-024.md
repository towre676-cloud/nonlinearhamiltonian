## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which algebraic facts certify a controlled gate, and what quantitative assumption is needed to call a circuit protected?

## Status

## Summary

CNOT is an exact permutation unitary and creates an entangled state from a separable input. A telescoping estimate bounds accumulated implementation error, but unitarity alone does not prove fault tolerance.

## Definitions

DefineCNOT|a,b⟩=|a,b⊕a⟩ for binary labels. The Hadamard gate sends|0⟩ to(|0⟩+|1⟩)/√2.

For unitary gatesU₁,…,U_m and unitary approximationsV₁,…,V_m assume||V_j−U_j||≤ε_j in operator norm.

Protection is a statement about specified errors, encoding, correction and a performance bound, not merely a name for the intended gate.

## Result statement

CNOT²=I andCNOT is unitary. CNOT(H⊗I)|00⟩=(|00⟩+|11⟩)/√2, whose one-qubit marginal isI/2. The two circuit products differ by at mostΣ_jε_j.

## Proof

CNOT permutes the four orthonormal basis states and is its own inverse, proving unitarity. Applying it to(|00⟩+|10⟩)/√2 gives the displayed Bell state. Taking a partial trace eliminates the two off-diagonal cross terms and leaves equal populations, so the pure joint state is not a product. For the error bound, subtractV_m⋯V₁−U_m⋯U₁ and insert mixed products one gate at a time. The resulting sum has termsV_m⋯V_{j+1}(V_j−U_j)U_{j−1}⋯U₁. Unitary factors have norm one, so submultiplicativity and the triangle inequality giveΣ ε_j. This is a worst-case coherent-error estimate and requires no stochastic independence.

## Worked example: setup

Take ten gates each with norm error at most10⁻³.

## Worked example: calculation

The final unitary error is at most10⁻². Acting on any normalized state gives state-vector error at most10⁻²; an expectation of an observable of norm one changes by at most2·10⁻². This bound does not imply a threshold theorem or a percentage of operational coherence.

## Worked example: conclusion

A correct gate and a protected implementation are separate mathematical claims with a calculable bridge only after errors are specified.

## Limits

The calculation supplies no material implementation or hardware error rate. Fault-tolerant thresholds involve coding, locality, noise and repeated recovery assumptions absent from the recovered formulas.

## Extension

Choose an explicit encoding and noise channel, verify the Knill–Laflamme conditions and compute a logical error bound after recovery. Compare it with the coherent telescoping bound before claiming protection.
