## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a hinged beam’s boundary domain contribute to its spectrum and buckling threshold?

## Status

Classical or newly chosen explicit model developed with proof; historical conjectures delimited

## Summary

The recovered discussion correctly emphasized boundary conditions but assigned response formulas without fixing a time convention. A constant-coefficient hinged beam supplies an exact spectrum, a domain, and a sign check for its frequency kernel.

## Definitions

On[0, L], imposew(0)=w(L)=w″(0)=w″(L)=0 and let A=D∂ₓ⁴+P∂ₓ², with D>0.

For a damped equationmw_tt+η w_t+Aw=0 use the time convention e^(−i ω t), withm, η≥0.

## Result name

Hinged spectrum and positive-form threshold

## Result statement

The sine modes have eigenvalues D(n π/L)⁴−P(n π/L)². The form⟨w, Aw⟩=D||w″||²−P||w′||² is positive if P<D π²/L². The Fourier response denominator is Dk⁴−Pk²−m ω²−i ηω under the stated convention.

## Proof

Integrating the fourth derivative twice and using both hinged endpoint conditions removes boundary terms. Integrating the second derivative once gives the negative gradient norm, proving the form. The sine basis satisfies both endpoint conditions and differentiates to the displayed eigenvalues. Since||w″||²≥(π/L)²||w′||² by sine expansion, the threshold gives a strictly positive form. Conversely the first sine mode has zero form at P=D π²/L² and negative form above it, so the threshold is sharp. Finally substitutinge^(ikx−i ω t) gives∂ₓ⁴=k⁴,∂ₓ²=−k²,∂_t²=−ω²,∂_t=−i ω. A plusi ηω term would correspond to the opposite time convention, not a different damping law.

## Worked example: setup

Take D=1, L=π, m=1 and P=1/2.

## Worked example: calculation

The first two stiffness eigenvalues are1−1/2=1/2 and16−2=14. At P=1 the first mode becomes neutral. With η>0 and positive stiffness, each modal energy E_n=(|a_n′|²+λ_n|a_n|²)/2 satisfies E_n′=−η|a_n′|²≤0.

## Worked example: conclusion

The boundary domain, stiffness spectrum, threshold and damping sign are all fixed by the equation rather than an analogy.

## Limits

Variable coefficients, sixth-order terms, and different endpoints define different operators. The recovered numerical residue and engineering specifications lack the full response function needed for independent verification.
