## Revised question retained in the annual source

Does the asserted shadow identity have the correct weight and a convergent theta argument?

## Status

## Summary

The prior assistant claimed a weight3/2 completion with E₈ theta shadow and uniqueness. Before any coefficient fitting, the operator’s transformation weight and the analytic domain must agree. Two elementary checks already expose the missing structure.

## Definitions

For τ=x+iy in the upper half-plane define ξ_kF=2iy^k overline(∂F/∂overlineτ). On appropriately transforming harmonic Maass forms it has weight2−k. A positive-definite rank-eight theta series has weight four and converges for positive imaginary argument.

## Result name

A type obstruction, with a local differential example

## Result statement

At k=3/2 the shadow weight is1/2, not four. Moreover θ_E8(−4τ) is not defined by its positive-definite theta series for y>0, because its terms grow rather than decay.

## Proof

The weight2−k follows by applying the antiholomorphic chain rule to the weight-k transformation and conjugating; this is the standard ξ-operator covariance. Independently, a theta term exp(πi(−4τ)||v||²) has modulus exp(4πy||v||²), so the terms do not even tend to zero along lattice vectors. Thus the literal historical right-hand side is not a holomorphic theta function on the chosen domain. To show the differential operator concretely, take F(y)=y^(1−k). Then ∂overlineτF=(i/2)(1−k)y^(−k), so ξ_kF=1−k. The weight-k Laplacian also annihilates this local function, but that does not make it a global modular form.

## Worked example: calculation

F=y^(−1/2) gives ξ_{3/2}F=−1/2. This checks the derivative normalization and sign. It supplies no transformation law or cusp condition and therefore cannot repair the claimed global completion.

## Worked example: conclusion

A shadow identity needs compatible weight, multiplier, representation, domain and growth conditions before uniqueness is meaningful.

## Limits

These checks reject the literal assertion, not every possible vector-valued mock-Jacobi construction. A corrected theory may involve different theta kernels and operators.
