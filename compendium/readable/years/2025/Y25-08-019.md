## Summary

The August17–18 source alternated between rank-three/rank-four results and unsupported rank-five claims. The group order is exact, but orbit size and design-matrix rank require separate calculations. A finite counterexample prevents symmetry terminology from manufacturing empirical evidence.

## Definitions

The Weyl groups B₅ and C₅ act by arbitrary coordinate permutations and independent sign changes on R⁵. Their order is2⁵·5!=3840. An orbit has size group order divided by stabilizer order. A log-monomial model is linear regression after taking declared dimensionless logarithms.

## Proof

There are5! permutations and2⁵ sign choices. Under the stated generic condition no nonidentity choice fixes the vector, so the stabilizer is trivial. For(1,1,0,0,0), choose the two nonzero positions in ten ways and their signs in four ways; permuting equal entries or flipping zeros changes nothing, giving40. For feature rank, a constant column becomes zero after centering. More generally exact linear dependence between declared features bounds the rank independently of their labels. An invariant polynomial ring can have five algebraically independent generators while a particular finite dataset explores fewer independent directions.
