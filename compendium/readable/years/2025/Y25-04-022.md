## Summary

The recovered formula places a category and anE8 object in a span without defining the common ambient category or maps. A homotopy pushout is not an instruction to add labels. The simplest space-level span already differs from its ordinary pushout.

## Definitions

Take the span*←S0→*, whereS0 consists of two points and both arrows collapse to the corresponding point.

A homotopy pushout can be modeled by the double mapping cylinder: attachS0×[0,1] to the two target points at its ends.

## Result name

An exact homotopy pushout

## Result statement

The ordinary pushout is a single point, while the homotopy pushout is a circle withH1≅Z.

## Proof

In the ordinary quotient, each point ofS0 identifies the two target points, leaving just one point. In the double mapping cylinder, the two components ofS0 create two separate intervals joining the same pair of vertices. Their union is a circle. Its chain complex hasC1=Z²,C0=Z²; orient both edges fromleft vertex toright vertex, so the boundary matrix has two identical columns(−1,1). Its rank is1 and its kernel is generated by the edge difference. There are no2-cells, givingH1≅Z andH0≅Z. This proves the homotopy distinction directly rather than relying on a picture. It also shows why the arrows and model category matter: replacing a homotopy construction by an ordinary quotient can erase a cycle.
