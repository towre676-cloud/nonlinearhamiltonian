## Revised question retained in the annual source

Reconstruction: What explicit adjunction makes an idempotent operation a reflector, and why does that not prove every claimed realization compatibility?

## Status

Developed mathematical repair or classical result; speculative source claims delimited

## Summary

The historical correction marked the étale modality and implementation incomplete. An elementary representation example supplies genuine unit, idempotence and adjunction data. It also demonstrates that exactness or finite-limit preservation does not automatically make a reflector strong monoidal for a tensor product.

## Definitions

Work over R with a finite-dimensional representation of C₂, specified by S²=I. Let V_+=ker(S−I) and V_−=ker(S+I), with projections P_±=(I±S)/2.

Let I include trivial representations and R(V)=V_+. A map to a trivial representation must satisfy fS=f.

## Result name

A concrete reflector and a tensor obstruction

## Result statement

R is left adjoint to I through Hom(V_+,W)≅Hom_C₂(V,IW). The induced idempotent monad IR projects to the trivial summand and is exact. But R(V)⊗R(W)→R(V⊗W) need not be an isomorphism.

## Proof

P_+²=P_+ follows from S²=I, and P_++P_−=I with P_+P_−=0 splits V into the two eigenspaces. If fS=f, then fP_+=f and f kills V_−, so f factors uniquely through V_+. This gives the adjunction and its naturality directly. An equivariant exact sequence splits into independent plus and minus sequences by these natural projections, proving exactness of R. For the tensor claim, take both V and W to be the one-dimensional sign representation. Each invariant subspace is zero, so R(V)⊗R(W)=0. Their tensor has action(−1)(−1)=+1, so R(V⊗W)=R, disproving strong tensor preservation.

## Worked example: setup

Use S=diag(1,−1) and compare its plus projection with two sign factors.

## Worked example: calculation

P_+=diag(1,0), with rank one and P_+²=P_+. A sign vector has zero plus projection, but the tensor of two sign vectors is invariant and nonzero. The verifier checks the matrices and tensor signs exactly. No postulate is counted as an implementation proof.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example distinguishes categorical idempotence, exactness and monoidal compatibility, each of which has different obligations.

## Limits

This finite ordinary-category reflector is not the historical higher étale modality. HoTT modalities require their own dependent elimination and stability conditions; a linear idempotent alone is not enough.

## Extension

State the actual ambient category and realization functor, construct its unit and comparison morphisms, and prove each required limit or tensor law. Test conservativity and monoidal strength separately before asserting a unified comparison theorem.
