## Summary

## Result statement

## Proof

For the measure theorem, the criterion is invariant under complements. Applying it successively proves finite disjoint splitting and closure under finite unions. For a disjoint measurable sequence E_j, split an arbitrary A by the first N sets; monotonicity and the finite splitting give the required lower bound byΣ_{j≤N}μ*(A∩E_j). Passing to the supremum and combining countable subadditivity proves measurability of the union and countable additivity on measurable sets. For the spike, every fixed x>0 eventually lies outside(0,1/n), but∫|f_n|^p=n·(1/n)=1. Thus norm convergence fails despite pointwise convergence. A common integrable dominating pth power would repair this through dominated convergence.
