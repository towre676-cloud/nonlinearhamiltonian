## Proof

A triangle has three edges. A constant labeling cuts none; a nonconstant labeling places one vertex on one side and two on the other and cuts exactly the two incident cross edges. Enumeration of eight labels is complete. For the quantum formula, conjugateZ1Z2 by the two X rotations. The resulting terms are cos²(2β)Z1Z2, sin(2β)cos(2β)(Z1Y2+Y1Z2), and sin²(2β)Y1Y2. Conjugating the cross terms by the diagonal cost phase and evaluating in|++〉 yields〈Z1Z2〉=−sin(4β)sinγ; the other contributions vanish. Substitution intoC gives the expression. Direct four-component complex arithmetic independently checks the sign convention.
