## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can the exceptional symmetry ofS₆ be exhibited by a finite construction rather than inferred from an analogy with particle generations?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

Perfect matchings ofK₆ form fifteen synthemes and six complete factorizations called pentads. Permuting the six original vertices acts on these six pentads and gives an outer automorphism, with all720 images independently enumerable.

## Definitions

A duad is an unordered pair of six labels. A syntheme is a partition of those labels into three duads, equivalently a perfect matching ofK₆.

A pentad is a set of five synthemes whose fifteen edges are pairwise distinct, hence cover all edges ofK₆ exactly once.

An inner automorphism ofS₆ is conjugation by a permutation and therefore preserves cycle type.

## Result name

Explicit exceptional permutation action

## Result statement

There are fifteen synthemes and six pentads. The actionS₆→Sym(pentads) is bijective. A vertex transposition acts on pentads as three disjoint transpositions, so this automorphism is outer.

## Proof

Construct a matching by pairing the least unused vertex with each remaining vertex and recursing; there are5·3·1=15 outcomes. Enumerate all five-subsets of these fifteen matchings and retain exactly those whose edge union has size fifteen. This finite exhaustive procedure yields six pentads, retained in the verification certificate. Relabeling vertices preserves disjointness and coverage, so it permutes the six retained objects and defines a homomorphism. Enumerating all720 vertex permutations yields720 distinct permutations of the pentads, proving injectivity and, since the target also has order720, surjectivity. The image of a transposition has no fixed pentad and order two, so its cycle type is2³. Conjugation cannot take cycle type2·1⁴ to2³, proving outerness. The construction and exhaustive checks together are a finite proof certificate; the source supplies no physical identification for this automorphism.

## Worked example: setup

Apply the label swap(0 1) to each of the six certified pentads.

## Worked example: calculation

The pentads pair into three exchanged pairs. Applying the swap twice restores every matching, but one application fixes none of the six pentads. This contrasts with its four fixed original labels and makes the change of cycle type directly visible.

## Worked example: conclusion

The exceptional automorphism is now fully specified by finite combinatorial data and a complete enumeration.

## Limits

The numbering of the six pentads chooses a particular automorphism; different numberings differ by an inner automorphism. This does not identify particle families, Monster representations or a continuous gauge group.

## Extension

Construct the induced permutation on conjugacy classes and check that transpositions exchange with triple transpositions. Then restrict toA₆ and distinguish inner automorphisms ofA₆ from those induced by odd permutations ofS₆.
