## Definitions

LetG=F2² label invertible simple objects. Usequadratic functionq(a,b)=(−1)^(ab).

Its associated double-braiding bicharacter isB(v,w)=q(v+w)/(q(v)q(w))=(−1)^(ad+bc) forv=(a,b),w=(c,d). A label is transparent ifB(v,w)=1 for allw.

## Result name

Exact triviality of the transparent subgroup

## Result statement

Onlyv=0 is transparent. The normalized matrixS_vw=B(v,w)/2 is a real orthogonal4×4 matrix.

## Proof

Ifv=(a,b) is transparent, choosew=(0,1) to obtain(−1)^a=1, soa=0. Choosingw=(1,0) similarly givesb=0. For orthogonality, the inner product of rowsv,v′ is(1/4)Σ_wB(v−v′,w). This character sum is1 ifv=v′ and0 otherwise: a nontrivial character has equally many positive and negative values, as pairingw withw+w0 flips its sign. ThusSSᵀ=I. In the pointed realization, trivial transparent subgroup is the finite algebraic form of nondegeneracy. In a symmetric category, by contrast, all double braidings are identity and every object is transparent.
