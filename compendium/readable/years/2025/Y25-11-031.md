## Revised question retained in the annual source

When does smoothing a potential preserve its Poisson equation?

## Status

Classical or newly chosen explicit model developed with proof; historical conjectures delimited

## Summary

The recovered universal smoothing proposal connected Barneskernels, subdivision, neural layers and gravitational potentials. A precise convolution theorem is useful and provable, but it requires regularity, translation invariance and a correctly normalized kernel.

## Definitions

On R^dlet η_ε be a smooth nonnegative mollifier of total massone.

Assume Φ and ρ are distributions satisfying ΔΦ=ρ, with convolution η_ε*Φ well-defined.

## Result name

Poisson equation commutes with mollification

## Result statement

Δ(η_ε*Φ)=η_ε*ρ in distributions. On a torus, a solution of ΔΦ=ρ requires mean ρ=0, and smoothing preserves that compatibility condition.

## Proof

Distributional differentiation commutes with convolution by a test function:testing against ψ and integrating by parts transfers Δ to ψ and then to Φ, with no boundary term on R^dor the torus. Thus Δ(η_ε*Φ)=η_ε*(ΔΦ)=η_ε*ρ. Integrating a torus Laplacian gives zero, proving the necessarymeancondition. Since η has massone, the mean of η*ρ equals the mean of ρ. In Fourier coordinates on a torus, the equation is−|k|²Φ̂(k)=ρ̂(k)for k≠0; both smoothed coefficients are multiplied by η̂_ε(k), so the identity is visible mode by mode. The zero potential mode remains an arbitrary additive constant.

## Worked example: setup

Take ρ(x)=cosx on a2π periodic circleand Φ(x)=−cosx. Use a heat mollifier with multiplier e^(−ε²k²).

## Worked example: calculation

The smoothed density ise^(−ε²)cosxand the smoothed potential is−e^(−ε²)cosx; the second derivative of the latter is exactly the former. If ρ=1instead, there is no periodic Poissonsolution because its mean is nonzero.

## Worked example: conclusion

The legitimate operator-smoothing relation is a precise PDE commutation theorem with an exact example.

## Limits

An arbitrary Barnesorlearnedkernelneed not have these properties, and smoothing a nonlinear operator generally does not commute with its nonlinearity. No universal computing or physical equivalence follows.

## Extension

For a proposed kernel, prove normalization, regularity and Fourier bounds, and calculate the commutator with any variable-coefficient or nonlinear operator rather than assuming it vanishes.
