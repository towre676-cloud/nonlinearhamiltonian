## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does a CFL bound prove for one actual discretization, and how can domain decomposition be tested without inventing scaling benchmarks?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

For periodic upwind advection, the CFL interval is an exact maximum-norm stability condition. Correct halo exchange reproduces the serial update algebraically, but says nothing by itself about speedup.

## Definitions

Foru_t+cu_x=0,c>0, useu_j^{n+1}=(1−ν)u_j^n+νu_{j−1}^n withν=cΔt/Δx and periodic indices.

A one-cell halo stores the neighboring old-time value required at a subdomain boundary.

Strong-scaling speedup is measured wall time relative to a specified baseline; it cannot be inferred from a stable stencil.

## Result name

Stability interval and decomposition equivalence

## Result statement

For0≤ν≤1, the update is anℓ∞ contraction. Forν>1, the alternating Fourier mode has amplification1−2ν with modulus greater than one. Two subdomains with correct old-time halos give the identical update to one serial domain.

## Proof

In the stable interval each new value is a convex combination of two old values, so|u_j^{n+1}|≤max_k|u_k^n|. For the alternating modeu_j=(−1)^j, substitution gives(1−2ν)(−1)^j, proving the instability assertion. On a decomposed mesh, interior points use the same stencil. At the first point of each block, the needed predecessor is supplied by the halo; if it is the old-time predecessor from the adjacent block, the expression is exactly the serial one. Updating that halo from mixed time levels instead changes the method. Local refinement must therefore respect both a stability restriction and consistent interlevel synchronization.

## Worked example: setup

Use four periodic cellsu=(0,1,2,3),ν=1/2 and split after the second cell.

## Worked example: calculation

The serial result is(3/2,1/2,3/2,5/2). Supplying left halos three and one to the two blocks reproduces it exactly. The mean remains3/2 because the periodic shift has the same sum as the original vector.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example supplies meaningful stability and communication certificates without claiming an unmeasured MPI/GPU efficiency.

## Limits

Higher-order, multidimensional and relativistic systems have different characteristic speeds and stability regions. A CFL inequality is generally necessary but may not suffice for a chosen time integrator and boundary treatment.

## Extension

For an AMR interface, derive a conservative flux synchronization rule and verify equal-and-opposite interface fluxes. Then measure scaling with fixed problem, hardware, precision and synchronization policy.
