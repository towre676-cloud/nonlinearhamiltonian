## Revised question retained in the annual source

What precise cancellation theorem applies to the recovered spherical-harmonic portal functional?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered portal construction sought weights killing odd spherical modes and all degrees abovefour, then extended the idea to graph Laplacians. This is a valid projection problem once the kernel is defined. It does not require an unexplained golden-ratio determinant.

## Definitions

Use orthonormal spherical harmonics Y_l^mon S²and the L²inner product.

Letcbelong to the finite-dimensional space V=span{Y_l^m:l=0,2,4}, and define J(f)=⟨c, f⟩.

## Result name

Exact spectral cancellation and a stability bound

## Result statement

J annihilates every harmonic of odd degree and every degreeabovefour. For anyf∈L², J(f)=J(P_Vf), and|J(f)−J(g)|≤||c||₂||P_V(f−g)||₂.

## Proof

Orthogonality gives⟨Y_l^m, Y_j^n⟩=δ_lj δ_mn. Expandingcin degrees0,2,4therefore makes all excluded harmonic coefficients vanish in the pairing. Decomposef=P_Vf+(I−P_V)f; the second component is orthogonal toc, proving the identity. Cauchy–Schwarz yields the error bound. Antipodal symmetryc(−x)=c(x)alone kills odd degrees because Y_l^m(−x)=(−1)^l Y_l^m(x), but does not kill even degreesabovefour; the finite spectral cutoff is a separate condition. The same proof works on a finite graphwith a real symmetric Laplacianand an orthonormal eigenbasis.
