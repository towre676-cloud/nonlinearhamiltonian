## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which object records birth and death times, and why is a terminal colimit not an adequate definition of persistence?

## Status

Developed mathematical result with explicit hypotheses and checked example; historical claims repaired where necessary

## Summary

A finite triangle filtration has a one-cycle born at time two and killed at time three. ItsH₁ colimit is zero despite a nontrivial persistent interval.

## Definitions

LetX₀ contain three vertices. At time one add edgesAB andBC; at time two addAC; at time three fill the triangleABC.

Persistent homology is the vector-space diagramt↦H_i(X_t;k) together with maps induced by inclusions.

An interval modulek[b,d) has a one-dimensional space for b≤t<d, zero otherwise, and identity maps within its lifetime.

## Result name

Complete barcode of the triangle filtration

## Result statement

H₀ consists of one interval[0,∞) and two intervals[0,1). H₁ consists of[2,3). Higher homology vanishes. The colimitH₁ is zero, while the rank ofH₁(X₂)→H₁(X₂) is one.

## Proof

At time zero all three vertices are separate, soH₀ has dimension three. The two added edges form a tree, whose incidence matrix has rank two, merging all components without creatingH₁. The third edge raises the edge count to three while the incidence rank stays two, producing one cycle. Adding the face gives a nonzero boundary equal to that cycle, so it dies at time three. All inclusion maps follow directly from these chain generators. The interval decomposition is therefore explicit rather than merely asserted. The directed colimit identifies every vector with its later image; the cycle maps to zero after the filling and disappears. It records the eventual space but not how long a transient feature existed.

## Worked example: setup

Write the three edge boundaries in the vertex basis and the face boundary in the edge basis.

## Worked example: calculation

Use∂AB=B−A,∂BC=C−B,∂AC=C−A, so the cycleAB+BC−AC has zero boundary. The face boundary is exactlyAB+BC−AC. Exact rank calculation gives Betti sequencesH₀:(3,1,1,1),H₁:(0,0,1,0).

## Worked example: conclusion

The reconstructed definition preserves the actual diagram and its maps; replacing it bycolim_tH^i loses the feature this example is designed to detect.

## Limits

Interval decomposition here uses a one-parameter finite filtration over a field. Multi-parameter persistence has no general barcode classification by intervals. The motivic adjective requires additional coefficient and functorial data.

## Extension

For any supplied filtration, retain the rank invariant rank(H_i(X_s)→H_i(X_t)) as a certificate. Test whether a proposed simplification preserves these ranks, rather than comparing only the final Betti numbers.
