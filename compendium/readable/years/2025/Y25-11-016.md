## Revised question retained in the annual source

What rigorous dynamics underlies an irrational phase texture, and is dense motion maximal entropy?

## Summary

The recovered animation was interpreted as a dense torus flow and a maximal-entropy texture. The first claim can be true under rational independence; the second fails for a torus translation. The distinction is accessible through characters and the isometry property.

## Definitions

## Result name

Unique ergodicity and zero topological entropy

## Result statement

Time averages along this flow converge to Haar-space averages for every continuous function, so every orbit is dense. Every fixed-time translation map has topological entropyzero.

## Proof

For a nonconstant charactere_k, the time average is e_k(x)[e^(2π i T(k₁+k₂φ))−1]/[2π i T(k₁+k₂φ)]. Irrationality ensures the denominator coefficient is nonzero for k≠0, so this tends tozero uniformly in x. Constants retain their average. Trigonometric polynomials are uniformly dense in continuous functions, proving the averaging statement for every continuous function. A nonempty open set contains a nonnegative continuous function of positive Haar integral; its positive time average forces every orbit to enter that set, proving density. For entropy, a translation is an isometry, so the distance between two points under any number of iterates is their original distance. The number of ε-separated orbit segments is bounded by the finite ε-packing number of the torus, independent of segment length; logarithmic growth per iterate is thereforezero.

## Worked example: setup

Let an RGB texture use three continuous periodic scalar functions of the two phase angles.

## Worked example: calculation

Following the irrational orbit samples their joint torus distribution, with no exact period. This does not make the RGB vector lie on S²: it belongs to R³ or a color cube unless explicitly normalized, and normalization requires its norm never vanish. Clipping can also create flat regions and change the pushed-forward distribution.

## Worked example: conclusion

Dense quasiperiodic sampling is rigorously explained without confusing visual complexity with positive entropy.

## Limits

A torus embedding in R³ does not alter the translation dynamics. An animation’s finite frames cannot by themselves certify irrationality or ergodicity.
