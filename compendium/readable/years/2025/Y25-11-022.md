## Revised question retained in the annual source

What can harmonic blocks and cohomology transport mean rigorously for a finite mesh?

## Status

Classical or newly chosen explicit model developed with proof; historical conjectures delimited

## Summary

The recovered subdivision program used harmonic/curvature blocks and an SL(2g, Z)transport. A finite cochain complex has an exact Hodge decomposition, but an oriented surface’s integral intersection form restricts its cohomology transport more strongly than determinantone.

## Definitions

For finite inner-product cochain spaces with differentiald andd²=0, let Δ=d*d+dd*.

On an oriented genus-gsurface, the first integral cohomology carries a nondegenerate skew intersection matrix J.

## Result name

Finite Hodge decomposition and symplectic necessity

## Result statement

C^k=im d_(k−1)⊕ker Δ_k⊕im d_k*orthogonally, andker Δ_krepresents H^k. Any orientation-preserving surface-induced transport Msatisfies M^TJM=J, hence lies in Sp(2g, Z), not merely SL(2g, Z).

## Proof

The two image spaces are orthogonal because⟨du, d*v⟩=⟨d²u, v⟩=0. Also⟨x, Δ x⟩=||dx||²+||d*x||², so harmonic vectors are exactlykerd∩kerd*. The orthogonal complement of the two image spaces is precisely that intersection, proving the decomposition. Every cocycle is an exact part plus a harmonic part, and a harmonic exact vector has zero norm, so harmonic representatives are unique. For transport, the cup-product/intersection pairing is preserved by an orientation-preserving homeomorphism; writing this invariance in a basis gives M^TJM=J. In dimensiontwo this is equivalent todet M=1, but in higher dimensions it is stronger.

## Worked example: setup

For a three-edge cycle oriented consistently, take the incidence differentiald:C³→C³. Forg=2compare a determinant-one matrix with J=[[0, I₂],[−I₂,0]].

## Worked example: calculation

The harmonic edge space isspan(1,1,1), and its period is the sum of edge values. Let A=[[1,1],[0,1]]and M=diag(A, I₂). Then det M=1but M^TJM has upper-right block A^T≠I₂, so it is not symplectic and cannot represent the claimed oriented-surface transport.

## Worked example: conclusion

Harmonic degrees of freedom and allowed transport have exact finite definitions and an explicit obstruction.

## Limits

A numerical block labeled harmonic must be tied to an actual cochain complex and inner product. No recovered modified subdivision map was proved to commute withd or preserve this pairing.
