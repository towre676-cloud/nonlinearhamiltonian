## Result statement

Any fixed numerical cutoff forλ1 orR changes under metric scaling. For the round sphere,r(t)²=r(0)²−2(n−1)t up to its extinction time.

## Proof

In coordinates the inverse metric scales byc⁻², while the constant metric scaling does not change the Levi-Civita connection. The Laplace operator and the contraction defining scalar curvature thus each acquirec⁻². On the unit sphereRic(g_unit)=(n−1)g_unit, andRic(r²g_unit) as a covariant tensor is the same(n−1)g_unit. Substitution into the flow gives(r²)′=−2(n−1). A Lie derivative termL_Xg corresponds, under a suitably matched time-dependent pullback, to changing coordinates; an extraT_ij needs its own symmetry and regularity assumptions. None of these symbols determines an external numerical phase threshold.
