## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which frequency ratios make the stated trochoid close, and which extra data define a three-dimensional version?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The August3 spirograph discussion linked geometry to Lie groups and speculative applications. The exact mathematical kernel is a sum of rotating complex vectors. Closure can be proved from its frequencies without endorsing an application or assuming every spatial curve is a group orbit.

## Definitions

For R>r>0 and d>0 define z(t)=(R−r)e^(it)+d e^(−i(R−r)t/r). Assume its two distinct frequencies have nonzero coefficients. A period means z(t+T)=z(t) for every t, not merely one accidental self-intersection.

## Result name

Rational frequency ratio gives a closed curve

## Result statement

If R/r=a/b in lowest positive terms,2πb is a period. If R/r is irrational, the function has no positive period. Degenerate zero-coefficient cases must be treated separately.

## Proof

The two exponentials return together at T=2πb because their phases change by2πb and−2π(a−b). Conversely equality of the two trigonometric sums for every t implies that each distinct-frequency coefficient has the same phase before and after translation; linear independence follows, for example, by evaluating the function and its derivative. Thus e^(iT)=1 and e^(−i(R−r)T/r)=1. The first gives T=2πm, and the second makes m(R−r)/r an integer, impossible for irrational R/r. This is a theorem about the declared parametrization. A third coordinate such as hsin(ωt) adds a further frequency whose commensurability must also be checked.

## Worked example: setup

Use R=5,r=2,d=1.

## Worked example: calculation

The frequencies are1 and−3/2, so T=4π works. A3D lift with vertical frequency1/3 has common period12π, whereas an irrational vertical frequency destroys periodicity.

## Worked example: conclusion

Closure is governed by all active frequencies, rather than by a visual resemblance or an arbitrary Lie-group label.

## Limits

The proof gives no fabrication, biological or drug-design result. Self-intersection, knot type and embeddedness need further analysis.

## Extension

For a specified spatial lift, classify self-intersections and prove whether the closed image is an embedding before assigning knot invariants.
