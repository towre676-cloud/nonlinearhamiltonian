## Revised question retained in the annual source

What concrete finite-group identity supports the named induction/restriction framework?

## Status

## Summary

The July4 material invoked spectral Mackey functors, chromatic theories and descent. The finite permutation case provides a complete classical Mackey calculation. It supplies an actual double-coset decomposition without pretending to construct the equivariant spectral machinery.

## Definitions

Let G=S₃ and H={1,(12)}. The induced trivial representation Ind_H^G1 is the permutation representation on the three left cosets G/H. Restrict it back to H and decompose by the H-orbits on those cosets.

## Result name

An explicit double-coset decomposition

## Result statement

Res_H^G Ind_H^G1≅1_H⊕Ind_{1}^{H}1. Over characteristic zero this is2·1_H⊕sign_H and has dimension three.

## Proof

One coset is H itself and is fixed by H. The other two cosets are exchanged by(12), so their permutation module is the regular representation of C₂. A fixed orbit contributes a trivial line; a free two-element orbit contributes Ind_1^H1. Equivalently H∖G/H has representatives1 and(13). The first intersection H∩H is H, and the second H∩(13)H(13)⁻¹ is trivial, giving exactly the two terms. The two-dimensional regular module splits into symmetric and antisymmetric lines by the vectors(1,1) and(1,−1). This proves the representation identity directly and illustrates the orbit argument underlying the general Mackey formula.

## Worked example: calculation

The three-coset permutation character is(3,1): the identity fixes all cosets, and the transposition fixes one. The right side2·trivial+sign gives(3,2−1)=(3,1), matching the explicit orbit decomposition.

The named correspondence now has defined groups, spaces and maps with a complete finite proof.

## Limits

This does not construct a functor between symmetric spectra and genuine G-spectra, prove Segal completion or identify a Morava-theoretic obstruction.

## Extension

Specify the equivariant category and its transfer/restriction maps, then verify the relevant double-coset and coherence identities rather than importing them from analogy.
