## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which quadratic extension of Q(√10) is unramified, and does the proposed polynomial generate it?

## Status

Developed classical mathematics with explicit hypotheses and scoped evidence

## Summary

The assistant identified K(i) as a Hilbert class field and later gave incompatible narrow data. Discriminants and real embeddings give decisive exact tests. The resulting correction is an actual class-field identification, using the independently proved class number two.

## Definitions

For a biquadratic extension of Q, its field discriminant is the product of the discriminants of its three quadratic subfields. For L/K, Disc(L/Q)=Disc(K/Q)^[L:K]·Norm_K/Q(disc(L/K)). Use the ordinary Hilbert class field, unramified also at real places.

## Result name

The unramified quadratic extension

## Result statement

The Hilbert class field of K=Q(√10) is L=Q(√2,√5). The proposed K(i) is ramified and is not that field. The polynomial x⁴−18x²+121 is instead satisfied by√10+i.

## Proof

The quadratic discriminants for L are8,5,40, whose product is1600=40². Hence the relative discriminant has norm one and is the unit ideal, so no finite prime ramifies. L is totally real, so no real place ramifies either. It is a nontrivial degree-two extension of K. Since Cl(K) has order two, class field theory identifies it with the Hilbert class field. For K(i), the subfield discriminants are40,−4,−40, whose product is6400. Its relative discriminant norm is6400/40²=4, already excluding finite unramifiedness; its complex embeddings also ramify the real places. Finally(√10+i)²=9+2i√10, so squaring the shifted square gives the stated quartic.

## Worked example: calculation

√2+√5 satisfies x⁴−14x²+9=0, whereas√10+i satisfies x⁴−18x²+121=0. A polynomial defining a degree-four extension does not identify its ramification or make it the desired class field. Polynomial discriminants may also differ from field discriminants by an index square.

## Worked example: conclusion

The correction distinguishes a genuine unramified extension from a plausible-looking but wrong defining polynomial.

## Limits
