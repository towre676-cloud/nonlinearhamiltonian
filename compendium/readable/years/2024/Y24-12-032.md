Projective point count and a hyperaddition quotient

## Result statement

|P¹(F_q)|=q+1, whose counting polynomial has value2 atq=1, matchingχ(P¹(C))=2. For a field with at least three elements, quotienting nonzero scalars to one class yields the Krasner hyperaddition1⊞1={0,1}.

## Proof

F_q² hasq²−1 nonzero vectors and each line containsq−1 of them, so the quotient count is(q²−1)/(q−1)=q+1. P¹(C) has one0-cell and one real2-cell, giving Euler characteristic2. This explains a common counting heuristic atq=1; it does not create a field with one element. For the hyperfield quotient, nonzero a and b can sum to zero by choosing b=−a. They can also sum to a nonzero element when the field has at least three elements, with an appropriate nonzero pair. Thus the sum of the single nonzero equivalence class with itself contains both0 and1. Multiplication remains well-defined and1^(−1)=1, but addition is not an ordinary single-valued operation.

## Limits

Different F₁ geometries formalize different data, so a counting-polynomial specialization is not a universal F₁ point definition. No quantum error-correction theorem follows from hyperfield inverses.
