## Revised question retained in the annual source

How should Jordan structure be certified exactly, and when can symmetry help compute it?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; historical claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered code proposal mixed Jordan decomposition with space-group operations. Similarity preserves Jordan form; an unrelated geometric symmetry does not improve it. Nullity increments supply exact certificates, while a simple perturbation explains the instability of numerical Jordan decisions.

## Definitions

For an eigenvalue λ let d_k=dim ker(A−λI)^k, with d₀=0.

A commuting symmetry U satisfies AU=UA and therefore preserves every generalized eigenspace.

## Result name

Jordan block counts from nullities

## Result statement

The number of Jordan blocks for λ of size at least k is d_k−d_{k−1}; the number of blocks of size exactly k is 2d_k−d_{k−1}−d_{k+1}.

## Proof

On a Jordan block of size s, the k-th power of its nilpotent part has nullity min(k,s). Its successive increment is one precisely when s≥k. Summing over blocks gives the first formula, and subtracting consecutive increments gives the second. This argument is exact and does not choose a tolerance. If U commutes with A, then U commutes with every power of A−λI and maps its kernel into itself, so known symmetry sectors can reduce the computation. A generic change of coordinates S only conjugates A to S^(−1)AS and cannot alter the block sizes. Numerical singular-value thresholds estimate approximate nullities; they do not turn a near-defective matrix into a certified exact Jordan form.

## Worked example: setup

Take the recovered A=[[5,4,2],[0,1,0],[0,0,3]], and compare N_ε=[[0,1],[ε,0]].

## Worked example: calculation

A has distinct eigenvalues 5,1,3 and eigenvectors (1,0,0), (−1,1,0), (−1,0,1), so it is diagonalizable. For ε=0, N_ε has one size-two nilpotent block. For every nonzero ε it has two distinct complex eigenvalues ±√ε and is diagonalizable. An arbitrarily small perturbation changes the exact Jordan structure.

## Worked example: conclusion

The matrix example needs no generalized eigenvector, and exact nullity formulas provide a reproducible standard for code verification.

## Limits

A space group acts on a specified physical or function space; its representation must commute with the matrix before it is useful. Exact rational inputs and floating-point approximations require different claims.

## Extension

Use Schur form for stable numerical spectral calculations, and reserve Jordan certification for exact arithmetic or rigorously bounded perturbation questions.
