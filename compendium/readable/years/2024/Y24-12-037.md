## Revised question retained in the annual source

What concrete dilation theorem can connect a transfer operator to a physical-state map?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; historical claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered operator-theoretic request included Stinespring alongside transfer and quiver coefficients. A finite channel has a complete elementary dilation proof. Arbitrary transfer matrices do not automatically preserve density matrices; positivity and normalization must be checked.

## Definitions

A Kraus map isΦ(ρ)=Σ_aK_aρK_a*, withΣ_aK_a*K_a=I.

A density matrix is positive semidefinite with trace one; an auxiliary environment has orthonormal basis|a⟩.

## Result name

Exact isometric dilation

## Result statement

DefineVψ=Σ_aK_aψ⊗|a⟩. ThenV*V=I andΦ(ρ)=Tr_env(VρV*). In particular the map is trace-preserving and completely positive.

## Proof

For vectorsψ,φ, orthogonality of|a⟩ gives⟨Vψ,Vφ⟩=Σ_a⟨ψ,K_a*K_aφ⟩=⟨ψ,φ⟩, proving the isometry. ExpandingVρV* produces termsK_aρK_b*⊗|a⟩⟨b|; partial tracing keeps exactlya=b, yieldingΦ. Positivity holds after adjoining any finite ancilla because the extended map is a sum of terms(I⊗K_a)R(I⊗K_a)* forR≥0. Trace preservation follows from cyclicity and the normalizationΣK_a*K_a=I. This constructs the finite dilation directly rather than invoking an unspecified continuum theorem.

## Worked example: setup

TakeK₀=√p I andK₁=√(1−p)Z, with0≤p≤1, acting on a qubit.

## Worked example: calculation

Forρ=[[a,c],[overline c,1−a]],Φ(ρ)=[[a,(2p−1)c],[(2p−1)overline c,1−a]]. Populations remain unchanged and coherence is multiplied by2p−1. Atp=1/2 the output is completely dephased, while the dilationV preserves the norm of every pure input by entangling it with the environment.

## Worked example: conclusion

The state map has an exact dilation and normalization proof, giving a rigorous template for any recovered operator proposal.

## Limits

A general classical transfer operator may preserve a different cone or measure and need not be a quantum channel. No historical Kraus operators were recovered, so this example is a revised mathematical construction rather than an attributed old implementation.

## Extension

Given a proposed finite superoperator, compute its Choi matrix and test positivity plus the correct partial-trace normalization before searching for Kraus or dilation data.
