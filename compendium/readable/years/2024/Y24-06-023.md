## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which completely positive channel models the stated decoherence, and which observables can distinguish its effects?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or finite example and explicit scope

## Summary

The recovered assistant passage mentions CNOT entanglement and Lindblad decoherence. A Bell pair undergoing local dephasing yields an exact channel, a closed-form density matrix and a precise observability distinction between individual marginals and joint correlations.

## Definitions

Let |Φ⁺⟩=(|00⟩+|11⟩)/√2, obtainable by a Hadamard on the first qubit followed by CNOT. Use Pauli matrices X and Z.

For γ≥0, take Lindblad operator L=√(γ/2)Z on the first qubit. Its generator is D(ρ)=(γ/2)(ZρZ−ρ). Set q=e^(−γt) for t≥0.

## Result name

Entanglement, dephasing and observability in a two-qubit calculation

## Result statement

The resulting channel is E_t(ρ)=((1+q)/2)ρ+((1−q)/2)ZρZ. It is trace preserving and completely positive. Applied to the first half of |Φ⁺⟩, it leaves each one-qubit marginal I/2, but changes the joint expectation ⟨X⊗X⟩ from1 to q while ⟨Z⊗Z⟩ remains1.

## Proof

In the Z basis, ZρZ keeps diagonal entries and reverses off-diagonal signs. Thus the generator leaves diagonal entries fixed and gives derivative−γρ_01 for off-diagonal entries; the solution multiplies them by q. The displayed mixture has exactly that action. Its Kraus operators are K₀=√((1+q)/2)I and K₁=√((1−q)/2)Z. Both coefficients are nonnegative because0≤q≤1, and ΣK_j†K_j=I. A Kraus representation proves complete positivity, including after tensoring with an arbitrary ancilla, while the last identity proves trace preservation. For the Bell input, the only nonzero entries are the two populations1/2 and coherences q/2 linking00 and11. Partial traces remove those coherences, leaving I/2, whereas X⊗X exchanges00 and11 and therefore reads their sum q. Z⊗Z is+1 on both populated states.

## Worked example: setup

Compare no dephasing q=1 with partial dephasing q=1/4.

## Worked example: calculation

Every individual computational-basis or rotated-basis measurement has the same uniform marginal in both cases. The joint X⊗X expectation changes from1 to1/4, and the eigenvalues of the two-qubit state are(1+q)/2,(1−q)/2,0,0. At q=1/4 these are5/8,3/8,0,0, explicitly positive and normalized. Complete dephasing q=0 gives the classical mixture(|00⟩⟨00|+|11⟩⟨11|)/2, while every q>0 retains nonzero coherence in this basis.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example proves a specific decoherence evolution and specifies exactly which observation is blind to its changing parameter.

## Limits

Lindblad form assumes a specified Markovian quantum dynamical model; it does not derive a biological mechanism. Negative time or γ<0 makes the displayed mixture coefficients unsuitable for this channel family. Marginal blindness here is not a claim that all measurements are uninformative.

## Extension

Specify a measurement family, calculate its probabilities under the channel and test whether its parameter map is injective before claiming decoherence is experimentally identifiable.
