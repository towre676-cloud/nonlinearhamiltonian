## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which geometric consequences follow from the recovered SU(2) exponential, and does an exponentially weighted chord distance remain a metric?

## Status

## Summary

The historical model mixed an SU(2) rotation, decreasing angles, a modified chord distance, and an area sequence. Each admits an exact finite calculation. The distance requires a triangle inequality that arbitrary pair-dependent damping can destroy.

## Definitions

The Pauli matrices obey (n·σ)²=I for a real unit vector n.

Let d₀ be chord distance on a sphere; define a candidate d_ij=d₀,ij e^(−αE_ij) with symmetric nonnegative pair weights.

## Result name

SU(2) exponential and failure of automatic metricity

## Result statement

exp(iθn·σ)=cosθ I+i sinθ n·σ is unitary of determinant one. Arbitrary positive symmetric factors multiplying a metric need not preserve the triangle inequality. The sequence √(n(n+2)) equals n+1−1/[2(n+1)]+O(n^(−3)).

## Proof

Separate even and odd terms in the exponential series using (n·σ)²=I; this gives the trigonometric formula. Its adjoint replaces i by −i, and multiplication gives identity. Its eigenvalues are e^(±iθ), hence determinant one; the induced rotation of spatial observables has angle 2θ. For the asymptotic, write √(n(n+2))=(n+1)√(1−1/(n+1)²) and apply the Taylor expansion √(1−x)=1−x/2+O(x²). For metricity, symmetry and positivity survive positive symmetric weighting, but the triangle inequality has no reason to do so and needs an independent hypothesis.

## Worked example: setup

Choose three equidistant points on a unit circle with common chord length √3. Assign factors 1/10,1/10,1 to pairs 12,23,13 respectively.

## Worked example: calculation

The modified distances are √3/10,√3/10,√3. The inequality d₁₃≤d₁₂+d₂₃ becomes √3≤√3/5, which is false. These factors are of the exponential form with α=1 and E₁₂=E₂₃=log10,E₁₃=0. Separately θ_n=θ₀e^(−n/ξ) is merely a chosen sequence; it does not repair metricity.

## Worked example: conclusion

The group formula and sequence asymptotic are exact, while the proposed weighted distance fails a basic required axiom in an explicit example.

## Limits

An arbitrary area scale multiplying the sequence does not prove a quantum-geometry spectrum. The exponential unitary is not a dissipative master equation, and decreasing a rotation angle does not establish friction.

## Extension

Construct a genuine weighted path metric by summing positive edge lengths and taking shortest paths, then compare it to the original pairwise expression.
