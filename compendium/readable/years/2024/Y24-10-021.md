## Revised question retained in the annual source

What exact local-to-global identity makes a rational height independent of coordinates?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered height discussion adds categorical, geometric and homotopical contributions and gives unexplained numerical totals. A classical Weil height is instead a well-defined function because normalized local absolute values satisfy the product formula. That condition can be checked completely overQ.

## Definitions

Forp prime, set|x|_p=p^(−v_p(x)); use the ordinary absolute value at∞.

For reduceda/b withb>0, defineh(a/b)=Σ_vlogmax(1,|a/b|_v). Only finitely many terms are nonzero.

## Result name

An exact rational height formula

## Result statement

h(a/b)=logmax(|a|,b). The projective formulah([a:b])=Σ_vlogmax(|a|_v,|b|_v) is invariant under simultaneous nonzero rational scaling.

## Proof

For reduceda,b, a finite place contributes tologmax(1,|a/b|_p) exactly whenp dividesb, and its contribution isv_p(b)logp. Their sum islogb. The infinite contribution islogmax(1,|a|/b), yieldinglogmax(|a|,b). For projective scaling byc, each local maximum is multiplied by|c|_v, so the total changes byΣ_vlog|c|_v. Writingc=±∏p^e_p giveslog|c|∞=Σe_plogp andΣ_plog|c|_p=−Σe_plogp, so the change is zero. This is the product formula, proved here directly by rational factorization.

Local contributions become an arithmetic invariant through an exact normalization identity. Summing unexplained numerical categories does not establish that identity.
