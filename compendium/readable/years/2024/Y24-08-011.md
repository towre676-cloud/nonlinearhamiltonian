## Summary

The closed unit disk over a nonarchimedean field gives a concrete way to distinguish formal models, analytic functions and seminorm points. The Gauss norm can be proved multiplicative, and a simple polynomial shows that the resulting Berkovich point is not evaluation at a single ordinary field-valued point.

## Definitions

Let K=Q_p with its usual nonarchimedean absolute value. The Tate algebra K⟨T⟩ consists of seriesΣa_nT^n with a_n→0, endowed with Gauss norm max_n|a_n|. A standard integral formal model uses Z_p⟨T⟩. A Berkovich point is an appropriate bounded multiplicative seminorm extending the absolute value on K.

## Result name

The Gauss norm is multiplicative and differs from point evaluation

## Result statement

For polynomials f,g over K, ||fg||_G=||f||_G||g||_G. Evaluation at any a with|a|≤1 satisfies|f(a)|≤||f||_G. The Gauss seminorm is not evaluation at any single such a∈K.

## Proof

The coefficient triangle inequality gives the upper bound. Rescale each nonzero polynomial by a coefficient of maximal absolute value, so both have integral coefficients and a nonzero reduction in F_p[T]. Their reductions have a nonzero product because that polynomial ring is a domain. Some coefficient of the product is therefore a unit, proving the lower bound after restoring the scales. The evaluation inequality follows termwise from the ultrametric inequality and|a|≤1. For any proposed evaluation point a, the polynomial T−a has Gauss norm one but evaluates to zero at a, disproving equality of these seminorms.

## Worked example: setup

Use p=3, f=1+3T and g=1+T.

## Worked example: calculation

Both Gauss norms are one, and fg=1+4T+3T² still has norm one. Their reductions are1 and1+T, whose product is nonzero. The formal reduction of the integral model records a special fiber over F₃, whereas the generic analytic disk concerns a K-algebra. The Gauss point records the whole unit-disk norm rather than choosing one residue or evaluation coordinate.

## Worked example: conclusion

The constructions are related by precise model and analytification procedures, not by interchangeable terminology.

## Limits

This polynomial proof is not a construction of all rigid or Berkovich geometry. Extensions to convergent series use completion, and generic-fiber conventions depend on the formal topology. No operadic correction is supplied by the mere existence of these spaces.

## Extension

Specify an actual affinoid algebra or formal model, determine its reduction and compare evaluation points with its multiplicative seminorm points.
