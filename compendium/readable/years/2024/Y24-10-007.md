## Revised question retained in the annual source

How can local real and p-adic terms be assembled into a mathematically defined global object?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered advanced operator adds terms belonging to different local settings. A sum of operators requires a common space and convergence. The restricted-product definition of adeles and a simple weighted tensor model clarify two distinct constructions that should not be conflated.

## Definitions

The adele ring A_Q consists of(x∞,(x_p)_p) with x∞∈R,x_p∈Q_p and x_p∈Z_p for all but finitely many primes.

On an infinite tensor product with a chosen reference vector, let P_k be commuting norm-one projections acting on the kth factor.

## Result name

A convergent local sum need not have compact resolvent

## Result statement

B=Σ_(k≥1)2^(−k)P_k converges in operator norm and is bounded self-adjoint. In the reference-sector binary tensor model it has infinitely many orthogonal eigenvectors with eigenvalues2^(−k), so it has no compact resolvent.

## Proof

The tail norm is bounded byΣ_(k>N)2^(−k)=2^(−N), proving norm convergence and self-adjointness. Choose each local space C² with reference vector e0, and P_ke1=e1,P_ke0=0. The vector v_k with e1 at sitek and e0 elsewhere is a legal finite excitation. These vectors are orthonormal and Bv_k=2^(−k)v_k. If(B+iI)⁻¹ were compact, its images of this bounded orthonormal sequence would have a convergent subsequence. But the images are(2^(−k)+i)⁻¹v_k, whose norms tend to1 and whose pairwise distances stay bounded away from zero. Compactness is impossible. Norm convergence of local terms therefore supplies a legitimate operator but not a discrete spectrum with eigenvalues tending to infinity.

## Worked example: setup

Compare the true adele x with x_p=1/p for every prime against a valid rational diagonal embedding.

## Worked example: calculation

The all-prime tuple1/p is not an adele, because1/p∉Z_p at every p. In contrast, a rational number a/b belongs to Z_p at all primes not dividing b, so its diagonal embedding is an adele. The restricted condition is structural, not an optional numerical cutoff.

## Worked example: conclusion

Both global space and spectral compactness need proof. Writing a real term beside infinitely many p-adic terms does not provide either.

## Limits

The tensor example is illustrative and is not identified with L²(A_Q). Restricted tensor products also require distinguished local vectors or subspaces and compatible measures. No global trace formula is inferred.

## Extension

Choose a precise adelic representation and common dense core. State local actions there and prove convergence or form bounds for the global sum before studying its trace or spectrum.
