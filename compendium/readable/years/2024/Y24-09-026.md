## Definitions

The real normed division algebras R,C,H,O have dimensions1,2,4,8. Their triality Lie algebras have dimensions0,2,9,28.

In the symmetric magic-square construction the underlying vector space is tri(A)⊕tri(B)⊕(A⊗B)³. A specific classical bracket, not an arbitrary direct-sum bracket, makes this a Lie algebra.

## Result name

Dimension audit and uniform bracket scaling

## Result statement

The dimension is t_A+t_B+3d_Ad_B. The resulting4×4 dimension table is [3,8,21,52]; [8,16,35,78]; [21,35,66,133]; [52,78,133,248]. Uniformly scaling any Lie bracket by a nonzero scalar gives an isomorphic Lie algebra.

## Proof

The dimension formula follows from dimensions of a direct sum and dim(A⊗B)=d_Ad_B. Substituting the four dimension pairs gives every table entry; for A=B=O it is28+28+3·64=248. Existence of the classical triality bracket and its exceptional identifications are cited structural inputs. For the scaling claim, let [x,y]_c=c[x,y]. Bilinearity and skew symmetry persist, and each term of the Jacobi sum acquires c², so it vanishes. The map F(x)=cx satisfies F([x,y]_c)=c²[x,y]=[F(x),F(y)] and is invertible for c≠0. Thus a uniform numerical deformation of structure constants supplies no new Lie-algebra isomorphism class. A nonlinear alteration of selected constants instead requires a fresh Jacobi check.

Its dimension remains248, while the dual Coxeter number of the complex simple type E8 is30 in the standard long-root normalization. Rescaling basis coordinates does not change that root-system type. At c=0 the bracket becomes zero and the algebra is abelian, showing that the nonzero-scalar assumption is essential.

## Limits

This entry proves the dimension arithmetic and scaling claim, not the full exceptional Lie-algebra construction. Real form, bracket conventions and root normalization must be fixed before comparing further invariants.

## Extension
