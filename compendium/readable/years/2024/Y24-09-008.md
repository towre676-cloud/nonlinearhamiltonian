## Revised question retained in the annual source

If a deformation changes a scalar field by at most ε, what persistent-topology comparison follows directly?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

Persistence becomes precise after choosing both a space and a filtration. The most elementary useful theorem is a pair of sublevel-set inclusions. It supplies a stable comparison without assuming the homology of an undefined geometric object.

## Definitions

For a fixed space X and a function f:X→R, let F_t={x:f(x)≤t}.

Write G_t for the sublevel sets of g. A shift by ε means replacing the parameter t by t+ε.

## Result name

Uniform perturbations yield an interleaving

## Result statement

If sup_X|f−g|≤ε, then F_t⊆G_(t+ε) and G_t⊆F_(t+ε) for every t. Applying homology gives maps whose twofold composites are the ordinary filtration maps shifted by2ε.

## Proof

If x∈F_t then g(x)≤f(x)+ε≤t+ε, which proves the first inclusion. Interchanging f and g proves the second. The composite inclusion F_t→G_(t+ε)→F_(t+2ε) sends each point to itself, so functoriality makes its induced homology map equal the structure map H_k(F_t)→H_k(F_(t+2ε)). The other composite works identically. This is the definition of an ε-interleaving at the level of persistent homology. For finite filtered complexes, additional algebraic results identify this with a bottleneck matching bound on interval decompositions; that stronger correspondence is a classical theorem, not proved merely by drawing similar curves.

## Worked example: setup

Take a graph with two vertices joined by one edge. Give its vertices f-values0 and2 and its edge value2; set g-values1 and2 on the vertices and3 on the edge.

## Worked example: calculation

With the lower-star convention replaced here by these explicitly assigned cell values, both are valid filtrations because each edge appears no earlier than its endpoints. The cellwise sup difference is1. In degree0, f has one eternal component and a zero-length merge event at2; g has a component born at1 and a second born at2 that dies at3. Their changes fit the1-shift inclusions directly.

## Worked example: conclusion

A small field perturbation controls persistence parameters, even when a short-lived component appears. It does not force every Betti number at each identical threshold to agree.

## Limits

The space and filtration remain fixed in this argument. Changing sampling, adjacency, coefficients or a geometric reconstruction requires a separate comparison map. Infinite spaces may need finiteness assumptions for barcode language.
