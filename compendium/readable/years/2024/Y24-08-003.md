## Proof

Linearity of expectation gives the mean. Centering R and expanding E[(Σw_i(R_i−m_i))²] gives the covariance quadratic form, including both off-diagonal cross terms. Compute the diagonal contributions .0064,.00765625,.005625 and the doubled cross contribution .0093; their sum is .02898125. The covariance is symmetric and its leading principal minors are positive, which can be checked with exact fractions, so it is a valid positive-definite matrix for this abstract model. Positive definiteness gives a nonnegative variance for every nonzero weighted combination.
