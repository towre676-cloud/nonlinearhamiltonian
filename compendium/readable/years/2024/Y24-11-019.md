## Revised question retained in the annual source

What does the recovered relation N(E)=S+Tα constrain, and what does it leave undecided?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; historical claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The integrated density of states counts states below an energy per lattice site. In a spectral gap it can have arithmetic labels. The label constrains a gap value, not an arbitrary eigenvalue, and its topological interpretation requires a specified two-parameter family.

## Definitions

For a rational flux P/Q, a gap above r of the Q band branches has normalized IDS r/Q.

A gap label N=S+Tα has integers S,T; at rational flux it implies r=QS+PT.

## Result name

Rational label congruence and ambiguity

## Result statement

If gcd(P,Q)=1, the equation r=QS+PT has integer solutions and determines T modulo Q. It does not determine a unique integer T without an additional selection rule or a Chern-number calculation.

## Proof

Bézout’s identity supplies integers a,b with aP+bQ=1. Multiplying by r gives T=ar,S=br as one solution. If (S,T) and (S′,T′) are solutions, Q(S−S′)=−P(T−T′). Coprimality forces T−T′ to be a multiple of Q and S−S′ the opposite multiple of P. Conversely every such pair solves the equation. Thus the label is a congruence class. For an actual isolated Bloch spectral projection over a two-torus, its first Chern number can select the Hall integer, but one must prove the projection is smooth and the gap stays open. An arithmetic equation alone neither proves existence of the gap nor chooses its orientation convention.

## Worked example: setup

Take α=2/5 and a putative gap above r=1 band.

## Worked example: calculation

1=5S+2T has solution S=1,T=−2, and all solutions are S=1−2k,T=−2+5k. A conventional representative |T|≤5/2 gives T=−2. For r=2, T=1,S=0 is a representative. If those gaps close, the equations remain soluble but no isolated spectral projection exists there; the labels do not create missing gaps.

## Worked example: conclusion

The old Wannier formula becomes an exact solvable arithmetic condition, with its spectral and topological assumptions separated.

## Limits

The irrational gap-label group Z+αZ is a deep operator-algebraic result; dense possible labels do not imply every label is realized by an open gap at every coupling. No irrational “infinitely many energy solutions” claim follows merely by algebra.

## Extension

For a fixed open rational gap, evaluate the Berry-curvature integral of the occupied Bloch bundle and verify it matches the chosen Diophantine integer under the same orientation conventions.
