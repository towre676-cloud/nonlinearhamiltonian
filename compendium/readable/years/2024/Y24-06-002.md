## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which matrices generate the claimed Lie algebra, and which actual flow has periodic rather than expanding trajectories?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or finite example and explicit scope

## Summary

The cyclic-universe passage supplied brackets, derived algebras and invariant subalgebras but no model whose periodicity followed from them. Matrix examples separate closure and solvability from the spectral condition that produces a cyclic orbit.

## Definitions

For matrices use [A,B]=AB−BA and the derived algebra g′=span of all brackets.

Set E=[[0,1],[0,0]], F=[[0,0],[1,0]], H=diag(1,−1), and J=E−F.

## Result name

Lie-bracket closure does not imply cyclic time evolution

## Result statement

The relations [H,E]=2E, [H,F]=−2F and [E,F]=H generate sl₂. Its derived algebra is itself. Nevertheless exp(tJ)=Icos t+Jsin t is periodic, while exp(tH)=diag(e^t,e^(−t)) is not periodic except on the zero state.

## Proof

Direct multiplication gives the three bracket relations and shows that every basis generator lies in the bracket span, proving g′=g. Since J²=−I, its exponential series separates into even and odd powers and gives the trigonometric formula. Since H is diagonal, its exponential has the displayed expanding and contracting factors. A nonzero vector fixed after positive time under exp(tH) would require one of e^t or e^(−t) to equal one, impossible for positive t. The algebra containing both generators therefore does not force either of their one-parameter subgroups to share the other’s behavior.

## Worked example: calculation

Under J the path is (cos t,−sin t), has Euclidean norm one and period2π. Under H it is (e^t,0), with exponentially changing norm. Also span{H,E} is a closed two-dimensional subalgebra with derived algebra span{E} and next derived algebra zero, whereas adding F yields the nonsolvable sl₂ algebra. These are exact algebraic distinctions independent of any cosmological labels. Both generators have trace zero, so both flows preserve oriented area: their determinants are always one. Area preservation therefore does not imply bounded trajectories or recurrence. This supplies an additional exact counterexample to replacing a dynamical periodicity argument by a Lie-algebra invariant.

## Worked example: conclusion

A claim about cycles needs a specified vector field, initial state and periodicity calculation. Bracket closure is a different theorem.
