## Proof

The common million-km² factor cancels, and rational arithmetic gives the displayed quotient. For positive B, the ratio increases with A and decreases with B, proving the interval enclosure. For packing, finite additivity gives area of the union of N disjoint-interior congruent copies as NB, and containment bounds this by A. The converse fails because area does not control width or shape. A rectangle of size 20 by 1/2 has area ten, larger than the area π of a unit-radius disk, but cannot contain even one such disk: the disk has vertical extent two under every rotation, exceeding the strip width. Thus surplus area can coexist with zero feasible copies. If the intended denominator is instead a union of selected zones, its area must first be defined; for two zones, |Z₁∪Z₂|=|Z₁|+|Z₂|−|Z₁∩Z₂|.
