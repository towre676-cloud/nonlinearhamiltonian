## Revised question retained in the annual source

What actual perfectoid object can be exhibited before modifying its structure sheaf?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered formula refers to a modified perfectoid structure sheaf, but no underlying ring or valuation is supplied. An explicit characteristic-p field gives a correct base example and makes the roles of nondiscreteness and completeness visible.

## Definitions

Fixp and start withK0=⋃_(n≥0)F_p((t^(1/p^n))), witht-adic valuation normalized byv(t)=1. LetK be its completion.

A rank-one valued field of residue characteristicp is perfectoid when it is complete, its valuation is nondiscrete and Frobenius is surjective on its power-bounded ring modulop.

## Result name

An explicit characteristic-p perfectoid field

## Result statement

The fieldK is perfectoid. Its value group containsp^(−n), so the valuation is nondiscrete, and Frobenius onK is bijective.

## Proof

Every Laurent series overF_p in the variablet^(1/p^n) has a pth root obtained by dividing its exponents byp and taking the unique pth roots of coefficients inF_p. This root lies at the next level, so Frobenius is bijective onK0. Ifx_m is a Cauchy sequence inK0, its rootsy_m satisfy|y_m−y_l|^p=|x_m−x_l| in characteristicp. They are therefore Cauchy and converge inK. Their limit is a pth root of the limit ofx_m, by continuity of multiplication. Frobenius is injective in a field, so it is bijective onK. It preserves the valuation ring in both directions. Sincep=0 inK, the quotient of that ring byp is the ring itself, and the required surjectivity follows. Completeness holds by construction and nondiscreteness follows fromv(t^(1/p^n))=p^(−n)→0.

## Worked example: setup

Atp=2, take the element1+t^(1/2).

## Worked example: calculation

Its square is1+t because the cross term vanishes in characteristic2. Its square root1+t^(1/4) appears at the next level. Arbitrarily small positive valuations are realized byt^(1/2^n), so this is not a discretely valued Laurent-series field.

## Worked example: conclusion

The definition can be verified on an explicit object before any cohomological comparison or height weighting is proposed.

## Limits

A multiplicative tilting correspondence does not identify characteristic0 and characteristicp structure sheaves as rings. Arbitrary exponentials of real-valued heights need not belong to a p-adic coefficient ring or define sheaf restriction maps.

## Extension

For a modification, specify the coefficient ring and the local sections of the intended sheaf, prove restriction compatibility and ring closure, then formulate a comparison map with stated coefficients.
