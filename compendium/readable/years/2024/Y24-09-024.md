## Revised question retained in the annual source

What concrete group-theoretic tests constrain a proposed exceptional automorphism of A6?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered passage names A6 outer automorphisms without specifying an actual map. Before proposing such a map, one can compute conjugacy classes and enforce automorphism invariants. This prevents an informal relabeling of cycle types from masquerading as an automorphism.

## Definitions

A6 consists of the360 even permutations of six symbols. An automorphism is a bijective homomorphism A6→A6.

An inner automorphism is conjugation by a group element. Outer means its class modulo inner automorphisms is nontrivial.

## Result name

Necessary invariants and an exact class inventory

## Result statement

Every automorphism preserves element order, centralizer size and conjugacy-class size. A6 has class sizes1,45,40,40,90,72,72, corresponding to types1,(2,2),(3),(3,3),(4,2) and two split5-cycle classes.

## Proof

For an automorphism φ, φ(g^m)=φ(g)^m, and applying φ⁻¹ gives equality of orders. The map x↦φ(x) bijects C(g) with C(φ(g)), because commuting equations are preserved in both directions. Orbit–stabilizer then preserves class size. Count double transpositions by choosing their four symbols and one of three pairings:15·3=45. Three-cycles number C(6,3)·2=40. Products of two disjoint three-cycles number(1/2)C(6,3)·2²=40. Type(4,2) has C(6,4)·6=90. There are6·24=144 five-cycles. Their S6 class splits in A6: the centralizer of a five-cycle is its cyclic order5 subgroup and contains no odd permutation, so each A6 orbit has360/5=72 elements. The sizes total360 and exhaust the group.

## Worked example: calculation

The first proposal fails immediately because orders3 and5 differ. The second passes these necessary tests: both relevant classes have size40 and centralizer order9. Passing does not prove that an extension to all360 elements is a homomorphism. A full certificate must verify products, or provide a structural construction such as the exceptional S6 action and its restriction.
