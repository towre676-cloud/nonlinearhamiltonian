## Result statement

The squared Haar coefficients sum to the original squared data norm. For the stated gap vector the energy is65, decomposed as441/8 at the coarsest mean,9/8 at the coarsest detail,9/4 at the next details and13/2 at the finest details.

## Proof

For each pair, ((a+b)/√2)²+((a−b)/√2)²=a²+b². Every recursive stage is therefore orthogonal and preserves total squared norm. The initial energy is1+4+4+16+4+16+4+16=65. The first-stage details are−1/√2,−2/√2,−2/√2,−2/√2, whose squared sum is13/2. The next-stage details are−3/2 and0, contributing9/4. The final detail is−3/(2√2), contributing9/8, while the final average is21/(2√2), contributing441/8. Adding these rational energies gives(441+9+18+52)/8=65. The computation is exact even if floating-point square roots are used for a visual display.
