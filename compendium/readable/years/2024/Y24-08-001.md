## Revised question retained in the annual source

What diagrams are produced by which filtration, and what exact distance distinguishes a real change from a coordinate perturbation?

## Status

Revised classical development with scoped worked result

## Summary

Persistence diagrams are multisets of birth-death pairs rather than ordinary point clouds with a fixed number of points. Their distance permits matching a feature to the diagonal, so comparing only correspondingly numbered points is insufficient. A two-feature example can be solved exhaustively and shows how a precise diagram question differs from an unspecified claim about three-dimensional features.

## Definitions

A finite off-diagonal persistence diagram consists of pairs(b,d) with b<d. Use L∞ point cost max(|b−b′|,|d−d′|); matching(b,d) to the diagonal costs(d−b)/2. The bottleneck distance minimizes the maximum cost over partial bijections, with unmatched points matched to the diagonal.

## Result name

An exact finite bottleneck certificate

## Result statement

For D={(0,3),(1,2)} and E={(1/10,16/5),(4/5,23/10)}, the bottleneck distance is3/10. Pairing the long features and the short features attains it, and no alternative matching has a smaller maximum cost.

## Proof

The long-to-long cost is1/5 and the short-to-short cost is3/10. The cross pairing sends(0,3) to(4/5,23/10) at cost4/5, already too large. Leaving any feature unmatched has diagonal cost at least1/2 for the short D feature,3/4 for the short E feature, or more for either long feature. Thus no partial matching other than the direct full matching can have maximum below3/10. This proves optimality without relying on a numerical assignment routine. The same finite enumeration can certify other small diagrams.

## Worked example: calculation

The direct matching has squared2-Wasserstein cost(1/5)²+(3/10)²=13/100. Every cross or diagonal alternative has a larger squared sum in this example, giving distance√13/10. These two metrics summarize perturbation differently even though they share the minimizing pairing here.

## Worked example: conclusion

An exact distance calculation is meaningful only after the filtration and coefficient conventions defining the diagrams are supplied.

## Limits

The old source supplies no actual filtration, diagrams or measured time series. The example proves a metric calculation, not stability of an unspecified processing pipeline. General persistence stability theorems need their own hypotheses.

## Extension

Construct a finite lower-star filtration explicitly, compute its intervals and propagate a sup-norm bound on vertex values into a diagram-distance certificate.
