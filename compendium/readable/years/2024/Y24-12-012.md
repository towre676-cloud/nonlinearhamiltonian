## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which statements follow from a traveling-wave ansatz or a Laurent polynomial, and when is a layered field expression an equation?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; historical claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered formulas combined sinusoidal traveling waves, a Laurent polynomial, and multiplicative functions of a field and its derivatives. A wave ansatz and a discrete convolution symbol have precise meanings; a self-referential product still needs a specified equation and function spaces.

## Definitions

For k≠0, y(x,t)=A sin(kx−ωt) has phase speed ω/k.

On ℓ²(Z), let S be the unitary shift and L=(S+S^(−1))−(S²+S^(−2)).

## Result name

Wave dispersion and a bounded self-adjoint Laurent operator

## Result statement

The ansatz satisfies y_tt=c²y_xx exactly when ω²=c²k² or A=0. The Fourier symbol of L is ℓ(θ)=2cosθ−2cos2θ, so L is bounded self-adjoint with spectrum contained in [−4,9/4].

## Proof

Differentiating twice gives y_tt=−ω²y and y_xx=−k²y, proving the wave relation. Since S*=S^(−1), L*=L. Fourier transformation sends S to multiplication by e^(iθ) and hence L to multiplication by the stated real Laurent symbol. Put x=cosθ∈[−1,1]. Then ℓ=2x−2(2x²−1)=−4x²+2x+2. This concave quadratic has maximum 9/4 at x=1/4 and minimum −4 at x=−1; the full continuous range is therefore [−4,9/4]. This is sharper than the immediate norm bound four. A field expression ψ=f·μ(x,curlψ)·η(ψ_t,∇ψ) is an implicit nonlinear relation. For scalar ψ in three dimensions curlψ is undefined unless one introduces a vector field or another explicit interpretation.

## Worked example: setup

Take θ=0,π, and arccos(1/4).

## Worked example: calculation

The symbol values are 0,−4,9/4 respectively. Thus the operator is neither positive nor a dissipative generator without a sign or shift adjustment. For the wave ansatz, changing k while holding ω fixed changes phase speed; choosing ω and k independently does not prove the ansatz solves a prescribed wave equation.

## Worked example: conclusion

The recovered formulas become separate, well-defined mathematical objects with exact identities and a clear type check.

## Limits

This is not a derivation of a nonlinear fluid or electromagnetic model, and a Laurent polynomial is not automatically a Jones polynomial under any normalization.

## Extension

If an implicit nonlinear equation is intended, specify a Banach space and use a local contraction or implicit-function theorem with actual derivative bounds.
