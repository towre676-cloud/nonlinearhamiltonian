## Revised question retained in the annual source

What identities involving 24 are actually proved by the Eisenstein series, and why is E₂ quasimodular rather than modular?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered discussion linked the coefficient24 to splitting primes, Hilbert–Pólya, and musical patterns. Its genuine mathematical content is the normalized Eisenstein series and Dedekind eta product. These give exact identities but no deduction of RH or a particle coupling.

## Definitions

For Imτ>0 set q=e^(2πiτ), E₂(τ)=1−24Σ_{n≥1}σ₁(n)q^n.

Let η(τ)=q^(1/24)∏_{n≥1}(1−q^n), and Δ=η²⁴=(E₄³−E₆²)/1728.

## Result name

Logarithmic derivative and the elliptic fixed point

## Result statement

q d(logη)/dq=E₂/24. E₂(−1/τ)=τ²E₂(τ)+6τ/(πi), while E₂*=E₂−3/(π Imτ) transforms with weight two. With j=E₄³/Δ, j(i)=1728.

## Proof

Logarithmically differentiate the eta product in |q|<1: qdlogη/dq=1/24−Σ_{n≥1}nq^n/(1−q^n). Expanding geometric series and collecting the coefficient of q^m gives Σ_{n|m}n=σ₁(m), proving the derivative identity. The quoted eta transformation η(−1/τ)=√(−iτ)η(τ), with a consistent branch, differentiates to the extra E₂ term; subtracting 3/(πImτ) cancels it using Im(−1/τ)=Imτ/|τ|². Finally the weight-six modular transformation gives E₆(i)=i⁶E₆(i)=−E₆(i), hence E₆(i)=0. Since Δ has no zero in the upper half-plane, its defining identity gives j(i)=1728.
