## Revised question retained in the annual source

What conditions make the recovered sum of crystal-field, spin, and extra operators a valid finite Hamiltonian?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; historical claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered Hamiltonian is a broad sum of operators with position- and time-dependent coefficients. It becomes mathematically defined only after a Hilbert space, operator domains, and coefficient conventions are supplied. A two-spin sector gives a fully explicit check.

## Definitions

On C²⊗C² set S_i^a=σ^a/2 on the i-th spin, with ℏ=1.

For a finite operator sum H(t)=Σc_r(t)A_r, assume A_r*=A_r and the coefficients c_r(t) are real and continuous.

## Result name

Hermitian finite evolution and exchange eigenvalues

## Result statement

H(t) is Hermitian and its evolution is unitary. The exchange operator J S₁·S₂ has eigenvalue J/4 on the three-dimensional triplet and−3J/4 on the singlet.

## Proof

Taking the adjoint of each term in the finite sum proves H*=H. For U′=−iHU,U(0)=I, differentiate U*U: U′*U+U*U′=iU*HU−iU*HU=0, hence U*U=I. Existence follows from a finite linear ODE with continuous coefficients. For the exchange spectrum, define S_tot=S₁+S₂ and expand S_tot²=S₁²+S₂²+2S₁·S₂. Each spin-half has S_i²=3I/4. The symmetric triplet has total spin one and S_tot²=2, while the antisymmetric singlet has total spin zero and S_tot²=0, yielding the energies. The same result follows from S₁·S₂=(Swap)/2−I/4.

## Worked example: setup

Take H=J S₁·S₂+h(S₁^z+S₂^z).

## Worked example: calculation

The states|↑↑⟩,(|↑↓⟩+|↓↑⟩)/√2,|↓↓⟩ have energies J/4+h,J/4,J/4−h; the singlet (|↑↓⟩−|↓↑⟩)/√2 has energy−3J/4. A further Hermitian termξ(t)Φ keeps evolution unitary for realξ(t), but generally mixes these eigenstates and need not preserve the exchange symmetry.

## Worked example: conclusion

The recovered operator form admits a concrete Hermiticity audit and a complete finite diagonalization.

## Limits

The historical coefficients and operators were not specified sufficiently to compute a material spectrum. A hyperfield multiplicative inverse is not the inverse of a linear operator or a second simultaneous configuration. No interacting continuum theory is constructed.

## Extension
