## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which dimension formula follows from a specified moment-map quotient, and what hypotheses prevent a formal count from being misleading?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered assistant passage invokes magnetic quivers and dimensions but does not supply a quiver, representation or quotient. A one-node framed example shows how a valid dimension count is obtained. It is a Higgs-type symplectic quotient model; it is not silently identified with an unspecified Coulomb branch.

## Definitions

Let z∈C^m and w∈(C^m)*. C* acts by(z,w)↦(tz,t⁻¹w), preserving the canonical symplectic form.

The complex moment map is μ(z,w)=w(z). Restrict to μ=0 and z≠0, and take the geometric C* quotient.

## Result name

An explicit cotangent-bundle quotient

## Result statement

The quotient {w(z)=0,z≠0}/C* is T*P^(m−1), with complex dimension2m−2, whose half is m−1. A hyperkähler interpretation additionally requires real moment-map and metric data.

## Proof

The vector z determines a line ℓ=Cz in C^m. The covector w vanishes on ℓ, and the rank-one tensor z⊗w defines a linear map C^m/ℓ→ℓ. Under(z,w)↦(tz,t⁻¹w) this map is unchanged. The space Hom(C^m/ℓ,ℓ) is canonically the cotangent space atℓ, since the tangent space is Hom(ℓ,C^m/ℓ). Conversely every such cotangent vector can be written z⊗w after choosing a nonzero z∈ℓ, with different choices related exactly by C*. This proves the bundle identification. In coordinates, the ambient complex dimension is2m; the moment-map equation has surjective differential on z≠0 because varying w can change w(z) arbitrarily. It removes one dimension. The free C* action removes another, giving2m−2. In the chart z1≠0, the remaining m−1 coordinates of z and m−1 free covector coordinates of w account for all2m−2 dimensions. Other charts glue by the usual cotangent transition law, so this local calculation is compatible with the global bundle description rather than merely an informal equation count.

## Worked example: setup

Take m=2 and work in the chart z1≠0.

## Worked example: calculation

Use the C* action to set z=(1,u). The equation w1+uw2=0 gives w=(−up,p), so(u,p) are two free complex coordinates. The canonical one-form w·dz becomes p du in this gauge, confirming the cotangent chart. For m=1 the equation forces w=0 and the quotient is a point, consistent with dimension0.

## Worked example: conclusion

A quotient dimension follows from actual independent equations and a free action, and can be checked in local coordinates.

## Limits

If z=0 is allowed, stabilizers and singular strata appear and the regular quotient argument no longer applies unchanged. A Coulomb branch has a different construction; a formal similarity of dimension formulas is insufficient to identify it with this example.

## Extension

For a specified quiver and dimension vectors, write all moment-map equations, identify the stability condition and verify freeness on the chosen locus. Record singular strata separately before assigning a global dimension formula.
