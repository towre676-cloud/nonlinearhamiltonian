## Revised question retained in the annual source

What elementary obstruction can refute an undefined comparison before advanced machinery is invoked?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered period and comparison maps omit their source and target categories and coefficient rings. An elementary point example shows why these omissions are decisive. Even when both sides have cohomology concentrated in degree0, different coefficient groups need not be isomorphic.

## Definitions

A quasi-isomorphism of complexes induces an isomorphism on every cohomology group in the stated coefficient category.

For a complex consisting of a ringR in degree0, its only cohomology isH0=R.

## Result name

A coefficient obstruction and a valid derived reduction

## Result statement

The degree0 complexesZ_p andF_p are not quasi-isomorphic as complexes of abelian groups. ButZ_p⊗^L_(Z_p)F_p≃F_p, so an explicitly stated base change gives a valid comparison.

## Proof

Every element of the additive groupF_p is killed byp. InZ_p, multiplication byp is injective and1 is not killed byp. Hence their degree0 cohomology groups are not isomorphic, ruling out a quasi-isomorphism. For the derived tensor product overZ_p, the moduleZ_p is free of rank1 over itself, so no higher resolution is needed:Z_p⊗_(Z_p)F_p=F_p concentrated in degree0. As a second caution, tensoringF_p with itself overZ_p is different: the free resolution[Z_p —p→ Z_p] gives after tensoring the complex[F_p —0→ F_p], with nonzeroTor1 as well as degree0. Thus even an apparently harmless coefficient substitution may introduce a higher derived term.

## Worked example: setup

Usep=3 and compare the three complexes just described.

## Worked example: calculation

H0(Z_3)=Z_3 andH0(F_3)=F_3 differ by torsion. The base-changedZ_3 complex has onlyF_3 in degree0. The self-derived tensorF_3⊗^L_(Z_3)F_3 hasF_3 in degrees0 and−1, so replacing derived tensor by ordinary tensor would lose a cohomology group.

## Worked example: conclusion

Coefficient and base-change data are part of a theorem’s content. They cannot be supplied by a shared notation or a claimed period-map analogy.

## Limits

This does not identify either historical symbol with these elementary complexes. It gives a necessary consistency test for any precise formulation. Perfectoid and prismatic comparisons have additional hypotheses beyond this coefficient check.

## Extension

Write the proposed map between explicit complexes over an explicit base ring, state every tensor operation as ordinary or derived, and test it first on a point and on a non-flat coefficient example.
