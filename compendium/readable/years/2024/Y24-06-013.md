## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which part of the recovered symmetry is a vector-field bracket, and what additional cocycle defines its central extension?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or finite example and explicit scope

## Summary

The Virasoro and Ward-identity fragment invokes an infinite-dimensional algebra. Its concrete content can be developed without claiming a full conformal field theory: compute the vector-field bracket, specify the central cocycle, and prove the Jacobi identity for that extension.

## Definitions

On Laurent polynomials let ℓ_m=−z^(m+1)d/dz for m∈Z. The Witt bracket is the commutator of derivations.

Introduce a central symbol C, with [C,L_m]=0, and set [L_m,L_n]=(m−n)L_(m+n)+((m³−m)/12)δ_(m+n,0)C. In a representation C may act by a scalar c.

## Result name

Witt brackets and the Virasoro central term

## Result statement

These brackets define the Virasoro Lie algebra. The span of L_−1,L_0,L_1 is an sl₂ subalgebra with no central contribution, and the noncentral quotient is the Witt algebra.

## Proof

Apply the commutator of ℓ_m and ℓ_n to z^r. The two second-derivative contributions cancel, leaving (m−n)ℓ_(m+n)z^r. This proves the Witt bracket and Jacobi follows from the commutator algebra of linear operators. For the central extension it remains to check the central part of Jacobi. It vanishes unless m+n+p=0. When that sum is zero, its coefficient, up to the factor1/12, is the cyclic sum of (m−n)((m+n)³−(m+n)). Substitute p=−m−n and expand: the cubic and linear terms cancel separately. Antisymmetry follows because m³−m is odd when n=−m. Thus the displayed extension is a Lie algebra. For m=−1,0,1 the polynomial m³−m is zero, so their brackets close without C.

## Worked example: calculation

[L₁,L_−1]=2L₀, while [L₂,L_−2]=4L₀+C/2. Choosing E=L₁, F=−L_−1 and H=−2L₀ gives [H,E]=2E, [H,F]=−2F and [E,F]=H. Thus a finite sl₂ subalgebra is visible, but it does not remove the independent central direction detected by modes±2. The verifier checks the cocycle cancellation on a finite range as an arithmetic check; the polynomial calculation supplies the general proof.

## Worked example: conclusion

An explicitly normalized central extension replaces the vague assertion that conformal symmetry automatically supplies a particular physical model.

## Limits

The mode algebra alone supplies no Hilbert space, positive-energy representation, operator domains or stress-tensor correlators. Ward identities require those further data. The central element is algebraic and is not automatically a measured central charge.

## Extension

Specify a highest-weight module, its vector L₀-eigenvalue and the action of C, then calculate low-level Gram matrices before making positivity claims.
