What does the recovered five-point harmonic-oscillator matrix actually certify, and can its eigenvalues establish a zeta-zero correspondence?

## Summary

The historical list contained a negative eigenvalue for −d²/dx²+x² on a very coarse grid. The operator and boundary convention must be reconstructed before interpreting a spectrum. Positivity already supplies a decisive audit independent of numerical diagonalization.

## Definitions

Use grid x_j=−10+5j, j=0,…,4, and zero ghost values at j=−1,5.

Let L be the tridiagonal matrix with diagonal 2 and adjacent entries −1; set S=L/25+diag(100,25,0,25,100).

## Result name

Discrete quadratic-form positivity

## Result statement

For every nonzero v∈C^5, v*Sv=(1/25)Σ_{j=−1}^4|v_{j+1}−v_j|²+Σx_j²|v_j|²>0, where v_{−1}=v_5=0. Thus every eigenvalue is positive.

## Proof

Expand each squared difference. Every interior |v_j|² occurs twice and every adjacent cross-term occurs with coefficient −2, recovering v*Lv. If the sum of difference squares vanishes, every value equals both boundary ghost values, so v=0. The potential term is nonnegative. The spectral theorem now gives strict positivity of all eigenvalues. Gershgorin bounds additionally place the central row in [0,0.16], the two next rows in [25,25.16], and the endpoint rows in [100.04,100.12]. The lowest eigenvalue is at most the central Rayleigh quotient 0.08. Refinement and domain enlargement are separate error limits; a five-point grid of spacing five cannot resolve the continuum oscillator’s low modes accurately.

## Worked example: calculation

The negative first value violates positivity; 24.88 and 99.88 lie outside the displayed Gershgorin union. For the central coordinate vector e_2, e_2*Se_2=2/25=0.08. These facts are exact rational checks and do not depend on rounding an eigensolver output.

## Worked example: conclusion

The old list cannot be the spectrum of the specified positive matrix. Correct numerical spectra still would not establish a relation to zeros of an unrelated analytic function.

## Limits

The historical boundary conditions and source code were not recovered, so this is a corrected reproducible model, not a claim to reconstruct the exact old implementation. An identity Ψ(1/2+iλ)=0 requires an independently defined Ψ and a proof.
