## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which Lorentz-group component and metric signature are chosen, and how do translations and linear transformations compose?

## Status

Revised classical development with scoped worked result

## Summary

The product law is the central mathematical content of the symmetry claim. Writing it explicitly distinguishes a direct product from a semidirect one, identifies the inverse and gives a finite exact test of metric preservation. Dimension and group-component choices then become declared parts of the structure rather than interchangeable labels.

## Definitions

Use Minkowski metricη=diag(−1,1,1,1). A Lorentz matrix Λ satisfiesΛᵀηΛ=η. If desired restrict to the proper orthochronous component SO⁺(1,3); the source’s SO notation alone does not state a time-orientation restriction. A Poincaré element(a,Λ) acts byx↦Λx+a.

## Result name

Composition law and an exact boost-translation commutator

## Result statement

The product is(a,Λ)(b,M)=(a+Λb,ΛM), with inverse(−Λ⁻¹a,Λ⁻¹). Translations preserve differences and Lorentz matrices preserve their Minkowski square. The two subgroups generally do not commute.

## Proof

Compose the affine actions directly: Λ(Mx+b)+a=(ΛM)x+(a+Λb). Solving for an affine inverse gives the displayed formula. For two points, translations cancel, and the metric condition gives(Λ(x−y))ᵀηΛ(x−y)=(x−y)ᵀη(x−y). A pure Lorentz transformation conjugates a translation b to a translation Λb, so their commutator is translation byΛb−b. This is nonzero whenever b is not fixed by Λ.

## Worked example: setup

In the time-space plane use the exact boost L=[[5/3,4/3],[4/3,5/3]], embedded with identity on the other coordinates, and b=(1,0,0,0).

## Worked example: calculation

Its determinant is one and Lᵀdiag(−1,1)L=diag(−1,1). The translation commutator is(2/3,4/3,0,0), proving noncommutativity with exact fractions. The real Lie-group dimension in four spacetime dimensions is4+6=10; the six Lorentz directions correspond to the six independent antisymmetric generators relative toη.

## Worked example: conclusion

The representation fixes signature, action, composition and component conventions. Different dimensions or signatures define related but distinct groups.

## Limits

Conformal and super-Poincaré extensions introduce additional structure and are not obtained merely by changing a parameter in this finite matrix example. A symmetry group does not determine a field equation or spectrum on its own.

## Extension

Specify a representation and invariant operator, then calculate how its eigenspaces transform and which observables distinguish translation from boost covariance.
