## Revised question retained in the annual source

What is the domain of n, which constants and units are fixed, and what follows algebraically before any physical interpretation is attempted?

## Status

## Summary

The two formulas can be developed as stipulated scaling laws without endorsing a new gravitational or quantum model. Their mathematical consequences include exact ratios, logarithmic elasticities and a direct relation between the two observables. Finite agreement with such consequences remains insufficient to identify the law outside the observed domain.

## Definitions

Take n>0 dimensionless and G₀,Λ₀ fixed nonzero constants with their own declared units. Define G(n)=G₀n⁻² and L(n)=Λ₀n⁻⁸. The label L avoids confusing the second quantity with a linear operator in this example. If n is restricted to integers, derivatives concern a chosen continuous interpolation.

## Result name

Exact ratios and a nonidentifiability construction

## Result statement

For a,b>0, G(b)/G(a)=(a/b)² and L(b)/L(a)=(a/b)⁸. Moreover L(n)/Λ₀=[G(n)/G₀]⁴. On the stated continuous model, the logarithmic elasticities are−2 and−8.

## Proof

Divide each power law at two positive inputs to cancel its constant. Eliminating n gives the fourth-power relation. Differentiate logG=logG₀−2logn and the corresponding L expression when the constants and functions are positive; for fixed-sign nonzero quantities, use logarithms of absolute values. To see the finite-data limit, take any finite observed set{n_j} and define G̃(n)=G₀n⁻²exp(ε∏_j(n−n_j)). It agrees at every listed point yet differs elsewhere for ε≠0. Positivity is preserved when G₀>0, so that condition alone cannot eliminate the ambiguity.

## Worked example: setup

Use a=2,b=4 and constants normalized to one.

## Worked example: calculation

The G ratio is1/4 and the L ratio is1/256, exactly the fourth power of1/4. At n=2, G=1/4 and L=1/256. These are consequences of the assumptions, not independent evidence for them. The perturbed function using observed inputs2 and4 passes both G values while changing its derivative and its values at other inputs.

## Worked example: conclusion

A proposed scaling programme becomes falsifiable only after the meaning, domain and measurement of n are specified independently.

## Limits

No physical evolution of a quantum number, frequency-shift prediction or variation of constants is established. The historical framework supplies formulas but not an accepted governing theory or an uncertainty-calibrated dataset. Its acronym is not treated as a theorem.

## Extension

State an identifiable observation model for n and the constants, compare the power-law family with explicit alternatives, and report uncertainty in the exponents before transferring the formulas into a physical prediction.
