## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can the recovered mutation discussion be exhibited as a complete symbolic calculation rather than a quantum analogy?

## Status

## Summary

The simplest coefficient-free A₂ cluster algebra has a finite mutation pattern and an elementary Laurent phenomenon that can be proved directly. This provides a rigorous common starting point for the recovered cluster, quiver, and root-system vocabulary.

## Definitions

Work in Q(x,y) with x₁=x,x₂=y and recurrence x_{m−1}x_{m+1}=1+x_m.

Mutation replaces one cluster variable by the quotient demanded by this exchange relation; the variables are rational functions, not arbitrary numerical states.

## Result name

Five-periodic A₂ exchange sequence

## Result statement

The sequence is x,y,(1+y)/x,(1+x+y)/(xy),(1+x)/y,x,y,… . Thus x_{m+5}=x_m as rational functions and every displayed variable is Laurent in x,y with nonnegative integer coefficients.

## Proof

The first step gives x₃=(1+y)/x. The second gives x₄=(1+x₃)/y=(x+1+y)/(xy). Next x₅=(1+x₄)/x₃; simplifying its numerator xy+x+y+1=(x+1)(y+1) cancels the common factor y+1 and gives(x+1)/y. Then x₆=(1+x₅)/x₄ simplifies to x, and x₇=(1+x₆)/x₅ simplifies to y. Since the recurrence is deterministic in the rational function field, the pair repeats and proves the entire five-periodicity. Laurentness and positive coefficients are visible in each formula. Numerical specialization at zeros of denominators requires care: identities in a rational function field do not automatically define every mutation at a singular point.

## Worked example: setup

Specialize x=y=1 after the rational identities are established.

## Worked example: calculation

The values are1,1,2,3,2,1,1. The exchange checks include1·3=1+2,2·2=1+3,and3·1=1+2. The five clusters correspond to neighboring pairs in the pentagon, so no infinite set of distinct variables arises in this finite A₂ example.

## Worked example: conclusion

The mutation calculation is complete and produces an exact periodicity and Laurent certificate.

## Limits

This is the classical commutative coefficient-free algebra. Quantum cluster variables require a noncommutative torus and ordering factors; a unitary gate or entanglement interpretation does not follow from the rational recurrence.

## Extension

Introduce coefficients or a skew-commutation relation explicitly and rederive the exchange expressions, preserving the order of factors in a quantum version.
