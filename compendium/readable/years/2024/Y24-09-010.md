## Revised question retained in the annual source

What can be established explicitly when a deformation is said to carry Hochschild and K-theoretic invariants?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

Naming several invariants does not make them interchangeable. An exact matrix example supplies two computable definitions and shows why a topological-cycle rank cannot simply be reused as a Hochschild or K-theory rank.

## Definitions

For a complex algebra A, HH0(A)=A/[A,A], where [A,A] is the linear span of commutators xy−yx.

For a unital C*-algebra, K0 is the group completion of stable projection classes. For complex matrix algebras these classes are classified by ordinary rank after stabilization.

## Result name

An explicit matrix calculation

## Result statement

HH0(M₂(C))≅C via trace, whereas K0(M₂(C))≅Z via stable rank. With the rank-one projection as generator, the unit matrix has K0-class2.

## Proof

Trace annihilates every commutator, since Tr(XY)=Tr(YX). Conversely, write Eij for matrix units. The off-diagonal units satisfy E12=[E11,E12] and E21=[E22,E21], while E11−E22=[E12,E21]. These three matrices span the traceless subspace. Hence the commutator span is exactly the kernel of trace, so the quotient is one-dimensional and trace identifies it with C. For K0, a complex projection is unitarily diagonalizable with diagonal entries0 or1. Under direct sum, ranks add. Every nonnegative rank occurs among matrix algebras over M₂(C), and equality of rank gives stable equivalence. The resulting monoid is N and its group completion is Z. The identity in M₂(C) has rank2, explaining the normalization of its class. The trace used in the Hochschild quotient is unnormalized, so the identity matrix maps to2. A normalized trace would send it to1 and change the numerical comparison with K0, without changing either invariant group. This small normalization choice is another reason to state a proposed comparison map explicitly.

## Worked example: setup

Consider P=diag(1,0) and Q=diag(0,1).

## Worked example: calculation

P−Q=[E12,E21] vanishes in HH0. In K0 the projections P and Q are equivalent via the matrix unit E21, so [P]−[Q]=0 as well. However Tr(P)=1 is a complex scalar while [P] is an integer class; these live in different coefficient groups. The trace induces a particular map between them after choosing the normalization.

## Worked example: conclusion

Explicit definitions explain both agreements and distinctions. A common symbol or shared rank does not identify the theories.

## Limits

This proves only degree-zero Hochschild homology and K0 for this matrix algebra. Higher Hochschild/cyclic groups, topological K-theory of spaces and algebraic K-theory require their own definitions. No general deformation-invariance theorem is asserted.

## Extension

For a proposed starred algebra, first give its multiplication and involution. Then exhibit an algebra isomorphism or compute a genuinely changed invariant, rather than transferring a graph’s Betti number by analogy.
