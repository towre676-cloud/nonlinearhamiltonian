## Revised question retained in the annual source

What actual constraints does symmetry place on a Landau polynomial or a tight-binding Hamiltonian?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; historical claims corrected where needed

## Summary

The recovered mineral narrative proposed representation branching, a quartic free energy, and a tight-binding sum. These are useful mathematical models only after the symmetry action and coefficients are stated. Two small models give exact conclusions without inventing mineral assignments.

## Definitions

Take a real scalar order parameter η and F(η)=F₀+aη²+bη³+cη⁴ with c>0.

A two-site periodic chain with real hoppings t₁,t₂ has Bloch matrix H(k)=[[0,t₁+t₂e^(−ik)],[t₁+t₂e^(ik),0]].

## Result name

Parity restriction, coexistence, and two-band spectrum

## Result statement

Ifη↦−η is a symmetry, b=0; when b=0 the minimizing order parameter is0 for a≥0 and±√(−a/(2c)) for a<0. With b≠0 a nonzero minimum can coexist with0 at a=b²/(4c),η=−b/(2c). The Bloch energies are±|t₁+t₂e^(ik)|.

## Proof

Even symmetry requires F(η)=F(−η) for everyη, forcing the cubic coefficient to vanish. For b=0, F′=2η(a+2cη²), and comparing the nonzero critical values gives the stated minima. For coexistence withη≠0, equal free energies require a+bη+cη²=0; stationarity requires2a+3bη+4cη²=0. Subtracting twice the first equation yields bη+2cη²=0, givingη=−b/(2c) and a=b²/(4c). For the Bloch matrix, its characteristic polynomial isλ²−|t₁+t₂e^(ik)|², proving the band formula. The off-diagonal form anticommutes with diag(1,−1), enforcing energy reflection.

## Worked example: setup

Take b=2,c=1,a=1 and t₁=t₂=1.

## Worked example: calculation

F−F₀=η²(η+1)² has equal global minimaη=0 and−1, demonstrating the coexistence formula. The chain energies are±2|cos(k/2)| and touch at k=π. With t₁≠t₂ the minimum positive energy is|t₁−t₂|, so the central gap is2|t₁−t₂|.
