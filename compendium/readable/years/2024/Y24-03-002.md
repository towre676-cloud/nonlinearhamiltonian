## Definitions

A representation of a finite group G on V assigns linear maps ρ(g) compatible with composition. Its fixed space is V^G={v:ρ(g)v=v for every g}.

For pullback actions, one may use g↦(g⁻¹)* to obtain a left representation; for an involution this convention makes no difference.

The retrieved label “Picard intransigence” is nonstandard and does not by itself define a linear map whose corank could be taken.

## Result name

Replace Picard intransigence by a defined fixed-space calculation

## Result statement

For a finite real representation, P=|G|⁻¹Σ_gρ(g) is a projection onto V^G. Its kernel is the span of all ρ(g)v−v. For the factor-swap involution of P¹×P¹, the invariant divisor subspace has dimension one.

## Proof

Multiplying P by ρ(h) merely permutes the summands, so every vector Pv is fixed. Conversely a fixed vector is unchanged by averaging, hence P acts as its identity and P²=P. Each difference ρ(g)v−v is killed by P. In the other direction v−Pv is an average of v−ρ(g)v, so every kernel vector belongs to the difference span. This proves the decomposition without assigning a vague scalar to the group itself. In the product-surface basis F₁,F₂, the swap is J=[[0,1],[1,0]], and P=(I+J)/2. Its image is span(F₁+F₂), while its kernel is span(F₁−F₂).

## Worked example: setup

Use the factor swap and an automorphism acting by a nontrivial Möbius transformation on the first P¹ factor only.

## Worked example: calculation

The swap has rank(J−I)=1 and fixed dimension one. The factorwise Möbius automorphism acts trivially on both divisor classes, so its induced matrix is I despite being a nonidentity geometric map. Thus zero action rank does not mean the geometric automorphism is trivial.

## Worked example: conclusion

A repaired invariant is now computable and its blindness is exhibited, rather than hidden behind a new name.

## Limits

Averaging over an infinite group is not justified by a finite sum. Finite generation and the choice of coefficient field matter. This calculation does not supply a complete invariant of Picard varieties or automorphism groups.

## Extension

For finitely many specified automorphisms, stack the matrices g*−I and calculate their common kernel. Compare this exact fixed lattice with the coarser real subspace and identify the geometric information that both fail to detect.
