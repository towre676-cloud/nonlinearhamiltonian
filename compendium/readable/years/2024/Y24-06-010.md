## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which actual matrix satisfies the stated Yang–Baxter relation, and what further data are needed before calling it a compact quantum group?

## Status

## Summary

The recovered passage wrote a quantum SU_q(2) relation but did not return its Hopf-algebra or star conventions. An explicit fundamental Hecke-type braid matrix gives a complete finite algebraic calculation while keeping that missing structure visible.

## Definitions

For q≠0 set a=q−q⁻¹ and use the ordered basis00,01,10,11. Define Rcheck by 00↦q00, 11↦q11, 01↦10 and 10↦01+a10.

On three tensor factors, Rcheck₁₂=Rcheck⊗I and Rcheck₂₃=I⊗Rcheck. The braid equation is Rcheck₁₂Rcheck₂₃Rcheck₁₂=Rcheck₂₃Rcheck₁₂Rcheck₂₃.

## Result name

A braid R-matrix with a verified Hecke relation

## Result statement

The displayed matrix satisfies (Rcheck−qI)(Rcheck+q⁻¹I)=0 and the braid equation as identities in Laurent polynomials in q. With P the tensor swap and R=P Rcheck, this is equivalent to R₁₂R₁₃R₂₃=R₂₃R₁₃R₁₂.

## Proof

The 00 and11 basis states satisfy the quadratic relation immediately. On span{01,10}, the matrix is [[0,1],[1,a]], whose square is a times the matrix plus I; since a=q−q⁻¹, this is the same Hecke polynomial. For the braid equation, apply both compositions to the eight binary triples. The all-equal triples give q³ times themselves. The remaining triples reduce using a²+1=aq+q⁻², or equivalently a(q−a)=1−q⁻², after collecting coefficients; direct Laurent-polynomial multiplication verifies all eight without specializing q. Tensor swaps move factors13 into the corresponding adjacent braid positions, yielding the unbraided form.

## Worked example: setup

Specialize to q=1/2, so a=−3/2.

## Worked example: calculation

All matrix entries are rational, the eigenvalues are1/2 and−2, and exact rational multiplication verifies the two eight-by-eight braid products. The resulting matrix is not unitary in the ordinary Euclidean inner product; an algebraic solution of the Yang–Baxter equation is not automatically a physical quantum gate. At q=1 it reduces to the ordinary swap, recovering the symmetric tensor permutation. The two mixed-basis eigenvalues are roots of λ²−aλ−1, namely q and−q⁻¹. Their nonzero product−1 proves invertibility for every allowed q. At q=1/2 the eigenvalue magnitude2 also rules out Euclidean unitarity immediately, since a unitary matrix can only have eigenvalues of absolute value one.

## Worked example: conclusion

An explicit R-matrix supplies a checkable braid representation. It does not by itself define all the multiplication, coproduct, antipode and star data of SU_q(2).

## Limits

The proof and verifier concern the chosen algebraic matrix convention. Other normalizations, spectral-parameter matrices and compact-real-form conventions must be stated separately.
