## Revised question retained in the annual source

What follows from skew symmetry alone, and which extra differential and topological assumptions create a monopole obstruction?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or finite example and explicit scope

## Summary

The recovered antisymmetric-tensor passage associated skew symmetry with electromagnetic and monopole language. Linear algebra supplies dimension and determinant facts, while differential forms supply a separate flux obstruction. Neither should be inferred from the other without the missing equations and domain.

## Definitions

A real d×d skew-symmetric matrix satisfies Aᵀ=−A and has d(d−1)/2 independent entries. A two-form is F=(1/2)ΣF_ij dx_i∧dx_j.

An exact two-form has F=dA for a globally defined one-form A. A magnetic flux model on a sphere is F=g sinθ dθ∧dφ.

## Result name

Antisymmetry does not imply a magnetic monopole

## Result statement

Every odd-dimensional real skew matrix has determinant zero. Antisymmetry alone imposes no equation dF=0 or d*F=0. The sphere form with g≠0 has integral4πg and cannot equal dA for one global smooth potential on S², although it has compatible local potentials on two patches.

## Proof

For a skew matrix detA=detAᵀ=det(−A)=(−1)^d detA. In odd dimension this forces detA=0 over R. A two-form is automatically alternating, but its coefficients may be arbitrary smooth functions; their derivatives need not satisfy closure. On S² integrate the displayed form: g∫₀²πdφ∫₀πsinθdθ=4πg. If F=dA globally, Stokes on the closed oriented sphere would give integral zero, a contradiction. Away from the south pole use A_N=g(1−cosθ)dφ, and away from the north pole use A_S=−g(1+cosθ)dφ. Direct differentiation gives F on each patch; their difference on the overlap is2g dφ. Apparent coordinate singularities are removed on the pole where the corresponding coefficient vanishes.

## Worked example: calculation

In dimension4 there are six independent entries, so skew symmetry leaves a six-parameter linear space. For example blockdiag([[0,a],[−a,0]],[[0,b],[−b,0]]) has determinant a²b² and need not be singular. In contrast, the nonzero sphere flux is a global obstruction, not a count of components. If a global potential exists on a ball containing the sphere, then its exact curvature has zero flux through that sphere; removing the center changes the domain and can permit the nonexact class.

## Limits

No observed monopole or new interaction is implied. Charge quantization additionally requires a specified charged field, gauge group and transition-function condition; it does not follow from real two-form antisymmetry alone.

## Extension
