## Revised question retained in the annual source

What can dimensional reduction and the energy method establish for a fluid equation independently of the speculative representation-theory analogy?

## Status

Developed classical result with a checked example; historical claims corrected where needed

## Summary

A Reynolds number summarizes competing terms after nondimensionalization, while an energy identity gives a proved inequality under boundary and regularity assumptions. These statements are mathematical; neither imports finite-group character heights into a fluid model.

## Definitions

For incompressible flow on a periodic box, ∂_tu+(u·∇)u=−∇p+νΔu+f and ∇·u=0.

With velocity U and length L, Re=UL/ν; nondimensional time is L/U.

## Result name

Energy balance and nondimensional viscosity

## Result statement

A smooth periodic solution satisfies (1/2)d||u||²₂/dt+ν||∇u||²₂=⟨f,u⟩. Scaling x=Lx′,t=(L/U)t′,u=Uu′ makes the Laplacian coefficient 1/Re.

## Proof

Take the spatial inner product with u. The nonlinear term integrates to (1/2)∫u·∇|u|²=0 by periodicity and incompressibility. The pressure term is −∫∇p·u=∫p∇·u=0. Integration by parts gives ∫u·Δu=−||∇u||². This proves the identity. Substituting the scales shows the inertial terms have factor U²/L and diffusion has νU/L², whose ratio is ν/(UL). If f=0, kinetic energy decreases; with mean-zero u, a Poincaré bound gives exponential energy decay. These formal integrations require enough smoothness; for weak solutions an energy inequality is the appropriate statement, and general three-dimensional smooth global existence is not supplied.

## Worked example: setup

On a 2π-periodic box take u(x,y,z,t)=(a(t)sin y,0,0), f=0.

## Worked example: calculation

The convection term vanishes because u·∇=a sin y ∂_x and u is independent of x. The Laplacian is −u, so a′=−νa and a(t)=a(0)e^(−νt). Both sides of the energy identity agree exactly, and energy decays as e^(−2νt).

## Worked example: conclusion

A concrete solution verifies the scaling and energy law without any unproved dynamical or engineering inference.

## Limits

The historical supercritical microchannel parameters lie outside this simple incompressible constant-viscosity model. No design dimensions, performance estimates, or material assumptions are carried forward.
