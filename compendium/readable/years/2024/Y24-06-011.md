## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which crossing, writhe and variable conventions produce the recovered trefoil polynomial?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or finite example and explicit scope

## Summary

The historical q+q³−q⁴ value is compatible with a standard convention, but a polynomial without normalization does not identify which mirror or variable substitution is intended. A two-strand bracket calculation fixes those choices explicitly.

## Definitions

Use the Temperley–Lieb algebra on two strands with basis I,e, relation e²=δe and δ=−A²−A⁻². Take the positive braid generator g=A I+A⁻¹e.

The closure bracket has tr(I)=δ and tr(e)=1. For writhe w use f_D(A)=(−A³)^(−w)⟨D⟩ and set t=A⁻⁴.

## Result name

A conventioned trefoil Jones polynomial from the bracket algebra

## Result statement

The closure of g³ has bracket −A⁵−A⁻³+A⁻⁷. With w=3 its normalized polynomial is A⁻⁴+A⁻¹²−A⁻¹⁶=t+t³−t⁴. Mirroring replaces t by t⁻¹.

## Proof

Expand g³ using e²=δe: g³=A³I+(3A+3A⁻¹δ+A⁻³δ²)e. Apply the closure trace to get A³δ+3A+3A⁻¹δ+A⁻³δ². Substituting δ and collecting Laurent powers yields −A⁵−A⁻³+A⁻⁷. The normalization factor for w=3 is −A⁻⁹, producing the stated polynomial. This is a finite exact algebra calculation. Invariance under Reidemeister moves is the separate bracket theorem: the unnormalized bracket handles movesII andIII, and the writhe factor compensates moveI.

## Worked example: calculation

The chosen convention gives V(t)=t+t³−t⁴ and mirror polynomial t⁻¹+t⁻³−t⁻⁴. Both evaluate to1 at t=1, but their exponent supports differ. Changing crossing or A conventions can exchange these formulas, which explains why a recovered bare polynomial should not be declared right- or left-handed without the convention. The verifier performs the Laurent multiplication and writhe shift exactly. The normalization can be checked against a single positive curl: its bracket contributes−A³, while its writhe increases by one. Multiplication by(−A³)⁻¹ cancels that factor. A negative curl gives the reciprocal cancellation. This explains the role of writhe before applying the convention to the three-crossing example. The crossingless unknot has bracket and normalized value one under this same closure convention, fixing its overall scalar normalization.

## Worked example: conclusion

The recovered trefoil value is now supported by a complete calculation rather than a convention-free assertion.

## Limits

This does not make the Jones polynomial a complete knot invariant. Equal values in a normalization are not in general a proof of isotopy, and a framing-dependent bracket must not be confused with its normalized ambient-isotopy invariant.
