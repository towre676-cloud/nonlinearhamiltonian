## Revised question retained in the annual source

What is the precise normalization of the recovered spacing density, and what can a short zero list test?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered formula is the GUE Wigner surmise. It is an exact elementary density with unit mean and a useful model for unfolded spacings, rather than the exact universal nearest-neighbor law of large GUE matrices. This distinction prevents an approximate diagnostic from becoming a theorem about zeros.

## Definitions

Let p(s)=(32/π²)s²e^(−4s²/π) for s≥0 and p(s)=0 otherwise.

An unfolded spacing compares neighboring levels after a declared transformation that makes their mean local density approximately1.

## Result name

Normalization and first two moments

## Result statement

The density p integrates to1, E[s]=1 and E[s²]=3π/8. Its variance is3π/8−1.

## Proof

For a>0, ∫₀∞s^m e^(−as²)ds=(1/2)a^(−(m+1)/2)Γ((m+1)/2), by u=as². Set a=4/π. For m=2 the integral is√π/(4a^(3/2))=π²/32, canceling the prefactor. For m=3 it is1/(2a²)=π²/32, so the mean is1. For m=4 it is3√π/(8a^(5/2)); multiplication gives3π/8. These identities certify the density, not a fit to a particular finite sequence. For zeta ordinates, the mean density varies with height, so raw gaps should not be pooled across very different heights without an explicit unfolding convention. The surmise has quadratic repulsion near zero because p(s)/s² tends to32/π². Its tail is Gaussian in s, unlike an exponential spacing law. These local and tail features are separate testable properties; imposing sample mean1 alone cannot enforce either of them on a finite dataset.

## Worked example: calculation

The four gaps are6.887314,3.988818,5.414019,2.510185. Dividing by their sample mean4.700084 gives spacings with sample mean exactly1 by construction. That normalization consumes a degree of freedom and leaves only four observations; resemblance to a smooth density has no strong asymptotic content. The values were recovered as approximations, so no certified zero enclosure is claimed.

## Worked example: conclusion

The formula itself is precisely checkable. A four-spacing comparison is a small descriptive experiment with no implication of an exact spectral correspondence.

## Limits

Neither agreement with a random-matrix model nor disagreement in a tiny sample proves or refutes RH. The infinite GUE spacing law is more complicated than this surmise.

## Extension

Use a fixed smooth zero-counting approximation for unfolding, predeclare a large height window and examine several independent statistics. Keep sampling uncertainty separate from numerical error in the ordinates.
