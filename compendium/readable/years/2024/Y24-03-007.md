## Definitions

A feature vector z consists of declared measured or computed quantities. The weight vector w has reciprocal units so that the sum has a coherent unit.

A score is a function with a domain and codomain. The names consciousness and unified complexity in the source fragment supply neither the missing definitions nor a theorem connecting a score to those concepts.

## Result name

A complexity score requires units, weights and a stated inference

## Result statement

The score is invariant under the unit change exactly when its transformed weights satisfy w′=D⁻ᵀw. Any nonzero linear scalar score in dimension n>1 has a nontrivial blind subspace, so equal scores do not imply equal feature vectors.

## Proof

Equality w′ᵀDz=wᵀz for every z means Dᵀw′=w, giving the weight rule. Rank–nullity gives dim ker wᵀ=n−1 because the row has rank one. Therefore z and z+v have equal scores whenever v lies in that kernel. For an explicit two-feature score w=(2,1), the vector v=(1,−2) is invisible. These are exact limitations of the declared function; they do not depend on an interpretation of the features. Conversely, a vector of several independent scores Wz preserves all feature information precisely when W has full column rank, a checkable property that a single score cannot have for n>1.

## Worked example: setup

Compare z_A=(1,0) and z_B=(0,1) with w=(2,1), then rescale the first feature by one tenth.

## Worked example: calculation

Initially C_A=2>C_B=1. If the old weights are reused after D=diag(1/10,1), the scores become 1/5 and one and their ranking reverses. Correct weights w′=(20,1) restore the scores two and one. Also C(1,0)=C(0,2)=2, displaying an exact collision even before any measurement error.

## Limits

The proof addresses linear aggregates. Nonlinear scores need their own regularity, invariance and identifiability analysis. The recovered source only supplies five framework labels, so no original formula or scientific validation is asserted here.

## Extension

Specify a perturbation norm as well as weights. By Cauchy–Schwarz, |C(z+e)−C(z)|≤||w||₂||e||₂. Two scores separated by Δ retain their ordering under independent errors of radius ε whenever Δ>2ε||w||₂. This yields an honest robustness claim with an explicit margin.
