## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which space is intended by a sphere, triangle or torus, and which loops become contractible within that space?

## Status

Classical mathematics with newly authored derivation or finite example and explicit scope

## Summary

The recovered user questions explicitly asked about circle windings, spheres of different dimensions and nonspherical shapes. The space must be stated before its fundamental group: a filled triangle and its boundary have different answers, and an embedded picture does not determine a quotient surface.

## Definitions

Sⁿ={x∈R^(n+1):|x|=1}; S⁰ consists of two points. Fundamental groups are based in a chosen path component.

Write [a,b]=aba⁻¹b⁻¹. The torus is R²/Z²; RP² identifies antipodal points of S². A filled triangle includes its interior, while its boundary is a polygonal loop.

## Result name

Fundamental groups distinguish boundaries, fillings and quotients

## Result statement

π₁(S¹)=Z and π₁(Sⁿ)=0 for n≥2. The torus has π₁=Z²; π₁(RP²)=Z/2; the Klein bottle has presentation ⟨a,b|aba⁻¹=b⁻¹⟩; and an oriented genus-two surface has presentation ⟨a,b,c,d|[a,b][c,d]=1⟩. A triangle boundary has group Z, whereas a filled triangle has trivial group.

## Proof

Lift a based circle loop through t↦e^(2πit). Its endpoint is an integer, invariant under based homotopy; concatenation adds endpoints. A loop with endpoint zero contracts by interpolating its real lift, proving the circle result. For n≥2, cellular approximation deforms a loop in the usual CW structure of Sⁿ, with one zero-cell and one n-cell, into its one-skeleton, which is a point. The quotient R²→T² is a simply connected covering with deck group Z²; S²→RP² similarly has deck group Z/2. Cutting the Klein bottle and genus-two surface into their standard polygons and applying van Kampen gives the displayed edge-word relations. Finally a triangle boundary is homeomorphic to S¹, while straight-line contraction to an interior point stays inside the convex filled triangle.

## Worked example: calculation

Within the boundary the winding class is1 and cannot contract. In the filled triangle, H(s,t)=(1−s)γ(t)+s v contracts the loop to the base vertex v, fixes its endpoints and remains in the convex triangle. For the torus, the loop t↦(2t,3t) modZ² has class(2,3); it is noncontractible despite ending where it starts. The Klein relation conjugates b to b⁻¹ rather than commuting a and b, so its group must not be replaced by Z².

## Worked example: conclusion

The shape questions become precise through loop homotopy, explicit coverings and declared identifications.

## Limits

The presentations are classical theorems, not results inferred from numerical graph cycles. S⁰ has trivial fundamental group at either point but is disconnected. Higher homotopy groups of spheres are a different, substantially richer subject.

## Extension

For a chosen cell complex, state its attaching maps and use van Kampen before computing its abelianization; the latter gives H₁ and can lose noncommutative information.
