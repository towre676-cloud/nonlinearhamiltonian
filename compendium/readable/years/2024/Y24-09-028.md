## Revised question retained in the annual source

Does the recovered logistic-factor deformation define a global coordinate change on signed inputs?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The historical framework uses both a signed power deformation and a later logistic-factor formula. These are different maps. In the latter formula the correction is positive even for negative input, and the resulting map is not globally injective whenα>0 and0<β<1. This matters whenever global inversion or transported algebra is claimed.

## Definitions

Fixα,k>0 and0<β<1, and defineψ(x)=x+α|x|^β/(1+e^(−x/k)), withψ(0)=0.

Fornegative inputx=−t,t>0, writeψ(−t)=t[h(t)−1], whereh(t)=αt^(β−1)/(1+e^(t/k)).

## Result name

A second preimage of zero

## Result statement

There is a unique t0>0 withψ(−t0)=0, in addition toψ(0)=0. Thusψ is not injective onR. On the nonnegative half-line it is strictly increasing.

## Proof

The positive functionh is continuous, tends to infinity ast→0+ becauseβ−1<0, and tends to zero ast→∞. Its logarithmic derivative is(β−1)/t−e^(t/k)/[k(1+e^(t/k))], which is strictly negative. The intermediate value theorem and strict monotonicity give exactly one root ofh(t)=1. This proves the two distinct preimages without any floating-point assumption. Onx>0, differentiatingψ gives1 plus two positive terms, soψ′>1; continuity extends strict increase to zero. From the negative side,ψ′(x) tends to negative infinity asx→0−, whereas from the positive side it tends to positive infinity. The cusp is therefore qualitatively different from the monotone signed map.

## Worked example: setup

Takeα=1/30,β=2/3,k=1, a rational illustrative parameter choice.

## Worked example: calculation

Att_L=(α/3)^3=1/729000, the numeratorαt_L^(−1/3)=3, while1+e^t_L<3, soh(t_L)>1. Att_U=(α/2)^3=1/216000, the numerator equals2 while1+e^t_U>2, soh(t_U)<1. Hence the additional negative root lies strictly between−1/216000 and−1/729000. The small scale does not remove the exact noninjectivity.

## Worked example: conclusion

The logistic expression cannot be used as a bijective global coordinate map without restricting its domain or changing its definition.

## Limits

For a positive-only variable, the increasing half-line restriction may be appropriate and must be stated. Replacing the correction bysign(x)|x|^β times a suitable positive even factor defines a different map; it should not be silently substituted for the historical formula.

## Extension

Choose the intended signed or positive domain explicitly. For a repaired signed model, prove monotonicity and its inverse regularity before transporting algebraic operations or differentiating through it.
