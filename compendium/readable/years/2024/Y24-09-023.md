## Revised question retained in the annual source

What missing cancellation hypothesis makes the recovered local L²-to-H¹ estimate correct?

## Status

Classical result; recovered proposal repaired

## Summary

The recovered inequality is a standard atomic estimate once the function has mean zero. Omitting cancellation materially changes the theorem. The normalization can be proved in one line, but specifying the ambient real Hardy space and the atom conditions is essential.

## Definitions

On R^d, an L² Hardy atom supported in a ball B has integral0 and L² norm at most |B|^(−1/2).

The atomic H¹ norm is the infimum of Σ|λ_j| over representations g=Σλ_ja_j by such atoms. This chooses an equivalent Hardy norm convention.

## Result name

A correctly normalized one-atom estimate

## Result statement

If g∈L² is supported in B and ∫g=0, then ||g||_(H¹,atomic)≤|B|^(1/2)||g||₂. The zero function is included.

## Proof

For g≠0 put λ=|B|^(1/2)||g||₂ and a=g/λ. The support and mean-zero conditions are preserved. Moreover ||a||₂=|B|^(−1/2), so a is an atom. The single-term representation g=λa gives the stated upper bound by the infimum definition. Also Cauchy–Schwarz gives ||a||₁≤1, so atomic convergence in coefficient sum entails L¹ convergence and preserves zero integral. Consequently every function represented by these global atoms has integral0. A nonnegative nonzero compactly supported bump has positive integral and cannot satisfy this global atomic H¹ condition, even though it has finite support and finite L² norm. This is a direct obstruction to dropping the cancellation hypothesis.

## Worked example: setup

Let B=[−1,1] in R and g=1_[−1,0]−1_[0,1], with endpoints irrelevant in L².

## Worked example: calculation

The support has measure2, the integral is0 and ||g||₂=√2. Hence the coefficient bound is2. Indeed a=g/2 has L² norm1/√2 and is an atom, while ||g||₁=2 forces every atomic coefficient sum to be at least2. Thus its atomic H¹ norm is exactly2 in this convention.

## Worked example: conclusion

The example both proves the bound can be sharp and identifies the missing mean-zero assumption.

## Limits

Local Hardy spaces, Hardy spaces on compact groups and analytic Hardy spaces use related but different atom conventions. The constant changes under equivalent norm definitions. The historical notation did not specify which setting was intended.

## Extension

For a supported function with nonzero mean, split it into a canceled part plus its average times a fixed bump. Analyze the second part in an appropriate local Hardy space rather than applying the global estimate.
