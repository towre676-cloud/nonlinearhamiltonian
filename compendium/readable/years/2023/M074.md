## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which closure axioms are needed, and what additional set-theoretic assumption supports the chosen universe?

## Status

Classical result developed and worked

## Summary

Universe language is a relative-size discipline backed by a set-theoretic assumption. It is not a mechanism for eliminating every paradox without cost. The useful mathematical task is to state the closure axioms and show exactly which categorical constructions they permit.

## Definitions

A Grothendieck universe U is a transitive set closed under pairing, power sets and unions indexed by a set belonging to U, with the family’s members in U. To support ordinary infinite mathematics one asks ω∈U. A U-small set is an element of U, which differs from merely being a subset of U.

## Result name

The inaccessible-cardinal construction of a universe

## Result statement

If κ is a strongly inaccessible cardinal, then V_κ is a Grothendieck universe containing ω. Consequently U-small sets, maps, finite products and function sets can be handled inside one fixed relative-size level. The existence of such an uncountable inaccessible cardinal is an additional assumption, not a theorem of bare ZFC.

## Proof

Transitivity of the cumulative hierarchy is immediate. If x,y∈V_κ, their ranks are below κ, so pairing and power sets still have rank below κ because κ is a limit ordinal. For an indexed family (x_i)_{i∈I} with I∈V_κ, strong inaccessibility ensures every set in V_κ has cardinality below κ. Regularity then bounds the supremum of the fewer-than-κ ranks of x_i below κ, so their union remains in V_κ. For function sets X→Y, their graphs are subsets of X×Y and are therefore contained in a suitable iterated power-set construction inside U.

## Worked example: calculation

Ordered pairs can be constructed by the Kuratowski definition, yielding X×Y∈U. Every function graph lies in P(X×Y), and the collection of all such graphs is a subset of that power set, hence U-small under the closure axioms. By contrast, the entire collection of all U-small sets is U itself; asserting U∈U would contradict foundation. It belongs to a larger universe only when another size assumption is made.

## Worked example: conclusion

The construction makes size bookkeeping precise while retaining a visible logical boundary.

## Limits

This treatment proves the conditional construction, not consistency or existence of inaccessible cardinals. Degenerate finite universes and conventions excluding or including ω must be distinguished. Different foundational treatments can avoid universe axioms for specific constructions.

## Extension
