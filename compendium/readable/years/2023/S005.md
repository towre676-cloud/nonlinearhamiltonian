## Status

Classical framework with a proved synthesis and worked example

## Summary

A reaction–diffusion discretization becomes much more informative when its algebraic and probabilistic properties are checked together. For a finite undirected graph, one elementary step provides mass conservation, positivity, an entropy inequality and an explicit stability boundary.

## Definitions

Let a_ij=a_ji≥0, a_ii=0 define a connected finite weighted graph, with degrees d_i=Σ_j a_ij and d_max>0. Let L=diag(d)−A and P_h=I−hL.

For row probability vectors p, the update is p↦pP_h. The uniform distribution u is stationary whenever P_h is doubly stochastic.

## Result name

An explicit step is a Markov update precisely within its positivity range

## Result statement

For 0≤h≤1/d_max, P_h is nonnegative and doubly stochastic, so it conserves probability and cannot decrease Shannon entropy. At h≤1/(2d_max), all eigenvalues of P_h are nonnegative. For h>0 in that latter range, every zero-sum vector contracts in Euclidean norm by at most 1−hλ₂(L), where λ₂ is the spectral gap.

## Proof

Off-diagonal entries of P_h are h a_ij≥0, diagonal entries are 1−h d_i and each row sums to one. Symmetry gives column sums one. With φ(s)=s log s and φ(0)=0, Jensen applied to each column gives φ((pP)_j)≤Σ_i P_ij φ(p_i); summing and using row sums proves Σφ(pP)≤Σφ(p), which is the entropy statement. Symmetric diagonalization gives eigenvalues 1−hλ_i(L). The bound λ_max(L)≤2d_max follows from xᵀLx=Σ_(i<j)a_ij(x_i−x_j)²≤2Σ_i d_i x_i². Therefore h≤1/(2d_max) makes all update eigenvalues nonnegative. On the orthogonal complement of constants, the largest is 1−hλ₂. Squaring gives the corresponding residual-energy contraction.

## Worked example: setup

For a periodic one-dimensional grid with diffusion coefficient D and mesh size Δx, the nearest-neighbor generator has degree 2D/Δx².

## Worked example: calculation

The condition h≤1/d_max becomes r=DΔt/Δx²≤1/2, the usual positivity condition for the explicit stencil. Taking r≤1/4 also makes every Fourier multiplier nonnegative. At the endpoint r=1/2 on an even cycle, the alternating mode has multiplier −1 and does not decay: positivity and conservation hold, but convergence to equilibrium can fail through periodicity. A two-state swap gives the simplest example: p=(1,0) alternates with (0,1), preserving zero Shannon entropy.

## Worked example: conclusion

A valid nonnegative conservative update need not be a strictly mixing update. The spectral calculation identifies the missing hypothesis and quantifies convergence once it is supplied.

## Limits

Reaction terms, directed edges, nonuniform stationary measures and adaptive time steps need additional analysis. Entropy monotonicity for the uniform measure does not automatically transfer to arbitrary dynamics; a relative entropy against the correct stationary measure is often the relevant quantity.

## Extension

Design a discretization or graph update to satisfy a joint contract: mass, positivity, symmetry, entropy and a specified decay rate. Then verify each property algebraically before measuring prediction performance.
