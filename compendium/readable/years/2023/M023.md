## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can a q-dependent chain be defined with valid probabilities and a computable invariant distribution?

## Status

Finite family solved analytically

## Summary

Here q has one explicit meaning: the upward jump rate of a reflecting birth-death chain. This produces an actual probability model, stationary distribution and spectral answer.

## Definitions

The states are 0,…,N. From i<N the jump i→i+1 has rate q>0; from i>0 the jump i→i−1 has rate one.

The generator Q has diagonal entries equal to minus its outgoing rate and row sums zero. The process is irreducible on this finite state space.

Write Z=Σ_{i=0}^N qⁱ and π_i=qⁱ/Z. At q=1 this is uniform.

## Result name

Stationary distribution and complete nonzero spectrum

## Result statement

Detailed balance holds, and the eigenvalues of −Q are zero and λ_k=q+1−2sqrt(q)cos(kπ/(N+1)), k=1,…,N. The spectral gap is λ_1>0.

## Proof

For each adjacent pair, π_i q=π_{i+1}; summing these balances proves πQ=0. Conjugate −Q by diag(sqrt(π_i)) to obtain a symmetric tridiagonal matrix S with off-diagonal −s, s=sqrt(q), interior diagonal q+1 and endpoint diagonals q and one. The interior eigenvalue recurrence has solutions v_i=sin((i+1)θ)−s⁻¹sin(iθ) for λ=q+1−2s cosθ. The left endpoint condition holds directly. The right endpoint reduces to (2cosθ−s−s⁻¹)sin((N+1)θ)=0. Taking θ=kπ/(N+1), 1≤k≤N, gives N independent nonzero eigenmodes; the stationary mode sqrt(π) supplies zero. These N+1 eigenvectors exhaust the dimension.

## Worked example: setup

Take N=2 and q=4.

## Worked example: calculation

The stationary weights are (1,4,16)/21. The matrix −Q has characteristic roots 0,3,7. Exact determinant evaluation and balance checks in verify.py reproduce all three. At q=1 the gap is 2−2cos(π/(N+1)), asymptotic to π²/(N+1)².

## Worked example: conclusion

The parameter has a measurable effect on equilibrium concentration and mixing, with no unspecified q-randomness.

## Limits

Reflecting boundaries are essential to this finite-chain answer. An infinite state space has additional recurrence and normalizability conditions: Σqⁱ is finite only when 0<q<1. The gap formula is not a formula for arbitrary q-deformed chains.

## Extension

Use the exact reversible gap to bound L² convergence. For a point start x, a standard consequence is TV distance at time t at most (1/2)sqrt(1/π_x−1)e^(−λ_1 t). Study how boundary placement changes the useful mixing bound.
