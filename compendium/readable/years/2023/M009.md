## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which derivative and algebraic identities are actually needed to verify a sech² travelling wave?

## Status

Exact derivative and travelling-wave calculation

## Summary

The corrected sech² identity supplies enough structure to verify a whole travelling-wave family. Instead of recognizing a familiar shape, we derive the amplitude-speed relation required by a specified nonlinear PDE.

## Definitions

Let T=tanh z and f(z)=sech²z=1−T².

The derivatives satisfy T′=f, f′=−2fT and d/dz=(1−T²)d/dT when applied to polynomials in T.

For a wave u(x,t)=A f(κ(x−ct−x0)), primes on u below denote spatial derivatives.

## Result name

Amplitude and velocity are fixed by substitution

## Result statement

For nonzero κ and the equation u_t+6uu_x+u_xxx=0, the sech² ansatz is a solution when A=2κ² and c=4κ². These constants are linked by the equation; they cannot be chosen independently.

## Proof

Differentiate f′=−2fT to obtain f″=−2f′T−2fT′=4fT²−2f²=4f−6f². Differentiating again gives f‴=(4−12f)f′. After rescaling, u_xxx=4κ²u_x−(12κ²/A)u u_x. The travelling-wave time derivative is u_t=−c u_x. The PDE residual is therefore (4κ²−c)u_x+(6−12κ²/A)u u_x. The stated amplitude and speed annihilate both coefficients. Conversely, for a nonconstant profile with A≠0, vanishing for the whole wave forces those coefficients to vanish because u takes a nontrivial interval of values wherever u_x is nonzero. The verifier independently represents u=2(1−T²) as a polynomial and obtains an identically zero residual using exact integer coefficients.

## Worked example: setup

Use κ=1, x0=0.

## Worked example: calculation

Then u=2sech²(x−4t). Its maximum is two at x=4t and its spatial integral is four, since ∫sech²z dz=tanh z. The profile is localized and translates with speed four. A version with amplitude six and the same width would not solve this normalization of KdV.

## Worked example: conclusion

The identity repair now produces a complete exact check, conserved profile mass and a falsifiable relationship among width, height and speed.

## Limits

Changing the sign or coefficient of the nonlinear term changes the amplitude relation. This calculation verifies an explicit family and is not a classification of all solutions or a stability proof.

## Extension

Use the first integrals of the travelling-wave ODE to classify decaying profiles under explicit smoothness and boundary assumptions.
