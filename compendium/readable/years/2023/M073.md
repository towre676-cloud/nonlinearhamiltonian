## Revised question retained in the annual source

How does the divergence identity constrain admissible source equations?

## Status

Classical result developed and worked

## Summary

Covariant conservation is the compatibility condition supplied by the geometry of the field equation. It does not say that coordinate components are constant, or that a global conserved total energy exists in every spacetime. A homogeneous expanding metric gives an exact example of the distinction.

## Definitions

For the Levi-Civita connection, ∇g=0 and the contracted Bianchi identity is ∇^μG_μν=0. Let κ and Λ be constants. A perfect fluid has rest energy density ε, pressure p and four-velocity normalized by u·u=−1 in a c=1 convention.

## Result name

Source conservation and the FLRW continuity equation

## Result statement

The field equation G_μν+Λg_μν=κT_μν with κ≠0 implies ∇^μT_μν=0. In a homogeneous FLRW metric, this includes ε̇+3H(ε+p)=0, where H=ȧ/a. For constant equation-of-state parameter p=wε, εa^{3(1+w)} is constant.

## Proof

Apply the covariant divergence to the field equation. The geometric identity and metric compatibility eliminate the left side, and constant κ gives the conclusion. In comoving FLRW coordinates, expanding ∇_μT^{μ0}=0 combines the ordinary time derivative of ε with the connection terms associated with the changing spatial volume a³; isotropic pressure contributes three identical work terms. Substitution p=wε yields d logε/dt=−3(1+w)d loga/dt, whose integral gives the power law wherever ε and a are positive.

## Worked example: setup

Compare dust and radiation under a scale factor that doubles.

## Worked example: calculation

For dust w=0, energy density falls by 2³=8. For radiation w=1/3, it falls by 2⁴=16. Neither changing component violates covariant conservation. The additional radiation factor expresses the pressure-work or redshift contribution in this model.

## Worked example: conclusion

A conservation law can involve geometric transport and changing volume. Ordinary component constancy is not the right test.

## Limits

A global conserved charge generally needs an appropriate symmetry or boundary construction. If κ or Λ varies, additional derivative terms enter and the simple inference must be revised. Matter models must satisfy their own evolution equations consistently.

## Extension

Given a Killing field ξ, derive conservation of J^μ=T^{μν}ξ_ν for symmetric covariantly conserved T, and identify the boundary conditions needed to turn it into a constant integrated charge.
