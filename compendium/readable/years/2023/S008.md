## Status

Constructed finite model proved and exhaustively certified

## Summary

This worked design turns the projection formula in S006 into a complete exact optimization problem. Six coordinate channels, six declared mean-shift centers, nonuniform costs and six uncertainty balls give a unique cost13 optimum. A fully specified gain-per-cost greedy rule instead costs16. Every channel participates in an inclusion-minimal feasible panel, no single channel is cost-dominated, and none of the six fault constraints is implied by the others. A deliberately withheld direction is nevertheless invisible to the optimum panel. All of these claims concern this constructed mathematical instance, with no empirical dataset or application-performance claim.

## Definitions

Let X∼N(μ+d+e,I₆), with a known baseline μ and deterministic mean shift d+e. A coordinate panel S⊆{A,B,C,D,E,F} observes the selected coordinates. Its centered squared statistic T_S=Σ_{i∈S}(X_i−μ_i)² has a noncentral χ²_|S| law with noncentrality λ_S(d+e)=Σ_{i∈S}(d_i+e_i)². The empty panel is allowed in the finite enumeration but cannot meet the positive target.

The six costs and coverage sets are A:(6,{1,3,5}), B:(7,{1,2,3}), C:(6,{4}), D:(6,{2,3,5,6}), E:(7,{2,4,5,6}), F:(4,{1,6}). Define the six rational centers by d⁽ʲ⁾_i=2 when channel i covers target j and zero otherwise. Thus d⁽¹⁾=(2,2,0,0,0,2), d⁽²⁾=(0,2,0,2,2,0), d⁽³⁾=(2,2,0,2,0,0), d⁽⁴⁾=(0,0,2,0,2,0), d⁽⁵⁾=(2,0,0,2,2,0), and d⁽⁶⁾=(0,0,0,2,2,2).

For each center, the declared uncertainty set is the full Euclidean ball ||e||₂≤1 in six-dimensional whitened coordinates. Feasibility means inf_{||e||≤1}λ_S(d⁽ʲ⁾+e)≥1 for every j=1,…,6. Costs add across selected channels. Noncentrality is the declared design criterion; panels of different sizes have different null degrees of freedom and require their own significance calibration.

The greedy heuristic starts with no channels. At each step it chooses the unused channel maximizing the number of newly satisfied target constraints divided by its cost; ties would be settled alphabetically. It stops when all six constraints are satisfied. There are no maximizing ties along the path in this instance.

## Result name

Exact robust-margin reduction and a unique finite optimum

## Result statement

For any orthogonal coordinate projection P_S and ε≥0, inf_{||e||≤ε}||P_S(d+e)||²=max(||P_Sd||−ε,0)². Consequently the declared ε=1,target1 problem is feasible exactly when every center has selected energy at least4. The unique minimum-cost panel is {A,E}, of cost13. Greedy selects D,F,C and has cost16, a strict ratio16/13. The optimum is blind to the withheld center d*=2e_D, whose full-observation noncentrality is4.

## Proof

Triangle inequality and ||P_Se||≤||e|| give ||P_S(d+e)||≥max(||P_Sd||−ε,0). If ||P_Sd||>ε, choose e=−εP_Sd/||P_Sd||. If ||P_Sd||≤ε, choose e=−P_Sd. Both perturbations are allowed and attain the bound, including the zero case. For positive target1 and ε=1, the inequality max(√λ−1,0)²≥1 is equivalent to λ≥4. Every nonzero center coordinate is2, so the energy condition is equivalent to selecting at least one channel from that target’s coverage support. The statistical robustness problem has therefore reduced exactly, without approximation, to this declared weighted set-cover instance.

The panel A,E covers all targets and costs13. Any feasible panel containing C needs at least two additional channels: no pair C plus one other channel covers all six targets. The cheapest possible two other channels cost4+6, so such a panel costs at least16. A feasible panel not containing C must contain E, because only C and E cover target4. At total cost at most13, E can be accompanied by at most one other channel, since the two cheapest remaining costs are4 and6. E alone fails; E,F misses target3; E,D misses target1; E,A succeeds and costs13; E,B exceeds the budget. Thus A,E is the unique feasible panel at cost at most13. This gives an analytic optimality and uniqueness certificate in addition to the enumeration.

Initially D has gain/cost4/6=2/3, uniquely larger than E’s4/7 or any other ratio. After D, the missing targets are1 and4. F supplies target1 at ratio1/4, uniquely beating A’s1/6 and the other choices. After D,F only target4 remains; C’s1/6 beats E’s1/7. Greedy therefore returns D,F,C of total cost16. No claim is made that this heuristic is worst possible, or that the example estimates its usual behavior.

For the withheld vector2e_D, P_{A,E}d*=0. Its selected Gaussian law is exactly the same as the baseline law. Hence every test using only those observed coordinates, including repeated independent samples, has the same rejection probability under this alternative as under the null. Full observation retains norm2 and worst-case noncentrality(2−1)²=1 under the same unit uncertainty. More generally, any rank-k linear panel with k<6 has a nonzero kernel. Selecting an appropriately scaled shift in that kernel proves that no such panel can preserve all mean-shift directions. This is an information obstruction, not a failure of the finite optimization certificate.

The portable supplement_verify.py enumerates every one of the2⁶=64 coordinate panels. ## Worked example: calculation

There are34 feasible panels. The optimum A,E has center energies(4,4,4,4,8,4). Its six exact worst-case noncentralities are(1,1,1,1,9−4√2,1). For the five energy4 centers, subtracting one unit in their sole active retained coordinate attains1. For target5, subtract(1/√2,1/√2) in coordinates A,E; this perturbation has norm1 and attains9−4√2. Greedy D,F,C has center energies(4,4,4,4,4,8), and its minimum robust margin is also1 for the declared targets, at the higher cost16. A withheld center2e_D has selected noncentrality0 in A,E but4 in D,F,C and the full panel. For the simple Gaussian center alternative, the selected Kullback–Leibler divergence from baseline is half the selected noncentrality, so it is zero for the withheld direction in the optimum panel.

To see that the six channels and six constraints are substantive, inspect the additional exact witnesses. No channel has its entire target set contained in that of another channel of equal or smaller cost. Each channel belongs to at least one inclusion-minimal feasible panel. For each target j, selecting all channels that do not cover j satisfies the other five targets and fails exactly j. Thus no target constraint is implied by the other five. The verifier checks all these finite properties rather than relying on a picture of the incidence matrix.

## Worked example: conclusion

The finite optimum is exact, has a strict cost advantage over the stated greedy rule, and remains unable to guarantee unlisted directions. The constructive counterexample is compatible with all the declared-target certificates. Exact optimization, robustness to a specified ball, detector calibration and universal observability are distinct questions that can now be stated and checked separately.

## Limits

This is a deliberately constructed six-channel Gaussian location model with known identity covariance and additive deterministic mean uncertainty. Its costs, centers and radius are mathematical inputs, not measured quantities. The objective constrains worst-case noncentrality; it is not an empirical accuracy guarantee, and equal noncentralities in different degrees of freedom do not by themselves imply equal test power. Estimated covariance, covariance-changing faults, temporal dependence, nonlinear sensors and unmodeled shifts need separate analysis. The withheld vector demonstrates a scope boundary and does not invalidate optimality for the declared family. The general robust-margin formula is classical projection geometry, and the small exact instance is an illustrative certificate rather than a claim of new general optimization theory.

## Extension

Replace identity covariance by a fixed rational positive-definite matrix Σ and let each nonempty panel measure specified linear combinations with a full-row-rank matrix B. Then BΣBᵀ is invertible and λ_S=dᵀK_Bd, where K_B=Bᵀ(BΣBᵀ)⁻¹B; state the uncertainty ball in whitened coordinates. When B and Σ are rational, K_B is rational. The Euclidean orthogonal projector in whitened coordinates is instead P=Σ^(1/2)K_BΣ^(1/2), which need not be rational: for Σ=diag(1,2) and B=(1,1), K_B is the all-ones matrix divided by3, whereas P=[[1,√2],[√2,2]]/3. Redundant measurement rows require a separately stated pseudoinverse and observation-rank convention. For finitely many candidate full-row-rank panels, exhaustive enumeration still certifies the optimum, although robust thresholds can involve algebraic numbers. A further exact task is to classify the parameter cells in costs or radii where the optimal panel changes, while retaining the nullspace impossibility for any universal dimension reduction.
