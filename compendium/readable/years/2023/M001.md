## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which structure is actually intended: Robinson arithmetic Q, a q-difference calculus, a p-adic field, or a formal power-series ring? What maps, if any, connect the chosen structures?

## Status

Definitions resolved with a structural obstruction

## Summary

The original notation can be repaired completely at the level of objects. Robinson arithmetic Q is a first-order theory, Q_p is a valued field of characteristic zero, F_p[[q]] is a formal power-series ring of characteristic p, and D_q is an operator depending on a numerical or formal parameter. A proposed connection must name the relevant category and map; the shared letter Q supplies neither.

## Definitions

Robinson Q consists of axioms in the language of zero, successor, addition and multiplication; it is not a number field.

Q_p is the completion of Q for |x|_p=p^(−v_p(x)); Z_p is its valuation ring.

F_p[[q]] consists of formal series Σa_n q^n. Its q-adic topology measures vanishing order, not numerical size.

D_q f(x)=(f(qx)−f(x))/((q−1)x), initially for q≠1 and x≠0.

## Result name

Characteristic obstructs a claimed field identification

## Result statement

There is a canonical dense embedding Q→Q_p and a compatible reduction system Z_p→Z/p^nZ. There is no unital ring homomorphism Q_p→F_p[[q]], or in the reverse direction. In particular these objects cannot be identified by renaming their parameters.

## Proof

A unital homomorphism sends an integer n·1 to the same n·1. In characteristic p, p·1=0, whereas p is nonzero and invertible in Q_p. If φ:Q_p→F_p[[q]] were unital, then 1=φ(p p^(−1))=φ(p)φ(p^(−1))=0, a contradiction. In the reverse direction, applying φ to p·1=0 would give p·1=0 in Q_p, also impossible. The dense embedding Q→Q_p instead preserves characteristic and the field operations. The reductions from Z_p preserve ring operations but have nontrivial kernel p^nZ_p, so they are quotients rather than embeddings. This distinction already eliminates several possible informal identifications without requiring a difficult theorem.

## Worked example: calculation

The rational 1/3 maps into Q_7 and can be approximated by compatible residues modulo 7^n. The formal series (1−q)^(−1)=1+q+q²+⋯ belongs to F_7[[q]] because its coefficients satisfy a formal multiplication identity. Its formal variable q is not automatically the prime 7 or a neural-network parameter.

A corrected dictionary gives legitimate arrows and a proof that one tempting arrow cannot exist.

## Limits

An arithmetic theory can be interpreted in structures, and equal-characteristic local fields can resemble mixed-characteristic fields in selected respects. The characteristic obstruction does not rule out every model-theoretic or categorical comparison; it rules out the specified unital ring identifications.

## Extension

For every later use of q or Q, record its type, admissible values and morphisms. A meaningful bridge should transport a named theorem, not merely an expression.
