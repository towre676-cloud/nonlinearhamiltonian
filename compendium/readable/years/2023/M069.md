## Revised question retained in the annual source

What sufficient conditions prove stability or decay, and what actually follows when those sufficient conditions fail?

## Status

Classical result developed and worked

## Summary

A Lyapunov certificate is a sufficient argument tied to a chosen function. Failure of one function is not a system-instability certificate. The exact example below resolves that logical error and produces a contracting quadratic geometry for the same stable system.

## Definitions

For ẋ=Ax and symmetric P, define V(x)=xᵀPx. Positive definiteness P≻0 makes V comparable with ||x||². Along a trajectory, V̇=xᵀ(AᵀP+PA)x. A Hurwitz matrix has all eigenvalues in the open left half-plane.

## Result name

Quadratic certificate and a counterexample to the failed converse

## Result statement

If P≻0 and AᵀP+PA=−Q with Q≻0, then V(t)≤exp(−λ_min(Q)t/λ_max(P))V(0), proving exponential stability. A stable A can nevertheless make d||x||²/dt positive at some state.

## Proof

The derivative identity follows by the product rule. Since xᵀQx≥λ_min(Q)||x||² and V≤λ_max(P)||x||², V̇≤−[λ_min(Q)/λ_max(P)]V. Integration gives exponential decay of V; the lower eigenvalue bound on P translates this into a Euclidean state bound. The proof makes no assertion that every positive function has a negative derivative. For Hurwitz A, the integral P=∫₀∞exp(tAᵀ)Qexp(tA)dt provides the converse existence result, with convergence following from exponential matrix decay.

## Worked example: setup

Take A=[[-1,4],[0,−1]], whose eigenvalues are both −1.

## Worked example: calculation

At x=(1,1), Ax=(3,−1) and d(xᵀx)/dt=2xᵀAx=4>0. Yet P=[[1/2,1],[1,9/2]] has positive leading minor and determinant 5/4, and direct multiplication gives AᵀP+PA=−I. Thus the Euclidean norm can temporarily grow while the certified P-norm decreases.

## Worked example: conclusion

The apparent contradiction disappears once the certificate is interpreted in the right direction and its metric is stated.

## Limits

A certificate for one linearization is not automatically a global nonlinear proof. Semidefinite Q needs an invariance argument for asymptotic convergence, and strict inequalities matter near marginal modes.

## Extension

Find a common quadratic Lyapunov function for a specified family of matrices, or exhibit why switching among individually stable matrices defeats a proposed common bound.
