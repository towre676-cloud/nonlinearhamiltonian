## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which entropy or relative entropy is monotone under the specified dynamics, and what distribution serves as equilibrium?

## Status

Contraction proved with a strict counterexample for Shannon entropy

## Summary

Relative entropy has a robust monotonicity property relative to a stationary reference distribution. The unqualified claim that any diffusion must increase Shannon entropy is false.

## Definitions

Let P be a row-stochastic matrix on a finite state set and π a stationary probability with every π_i>0.

D(p||π)=Σ_i p_i log(p_i/π_i), with 0log0=0. The next distribution is pP.

Shannon entropy is H(p)=−Σ_i p_i log p_i and depends on the chosen state representation.

## Result name

Data processing gives D(pP||π)≤D(p||π)

## Result statement

The inequality holds for every initial distribution p. If P is doubly stochastic, it implies H(pP)≥H(p) by taking π uniform. Without this extra structure, Shannon entropy can decrease.

## Proof

Form joint arrays a_{ij}=p_iP_{ij} and b_{ij}=π_iP_{ij}. Their relative entropy is Σ_i p_i log(p_i/π_i), because each row of P sums to one. Summing out i sends the arrays to pP and πP=π. Apply the log-sum inequality separately in each column, then add over j. Equality in a column requires the ratios p_i/π_i to be constant among its positive incoming transitions. In particular a strictly positive P gives strict contraction unless p=π. The uniform-reference identity D(p||u)=log n−H(p) proves the doubly stochastic corollary.

## Worked example: setup

Use p=(1/2,1/2), π=(9/10,1/10), and P with both rows equal to π.

## Worked example: calculation

One step gives pP=π. Shannon entropy decreases from log2≈0.693147 to 0.325083. Meanwhile relative entropy decreases from log(5/3)≈0.510826 to zero. The transition probabilities are exact rationals; only the displayed logarithms are numerical.

## Worked example: conclusion

Convergence toward a concentrated equilibrium can reduce Shannon entropy without contradicting information contraction.

## Limits

This proves monotonicity, not a uniform convergence rate. Nonirreducible chains can preserve information in different closed classes. Continuous-state systems need densities, integrability and an appropriate reference measure.

## Extension

For a strictly positive distribution evolving under the reversible q-chain of M023, differentiate D along the semigroup. Detailed balance produces −(1/2)Σ_{ij}π_iQ_{ij}(r_i−r_j)(log r_i−log r_j)≤0, r_i=p_i/π_i. A log-Sobolev inequality would strengthen this to a quantitative entropy-decay rate.
