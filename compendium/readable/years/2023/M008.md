## Revised question retained in the annual source

Can a regrouping defect be bounded quantitatively, and which invariances must such a bound respect?

## Status

Sharp inequality proved with an equality example

## Summary

The earlier proposal to measure a regrouping defect can be carried to a sharp result. The defect is not just a qualitative marker: its exact universal scale follows from norm multiplicativity, and a basis triple attains that scale.

## Definitions

For octonions define d(a,b,c)=||(ab)c−a(bc)||.

Let n=||a||||b||||c||. Norm multiplicativity gives ||(ab)c||=||a(bc)||=n.

For unit factors the squared defect is 2−2⟨(ab)c,a(bc)⟩, using the Euclidean inner product.

## Result name

Sharp norm bound and equality criterion

## Result statement

For all real octonions, 0≤d(a,b,c)≤2||a||||b||||c||. If n>0, equality holds exactly when (ab)c=−a(bc). The constant two is optimal.

## Proof

Write u=(ab)c and v=a(bc). The triangle inequality gives ||u−v||≤||u||+||v||=2n. Alternatively, expand the squared norm: ||u−v||²=2n²−2⟨u,v⟩. Cauchy–Schwarz gives ⟨u,v⟩≥−n². Equality requires u and v to be oppositely directed; because their norms are equal and nonzero, this is exactly u=−v. The declared convention in M007 gives u=e7 and v=−e7 for a=e1,b=e2,c=e4, so the bound is attained at unit norm. Trilinearity also gives d(ra,sb,tc)=|rst|d(a,b,c) for real scalars. A norm-preserving algebra automorphism transports the associator and therefore preserves the defect.

## Worked example: setup

Evaluate the normalized defect on a maximally nonassociative basis triple and an associative quaternion triple.

## Worked example: calculation

For (e1,e2,e4), d=2. For (e1,e2,e3), both products equal −1, so d=0. The two calculations sit at opposite endpoints of the proved interval. The normalized quantity δ=d/(||a||||b||||c||) is consequently dimensionless on nonzero triples and ranges through values in [0,2].

## Worked example: conclusion

The revised concept now has a universal bound, a certified sharpness witness, scale behavior and an exact criterion for attaining the upper endpoint.

## Limits

This does not classify every zero-defect triple or every orbit of the automorphism group. A zero associator for one triple alone should not be promoted to a statement that an entire generated algebra is associative.

## Extension

Study the polynomial zero locus [a,b,c]=0 on three unit spheres and its orbit structure. Distinguish this classification problem from the sharp inequality already proved.
