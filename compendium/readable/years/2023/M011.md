## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which PDE and normalization are we solving, and how does a one-dimensional soliton embed into KP?

## Status

One-dimensional family and oblique KP line wave verified

## Summary

KdV is an evolution equation in one spatial coordinate. KP adds a second spatial coordinate and a sign that changes its transverse behavior. An exact KdV solution embeds in either stated KP sign when it is independent of the second coordinate, but this elementary inclusion does not solve the full two-dimensional problem.

## Definitions

Fix KdV as u_t+6uu_x+u_xxx=0.

Fix KP as (u_t+6uu_x+u_xxx)_x+3σu_yy=0 with σ=±1.

A line-wave phase is η=kx+ly−ωt+δ with real k≠0 and l,δ; set U(η)=(k²/2)sech²(η/2).

## Result name

An explicit KP line-wave dispersion relation

## Result statement

The profile u(x,y,t)=U(kx+ly−ωt+δ) solves the stated KP equation when ω=k³+3σl²/k. Taking l=0 recovers the KdV family of M009 after k=2κ.

## Proof

The travelling-wave derivative identity in the phase variable is U‴=U′−(6/k²)UU′. Thus the expression inside the outer x derivative is −ωU′+6kUU′+k³U‴=(k³−ω)U′. Its x derivative is k(k³−ω)U″, while u_yy=l²U″. The KP residual is [k(k³−ω)+3σl²]U″ and vanishes under the displayed relation. With l=0, η=2κ(x−4κ²t−x0), giving precisely 2κ²sech²(κ(x−4κ²t−x0)). This calculation fixes every coefficient and sign directly rather than inferring them from a remembered formula.

## Worked example: setup

Choose σ=+1, k=2, l=1 and δ=0.

## Worked example: calculation

Then ω=8+3/2=19/2, and u=2sech²((2x+y−(19/2)t)/2). The crest is the straight line 2x+y=(19/2)t. It is localized in the normal direction but extends along the line, so its total integral over the whole plane is not finite.

## Worked example: conclusion

The line wave is an exactly verified two-dimensional solution with a clear localization limitation.

## Limits

Neither transverse stability nor general KP multi-soliton classification follows from this substitution. The signs σ=±1 must remain explicit, and conventions in external references may use time or field rescalings.

## Extension

Introduce a tau-function compatible with the same convention and study a genuine interaction of nonparallel line solitons rather than adding individual profiles.
