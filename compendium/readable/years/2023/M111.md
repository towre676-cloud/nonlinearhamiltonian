## Revised question retained in the annual source

Which symmetry gives which identity among Green functions, and how is it preserved by the chosen regularization?

## Status

Classical result developed and example checked

## Summary

The identity is made concrete by verifying it at tree level, constructing a longitudinal vertex for a prescribed inverse propagator, and displaying the remaining transverse freedom. This explains both the power and the limitation of the constraint.

## Definitions

Factor the charge from the full electromagnetic vertex Γ^μ. With consistent momentum assignments, the identity is q_μΓ^μ(p+q,p)=S⁻¹(p+q)−S⁻¹(p).

A transverse addition T^μ satisfies q_μT^μ=0. Such an addition cannot be detected by this contracted identity alone.

## Result name

An explicit longitudinal construction

## Result statement

For a differentiable matrix-valued inverse propagator K(p)=S⁻¹(p), Γ_L^μ=∫₀¹(∂K/∂p_μ)(p+tq)dt satisfies the difference identity on any regular path. It is not the unique satisfying vertex.

## Proof

Contract with q and use the chain rule: q_μ∂K(p+tq)/∂p_μ=dK(p+tq)/dt. The fundamental theorem of calculus gives K(p+q)−K(p). Adding any transverse T preserves the contraction. At tree level K(p)=slash(p)−m and Γ^μ=γ^μ, reducing the identity to slash(q)=slash(p+q)−slash(p). These are direct algebraic checks of the kinematic relation; they do not assert that an arbitrarily chosen K and Γ arise from a complete interacting quantum field theory.

## Worked example: setup

For an illustrative polynomial matrix function, take K(p)=slash(p)−m−αp²I.

## Worked example: calculation

The construction gives Γ_L^μ=γ^μ−α(2p^μ+q^μ)I. Contracting yields slash(q)−α(2p·q+q²)I, exactly K(p+q)−K(p). One may add κ(q²p^μ−(p·q)q^μ)I, whose contraction is zero. The verifier checks this example in a two-coordinate Euclidean Clifford representation with rational inputs.

## Worked example: conclusion

The identity constrains differences of inverse propagators and the longitudinal vertex. It leaves dynamical transverse information undetermined. The polynomial example tests the logic without making a false renormalization claim.

## Limits

In QED the full identity is a statement about regularized and renormalized correlation functions. A symmetry-breaking regulator, inconsistent counterterms or an anomalous symmetry can require additional terms or restoration conditions. The algebraic construction above does not settle those issues.

## Extension

Specify an actual perturbative action and regulator, calculate the one-loop self-energy and vertex in the same convention, and check the renormalized identity including counterterms. Retain transverse form factors as independent output.
