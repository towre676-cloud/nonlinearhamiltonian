## Revised question retained in the annual source

In which coordinates or orthonormal frame are component units being stated, and how do both sides match?

## Status

Classical result developed and worked

## Summary

Dimensional statements about tensor components must name the coordinate or frame convention. Using a local orthonormal frame and x⁰=ct makes the audit simple, while also explaining why coordinate components in other charts can carry different units.

## Definitions

Use metric signature (−,+,+,+), spacetime coordinates of length dimension, and a dimensionless orthonormal metric. Write G_N for Newton’s constant, c for speed, ε for energy density and G_μν for the Einstein tensor; the two symbols G have unrelated types.

## Result name

A consistent dimensional audit of Einstein’s equation

## Result statement

In this convention G_μν and Λg_μν have dimensions L⁻²; T_μν has dimensions ML⁻¹T⁻². Since [G_N]=L³M⁻¹T⁻² and [c⁴]=L⁴T⁻⁴, (G_N/c⁴)T_μν has dimension L⁻². The factor 8π is dimensionless and is not selected by dimensional analysis.

## Proof

A connection coefficient contains one derivative of a dimensionless metric, giving L⁻¹, and curvature has a derivative of the connection or a product of two connections, giving L⁻². Energy per volume is (ML²T⁻²)/L³=ML⁻¹T⁻². Multiplying exponents in G_N c⁻⁴T gives zero mass exponent, length exponent −2 and zero time exponent. The cosmological constant must have the same curvature dimension when it multiplies the dimensionless metric. All statements are frame and coordinate dependent at the component level, while the tensor equation is geometric.

## Worked example: setup

Consider a perfect-fluid stress tensor in its local rest frame.

## Worked example: calculation

T_00=ε and T_ii=p in the orthonormal convention, so pressure and energy density have matching units. If instead time is represented by t rather than ct, changing the coordinate basis introduces c factors into the time components; one must transform the tensor rather than recycle the orthonormal component units.

## Worked example: conclusion

The repaired equation is G_μν+Λg_μν=(8πG_N/c⁴)T_μν. It passes a dimensional audit without confusing Newton’s constant, curvature or matter units.

## Limits

The audit cannot fix signs, the numerical coupling or a curvature convention. Those require definitions and a limiting calculation. Natural units c=1 suppress dimensions in notation but do not remove the need to restore them consistently.

## Extension

Carry the same equation through an explicit coordinate rescaling and verify the transformed component units and physical observables.
