## Overview

The dynamics material is organized around four choices that a model must make explicit: its state, its clock, its metric and its observations. The same eigenvalues can coexist with very different transient gains; the same geometric path can have different time parametrizations; the same observation can come from different hidden states. The archive’s strongest recurrent ideas are constructive ways to resolve these ambiguities. They include exact Lyapunov matrices, explicit period-two branches, conserved Hamiltonians, dimensionally complete mass balances and derived memory kernels. Each answers a particular question. Their connections become useful once the question and the retained state are stated before the calculation.

The stability family leads with an exact triangular matrix. Its eigenvalues are stable, its Euclidean norm can initially grow, and a positive matrix certifies decay in another norm. This gives a complete example of nonnormal amplification rather than a slogan about it. Continuous and discrete Gramians, common storage functions and hybrid dwell-time bounds extend the same reasoning. Switching incurs a logarithmic metric cost, so stability of the individual modes alone does not establish stability of every switching sequence. A two-line projection calculation supplies a further geometric model: the cycle contraction and the commutator norm are different functions of the same angle. The linked projection lab keeps that distinction visible.

The bifurcation family follows invariant branches and their multipliers. Exact logistic algebra proves the first fixed-point threshold, the born two-cycle and the next flip boundary. That substantial result is deliberately separated from the deeper infinite-cascade and universality questions. Return-map jets identify the cubic term left after a multiplier-minus-one map is composed with itself. Square-root cycle branches and the tanh pitchfork provide further local mechanisms with explicit conditions. None of these local facts alone identifies a global chaotic attractor or establishes a physical renormalization equivalence.

The mechanics and modeling family starts from conservation and units. Hamilton’s equations preserve a stated energy, and a simple turn gives different exponents for elapsed time and action because those quantities integrate reciprocal momentum and momentum respectively. Scalar RG coordinates preserve a fixed-point derivative under an invertible state-coordinate change with the same time variable; a time rescaling changes the rate. Population recurrences, radiation fluxes, drag, chemical yield and volume-to-mass calculations are organized by similarly explicit balance laws. Dimensional agreement narrows a model but does not supply its missing measurements or dimensionless coefficient.

The memory family finally asks whether a reduced state determines its future. Eliminating a hidden linear coordinate produces both a convolution kernel and a source from the hidden initial condition. Omitting them changes the equation. Mechanical words, substitutions and relative periodicity pose discrete versions of the retained-information question. A balanced aperiodic rotation coding can be completely deterministic and have an exact frequency, so irregular appearance is not a chaos certificate. Across these four families, the dated progression traces changing source topics from 2023 onward; the present proofs and worked models are a new organization of that material, with their scope stated independently of historical attribution.
