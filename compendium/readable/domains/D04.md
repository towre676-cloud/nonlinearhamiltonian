## Organizing question

Which constructions preserve the object, and what explicit maps certify preservation?

## Overview

This domain supplies the architecture used to compose the rest of the atlas. Its organizing unit is a construction together with the equations that make it valid. A category needs a declared class of morphisms and a composition law; a dynamical intertwiner must commute with evolution. A quotient needs an equivalence relation; its universal factorization identifies exactly which maps survive the loss of distinctions. A pushout needs two arrows from shared data and produces one object encoding all compatible pairs of outgoing maps. These examples give a precise language for composing mathematics without treating a common label or equal dimension as a structural identification.

The next layer is homological. A complex is more than a sequence of vector spaces because its differential must square to zero. Signed totalization, shifts and cones make this requirement explicit across degrees. A chain contraction gives a finite certificate that a reduction preserves cohomology. Its homotopy exhibits the difference between the identity and a reduced reconstruction as a boundary correction. This is the appropriate bridge to smaller models: one may eliminate a contractible pair while preserving homology, even though total coordinate dimension changes. The chapter therefore separates presentation size, rank information and invariant content.

Derived constructions retain information that ordinary products and kernels erase. Tor and Ext arise by resolving an object before applying a nonexact functor. A nonzero extension class can distinguish modules with identical dimension; the size of the whole Ext group cannot decide whether one selected extension splits. Similarly a tangent complex supplies potential obstruction spaces, while the actual higher-order lifting equation determines an obstruction class. The double-point and finite Maurer–Cartan examples show both outcomes: a nonzero first-order direction can fail to lift, while a nonzero quadratic cocycle can be removable because it is a boundary. These are constructive alternatives with explicit witnesses.

Finally, filtered and infinite constructions state what remains unknown after a partial computation. The associated graded forgets how layers attach. Page differentials can kill apparent classes, and integral extensions can distinguish groups with identical limiting graded pieces. Inverse limits introduce genuinely infinite compatibility constraints that finite truncations do not settle. Perverse shifts, coefficient base change and t-structures belong in this same discipline of declared data: they change the interpretation of degrees and comparisons. The dated source continuity is strongest from the 2024 categorical and spectral questions through the 2025 derived examples to the 2026 exact signs, limits and obstruction calculations. Those dates locate topics in the available corpus; they do not date the creation of the new proofs here.

The interactive capacity is consequently a certificate interface. Finite matrices can test d²=0, construct a cone, verify a contraction and compare homology. They cannot manufacture the missing differential, functor or higher coherence map of an unspecified object. The chapter’s open frontier is to replace those absent operators with explicit constructions and then verify comparison maps at the correct coefficient and categorical level.
