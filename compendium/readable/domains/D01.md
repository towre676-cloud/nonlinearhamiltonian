## Organizing question

How can an exact arithmetic construction retain its scalar domain, local data and completeness certificate as it is transported into a larger mathematical framework?

## Overview

The strongest arithmetic thread across the recovered treatments is the replacement of suggestive numerical association with a declared object and a certificate. A number such as 384 becomes useful after it is assigned to the degree of a cyclotomic field. A recurrence becomes a classification after descent proves every solution lies in its seed orbits. A polynomial becomes an arithmetic field description after irreducibility, discriminant and ramification are checked. A residue becomes a root certificate after compatibility, a unit derivative and completeness supply the infinite conclusion. These transitions are the organizing structure of this chapter.

The entry point is scalar type. The 2023 distinction between Q, Q_p and characteristic-p series rings prevents invalid maps before any calculation begins. The same issue returns in finite-precision Frobenius tests and formal-series invariants: zero modulo p^N can hide a nonzero obstruction, and an unknown tail can hide the first unit coefficient. The p-adic family therefore couples exact digits with a proof about the completed object. Valuation arithmetic then gives the complementary integer picture. Prime exponents turn monomial constraints into affine integer lattices, divisor order into a product of chains, and factorials into a p-power part plus a stripped unit. The gcd-matrix factorization demonstrates how such arithmetic can legitimately create an energy form through an explicit incidence map.

Two families support implemented finite interactive models. The conductor-1680 model enumerates 384 units, reduces them to eight primitive order-30 classes and verifies 48 points in every fibre. The tower formula then gives exact trace transport. The Pell model generates positive and negative norm orbits, displays inverse steps and computes periodic congruence acceptance. Its written seed reduction is essential: enumeration explains a finite modular period, while descent proves global coverage. Both labs are worked models with exact inputs and outputs, rather than general arithmetic-solving services.

Number fields provide the main obstruction language. The S₃ closure of the discriminant-minus-23 cubic forbids cyclotomic containment. The Q(√10) examples distinguish a genuine unramified class-field candidate from an extension of the right degree with the wrong ramification. Cubic norm formulas preserve the lattice defining the original binary equation, showing exactly why a unit action can leave the solution plane. The completely proved Mordell example y²=x³−2 illustrates the stronger standard: a norm-Euclidean ring and coprimality argument can deliver a global finite answer, whereas an ambient unit identity alone cannot.

The final two families connect this arithmetic to analytic and modular structure. Modular forms require a group, weight and normalization before dimensions and Hecke relations make sense. Euler products require a convergence half-plane before coefficient algebra becomes analysis. Pointwise μΛ, convolution μ*log, prime counting and prime-power counting remain different objects. These distinctions make cross-domain bridges more productive because each bridge names its actual map. The chapter does not force a chronological storyline where sources are absent: the conductor thread is supported in 2025–2026, the explicit Pell thread in 2024 and 2026, and the modular thread primarily in 2024–2025. The common framework is a new synthesis of those supported mathematical treatments.
