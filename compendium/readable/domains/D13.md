## Overview

This domain supplies exact finite objects on which the atlas’s more abstract ideas can be tested. Its organizing operation is a disjoint decomposition: remove the largest tableau entry, choose the next binary project coordinate, separate a graph along a boundary or decompose a path flow. Such a decomposition is useful only if its pieces represent the declared original objects exactly once. Generating products and completion recurrences make those multiplicities explicit. The resulting counts then support a stronger interface: reversible addresses, exact sampling, conditional populations and a unique optimum proved by a singleton threshold census. This is why population definitions and skipped-coordinate factors belong in the mathematics rather than being deferred to implementation.

Graphs contribute a complementary lesson about observations. An adjacency matrix contains more than its eigenvalues, and a full heat operator contains more than its trace or diagonal. The 2023 rook–Shrikhande collision demonstrates an exact loss of structure. The 2026 seven-vertex pair refines the issue: the entire binary size-cut census agrees, while one integer-height coefficient separates the graphs. This is a model for constructive research throughout the report. First prove the collision at the weaker observation; then identify a richer observable and calculate a finite separating witness. It gives a defensible improvement without claiming that the stronger invariant solves every graph-isomorphism problem.

Incidence matrices link this graph thread to topology and optimization. Their rank yields cycle dimension, and spanning forests produce explicit integer cycle bases. The finite DAG path-difference theorem explains which conservation directions can be expressed as differences of admissible routes. Directed acyclicity does not remove undirected circulation: the diamond is the smallest useful reminder. At the optimization boundary, a matching witnesses a necessary number of vertex calls and a cover supplies a sufficient number. The two meet in a complete proof only after the permitted action is fixed. Unrecovered historical call rules and extremal point lists therefore remain concrete retrieval obligations.

The final family turns these principles into composition. Sum–product and min–plus elimination preserve different semiring values through distributivity. A compact separator stores exactly the interaction that future factors can observe. Erasing a physical lane or adding an auxiliary gate is safe only under a corresponding fibre theorem. Equal optimum values can coexist with unequal counting measures, as the 43,680 physical channel assignments and 256 bank tuples show. The interactive capacities follow directly: L02 exposes counts and addresses, L03 inspects a complete graph-invariant collision, and L09 computes boundaries and homology on small declared complexes. These are bounded, inspectable mathematical models. The general elimination, large Petersen optimization and unrecovered routing benchmarks remain recipes or source claims until their actual instances and complete certificates are supplied.
