Limits: Six fixed items; requested rank is clamped to the feasible range. The recovered annual records do not contain the complete Petersen-50 input table. Inverse ranking returns −1 for an infeasible query mask.

[L03 — A graph census collision that higher heights resolve](../atlas/output/Mathematics_Atlas_2023_2026.html#laboratory)

Exact integer census; floating evaluation of its polynomial

Limits: Two explicit seven-vertex graphs. Equality of the binary census is verified for this pair only; it is not a universal graph reconstruction theorem.

[L06 — Exact polynomial Moyal bracket](../atlas/output/Mathematics_Atlas_2023_2026.html#laboratory)

Exact BigInt falling factorials and rational coefficients

Limits: One canonical pair (q,p), polynomial monomials, and the stated Weyl–Moyal convention. It does not classify arbitrary polynomial subspaces, nonlinear star products or all deformations.

[L07 — Cyclotomic phase fibres](../atlas/output/Mathematics_Atlas_2023_2026.html#laboratory)

Enumeration of all 384 units modulo 1680

Limits: Exact phase counting. Projective braid-image closure requires the actual generating matrices and cannot be inferred from phase counts alone.

[L09 — Homology changes when a face is filled](../atlas/output/Mathematics_Atlas_2023_2026.html#laboratory)

Exact rational boundary ranks

Limits: Homology over Q of these small finite complexes. It does not compute integral torsion, sheaf cohomology or arbitrary persistent homology.

[L10 — Complete Pell orbits, exact integer navigation](../atlas/output/Mathematics_Atlas_2023_2026.html#laboratory)

Exact integer unit recurrence and norm identity

Limits: The norm equation x²−2y²=−7 with its two reduced orbit seeds. The general Pell theory and other quadratic fields need their own reduction and local analysis.
