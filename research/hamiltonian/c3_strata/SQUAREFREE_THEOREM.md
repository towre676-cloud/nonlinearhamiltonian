# Squarefree binary sextics: exact closure and discriminant saturation

November 8, 2026. Proposed extension of the maintained root-strata theorem,
based on main commit `b17ff8568ff81067274e87486ced2a397f2a53e3`.

## Theorem

Let K be any characteristic-zero field and H a homogeneous binary sextic
with six distinct geometric roots. With the maintained conventions

\[
T_k(F,G)=\sum_{j=0}^k(-1)^j\binom{k}{j}
  (\partial_q^{k-j}\partial_p^j F)
  (\partial_q^j\partial_p^{k-j}G),\qquad D_H=T_1(H,\cdot),
\]

define \(S^r(H)\) by the vanishing of both \(T_3(H,D_H^iG)\) and
\(T_5(H,D_H^iG)\) for \(0\le i\le r\). Let \(D_\infty(H)\) be the
intersection over all r. Then

\[
\boxed{S^1(H)=S^r(H)=D_\infty(H)=\operatorname{span}_K\{1,H\}
\quad(r\ge1).}
\]

Thus C3 holds throughout the squarefree stratum. There is no exceptional
squarefree coefficient locus. This conclusion is a computer-assisted
characteristic-zero proof, conditional on the inherited static-tail theorem
already used by the maintained classification. It is not a proof-assistant
formalization or independent external review.

The new finite proof establishes every degree 1 through 16. The existing
static-tail certificate excludes even static partners in degrees at least 17
for squarefree H. No repeated-root statement or certificate is replaced.
Together with the maintained repeated-root classification, C3 therefore fails
among nonzero homogeneous binary sextics exactly on root type (4,1,1).

## 1. A global simple-root normal form

Work over an algebraic closure; descent is addressed below. Move any simple
root to q=0. After a nonzero scalar multiplication the coefficient of qp^5
is 1. A shear \(p\mapsto p+u q\) removes the q^2p^4 coefficient: its change
is 5u. This gives

\[
H_{a,b,c,d}=qp^5+a q^3p^3+b q^4p^2+c q^5p+d q^6.
\]

No assumption on a, b, c or d is made. In particular their zero loci are
included. GL₂ covariance, already established in the maintained theorem, is

\[
T_k(F\circ A,G\circ A)=\det(A)^kT_k(F,G)\circ A.
\]

For the i-th dynamical iterate the factor is \(\det(A)^{k+i}\).
Rescaling H by t multiplies that gate by \(t^{i+1}\). All these factors
are nonzero, so the zero conditions and kernel dimensions are preserved.

The root q=0 is automatically simple in this normal form. Its other roots
are the roots of the monic quintic

\[
f(x)=x^5+a x^3+b x^2+c x+d.
\]

Consequently H is squarefree exactly when
\(\Delta(a,b,c,d)=\operatorname{disc}_x(f)\ne0\). The certificate stores
this exact polynomial sparsely; the verifier recomputes it independently.
This is a normal form for every squarefree GL₂ orbit, rather than a generic
chart that can omit exceptional squarefree forms.

## 2. Constant pivots reduce each gate matrix to two columns

Let \(V_n\) have ordered basis \(p^n,qp^{n-1},\ldots,q^n\), and form

\[
M_n=\begin{bmatrix}T_3(H,\cdot)\\T_5(H,\cdot)\\
T_3(H,D_H\cdot)\\T_5(H,D_H\cdot)\end{bmatrix}_{V_n}.
\]

Rows are sorted by (iterate depth, gate order, q exponent, p exponent).
For each j=2,...,n, select the row with q exponent j−2 in T₃. Its
coefficient in column j is

\[
60j(j-1)(n-2j+2).
\]

If this coefficient is zero, instead select that q exponent in T₃D_H.
Its coefficient in column j is

\[
60j(j-1)(n-6j)(n+6-2j).
\]

At the only resonant value n=2j−2 this is
\(-240j(j-1)(4j+2)\ne0\). Every selected row has zero entries in columns
greater than j. Hence the submatrix on columns 2,...,n is lower triangular
with nonzero rational constant diagonal. This includes the small resonant
degrees 2 and 4, where a static-only shortcut would lose a pivot.

Forward substitution expresses every possible kernel vector uniquely as

\[
g=L_n(a,b,c,d)\begin{bmatrix}g_0\\g_1\end{bmatrix},\qquad
L_n[0:2,:]=I_2,
\]

where \(L_n(a,b,c,d)\) is the recorded reconstruction matrix.
Its entries are in Q[a,b,c,d]; only nonzero rational numbers are divided
out. Put \(R_n=M_nL_n\). Then \(\ker M_n\cong\ker R_n\) at **every**
parameter value. The verifier checks the pivot structure and all pivot
equations directly, rather than trusting a generic row reduction.

For n=6, the second column of the reconstruction is exactly H, whose
qp^5 coefficient is 1. Therefore \(R_6[:,1]=0\); the first column tests
the complement g₁=0 to the unavoidable core. For all other n in 1,...,16,
both residual columns must be independent.

## 3. Exact determinantal ideal membership

For n≠6 let I_n be the ideal of all 2-by-2 minors of R_n; let I_6 be
the ideal generated by the entries of R_6[:,0]. The constant-pivot
reduction identifies these with the relevant maximal-minor ideals of
M_n (on the stated core complement for n=6), up to units in Q.

`squarefree_certificate.json` proves the stronger-than-radical statement

\[
\boxed{\Delta\in I_n\quad\text{for every }n=1,\ldots,16.}
\]

It uses selected minors, not an assumption that one previously nonzero
sample minor stays nonzero. There are 185 selected reduced minors in total:
181 of order two and four of order one at degree six. Some selected
generators can be zero or redundant; independence of the generators is
neither claimed nor needed. The replay verifies 151 intermediate
polynomial identities and 16 final discriminant identities.

Every intermediate node is recorded as
\(P_j=\sum_{i<j}u_{ji}P_i\), with rational polynomial coefficients and
the initial nodes equal to the selected minors. Each final identity is
\(\Delta=\sum_i v_iP_i\). Thus membership is established by multiplication
and addition in Q[a,b,c,d], independent of a Groebner-basis algorithm's
correctness or completeness. The largest certificate uses degree 16,
with a 68-by-17 original matrix and 15 constant pivots.

In particular, exact discriminant saturation is settled in every degree:

\[
I_n:\Delta^\infty=(1).
\]

Equivalently, adjoining z gives an explicit unit identity

\[
1=z\sum_i v_iP_i-(z\Delta-1)
\quad\text{in }I_n+(z\Delta-1).
\]

No high-degree power of the discriminant, numerical rank tolerance, or
positive-characteristic lift is required. If Δ≠0, the residual rank is two
for n≠6 and one for n=6. The finite homogeneous kernel dimensions are
therefore

| Degree | Dimension of S¹_n for every squarefree H |
|---|---:|
| 0 | 1, generated by the constant |
| 1–5 | 0 |
| 6 | 1, generated by H |
| 7–16 | 0 |
| ≥17 | 0, by the inherited static-tail theorem |

## 4. Elimination geometry and modular diagnostics

`squarefree_experiments.py` separately reproduces 46 exact partner-chart
eliminations. Write R_n=[A_n B_n]. The residual torus q↦λq, followed by
rescaling H by λ⁻¹, acts on (a,b,c,d) with weights (2,3,4,5).
For n≠6 a nonzero pair (g₀,g₁) is either (1,0), (0,1), or can be
normalized to (1,1) using this torus and a scalar on G. These are the
ideals generated by A_n, B_n, and A_n+B_n. At n=6 one subtracts the
H component first, leaving only the A_n chart.

Over Q their reduced grevlex Groebner bases are (a,b,c,d), except for:

| Degree and chart | Reduced basis |
|---|---|
| 2, A | a², b, c, d |
| 2, A+B | d²−d/8192, a+1280d, b−320d, c+30d |

The origin represents qp^5, of root type (5,1). The sole nonzero
geometric point in the second exceptional chart is

\[
(a,b,c,d)=(-5/32,5/128,-15/4096,1/8192).
\]

It gives the already classified repeated-root form and partner

\[
H=q(p-q/8)^4(p+q/2),\qquad G=(p-q/8)(p+9q/8).
\]

Both gates kill G and DG. The subsequent iterate gives

\[
T_3(H,D_H^2G)=-\frac{2025}{134217728}(8p-q)^{10}\ne0.
\]

This is the known (4,1,1) failure, recovered by the new elimination; it
is not a squarefree exception. The theorem rests on the independently
replayed determinantal identities in §3, so these Groebner outputs are
additional geometric information rather than a necessary trust step.

The seeded modular diagnostic checks 64 squarefree normalized forms over
each of F₁₀₁ and F₁₀₃: 128 forms and 2,048 full gate-matrix ranks. It
finds no rank defects beyond the degree-six H kernel. All sampled inputs
and ranks are recorded. These finite-field observations are not used to
infer any characteristic-zero theorem.

## 5. All degrees, descent, and preservation

The unchanged static-tail checker verifies threshold 17, 181 elimination
steps, 31 contradictions, and two repeated-root target branches. Its
conclusion excludes static partners in n≥17 when H is squarefree.
This is an all-degree certificate, not high-degree sampling.

The conditions are graded: for each fixed depth and gate, distinct
homogeneous input degrees have distinct output degrees. Consequently no
mixed-degree cancellation can create another partner. The finite result
and inherited tail prove S¹=span{1,H}. D annihilates both generators,
so every further refinement and the invariant core equal that same space.

For a nonsplitting characteristic-zero field K, extend to its algebraic
closure to make the normal-form argument. Finite-dimensional kernels of
matrices over K commute with field extension degree by degree. The
one-dimensional degree-six kernel contains H over K and is therefore KH;
the other asserted zero kernels descend as well. No rational-root
assumption is imposed.

The previous repeated-root theorem, its rational counterexample and
second-iterate obstruction, the triple-root Bezout certificates, and the
restricted-family theorem are unchanged. The original 16 nonzero sample
minors remain valid as a regression example; the new universal identities
now resolve the exceptional locus that those sample minors left open.

## Reproduction and trust boundary

From the repository root, using Python 3 and SymPy 1.14.0:

```bash
python research/hamiltonian/c3_strata/squarefree_certificate.py --output /tmp/squarefree-regenerated.json
python research/hamiltonian/c3_strata/verify_squarefree.py /tmp/squarefree-regenerated.json
python -m unittest discover -s research/hamiltonian/c3_strata -p test_squarefree.py -v
python -m unittest discover -s research/hamiltonian/c3_strata -p test_strata.py -v
python research/hamiltonian/c3_uniform/inputs/check_binary_static.py research/hamiltonian/c3_uniform/inputs/binary_static_d6.json
python research/hamiltonian/c3_uniform/monomial_proof.py
# Optional discovery/diagnostics, not required for the theorem:
python research/hamiltonian/c3_strata/squarefree_experiments.py --output /tmp/squarefree-experiments.json
```

The producer uses the maintained falling-factorial coefficient engine.
The independent verifier rebuilds the gates by literal symbolic
differentiation and checks explicit identities, using no producer imports,
Groebner calculations, floating point, or modular ranks. It raises explicit
exceptions rather than relying on assertions. Focused tests reject missing
degrees, corrupt discriminants and identities, circular references, omitted
resonant pivots, changed reconstruction data, and invalid minor orders.
They also test GL₂ covariance with determinant five.

Python/SymPy arithmetic, the written covariance and normal-form arguments,
and the inherited static-tail theorem remain the trust boundary. Formal
verification and external review remain open; the squarefree geometric
classification itself is resolved by these certificates.
