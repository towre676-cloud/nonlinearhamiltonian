# General non-affine sixfold-leading discovery engine

For an arbitrary rational sextic with a sixfold leading root, make a
rational symplectic coordinate change so the leading homogeneous part
is \(q^6\). The remaining perturbation is arbitrary in degree at most 5:

\[
H=q^6+\sum_{i+j\le5}c_{ij}q^ip^j.
\]

There are 21 lower-order coefficient parameters (including the harmless
constant). This module can accept any chosen symbolic subfamily; a complete
21-parameter all-degree classification is **not** claimed.

## Universal leading-degree theorem

For every degree \(N\), the highest-degree homogeneous part of
\(T_3(H,G)=0\) is exactly

\[
120q^3\partial_p^3 G_N=0.
\]

Therefore the leading term of any nonzero static partner satisfies

\[
G_N=Aq^N+Bq^{N-1}p+Cq^{N-2}p^2
\]

with the natural omissions when \(N<2\). This is valid for all \(N\)
and all lower-order perturbations, not a finite sample. It supplies the
degree filtration for subsequent recurrence and exceptional-locus work.

## General exact discovery API

`general_sixfold_discovery.py` constructs the *untruncated*
rational/symbolic observation matrix

[
mathcal O_{N,r}(H)=(T_3(H,D^j(-)),T_5(H,D^j(-)))_{0le jle r},
quad D=T_1(H,-),
]

on all input polynomials of degree at most (N). It can compute the
entire determinantal ideal defining the locus of rank at most a chosen
threshold. An exact minor budget is enforced **before computation**:
the engine rejects searches it cannot finish rather than silently
subsample minors and claim completeness. Exact rational specializations
record kernel bases and dimensions.

For the two-parameter non-affine family

[
H_{a,b}=q^6+ap^3+bqp^2,
]

the complete degree-(le2), depth-one rank-(le3) ideal is

[
oxed{(a^3b^2)}.
]

Its algebraic zero set is the union of the parameter axes (a=0) and
(b=0). This is a complete **bounded incidence** classification for
that window, not an all-degree theorem about (H_{a,b}).

| Parameters | (dim S^1_{le2}) | (dim S^2_{le2}) |
|---|---:|---:|
| (a=b=0) | 6 | 6 |
| (a=1,b=0) | 3 | 1 |
| (a=0,b=1) | 4 | 2 |
| (a=b=1) | 2 | 1 |

An independent verifier expands the defining Weyl–Moyal star exponential
and checks these dimensions against the direct transvectant engine.

## All-degree cubic static tail and generic core

A stronger exact theorem is available for the full cubic perturbation slice

\[
H=q^6+u q^3+v q^2p+w qp^2+x p^3,\qquad x\ne0.
\]

For **every polynomial degree**, the static correction-free space is exactly
15-dimensional, and every static partner has total degree at most nine.
This is not inferred from a bounded rank computation.

To see this, the fifth gate is
\[
T_5(H,G)=720q\,\partial_p^5G.
\]
Thus \(G=\sum_{j=0}^4 g_j(q)p^j\). The third gate becomes
\[
(20q^3+u)G_{ppp}-vG_{qpp}+wG_{qqp}-xG_{qqq}=0,
\]
whose \(p^j\) coefficient is the triangular equation
\[
x g_j'''=(20q^3+u)(j+3)(j+2)(j+1)g_{j+3}
-v(j+2)(j+1)g_{j+2}'+w(j+1)g_{j+1}''.
\]
Set \(g_j=0\) for \(j>4\). Because \(x\ne0\), this determines \(g_j'''\)
from already determined higher-\(p\) coefficients. Each \(g_j\) has exactly
three free quadratic integration constants, yielding \(5\cdot3=15\)
parameters. The degree bounds from descending induction are
\[
\deg g_4\le2,\quad\deg g_3\le3,\quad\deg g_2\le4,\quad
\deg g_1\le8,\quad\deg g_0\le9.
\]
An explicit polynomial basis is generated by
`cubic_static_recurrence.static_basis` and all 15 vectors have
both gates checked symbolically.

**Generic all-degree refinement theorem.** On a nonempty Zariski-open
subset of the rational parameter space (x
e0),

[
oxed{
S^1(H)=operatorname{span}{1,q,H},qquad
S^2(H)=D_infty(H)=operatorname{span}{1,H}.
}
]

Indeed (1,q,H) always satisfy the first refinement: (Dq=-H_p) has
total degree at most two, so its third/fifth gates vanish; (1,H) are
first integrals. The exact 15-dimensional static reduction bounds the
refinement ranks by 12 and 13. At the rational parameter point
((u,v,w,x)=(0,0,1,1)), explicit minors certify those ranks:

| Stage | Matrix rank | Exact minor determinant |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| First refinement | 12 | (-69543841933526310447934655692800000000) |
| Second refinement | 13 | (901288191458500983405233137778688000000000) |

The exact row and column indices, full matrix construction, and independent
rank checks are in `cubic_static_recurrence.generic_minor_certificate`.
Each nonzero specialization witnesses a nonzero polynomial/rational-function
minor in the parameters. The intersection of their nonvanishing loci is a
nonempty Zariski-open set, establishing the generic ranks without degree
cutoffs. The surviving second-stage basis is Poisson-invariant.

Exceptional cubic parameters are the proper algebraic rank-drop varieties
cut out by these full 15-column matrices. Their complete decomposition,
and arbitrary lower perturbations of degrees four and five, **remain open**.

For example, (u=v=w=0,x=1) has dimensions
(15	o4	o2) rather than the generic (15	o3	o2).
This is a genuine exceptional first-refinement stratum.
