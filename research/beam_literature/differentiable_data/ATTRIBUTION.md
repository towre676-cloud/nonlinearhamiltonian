# Memory-scaling data attribution

The two CSV files in this directory originate from Arjun Dhamrait and collaborators, Supplemental Materials: The memory scaling of reverse-mode differentiation in particle accelerator simulations with space charge, concept DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17822325, resolved version record https://zenodo.org/records/17822326. They accompany https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27282 and DOI https://doi.org/10.1145/3815572.3815749.

The Zenodo metadata declares CC-BY-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The downloaded CSV bytes are unchanged. The local replay and its ordinary least-squares model are our analysis, not the authors' original robust regression. Original checksums, resolved URLs and our SHA-256 hashes are in sources.json. No experimental or industrial speedup is inferred from these memory records.
