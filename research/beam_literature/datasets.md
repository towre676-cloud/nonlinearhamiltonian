# Public beam data and runnable benchmark assets

Audit date: 2026-10-23 UTC. This is a targeted primary-source survey of accelerator tuning, diagnostics, phase-space inference and storage-ring acceptance. It is not an assertion that every dataset worldwide has been found. Public files, executable examples and data available only on request are classified separately. No facility account or live machine was accessed.

The most immediately useful new benchmark asset is the **official public HMBA storage-ring lattice**. Its independent-particle acceptance queries permit adaptive evaluation of selected initial conditions, whereas one selected macroparticle cannot replace the collective self-field in a PIC calculation. This is an inference about workload structure, not a measured tenfold result. For the original beamline programme, **DESY ARES** supplies real tuning histories and a reproducible benchmark, while **SNS tomography** supplies the measured non-Gaussian profiles and correlations that our Gaussian FODO fixture lacks. None of these archives alone establishes a closed-loop speedup over a strong ordinary controller.

## Concrete ingestion, including a source discrepancy

We downloaded only small representative material. A 65,536-byte HTTP range from the end of the SNS archive supplied its complete central directory and one real measured wire-profile member. Python's ZIP reader verified the member CRC. The extracted profile has 63,228 bytes and SHA-256 `d473a7541bb14361e27da46ea8454dca68beb02a93c9014562b88459f6967f2f`. Its acquisition header is 2024-08-19 19:10:01 EDT. The accompanying archive note identifies that acquisition as production optics at 1.0 GeV; a separate 1.3 GeV acquisition and deliberately shifted phase advances are also present. Raw wire coordinates require the documented calibration and optics transformations before becoming physical covariance constraints. No bulk archive was downloaded.

For LANSCE, the retrieved RF file is an actual `(100, 8)` float32 array. A separate 256-byte range from the much larger phase-space file declares `(100, 48, 15, 256, 256)` float32, consistent with its published byte count. The README instead claims `(500, 8)` and 500 phase-space examples. Both observations are preserved rather than silently trusting the narrative. The full phase-space payload has not been checksum-validated or loaded, so the array header is an inspected declaration, not a bulk-data-integrity certificate. Do not conflate this published subset with all runs described in later papers.

Run `python research/beam_literature/check_dataset_samples.py` to recheck the small licensed samples, lattice hashes and retained shape discrepancy without network or bulk downloads. No downloaded pickle or model file was executed. The ingestion manifest records source URLs, attribution, licenses and hashes. Large data remain at their source.

## What is and is not a suitable next test

For a next **tenfold computational** experiment, the storage-ring case is the better immediate alternative: use a fixed public nonlinear lattice and evaluate dynamic/momentum acceptance for independent initial conditions. This gives a clean, expensive query that can be selected adaptively. The outcome is survival/acceptance under a declared finite turn count, not a theorem of infinite-time stability. Once the low-level physics is fixed, use new magnet settings held out from all model selection and count complete simulation/optimizer costs.

The public HMBA MAT file has 122 records, including its RingParam metadata record, in a 26.3742879523 m cell, periodicity 32 and energy 6 GeV. The represented full circumference is 843.977214474 m. There are six separately named sextupoles (`SD1A`, `SD1B`, `SD1D`, `SD1E`, `SF2A`, `SF2E`) and two multipoles with octupole coefficients (`OF1B`, `OF1D`). Mirror partners initially share strengths; allowing them to vary independently changes the symmetry contract and must be stated. A chromaticity-response nullspace is a local or ideal-model constraint only after its rank and preservation are checked. The RF cavity initially uses `IdentityPass`, so six-dimensional/radiation settings cannot be presumed active. The exact source commit is `1ed84a3f1b76a1cb2bd6deb48e26b5a30bb7256c`, verified by the commit API and commit-pinned re-downloads; the commit tree is `879c9387046d0bcfe5af4c34ef5ad9b2db5ce2f7`.

The integration owner separately completed a [PyAT smoke test](public_ring_smoke.json), expanding 121 physical cell elements to 3,872 full-ring elements and setting periodicity to one. For 64 initial points and 128 full turns, individual and batched final states agreed exactly, with 42 survivors and 22 losses. Warm, single-thread batching saved only about 1.08-fold. This is feasibility and cost-model evidence, with RF/radiation/space charge/errors disabled; it supplies no optimization or tenfold result. This dataset lane performed the independent MAT/provenance inspection, not that tracking experiment.

The strongest ordinary acceptance competitors are already available. [PyAT acceptance](https://atcollab.github.io/at/p/api/at.acceptance.acceptance.html) supports recursive boundary searches, momentum acceptance, parallel tracking and shared work across multiple observation points; [its package index](https://atcollab.github.io/at/p/api/at.acceptance.html) also exposes flood-fill acceptance. A comparison solely against a serial full grid would be inadequate. [Sirius pyModels](https://github.com/lnls-fac/pymodels), with its MIT license, is a useful second public lattice family, but this audit did not execute its external pyaccel/trackcpp dependency chain. The downloaded SPEAR3 source supplies a smaller alternative in the same Apache-licensed AT tree. HMBA is not evidence that the newly published mpBAX study's SSRL-X lattice, training state or raw logs are public.

For the original **collective beamline** task, use ARES to reproduce established controller comparisons and SNS to attack the output contract. Split by complete trial, acquisition day, beam condition and operating region, not nearby samples or frames. A held-out projection from the same beam is useful consistency evidence but is not an independent facility tune. A dataset collected along one optimizer's actions generally cannot answer how another optimizer would perform at unmeasured settings; a documented virtual accelerator or new reference calls are needed. The same restriction applies to offline nearest-neighbor replay.

There is substantial prior art behind our covariance discussion. [MENT](https://github.com/austin-hoover/ment) already fits covariance from projections and expressly treats phase-space reconstruction as an underdetermined inference relative to a prior. Its lineage includes maximum-entropy source reconstruction in 1979 and four-dimensional beam tomography in 1981. Our finite-width blind-mode example is useful evidence for this established observability issue. It does not establish novelty for covariance fitting, projection selection or maximum-entropy reconstruction. Any claimed gain must beat their simple covariance-fit path when the requested output is only a covariance, rather than unnecessarily reconstructing an entire density.

## Sources with real results but no verified public population

[SwissFEL longitudinal virtual diagnostics, DOI 10.1063/5.0179712](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/rsi/article/95/1/015110/3022973/Machine-learning-based-longitudinal-virtual), reports data available from the corresponding author on reasonable request. [The 2026 multi-facility MAX IV/FERMI/SwissFEL diagnostic study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-47195-1) likewise states an on-request availability policy. These are relevant operational evidence, but not immediately downloadable benchmarks verified here. We did not contact anyone. A high test-image score is also not an optimizer-cost result.

For AWA surrogate optimization, [Edelen et al., PRAB 23, 044601](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevAccelBeams.23.044601) is essential prior work. It describes simulation populations and optimization verification, but the inspected article does not itself establish a downloadable complete raw population. Its stated framework already includes model warm starts with local correction and simulation/measurement discrepancy learning. Those ideas should be treated as established competitors. The available GPSR and MENT phase-space examples answer a different diagnostic question from the original AWA optimization population.

The immediately defensible programme is therefore a paired test: a public storage-ring acceptance task for selective expensive computation, and measured SNS/ARES evidence to enforce an honest output/validation contract. **No public archive found here guarantees tenfold gains over the best ordinary workflow.** It supplies the means to make that claim falsifiable without fabricating facility access or discarding the covariance failures we already observed.
