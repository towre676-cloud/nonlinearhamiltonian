It also records all 71 concept families, the four annual reference lists and the Hamiltonian research context. A reference is evidence of a retained citation; it does not imply that every theorem in that resource is invoked or reproved here.

Use the [domain and cross-domain reading routes](READING_ROUTES.md), [research queue](RESEARCH_QUEUE.md) and [source-by-source index](SOURCE_INDEX.md). Machine-readable provenance, exact URL variants, labels and usage paths are in [references.json](references.json).

## Counting and URL identity

Conservative canonical document URL: lowercase scheme/host and remove only fragment. Preserve scheme, path case, query and all exact original URLs. No mirror, redirect, DOI/arXiv-version or HTTP/HTTPS equivalence is guessed.

source_uid_count counts unique treatments that explicitly retain the URL in their reference fields, including separately marked original historical refs. Family, annual-list and Hamiltonian-context citations are counted separately; family membership does not imply direct source citation.

A source count links to its complete treatment list. A count of zero can therefore be correct for a family-only, annual-list-only or Hamiltonian-context resource. Resources hosted as third-party PDFs are linked rather than redistributed.
